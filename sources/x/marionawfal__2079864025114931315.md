🇺🇸 NATO allies agreed on $3B budget for new defense deals and joint ventures at the summit in Ankara.

The package includes missile production, maintenance facilities, drone surveillance, and weapons manufacturing projects across Europe involving companies like Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, RTX, Boeing, and Anduril. 

Supporters say it’ll strengthen NATO’s defenses. Critics will probably call it what it is: an enormous bill for the privilege of building a more efficient war machine, with everyone clapping politely as the industrial bazooka gets another upgrade.
