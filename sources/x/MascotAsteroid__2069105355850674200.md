Asteroid Day is an annual global awareness event held on June 30 to educate people about asteroids, their scientific importance, and the potential risk of asteroid impacts on Earth.

Asteroid Day was inspired by the anniversary of the 1908 Tunguska event, when a large asteroid fragment exploded over Siberia, flattening about 2,000 square kilometers of forest. In 2016, the United Nations officially recognized June 30 as the International Day of the Asteroid.

Now that Asteroid is also the mascot of @SpaceX, this holiday has become an interesting overlap! SpaceX contributes to asteroid research and their mascot is Asteroid. We look forward to this being the first time the holiday has such an overlap.
