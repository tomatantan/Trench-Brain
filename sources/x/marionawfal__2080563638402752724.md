🇸🇦🇮🇷 Saudi oil is being pushed through the Suez route as Hormuz and Bab el-Mandeb stay disrupted, but the “backup plan” is expensive.

According to LSEG data, fuel costs for a Saudi oil tanker nearly double from about $1.26M to $2.87M while transiting the Suez Canal adds roughly $1 million in canal fees.

The longer voyage also means higher insurance premiums, longer delivery times, and fewer tankers available for other shipments, adding further pressure to global oil markets.
