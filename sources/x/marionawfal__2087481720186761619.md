🇮🇱🇹🇷🇨🇾 Israel had a simple reply to Turkey's criticism: look at your own footprint first.

"Turkey has the audacity to criticize Israel. But what are the facts on the ground?"

The ministry then rattled off thousands of Turkish troops stationed abroad and bases in Cyprus, Syria and Iraq.

Its list: Turkey occupies 36% of Cyprus, 5% of Syria and roughly 2,000 square kilometres of Iraq.

Israel then framed its own footprint as minimal by comparison, saying it "temporarily controls 0.1%" of Syrian territory as a security zone to protect its citizens.

That 0.1% doesn't count the Golan Heights, which Israel took from Syria in 1967 and annexed in 1981, and which the UN still regards as occupied.

Two countries, a lot of borders that aren't quite theirs.
