🇺🇦🇧🇾 Ukraine is clearing villages on the Belarus border.

The Chernihiv Defense Council ordered a mandatory evacuation of 12 settlements across four border communities, acting on a direct request from the military.

Roughly 1,000 people are still living in them, and 120 of those are children.

The operation kicks off July 1, with crews handling routes, transport, and temporary housing, and the goal is to have everyone out inside 2 months.

What sets this one apart is the geography. Most of the villages hug the Russian border, but a couple sit right on the Belarus line, the exact direction Kyiv has been watching for any hint of a fresh offensive.

You don't empty a border town on a hunch.
