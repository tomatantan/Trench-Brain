What bulls are looking for is the language that CPI spikes were heavily driven by oil. That would be best hint towards future cuts we’ll probably get. If next CPI comes in even a bit lower, we unlock next leg in all this rallying. (NVDA at 10T etc.)

With the Fable ban, the heightening in “national security” posturing against China is enough of a justification to believe the AI trade will continue to be too big to fail & propped up by this admin. 

There’s always FUD dips or months of drawdowns but peak price window feels like it’s drifting towards start of 2027. 

All of this is going to be dependent on the frontier labs creating another round of groundbreaking advancements in the models though. Easier said than done.

In 2025, the entire AI industry felt like it was drifting lower throughout the whole year, it wasn’t until Opus 4.5 (a real step change) came out that the entire sector woke up violently.

So things we’re looking for:
-oil staying down so rates can be cut
-frontier labs delivering another groundbreaking model (mythos doesn’t count imo, needs to actually be crazier)
-innovation that makes frontier models radically cheaper to run

I think the biggest risk to the whole sector is if we get a string of disappointing models from the major labs. It would kill the future growth narrative. 

At this point though feels like smartest brains on the planet are working on that, so that’s the continuation bet. It’s still a bet on the labs, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and now SpaceX.
