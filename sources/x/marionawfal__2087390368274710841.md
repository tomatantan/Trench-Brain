🇺🇸 A 13-year-old boy in Utah spent his entire summer building a custom wheelchair for a 21-month-old girl he’s never met.

Zac Waters put in more than 300 hours designing, printing and assembling it after discovering a nonprofit that makes assistive devices. 

He added pink accents because that’s her favorite color, a removable cup holder and tray, soft straps that won’t dig in, and adjustable parts so the chair can grow with her.

When they asked why, he said it combines the two things he loves most: 3D printing and helping people who need it.
