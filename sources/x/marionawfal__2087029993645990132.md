-U.S. officials tell the Wall Street Journal the president has settled on economic pressure, giving sanctions and the naval blockade of Iranian ports more time to do damage

-"They have no money," he told reporters today. "Iran is broke, totally broke"

-UN Ambassador Mike Waltz said Tehran fears Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent more than Pete Hegseth, arguing the regime will absorb bombings but panics over a collapsing currency and the worst inflation in 80 years

-A late July AP NORC poll put approval of Trump's handling of the war at 28 percent, down from 34 percent a month earlier, with Republicans nervous about the midterms
