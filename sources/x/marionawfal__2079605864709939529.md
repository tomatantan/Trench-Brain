🇺🇸🇱🇧 3 options are on the table for disarming Hezbollah

1: the U.S. trains, equips and vets elite Lebanese army units that take over southern Lebanon from Israeli forces, then attempt disarmament if it comes to that. 

2: Syria squeezes the supply routes. 

3: Israel keeps bombing.

The first option has a problem its own architects have named. U.S. and Lebanese security exchanges concluded the Lebanese army lacks the institutional capability and firepower to confront Hezbollah. Which is why the training focuses on planning, intelligence and coordination rather than combat.

Sources briefed on it cite Afghanistan and Iraq as the failure modes, over corruption, sectarian division and Hezbollah's reach inside state institutions.

Option 1 is standing up a national army against a militia embedded inside the same state. Option 3 is what's already happening. There isn't a third thing here so much as a delay before choosing between the first 2.
