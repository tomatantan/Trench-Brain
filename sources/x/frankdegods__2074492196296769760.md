This is the first AI dip I can remember where it feels like a majority of analysts are buying dips and saying the trade is still intact.

In past drawdowns like DeepSeek, Burry's circular deal FUD or Iran War, it felt like consensus was that the bubble has popped.

I remember sometimes feeling like the only bull remaining on the AI trade in my circles at the time. That's certainly not the case anymore. 

A lot of the biggest outperformers from the 1st half of the year have now amassed massive followings and there are armies of retail buying almost every dip.

Retail buying in terms of % of daily volume is basically same as it was at the peak in 2021. If it isn't obvious, everyone is heavily leveraged at the highs and if software/hyperscalers start to see momentum, then that could cause the final leg of the deleveraging.

Based on sentiment, we still are not near the fear levels that warrant a true bottom on the AI build out trade. 

I wouldn't be surprised if the narrative of companies cutting costs on AI capex continues to gain traction until the MAG7 earnings near the end of the month. Chips, semis, memory, all this stuff keeps taking hits and chops at the lows.

As someone that has no intention to sell any of the positions I've been building since early 2025, I can only hope that the blood continues until the majority believes the trade is genuinely over once again.
