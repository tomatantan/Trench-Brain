🇻🇪 The world is rushing help to Venezuela.

After the 7.2 and 7.5 quakes hit about 100 miles west of Caracas, killing at least 188, injuring more than 1,500, and leaving around 200 trapped under some 250 damaged buildings, governments and aid groups are moving fast. 

Here's who's sending what.

🇺🇳 United Nations

Aid chief Tom Fletcher says the UN is coordinating international urban search-and-rescue teams, and he warned that 8 million Venezuelans already needed humanitarian aid before the quake even struck.

🇺🇸 United States

Search-and-rescue teams have deployed from Virginia and Los Angeles, with more to follow. Rubio said rescue is the country's most immediate need right now.

🇻🇦 Vatican

Pope Leo XIV sent €100,000, around $114,000, from the Vatican's charity fund.

🍽️ World Central Kitchen

Jose Andres's team is already handing out meals in Caracas, and his Longer Tables Fund is putting in $1 million right away.

🇸🇻 El Salvador

Bukele has 300 rescue workers and paramedics ready to travel, along with 50 tons of medical supplies.

🇲🇽 Mexico

A team of rescuers and medics left today, and Sheinbaum says Mexico will decide tomorrow whether to send more.

🇪🇸 Spain

A military plane is carrying 57 search-and-rescue soldiers and 40 firefighters from the Madrid region, and Spain's development agency plans to set up a field hospital.

🇮🇹 Italy

An advance Civil Protection team is heading in to coordinate the rescuers arriving later, with Tajani mobilizing firefighters, the defense ministry, and the air force.

The list is still growing.
