🚨🇺🇸 BREAKING: Trump wants FIFA boss Gianni Infantino to be the next UN secretary-general

-Trump believes Infantino "is respected by everyone around the world" and has "a special ability to bring people together," per a source close to the president

-The two grew close through the World Cup, with Infantino handing Trump FIFA's inaugural Peace Prize in December after repeated Nobel snubs, golden trophies delivered to the Oval Office, and Trump front and center at Sunday's final

-The path is steep: Security Council endorsement including five veto-holders, then General Assembly confirmation, against the soft rotation favoring a Latin American candidate to replace the retiring Guterres in December

-It's unclear Infantino even wants the job, which pays about $418,000 against his $6 million FIFA salary, and he's already declared for FIFA re-election in March

-The favor ledger runs both ways: Trump intervened to overturn Folarin Balogun's suspension before the Belgium match, outraging European federations, with ex-FIFA chief Sepp Blatter now clamoring for "new leadership"

The nomination fits the pattern Trump has demonstrated across two terms: loyalty ranks above every other trait, competence included, when he hands out the biggest jobs.

Infantino has ingratiated himself with Trump as thoroughly as any figure in world sport, peace prizes, trophy visits, rule-bending for the American roster, and the payoff on offer is the most prominent diplomatic post on Earth.
