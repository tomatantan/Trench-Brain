🇺🇸 Trump makes the pitch for data centers to communities pushing back:

"Any community that turns down AI is making a big mistake. 

Your house is going to be more valuable. 

Data centers, if you look at the stats, everything's good around them."

He's right that the money is real. 

AI investment has driven a huge share of US economic growth, and the construction and tax base are genuine wins for towns that land one.

he local fights are usually about the other stats, though. Data centers pull enormous amounts of power and water, which is a hard sell in a summer where electricity bills and $4 gas are already the story. 

That's the tension he'll have to answer, and "everything's good around them" won't settle it for a county worried about its grid.
