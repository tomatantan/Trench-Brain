unit bias in equities is interesting bc it's structural not physiological:

1. it used to be difficult to buy fractional shares, so share splits did make stock more accessible. this still drives some legacy behavior

2. option prices are derived from price. calls on a $1k stock cost more than a $10 stock even assuming the same company mcap and everything. this matters because retail yolo'ing calls actually drives stocks up, so there is a real incentive to lower share price through splits to enable more retail bidding (can't do fractional options *yet*)

neither of these really apply to crypto bc you can buy everything fractionally and options aren't really relevant compared to perps & other derivs.
