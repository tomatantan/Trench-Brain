🇺🇸🇮🇱 A Palestinian American family has been trapped inside their own home in the West Bank village of Qusra since Sunday, after Israeli settlers set up a structure blocking their front door and cut off their electricity.

The family, a couple and their two young daughters aged 2 and 4, couldn't leave or get food in. 

The homeowner's relative in Ohio watched it unfold over security cameras. 

After 5 days and growing U.S. pressure, Israeli forces finally moved in overnight to clear the settlers out.

Then came the fight over who deserves credit. 

Some said the White House had to "intervene" and was demanding answers from Israel over why the siege was allowed to drag on.

Ambassador Mike Huckabee called that "another lie," insisting the embassy was on it the whole time.

Huckabee:

"The IDF & Israel Police have gone at our request to remove the Israeli terrorists doing this. 

The actions of those doing this to this family's home is criminal. 

The WH hasn't 'intervened' because we have kept DC apprised of the situation already."

He called it a "horrific act of terror" meant to intimidate the family, with "no excuse for such thuggish behavior."
