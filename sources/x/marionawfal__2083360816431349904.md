🚨🇺🇸 A camera caught Bessent's Camp David notepad reading "Buy Japanese Yen $5-10 bil."

-The photo was taken over the Treasury Secretary's shoulder at 11:33 a.m. during the on-the-record portion of Trump's cabinet meeting, showing an underscored "To Do" followed by "Buy Japanese Yen (JPY) $5-10 bil"

-It surfaced after Reuters reported Treasury had told a number of banks, through the New York Fed, that it might step into the yen market that day and should "stand ready for future action"

-The yen had been scraping four-decade lows, trading as weak as 163.65 to the dollar Thursday before Japanese authorities intervened, then strengthening to 159.09 and jumping again late Friday to around 157.6

-Bessent said this week the yen "seems very undervalued to me" and that "excess volatility in the yen isn't healthy," while Japan's top currency official said the American support "goes beyond psychological support"

The last time Treasury propped up the yen was 2011, a coordinated G7 rescue after Japan's earthquake and tsunami.
