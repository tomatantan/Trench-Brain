“Human recreation can take place at countless other beaches throughout San Diego, but suitable pupping habitat is far more limited."

Model and advocate Barrett Pall, a San Diego resident, launched a petition calling on local officials to permanently close La Jolla Children’s Pool Beach to the public to protect its harbor seal population. The petition has already drawn thousands of signatures.

The petition comes after years of unsafe encounters between visitors and seals at the beach, most recently an incident in which a teenager was filmed harassing and kicking a seal. The incident is now under federal investigation. Pall is calling for public access to Children’s Pool Beach itself to be prohibited, “while maintaining public access to surrounding sidewalks, overlooks, and viewing areas.”
