🇺🇸 4 teenagers just got tied to nearly 80 burglaries across the Bay Area

The method: hit parked cars overnight, steal the garage door openers left inside, then use them to walk straight into people's garages while everyone slept.

37 hits in Concord alone. 40 more across Contra Costa and Solano counties. One garage alone gave up 14 firearms. 

Surveillance caught them pointing a stolen gun at an interior door, apparently waiting to see who walked through it.

But 4 kids can't move that much stolen property alone. Police traced the power tools to a 37-year-old Oakland man who was allegedly buying straight from the teens after every job. 

His house: ~$250,000 in stolen goods, including a forklift and an ATV.

Shutting down a teenage burglary ring took a SWAT team, 4 police departments, and the U.S. Marshals.

Property crime with a full supply chain, and the kids were just the labor.
