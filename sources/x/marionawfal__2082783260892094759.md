🇯🇴🇺🇸 Hundreds of Jordanian political, legal, and public figures signed an open letter last week calling for the WITHDRAWAL of U.S. forces from the country...  

It's a rare public challenge in a place that has long cracked down on dissent.  

Jordan hosts around 4,000 American troops and serves as a key hub for U.S. operations in the region.  

As Iranian missiles and drones increasingly target those bases, sirens keep going off and the country is being pulled deeper into a conflict most want no part of.  

The letter argues that the U.S. presence exposes Jordan to security, political, and economic risks and raises the chance of being dragged into a wider war.  

Tourism, which makes up a big share of the economy, has already been hit hard.  

The government relies heavily on American aid ($1.65 billion last year) and has remained firmly aligned with Washington.  

Signatories say they are speaking out of national responsibility, even while knowing the risks of prison.  

Lawmakers are openly clashing over the issue, and Jordanian media largely ignored the letter.
