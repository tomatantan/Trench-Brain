🇺🇸🇨🇳 Apple has trained its own AI model specifically for China, with help from Chinese tech giant Alibaba.

It's a remarkable workaround to China's AI restrictions: ChatGPT and Claude aren't available there, so Apple is building a separate AI ecosystem for Chinese iPhones while also incorporating Alibaba's Qwen and other domestic technology.

Regulators were quick to approve, making Apple the first foreign company to offer its own proprietary AI model in China.
