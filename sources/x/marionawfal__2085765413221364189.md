🇺🇦 🇷🇺 Footage shows Ukrainian FPV interceptor successfully neutralizing a Russian fiber-optic quadcopter using a net.

Fiber-optic drones (which use a thin cable for control instead of radio signals) are harder to jam with electronic warfare. In response, Ukraine has developed and deployed small FPV drones that chase these targets and deploy a net to physically capture or disable them mid-air.

This is a low-cost, kinetic counter-drone method that has become more common as both sides adapt to evolving drone tactics on the battlefield.
