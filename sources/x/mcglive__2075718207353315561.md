.@FloodCapital dropped the alpha on why onchain options never worked, until now.

The whole problem has been delta hedging. Sell a call, and as spot moves toward the strike, your delta drifts toward 1, so MM's have to constantly resize to stay neutral. 

Onchain, there was never a venue deep enough to hedge against, so options never got liquidity.

@SynapseProtocol's fix: a hedge...deposit on Hyperliquid, write calls and puts on Hypercall with portfolio margin, balance delta in real time.
