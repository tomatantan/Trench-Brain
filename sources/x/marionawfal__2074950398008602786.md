🇵🇸🇮🇱 The West Bank is collapsing in slow motion. Nobody has a plan for what happens when it does.

The Palestinian Authority's GDP has contracted over 20% since October 2023. Unemployment is above 30%. Short-term poverty has more than doubled. 

The governing body is described as "all but insolvent" in a new report built on 100 interviews across the region.

Israel's own finance minister, Bezalel Smotrich, cut off all tax transfers to the PA last year and hasn't sent a single shekel since. Those transfers made up roughly two-thirds of the PA's entire budget. 

He's also been using the banking waiver that allows Palestinian banks to function as a bargaining chip, extending it bi-weekly just to remind everyone he controls it.

Smotrich has said outright that collapsing the PA would help bury the idea of a Palestinian state. He's proposed annexing 82% of the West Bank. 

And Netanyahu, for his part, spent years actively encouraging Qatar to fund Hamas in Gaza specifically to keep the PA weak, a strategy multiple political allies confirmed on the record.

Israel's own intelligence agency warns that a PA collapse means chaos directly on Jerusalem's border. The PA employs 30,000 armed security personnel who haven't been paid full salaries since 2021. 

When armed men stop getting paid, the Middle East has a well-documented answer for what comes next.

The PA is corrupt, unelected, and almost universally disliked. It's also the only thing standing between the West Bank and something far worse...
