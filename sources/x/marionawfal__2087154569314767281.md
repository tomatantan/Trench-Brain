🇺🇸🇸🇾🇱🇧 New York’s first lady is heading to Syria and Lebanon next month, along with a taxpayer-funded NYPD security detail.

Rama Duwaji, wife of Mamdani, will travel to both countries starting September 20.

Both are on the State Department’s “Do Not Travel” list.

City officials say the police escort was added on the NYPD’s strong recommendation, after she previously traveled overseas without protection.

The mayor’s office insists the trip is a private family visit, not official city business.
