🇮🇷 The most dangerous target in this war may be something as mundane as a water plant

Buried in the latest strikes is a detail with catastrophic potential.

The Gulf runs on desalination: Kuwait, Bahrain, and Qatar draw nearly all their fresh water from it, the UAE close behind. Iran barely does, under one percent, which means the vulnerability runs entirely one direction.

That asymmetry is the whole danger. 

If strikes on Iranian water infrastructure invite retaliation against Gulf desal plants, the war stops being about oil and ships and becomes about whether cities can drink. 

That is the true red line, the one that empties capitals rather than markets.

Both sides now threaten regional infrastructure. 

Water is the rung nobody survives climbing.
