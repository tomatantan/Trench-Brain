🇷🇺🇮🇷 The world's diesel supply is getting squeezed hard, and the wars are the reason. Obviously.

Goldman says diesel is now "at the epicenter" of a global fuel crunch, with refinery activity at its lowest seasonal level since the pandemic.

Refinery outages in Russia and the Middle East from the ongoing wars, plus slower processing in China, knocked 6.5 million barrels a day off global throughput.

Diesel exports are down 35% from a year ago, and Europe's diesel futures just hit a near 3-month high.

Meanwhile U.S. refiners are running flat out above 97%, cashing in on the chaos.

And the majors are feasting. Shell just posted a $9.8 billion quarterly profit, more than double last year and its second-highest ever, with its refineries running at 102% of capacity.

Who would have thought bombing oil-producing regions for months would show up at the pump?
