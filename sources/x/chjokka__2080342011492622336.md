If the environment on a chain is dominated by rotators, the smartest play is positioning in utility coins you think will still be relevant in 2 to 3 weeks, assuming the devs keep building.

The rotators will rotate back into these coins on big announcements, product updates, or once the stats/usage starts improving.

The smarter, mentally stronger, more experienced traders who diamond-hand the coin will reap the rewards and sell gradually into these pumps once price is up multiple Xs.

You can also chase volume and gamble on shortsighted metas, but imo positioning in good utility coins is always a safer bet than holding memes with no intrinsic value when a chain has the kind of environment Robinhood has rn 🤷‍♂️
