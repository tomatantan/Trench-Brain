🇬🇷 Greece is seeing more births to Greek mothers and fewer irregular arrivals at the same time.

The share of newborns with Greek mothers climbed to about 90.5% in the first quarter of 2026.

Meanwhile the new strict rules, detention for most economic migrants, fast-track processing, and prison as the alternative to voluntary return, have driven arrivals down hard.

The Egypt-Libya route fell from over 3,200 in the first half of last year to 802 this year, returns on that path jumped, overall returns rose 40%, and Aegean arrivals dropped around 65%.

2 trends moving in the same direction for once.
