Saudi Arabia Just Made Its Position Clear to JD Vance

Despite joint U.S.-Saudi strikes on Iran-backed militias in Iraq, Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman told the vice president the kingdom still favors de-escalation with Iran.

Attacks on Saudi infrastructure crossed a red line and required a response, but the strikes were meant to deter the Houthis, not escalate further. He said Netanyahu is pushing more conflict while Saudi Arabia prefers calm, can handle the Houthis without direct U.S. help, and is still talking with them.
