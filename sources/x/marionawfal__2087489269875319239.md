🇩🇪🇦🇫 Germany wanted to deport more Afghans, so it cut a deal that helped put Taliban officials inside Afghanistan’s diplomatic missions in Berlin and Bonn.

In return, Berlin can ramp up deportations to Afghanistan, with officials saying as many as 3 charter flights a month are now possible.

But around 35,000 Afghans came to Germany through programs specifically meant for people considered vulnerable to the Taliban, including activists and people who worked with German forces.

Some now fear having to deal with Taliban-run diplomatic offices to sort out passports and residency paperwork.

An activist says a Taliban member once warned her: “Just wait until our embassy in Germany reopens. Then we’ll find you there.”

Imagine fleeing the Taliban all the way to Germany, only to find them waiting behind the embassy desk.
