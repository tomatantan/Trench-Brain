KINTARA BALANCE UPDATE ⚔️

Glade Cave 🕷️
Sword tiers now properly affect Glade Cave damage. Venomweaver is 6,000 HP, mini-spiders 120 HP, and Silver Swords cut the boss fight to roughly two thirds of the previous swings.

The Dunes 🦂
King Scorpia is now 2,500 HP, down from 3,500, with his detection range increased from 8 to 12 tiles. Giant Scorpions now detect from 6 tiles and chase until 9.

Drops 🎁
King Scorpia now has a 1/75 Medium Scroll drop alongside his existing table. Venomweaver’s Hard Scroll moves to 1/2,000.

XP ✨
Giant Scorpions 110, Magma Brutes 100, King Scorpia 1,300, Venomweaver 750

Guilds 🛡️
Creation now costs 100 Gold, down from 250. Member cap upgrades now cost 25 / 30 / 50 Gold, down from 50 / 100 / 200.

Updates across combat, XP, drops and Guilds, making progression more meaningful, rewarding and better tuned across Kintara.
