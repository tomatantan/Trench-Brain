🇺🇸🇮🇷🇺🇦 The U.S. burned through years of missiles fighting Iran.

More than 1,500 Patriot interceptors gone in a war now past 5 months, with fewer than 1,700 left in the whole U.S. inventory. Refilling that could take over 2 years.

To keep up with Iran's barrages, the Pentagon stripped air defenses out of Europe and Asia. Tomahawks and destroyers meant for a possible fight over Taiwan got pulled into the Middle East.

Ukraine is feeling it first. Zelensky says interceptors reaching Kyiv dropped threefold this year, and this week Ukraine stopped none of 28 Russian missiles. 17 people died.

Russia and China are watching, tracking U.S. stockpiles by satellite and budget docs. One analyst called it a "generational annihilation of the means of conventional deterrence."

The White House says there's no shortage. Trump has also launched a leak hunt and wants people prosecuted for even talking about it.

Two years to reload, and the U.S.'s enemies aren't exactly known for their patience.
