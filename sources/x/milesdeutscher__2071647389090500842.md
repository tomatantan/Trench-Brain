One of the worst things about a 9-5 is how incredibly limiting it actually is.

You don't realize it at the time, but your entire job is built around linear thinking.

What to do, how to do it, who to do it with - the constraints are already decided for you.

One of the best things about entrepreneurship is the shift from linear thinking to lateral thinking.

Business ownership forces creativity.

You're constantly thinking outside the box, solving problems sideways instead of following a checklist.

Everyone should start a side hustle or push into some vertical that forces lateral thinking.

Not for the money. For the rewiring.
