Looks like a lot of people overlooked Rain’s acquisitions of Ansa and Uptop.

Ansa gives Rain the infrastructure to let merchants create Starbucks style wallets with balances inside the app so customers can spend it online or instore via existing POS system so Rain can offer this to any merchant moving forward. 
 
Uptop gives Rain the onchain loyalty layer, allowing merchants to launch branded points and rewards programs all on chain.

By combining the two you could imagine stored wallet balances, rewards or points becoming interoperable across merchants within the Rain network, with onchain infra being able to settle all of the above.

e.g Starbucks could offer $10 in bonus value for every $100 deposited, with that $10 programmed to work across partners like Delta or Marriott, all through the app.

We’ve always talked about how onchain finance could enable programmable + interoperable points programs, and I believe Rain is getting much closer to making this available to any merchant in the network

I’ve seen many startups try to build points and loyalty infra via crypto, but the hard part was integrating merchants, POS systems, payment rails and settlement to actually make the whole thing work. Rain + Ansa + Uptop brings all of those pieces together.

If it works, this could become one of the first real attempts at a broadly interoperable loyalty network all while bringing millions of users onchain and now I believe were going from Neobank -> Merchant shift.
