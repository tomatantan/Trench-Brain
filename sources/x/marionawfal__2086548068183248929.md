🇮🇩 A fire broke out at Nipah Mall in South Sulawesi, Indonesia tonight around 9:40 pm local time. 

Officials believe it started from an electrical short in a neon box. The flames reached the third and fourth floors, affecting storage areas and the Timezone games section, but didn’t spread into the main shops. 

The road in front was briefly closed as firefighters brought it under control in less than 20 minutes with no fatalities. 1 visitor fainted, and 1 staff member needed treatment for smoke inhalation. 

The exact cause is still under investigation.
