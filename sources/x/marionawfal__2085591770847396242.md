🇸🇦🇮🇷 The Gulf built pipelines to escape Hormuz and Iran has already hit both ends of them...

Start with the math, because it fails on its own. 

Roughly 20 MILLION barrels a day moved through Hormuz before the war. 

Saudi Arabia's Petroline tops out at 7 million and has been running flat out since March. 

The UAE's Fujairah line handles under 2 million. 

Even at maximum, the bypass covers a fraction of what the strait carried. Then look at what happened to those routes.

Iran struck Petroline in April and knocked roughly 700,000 barrels a day off its throughput.

Iranian drones hit Fujairah three separate times in March, burning storage tanks and stopping loadings. 

The Houthis now blockade the Red Sea, which is exactly where Petroline ends.

Moving oil overland hands Iran a different kind of target. Pumping stations and export terminals sit still, they have known coordinates, and they cannot outrun anything.
