🇺🇸 California lost more than a thousand hospice providers from Medicare.

CMS under the Trump administration removed 1,076 of them, almost half the total that existed at the high point.

Los Angeles County alone once hosted over 1,850, and they have been fully cut off, no more Medicare billing, and no more access to federal dollars.

The rapid growth of hospices in California, especially around Los Angeles, had turned into one of the clearest fraud magnets in the system.

The numbers now show how far the cleanup has gone.
