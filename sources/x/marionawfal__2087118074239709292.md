🇮🇷🇸🇦 Bab al-Mandab: Two Saudi commercial ships attacked by missiles in separate incidents, two hours apart.

This comes amidst the ongoing Houthi campaign against Saudi-linked shipping.

The Iran-backed group declared a naval blockade on Saudi vessels in late July. 

It has since claimed multiple missile and drone strikes on tankers and commercial ships in the Red Sea and approaches to the Bab al-Mandab, a critical chokepoint for global trade and energy shipments.

Two Pakistanis and an Indonesian national were reportedly killed aboard one of the ships.
