Permissionless prediction markets have always been nearly impossible to build.

@robbieklages and @B33Fbanks on how Hyperliquid is trying to crack it:

Rob: "Two things always blocked this. One, duplicate listings, you'd get seven 'who's the next president?' markets, all fragmenting liquidity. Two, the harm problem: someone lists an assassination market. That's literally what shut down Augur, one of Ethereum's first apps."

Beef (his read): "I think different front-ends will show or hide different markets, so curation happens at the interface. And to deploy a permissionless market, you have to stake 500,000 HYPE."

"With that much skin in the game, you're not going to risk it posting something harmful. Those markets wouldn't even be good anyway."
