Time doesn’t erase pain.

A couple of nights ago my wife and I went out to dinner. It had been a good day and I was in good spirits.

Near the end of our meal, a song came on the restaurant’s playlist. It happened to be one of my best friend’s favorite songs. He unexpectedly passed away two months ago.

I’ve shared more meals with him at that restaurant than I can count. It’s a small town, so there aren’t exactly endless options. Yet in all the years we’ve gone there, I can’t remember ever hearing that song played.

By the time I reached the car, a couple of tears had escaped from my eyelids.

Grief has a funny way of finding you. It doesn’t ask whether you’re having a good day. Sometimes it can come rushing back in with the smallest of triggers.

And that’s okay.

Too many people think healing means reaching a point where you no longer feel the pain. I don’t think that’s true. Some wounds never fully heal. They become scars. They still ache from time to time, but they no longer keep you from moving forward.

The same is true in our life as traders.

Whether it’s a devastating loss, a liquidation, or a life-changing mistake, trying to bury it or rush past it almost always guarantees you’ll carry it into your next decision.

Give yourself permission to grieve.

Study what happened. Learn from it. Grow because of it.

The goal isn’t to forget. The goal is to reach a place where the trauma doesn't bring emotions into your trades.

I don’t believe "time heals all wounds" - but it can teach you how to live with it.
