Clive Davis has died at age 94

One of the most influential music executives ever and a major force in shaping artists like Whitney Houston, Alicia Keys, Aretha Franklin, Bruce Springsteen, and many more.

He had a knack for spotting talent early and turning it into full-blown global superstardom. In a way, he helped define what the modern music industry even is.

But he was also a controversial figure in how that industry worked, the kind of old-school record exec people point to when talking about just how much control labels used to have over artists and their careers.

A huge, complicated legacy that shaped decades of music.
