🇹🇷🇺🇸 Washington's Turkey dilemma in one sentence: how do you pull Ankara out of Moscow's orbit without teaching every ally that defection pays...

Turkey was an original F-35 partner, 900 plus parts, a hundred jets ordered, pilots training in America. 

Then it bought Russia's S-400 over years of explicit warnings and got expelled, because a Russian radar living next to stealth operations becomes a data harvesting machine for defeating the jet everyone in NATO flies. 

Six Turkish airframes still sit mothballed in U.S. storage, alongside Turkey's money.

Now Trump wants Ankara back, and the strategic case is real. 

Turkey guards the Black Sea straits and fields one of NATO's largest militaries, and every year outside the program deepens its Russian dependence. 

But the law demands Turkey no longer possess the S-400 at all, and Congress is balking.

There is a second tripwire few are watching. 

U.S. law obligates Washington to preserve Israel's qualitative military edge in the region, and Netanyahu already publicly opposes the sale. 

Arming Erdogan with the same fifth generation jet Israel flies tests that statute directly.

The tell is simple. Ignore the speeches and watch the S-400s. 

If they stay on Turkish soil, this deal is theater.
