"This is actually one of the biggest Supreme Court decisions this term and maybe in a long time ... the court just overturned a 91-year-old case called Humphrey's Executor"

TuongVy Le on the major Supreme Court decision barely anyone is talking about

"I haven't really seen that many crypto people talking about it. The court just overturned a 91-year-old case called Humphrey's Executor, which said the President can't fire a commissioner of an independent federal agency at will. This does not happen every day"

"The plaintiff was FTC Commissioner Rebecca Slaughter, a friend of mine and a law school classmate. She, along with a fellow Democratic commissioner, was removed by the President last year without cause. For context, some agencies like the DOJ and Treasury answer directly to the President, who can remove them at will"

"But then you have agencies like the SEC, the CFTC, the FTC, and the FCC. These are independent agencies because Congress designed them to be insulated from politics, run by bipartisan commissions with staggered terms. For the past century, those commissioners generally could only be removed for cause, things like being inefficient, neglecting their duty, or committing fraud"
