🇺🇸🇮🇷 Oil's real price is around $50 a barrel. Everything above that is the war.

Global monetary expert Jeffrey Snider agrees the Trump administration has worked the oil market, and explains why the "two weeks from a deal" announcements kept coming every two weeks. 

Traders know that absent this conflict, oil returns to the 50s, so nobody wants to be caught holding long positions when peace breaks out.

Where Jeff parts ways is on how much that explains:

"That's not manipulation to me. 

That's the market saying shit's getting bad in the short run, and the inevitable consequences are like every other energy shock that we've seen in history, which is a global recession at the very least."

Speculators can be spooked with a headline. 

The physical barrels still have to show up, and the shape of the futures curve is pricing in something no press release can talk down.
