🇹🇷 A drone hit a Turkish cargo ship in the Black Sea, killing 1 crew member and wounding 2 others.

The MV Reyhan Sarı was hauling coal from Russia's Taman port to Trabzon when it was struck near Novorossiysk.

The Coast Guard flew the wounded to hospitals in Samsun, while the ship stayed afloat and reached Turkish waters on its own.

Ankara hasn't assigned blame, but suspicion points to a Ukrainian drone. Kyiv said it hit 13 vessels that day going after Russia's shadow fleet.
