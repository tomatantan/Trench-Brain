Big Bear's beloved bald eagle family is back at the center of a Fourth of July debate.

After months of watching Jackie and Shadow raise their eaglets on Friends of Big Bear Valley's live nest cam, Sandy and Luna have now taken their first flights. But at just 12 weeks old, advocates say they're still developing the strength and coordination they need to survive, and fireworks could startle them or cause them to get injured.

A petition is urging the City of Big Bear Lake to relocate its fireworks show farther from the nest or consider quieter alternatives while the young eagles continue adjusting to life outside the nest.
