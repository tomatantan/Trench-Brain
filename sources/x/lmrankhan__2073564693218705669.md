A lot of a startup’s long term success after product market fit comes down to hiring.

The startups with the best chance of winning stay lean and obsess over hiring quality talent. Every hire should raise the bar for the company.

The startups that struggle usually hire too fast, lower the hiring bar, and don’t screen talent well. 

One common mistake is hiring elite logos when the candidate isn’t that strong in reality. Another yellow flag is short stints at previous companies. If someone has spent less than two years at every company, I’d dig deeper into what they actually accomplished.

Over time hiring compounds and becomes one of the biggest reasons great startups lose their edge after PMF.

And if you’re a startup one of the easiest ways to spot weakness in a competitor is to go on LinkedIn and compare their headcount growth to their product/business growth.

If headcount is exploding while progress is stalling, you’ve probably found a weakness. Then look through some of their recent hires and look at their backgrounds. I’m sure you’ll spot a few yellow flags.

Best advice I can give is stay lean, hire bar raisers and use that to win long term.
