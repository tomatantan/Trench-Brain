Santiago Santos says the L1 network-effects thesis is broken, and the Robinhood fee split proves it.

"Venture always tells you first-mover advantages are real, moats, network effects. But these are open-source networks. Robinhood launched a chain on Arbitrum, and it's capturing 90% of the fee revenue."

"Arbitrum gets 9%. Ethereum, the L1, gets 1%."

"That is not the network effects we were promised. That is not the first-mover advantage we were promised."

"My position since last year: you can both love the technology and hate the price of the token. A $50B+ company launched a chain, users are coming onchain and enjoying it."

"Everything we wanted to build is happening. There's no reason to be upset, unless you're upset because you held ETH and it's down 60%. That's a different story."
