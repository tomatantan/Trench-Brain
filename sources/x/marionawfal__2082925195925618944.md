🇷🇺 🇺🇦 Political scientist Glenn Diesen says attacks on Russia's economy may be pushing the war in the opposite direction.

Strikes on refineries, warehouses, and commercial infrastructure are designed to pressure ordinary Russians and force the Kremlin toward negotiations. 

But he believes the strategy misunderstands Moscow's motivations.

Diesen says Russia views the conflict as existential and is unlikely to change course because of economic pain. 

Instead, he warns the pressure campaign could trigger a harsher response and further escalation.
