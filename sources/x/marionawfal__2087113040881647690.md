🚨🇸🇾 BREAKING: Bashar al-Assad has just been sentenced to DEATH by a Damascus court for the murder and torture of his own people, including many children.

Assad ruled Syria with an iron fist for more than two decades, until he was toppled and fled to Russia, presiding over a civil war that killed hundreds of thousands and a prison system notorious for torture and mass executions.

Now the Fourth Criminal Court has convicted him of premeditated murder, torture, arbitrary detention and crimes against humanity. 

He was tried in absentia along with his brother Maher, and both are to be pursued internationally.

9 ex-regime figures received death sentences in total, including Assad's cousin Atef Najib, whose brutal crackdown in Deraa helped spark the 2011 uprising in the first place.

It is unlikely this sentence will ever be carried out, but a Syrian court just told the world: "Assad should die."
