"There's no third category called token that you can fit into this stack. You can make it equity or you can make it debt. Making it something that coexists with debt and equity is difficult"

Ram on why having both tokens and equity together is so hard

"We've had Delaware law and corporations for hundreds of years, and we've been able to carve up cash flows into different kinds of debt. The residual after you pay off your employees and your debt holders is something called equity. There's no third category called token"

"The right idea is what rights does it attach to, where does it fit in the capital stack, that's the way to think about it. Overall, to simplify things, it's challenging to have both equity and tokens together"
