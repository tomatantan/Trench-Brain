Kanye West's summer tour is continuing to draw controversy. In Tampa, the pushback against his shows is being led by a U.S. senator.

Florida @SenRickScott is calling on the Tampa Sports Authority to cancel Ye's June 26 and 28 shows at Raymond James Stadium, arguing that taxpayers, including the Tampa Bay area's large Jewish community, should not be funding a publicly supported venue hosting an artist with a documented history of antisemitism. Scott appeared on Maria Bartiromo's Fox News show to discuss the petition.

The pressure extends beyond Tampa. San Antonio Mayor Gina Ortiz Jones is calling for the cancellation of his July 4 concert at the Alamodome, and Ye's shows in England, Poland, and France have already been pulled following similar backlash.
