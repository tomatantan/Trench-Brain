🇺🇸 White women's workforce participation falls to late-1980s levels

New U.S. government data shows white women's participation in the workforce is back to where it was in the late 1980s.

The rate stood at 56.8% in May 2026, still well below its late-'90s peak.

COVID caused the biggest drop, and while participation has recovered, it still hasn't returned to previous highs.

Experts say the trend is driven by a mix of factors, including an aging population, caregiving, and demographic changes.

Recent research also found that most of the growth in workforce participation since 2021 has come from Hispanic women rather than white women.

Overall, the data points to a long-term shift rather than a temporary dip.

Sources: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS), Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED)/ Writer: Daniyal
