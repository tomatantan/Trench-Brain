Found a new tool that makes things a little more efficient for me.

Basically if I want to make sense of the markets, I like having a lot of data on one screen to get 80/20. 

I came across @tradermap_io that aggregates a lot of stuff. 

It pulls whale trades, liquidations, large Hyperliquid positions, prediction-market activity, news and a few broader market indicators into one place.

So when price moves you can tell pretty quickly whether the bigger traders are positioning or getting wiped.

I wouldn't trade purely off what it shows. But as a fast read on what's going on, it's been solid.
