Lucas Tcheyan's bull market upside framework:

Category 1 — fundamental assets

→ Examples — Hyperliquid, Lighter, Aerodrome
→ Floor characteristic — higher (real revenue, real PMF)
→ Ceiling characteristic — lower (well-understood, well-priced)
→ Bull market performance — may actually underperform
→ Why — capital chases narrative and momentum, not fundamentals

Category 2 — speculative ceiling assets

→ Example — Zcash
→ Floor characteristic — very low (could tumble)
→ Ceiling characteristic — enormous (private money narrative in a bull)
→ The private money thesis — AI surveillance, financial monitoring, SSL adoption curve
→ Bear market framework — look for frontier markets
→ Definition — new markets emerging from crypto nobody was watching
→ Historical example — perps as frontier market in previous bear

The biggest upside in any bull market: frontier markets, new categories that nobody was paying attention to in the bear.
