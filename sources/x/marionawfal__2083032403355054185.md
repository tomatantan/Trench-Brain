🇮🇶 🇺🇸 Baghdad says it never approved the U.S.-Saudi strikes and calls them a violation of Iraqi sovereignty.

-Government spokesperson Haider al-Aboudi says Baghdad neither approved nor had prior knowledge of Wednesday's strikes against Iran-backed groups on its soil

-The National Security Ministerial Council condemned the operation as a breach of Iraq's sovereignty, with al-Aboudi insisting Iraq "has never and will never" let its territory be used to launch attacks on neighboring countries

-He said the government authorized no attacks on any sites or groups inside Iraq, and that diplomacy remains the only way to stop the region sliding further

-It directly contradicts Trump, who said the joint strikes were carried out with the Iraqi government's agreement

-The denial lands against Reuters reporting that Houthi fighters launched at Saudi oil facilities from Iraqi territory alongside local militias under IRGC supervision

Baghdad is stuck defending two positions that cannot both hold.

It insists nobody fires at the neighbors from Iraqi ground, while its Gulf neighbors, its own investigators and outside reporting all point to exactly that happening.
