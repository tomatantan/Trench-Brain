Keone Hon says banks and enterprises don’t want to build inside walled gardens.

“When we talk to enterprises, when we talk to banks, one of the consistent things that we hear is that they care about not building in a walled garden.”

“They care about not building inside of an ecosystem where there’s one anchor tenant who is going to end up preferencing themselves.”

“They care about building on credibly neutral, decentralized environments like Ethereum or Monad.”

“The real value Monad can deliver is a dependable, performant, well-engineered system.”

“One that delivers a high degree of decentralization and is credibly neutral.”

“Not just a centralized thing that is kind of run by one big company.”
