🚨🇺🇸 The Pentagon just committed over $120 BILLION to build more Patriot missiles and nuclear submarines...

-The Patriot contract with Lockheed Martin now runs to nearly $59 billion, a major expansion of the order announced back in April

-General Dynamics Electric Boat and HII Newport News landed $76.6 billion for nine Virginia-class and five Columbia-class submarines to be delivered by 2038

-The timing tracks the crisis exactly: CSIS pegs remaining Patriot stocks at roughly 827 interceptors, down from about 2,330, after months of defending bases across the Middle East

-Lockheed also holds a preliminary $35 billion multiyear deal for THAAD interceptors, part of the Pentagon shifting from yearly purchases to long contracts that give factories a reason to expand

-The catch: these are undefinitized contracts, meaning Congress still hasn't funded them, and lawmakers in both parties remain wary of the administration's $1.5 trillion military request

The money answers a problem it cannot fix in time. 

Each new Patriot runs about $4 million and takes years to produce, so a contract signed tonight does nothing for the interceptors Jordan is firing this week, and the submarines arrive over the next twelve years.
