Token pairings might be the most overlooked mechanic in this cycle...

@DineroDom0 and @mynt_josh  broke down how it actually works:

solana:DC6HP2A7SUy49WeazfeWgbwxVpzKdEPuB46mtb7XBzWx  is a meme token paired directly against StonkFun's own platform token. Buying solana:DC6HP2A7SUy49WeazfeWgbwxVpzKdEPuB46mtb7XBzWx  pushes buy pressure straight into the liquidity pool the pairing sits in, not a burn, not a holder distribution, just demand flowing into the underlying asset because it has to sit in that pool.

That solves a real problem: a $200-500 speculator wants exposure to a 10M$+ token, but is only comfortable trading in the 0-1M$ range. Rather than being priced out, they buy the paired token instead and get leveraged exposure to the bigger ecosystem. Same logic applies to $Manlet, paired against $ANSEM, giving small buyers a way into a $200M token they'd otherwise never touch directly.

The proof is in the chart: even with close to zero independent trading volume, solana:DC6HP2A7SUy49WeazfeWgbwxVpzKdEPuB46mtb7XBzWx  has moved in near-perfect tandem with Stonks's own price action since launch. On top of that, StonkFun itself has bought back and burned roughly 5% of supply while running about $100K a day in revenue.
