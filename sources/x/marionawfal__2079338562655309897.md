🇺🇸🇮🇷 Wall Street finished lower as investors watched for any sign of de-escalation with Iran and waited for Big Tech earnings.

-Dow fell 0.59%
-S&P 500 dropped 0.19%
-Nasdaq slipped 0.05%

Markets are hoping less fighting in the Middle East could reopen the Strait of Hormuz and ease pressure on oil, gasoline, and consumer prices. 

Alphabet, Tesla, and Intel are set to report earnings later this week.

Alphabet still gained 1.5% after a report that Google is developing a new Gemini-powered AI server chip, while Domino’s rose 2.1% after beating revenue expectations.
