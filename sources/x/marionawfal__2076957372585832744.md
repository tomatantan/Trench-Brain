🇫🇷 France has temporarily shut down 3 nuclear reactors and may cut output at 7 more as a heatwave drives up river temperatures.

The reason is environmental, not technical. 

French rules cap how much warm water plants can discharge into rivers to protect aquatic life, and the heat is pushing right up against those limits.

EDF says there's no nuclear safety risk, and expects the disruptions to ease over the coming days as the weather cools.
