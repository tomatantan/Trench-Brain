🇮🇷 The IRGC has issued a public warning to civilians across the Gulf, telling them to physically distance themselves from anywhere U.S. troops might be sleeping.

"Civilians in countries hosting US troops are warned to immediately stay at least 500 meters away from locations where American military personnel are secretly housed, for their own safety."

The full statement goes further, asking the public to report the new locations of relocated U.S. forces to an IRGC Telegram channel and website.

That is a safety notice on the surface and a crowdsourced targeting request underneath.
