.@OxSimpleFarmer walked through the @ClutchMarkets roadmap and phase four is the interesting one.

Like Pumpfun launched its own, a DEX is a great revenue moat when you already have a user base trading tokens anyway, they might as well trade on one that rewards them. "Buying local," as he put it.

Phase four is the sleeper: 
- Decentralized options exchange on stock tokens
- You take the stock tokens your broker holds, plug them into options, place liquidity above or below the stock price, and earn yield in premiums by selling calls or puts.

Why it's only possible on Robinhood Chain: options need oracle price feeds that are prohibitively expensive elsewhere. Robinhood Chain supplies them. The result, in to leverage without liquidations, something crypto has struggled to achieve.
