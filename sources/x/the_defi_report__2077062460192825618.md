Robinhood's L2 is the latest reminder that Ethereum's biggest wins increasingly happen above the base layer.

Its early activity alone is already outpacing what most L2s see in a full quarter.

How much of that value flows back to ETH holders? 

Last quarter, Ethereum's L2s ran 7.7x more daily transactions than the L1, but paid the L1 just $83k in rent, down 87% y/y. 

More activity above the base layer hasn't meant more value flowing into it.
