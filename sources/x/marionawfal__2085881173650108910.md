🇩🇪🇷🇺 Remember that explosive drone discovered a few days ago at Germany's Leipzig/Halle Airport?

U.S. intelligence, unsurprisingly, is indicating it's linked to Russia...

The drone was found 3 days ago in a secure part of the airport, meters away from a Ukrainian cargo aircraft.

Investigators determined it was an explosive device, but it had a defective detonator and did not function.

To be safe, the airport immediately suspended its overnight operations.

A second-suspected drone is also being investigated, after a DHL cargo plane had its nose damaged while the airport was closed.

U.S. intelligence has been warning that Putin may test NATO in new, limited attacks, ranging from cyber to sabotage...

Next time, it could be a lot worse.
