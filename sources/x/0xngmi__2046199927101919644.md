Seeing a lot of takes that assume that defillama's aave TVL is inflated by looping

That's NOT the case, because borrowed coins are removed from TVL

Imagine:
1.  A deposits 1m eth -> tvl = 1m
2. B deposits 1m steth and borrows 1m -> +1m - 1m
3. final tvl = 1m

On top of that, we monitor TVL sources and actively remove sources of doublecounting too

For example we found that ethena was depositing its collateral into aave and people were looping it, which actually led to tvl increasing from loops. Then we specially removed ethena's deposited TVL from aave TVL to avoid this

So our TVL numbers already have looping removed, idk where everyone is getting this idea that is not
