Andy's (@andyyy) take on a claim making the rounds, tokens with equity splits don't work: he agrees, and he's got the receipts. 

His read: when ownership and value accrual are split between shareholders and token holders, the team's obligation runs to the shareholders only. Token holders get nothing. 

His prediction: teams start winding down their tokens and reissuing tokenized equity instead. Hyperliquid, Morpho, and Near already work this way.
