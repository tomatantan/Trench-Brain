Cops just used a PIT maneuver to stop a crew of armed robbers targeting elderly people.

Jacksonville officers were tracking suspects who’d been robbing seniors outside banks. When they closed in, the group fled onto the Arlington Expressway. A sergeant performed a clean PIT maneuver all captured by COPS cameras (the first time they’d filmed in Jacksonville in 20 years).

The driver and passenger were arrested at the scene, and another who ran was caught later. Two of the suspects have already pleaded guilty.

Pretty impressive work.
