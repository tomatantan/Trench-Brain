🇪🇸🇮🇸 A rare total solar eclipse is hitting parts of Europe today, with millions getting a chance to watch the sky go dark.

Iceland and northern Spain are among the best spots, with totality lasting around 2 minutes.

Spain gets an especially wild view because the eclipse happens right before sunset, turning the sun into a thin crescent as it drops below the horizon.

Much of the rest of Europe, Canada, the northern U.S. and northwestern Africa will still get a partial eclipse.

The universe is showing off tonight, with the Perseid meteor shower peaking just hours after the eclipse.
