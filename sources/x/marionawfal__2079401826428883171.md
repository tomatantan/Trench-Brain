🚨🇺🇸 🇮🇷 A new U.S. intelligence assessment delivers the verdict Trump doesn't want: the strikes won't move Iran, and the war is stuck in "indefinite limbo."

-Per the Washington Post, analysts conclude Iran's government is unlikely to feel significant impact or soften its negotiating position from the new rounds of strikes, with the CIA-authored report already briefed to the administration

-The agencies see Washington and Tehran stuck "in an indefinite limbo between peace and war," and a May CIA analysis found Iran could survive the naval blockade for three to four MONTHS before severe hardship

-Former intelligence officer Jonathan Panikoff on the administration's hit-harder theory: "That assessment is almost certainly incorrect," because the regime's top priority is survival and it will absorb blows "despite the harm to its people and economy"

-Rubio acknowledged the internal split openly: Iranians in government tell the U.S. they want a deal, "but there's a tension" with hardliners, and he doesn't know what happens if the hardliners win

-Both Mojtaba and Ghalibaf spent the weekend urging "unity" as the condition for victory, the regime closing ranks

The assessment lands directly on the fork Axios described: ten nights of bombing, three American deaths, and the government's own analysts concluding the current course produces stalemate, not capitulation.

That's the intelligence community formally pricing Option 2, the massive joint campaign, as the only military path that changes anything, while pricing the 10-day ceasefire as the only exit that doesn't require it.
