Coming out of this bear market, we’re seeing new VCs actively deploying into crypto and fintech, especially around Alliance post Demo Day and suspect many of them will make their name in the next cycle.

When VCs leave crypto or refactor toward AI, robotics etc people often view it as negative for the space but I don’t.

It creates room for new investors to come up while also expanding crypto’s footprint into other industries. 

Hypothetically the most interesting future may be one where the best robotics or AI investors are also crypto investors and they can help make the case for crypto rails, while crypto becoming a primitive embedded across the frontier tech stack vs a separate category.

That crossover could end up being far more important than crypto staying in its own bubble.
