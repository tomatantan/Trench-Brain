How does @stonkpit handle token emissions? 

@Unclemac broke down the design:

Most protocols deflate by burning, cut circulating supply, price goes up, degens cheer. Instead of burning, it recycles: the same dirt that gets mined flows back into the mines to be re-mined and re-sold as entropy, holding circulating supply steady at ~10%.

The result after 7 days and ~10M transactions: net emissions are basically zero, less than 1% extra emitted across both mines.

He's the miners' adversary, constantly shoveling dirt back in as fast as they mine it out, to keep the system running indefinitely.
