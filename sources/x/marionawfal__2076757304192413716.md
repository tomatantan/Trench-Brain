🇺🇸🇸🇦🇮🇱 Saudi is laying down tough conditions for joining the Abraham Accords and normalizing with Israel: 

-Replace Netanyahu after elections
-Roll back Smotrich’s policies in Judea and Samaria
-And a clear Israeli commitment to a future Palestinian state

Riyadh has reportedly floated these ideas multiple times during the war, using backchannels to Washington.

Saudis are likely torn between wanting the deal and believing Netanyahu won’t deliver on the West Bank front.

Throughout the Iran conflict, Saudi weighed formal recognition in exchange for big concessions, but those talks stalled.

The late Senator Lindsey Graham was heavily involved in trying to bridge the gap.
