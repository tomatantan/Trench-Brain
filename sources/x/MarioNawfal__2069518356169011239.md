🇮🇷 IAEA Director General Grossi said inspections of Iran’s nuclear facilities will resume as soon as possible.

“Our top priority is to verify the location of Iran’s highly enriched uranium stockpile. We have an idea where it is, but Iran needs to confirm it.”

He said talks with Tehran to set inspection dates are coming soon, and that the agency will lead the process, with Iran deciding whether to invite the US or others.

After recent strikes on sites like Isfahan, Fordow, and Natanz, the IAEA says it lost full visibility on roughly 400+ kg of 60% enriched uranium.

This marks the 1st major push to restore oversight of Iran’s nuclear program since the ceasefire.
