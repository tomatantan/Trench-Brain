🇨🇳 While the Strait of Hormuz crisis was sending oil prices into panic mode, China seems to have stumbled into a very convenient backup plan: 3 billion electric taxi trips a month.

About half of China’s 1.3 million taxis are electric, close to 100% in major cities.

So when gas got pricier and the Iran war kicked off in February, people did the sensible thing and let somebody else do the driving.

The result: taxi trips jumped 6%, China burned 10% less gasoline and 14% less diesel in May, and road travel still hit record highs.
