🇺🇸 Trump's team just found a tariff loophole written in 1930.

The Supreme Court ruled in February that he couldn't use a 1977 emergency powers law to set tariffs, which killed the legal basis for almost all of them.

So his lawyers went digging through laws still sitting on the books and pulled out clauses nobody had touched in decades.

One of them dates to 1930 and was used this week to hit Canada with 50% duties. Another, written for currency crises, is now doing the work of blanket tariffs.

Together they're projected to raise $240 to $260 billion a year, roughly 80% of what the court took away.

Congress is now fighting to pull tariff power back, while a separate bill would hand him even more of it.
