Chainlink Labs' Head of Legal on the "GENIUS-compliant" myth, and why the real work starts after the bill passes, not before.

"It's funny when someone says 'GENIUS-compliant stablecoin,' because GENIUS isn't actually even in force yet."

"I'm old enough to remember Dodd-Frank. People expected immediate changes."

"Passage kicks off a multi-year rulemaking and interpretive process. We don't wake up the day after the president signs and see everything changed."

"Look at MiCA, the EU's version. It's only in full force now, and they're already talking about changing it. It took years after passage. It's a marathon, not a sprint."
