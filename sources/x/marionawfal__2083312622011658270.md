🚨🇷🇺 🇮🇷 Russia is feeding Iran the electronic intelligence that helps it find American forces and jam U.S. weapons.

-A U.S. official and a European official tell NBC that Moscow is giving Tehran signals intelligence, meaning information vacuumed up from communications networks, radar and weapons systems

-Combined with Russian satellite imagery and technical insights about how American gear works, it is likely helping Iran defend against U.S. airstrikes and sharpen its own attacks on ships, planes and other moving targets

-Officials say the help may partly explain why Iran has held up far better than Washington expected six months into the war, letting Tehran build target databases and read its own post-strike damage

-The damage speaks for itself: 18 American service members killed, and an estimated $10 BILLION to $30 billion in destruction to U.S. bases and equipment

-China is helping too on a smaller scale, giving Iran access to a surveillance satellite and planning to send shoulder-launched missiles, one of which likely downed an American F-15

-Trump's public position remains that he takes Putin at his word, saying of Russian help: "I don't think they've been doing it, certainly not at a high level. And if they have, it has been very unimpactful"

Washington has spent six months puzzling over why a country with no navy and no air force keeps finding American targets and surviving American strikes. 

The answer was never Iranian ingenuity alone. It was Russian eyes and Chinese hardware.

Which means the US isn't fighting Iran. It's fighting a coalition that costs Moscow and Beijing almost nothing while draining American munitions, money, and lives.
