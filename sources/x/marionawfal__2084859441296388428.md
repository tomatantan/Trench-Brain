🇺🇸 Nobody in America ever voted for a national surveillance network, and one got built anyway, one town council at a time

Every Flock camera gets approved locally. 

A city council debates its own streets, its own budget, its own crime problem, and votes yes. 

Reasonable enough on its own terms.

Then the plate read leaves town. 

Any agency anywhere holding a Flock contract can search it, so 6,000 communities each approved a camera and together built something none of them ever voted on. 

Roughly 20 BILLION reads a month across 80,000 cameras, and the ACLU says under 1 percent connect to any crime at all.

The legal cover is that your plate sits in public view. 

That doctrine was written for an officer watching one road. 

Nobody wrote it for a machine that remembers every road and hands the file to a cop 2,000 miles away who has never heard of your town.

Ask yourself who you would even complain to.
