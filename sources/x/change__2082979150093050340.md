Several cases involving Black Mississippians have raised questions about how Dr. Staci Turner, Mississippi’s chief medical examiner, has handled death investigations.

In Nolan Wells’ case, an independent pathologist said he could not rule out injury to the neck because parts of Wells’ internal neck structures — including the trachea — had been retained by the state during the initial autopsy and were not available for review.

In another case, the death of Delta State student Demartravion ‘Trey’ Reed was ruled a suicide, a finding his family disputes. In a third case, Damien Cameron’s 2021 death was ruled undetermined before three independent forensic pathologists, reviewing the case at the request of The New York Times and Mississippi Today, concluded it should have been ruled a homicide.

Over 6,000 people have signed a petition calling for an investigation into the medical examiner's office. You can read or sign at the link in our bio.
