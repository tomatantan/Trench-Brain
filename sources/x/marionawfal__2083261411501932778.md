🇦🇪 The UAE is spending big to lock down its own oil shipping.

ADNOC just bought five supertankers from Frontline for around $590 million, part of a push to control its own fleet as attacks in the Red Sea and Strait of Hormuz make renting tankers difficult and expensive.

And that's just the start. On top of those five:

3 gas carriers at around $115 million each
25 to 30 more vessels on order from various shipyards, from crude tankers to LNG and LPG carriers

The point is cost and control. When tankers get scarce and pricey because owners won't risk those waters, the UAE isn't fighting over rented ships or eating the war premiums hitting everyone else. It sails its own.

The timing tells the rest. Iraq, Qatar and Kuwait are struggling to place their oil because of the disruption, while the UAE keeps selling millions of barrels through direct deals and spot tenders. 

Owning the fleet is how it stays the Gulf producer that keeps moving product.
