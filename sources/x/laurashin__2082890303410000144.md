Mert on the Bitcoin privacy myth that TV shows keep pushing

"The concept of privacy is ironically implicitly assumed when you're talking about Bitcoin to anybody outside of the industry. If you ever watch a TV show like The Blacklist, the context in which Bitcoin comes around is generally some criminal asking another person for ransom"

"Every time I watch something like that I'm like, what are you doing? That is way more traceable than cash, it makes absolutely no sense. It's this deep belief where you think, oh it's cryptographic money, obviously it must be private"

"It's not even really understood that no, it's actually traceable. Digital gold, as the official Bitcoin account, which I'm not even sure who owns that on Twitter, literally says in their bio"
