🇺🇸🇨🇳 The financial scoreboard in the U.S.-China contest is tilting one way.

American investors pulled another $3.4B from major China ETFs over the last three months, the biggest yearly outflow streak in recent memory.

This is the market version of the longer contest: the U.S. has spent a decade tightening the screws with investment bans on militarylinked firms, outbound screening for semiconductors and AI, audit rules that force delistings, and expanded CFIUS reviews.

China answered with tighter capital controls, dualcirculation selfreliance, and heavier state direction of its own markets.

One system still lets private capital vote with its feet, and the other keeps more of it locked inside.

Right now the votes are leaving.
