🚨🇮🇷 Iran declares the U.S. in breach of the peace deal, and reserves the right to "any measures" in response.

-Iran's Foreign Ministry condemned the reimposed oil-sales ban as a "clear breach of Article 10" of the war-ending memorandum, holding Washington responsible for the consequences

-Tehran says the U.S. has repeatedly violated the deal over its twenty days of existence, both directly and through Israeli actions in Lebanon

-Iran warns it will take "any measures it deems necessary" to protect its interests and national security

-The statement lands as smoke still rises over Bandar Abbas from the overnight American strikes

Translation:

Islamic Republic of Iran
 Ministry of Foreign Affairs
 Date: 1405/4/17

In the name of God, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful

Statement of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs regarding the United States’ explicit violation of Clause 10 of the Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran strongly condemns the U.S. Treasury’s action in canceling the temporary suspension of sanctions on the sale of Iranian oil, which constitutes a blatant violation of Clause 10 of the War-Termination Memorandum of Understanding dated Khordad 28, 1405. It holds the U.S. government responsible for the consequences of this breach of commitment.

At a time when fewer than 20 days have passed since the signing of the Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding, the announcement of the cancellation of the general license issued on June 21 is another sign of the bad faith, instability, and unreliability of the ruling establishment of the United States. This comes as, over the past 20 days, the United States has repeatedly committed minor and major violations of various provisions of the memorandum, either directly or through the actions of the Zionist regime against Lebanon.

Since the signing of the memorandum on Khordad 28, the Islamic Republic of Iran has acted in complete good faith and has used all of its capacities to fulfill its obligations under the memorandum. Nevertheless, the U.S. government, as is its constant habit, has violated its own commitments while seeking to justify those violations under various pretexts.

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran, while warning of the consequences of America’s treaty-breaking, will take any action it deems necessary to protect its national interests and security.
