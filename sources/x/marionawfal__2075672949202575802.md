🇺🇸 The Trump administration just changed what "harm" means under the Endangered Species Act.

Destroying an endangered species' habitat is no longer automatically illegal. You now have to directly injure or kill the animal.

The habitat isn't protected. The animal technically is.

Good luck to the animal.
