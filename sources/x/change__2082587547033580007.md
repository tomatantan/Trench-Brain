Independent journalist and YouTuber Sean Paul Reyes @longislandaudit, known online as "The Long Island Auditor," was handcuffed and charged with trespassing in June after filming outside Suffolk County Police Benevolent Association headquarters in Brentwood, New York.

PBA President Louis Civello personally placed him in cuffs and had his vehicle towed while he was detained. Civello and the union say the arrest was lawful because Reyes ignored repeated orders to leave, but Reyes argues the property doesn't meet the legal definition of "enclosed" required for the charge to hold.

Reyes has launched a campaign calling on Tierney to drop the trespassing charge against him and open an investigation into Civello's conduct instead. Over 7,000 people have signed.
