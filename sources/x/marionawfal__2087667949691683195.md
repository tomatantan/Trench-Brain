🇺🇸🇨🇦 Canada and the U.S. still aren’t close to a tariff deal.

Canadian officials say they’re not satisfied with the latest American offer, which would ease some tariffs but not enough. 

Negotiators have been trading proposals for weeks ahead of Trump’s August 19 deadline for a possible 50% levy on hundreds of Canadian goods.

The U.S. wants better access to Canadian critical minerals plus agreements on security and energy. 

Canada is pushing for relief on steel, aluminum, lumber and auto tariffs, and wants to keep CUSMA alive beyond 2036.

Talks continue, but both sides remain far apart.
