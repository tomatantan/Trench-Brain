🇷🇺🇴🇲 An oil slick six times the size of Manhattan is swallowing an Omani whale reserve.

The ghost ship behind it, the Caroline Bezengi, has sat grounded off the Hallaniyat Islands since June, and it's been leaking close to a million barrels of Russian crude that whole time. Now the slick has reached the mainland.

The ship is part of Russia's shadow fleet, the aging tankers with no real owner and no real insurance that Moscow uses to dodge sanctions and keep its war money flowing.

Trouble started when something blew a hole in it off Yemen in June. Nobody claimed the attack, though it was threading two war zones on its way from Russia to India.

That's where Oman gets stuck. With no owner to bill and no insurance to claim, the cleanup bill falls on the country alone.

And the timing couldn't be worse, because the reserve beneath the slick holds the last 82 Arabian Sea humpback whales on Earth, while monsoon weather is blocking crews from even reaching the wreck.

So two months in, the ship is still leaking and Oman is still on its own.
