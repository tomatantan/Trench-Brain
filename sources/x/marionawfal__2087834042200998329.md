The boring part of tunneling might actually be what slows everything down.

Prufrock can keep digging, but only if thousands of pounds of concrete liner segments keep feeding it.

The Boring Company's new Liner Truck 3 handles that job with Tesla Model 3 battery and drive hardware and carries 22,000+ lb loads.

Remotely piloted with one operator controlling more than eight trucks.

The tunnel keeps moving without diesel exhaust or a driver sitting underground all day.

Bonus: it can haul standard freight containers too.

The driverless tunnel truck has a much bigger job than it looks.
