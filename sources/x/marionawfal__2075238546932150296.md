🇺🇸 When America chose the title of "president" for its leader, it was considered exceptionally unusual for a head of state.

This clip from HBO's John Adams depicts that early debate. Adams favored a more royal-sounding title.

Deriving from the Latin praesidere (meaning "to sit before"), president was historically reserved for ceremonial committee chairmen, university heads, or local club officers.

It carried no connotations of supreme political or sovereign power.

Today, we think nothing of it. At the time, pretty strange.
