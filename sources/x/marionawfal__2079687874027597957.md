🚨🇺🇸 🇮🇷 Hegseth puts the Iran war's price tag on the record: $37.5 BILLION and climbing.

-The Defense Secretary told the Senate Appropriations Committee the U.S.-Israel war on Iran has cost an estimated $37.5 billion so far, a jump of nearly $8 billion since the last public estimate

-The figure includes anticipated costs through September 30, and Hegseth used it to defend a request for some $67 billion in supplemental Pentagon funding

-It's his first public grilling by lawmakers since operations against Iran restarted this month, appearing alongside Joint Chiefs Chairman Gen. Dan Caine

-The testimony lands with the war's human ledger also on the table: FOUR American service members killed and nearly 100 injured since July 7

The number arrives at the worst possible moment for the case it has to make. 

Senators questioning a $67 billion request are the same ones reading this week's reports that the Pentagon withheld the injury toll and holding an intelligence assessment that says the strikes won't move Tehran. 

Eight billion dollars of new costs in one update is the war's burn rate stated plainly: roughly a billion dollars every three days, buying a stalemate the government's own analysts have now put in writing.
