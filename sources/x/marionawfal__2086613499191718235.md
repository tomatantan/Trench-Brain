🇺🇸 Don Nelson, one of the sharpest minds basketball ever had, has died at 86.

He won 5 rings as a player with the Celtics, then coached his way to 1,335 wins, the second most in NBA history.

The modern game is basically his idea. He invented the point forward and went small and fast decades before everyone else caught on.

He built the "Run TMC" Warriors, coached Dirk and Nash in Dallas, and in his last season gave a rookie named Stephen Curry his first shot.

The man saw where basketball was going. The whole league is playing his game now.
