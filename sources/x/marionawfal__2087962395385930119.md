🇨🇩 DRC's Ebola outbreak just spread to a 6th province. A man died in Bas-Uele after traveling there from a neighboring province already hit hard.

The head of the WHO said this week the outbreak is on pace to beat the deadliest Ebola outbreak ever recorded, the one that killed over 11,000 people in West Africa a decade ago. 

This one's already hit 4,500 cases and over 2,100 deaths, and it's getting there almost three times faster than that outbreak did.

Part of why: there's no approved vaccine or treatment for this particular strain. Health workers in one of the worst-hit areas just went on strike because they haven't been paid in three months, shutting down a treatment center. 

Add rebel threats and misinformation telling people Ebola isn't even real, and you get a regional director saying: "We are chasing the virus, the virus is ahead of us."

60-70% of new cases are showing up outside anyone's contact list. 

Two possible treatments just entered trials last month; the timing isn't in anyone's favor.
