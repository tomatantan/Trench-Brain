Everyone keeps saying Duolingo is cooked because GPT Live can correct your speech in real time. I don't buy it. Foundation models commoditize features, not distribution. 

Duolingo can just adopt the same models while keeping the hundreds of millions of users they've already acquired. The harder problem isn't building the AI, it's getting people to change behavior and open a different app every day. 

Could a startup build a better language experience via Chat LIve? Sure. But that doesn't mean Duolingo loses just more competitors to compete with.
