🇮🇷🇺🇸🇴🇲 Iran wants to turn Hormuz into a toll booth.

The pitch is dressed up as fees for security, safety, and environmental services, anything but the word toll, which Iran knows it can't legally slap on an international waterway.

By Tehran's own math the scheme could pull in up to $40 billion a year, and it wants the neighboring Gulf states cut in on the take so the whole thing looks like a regional arrangement instead of a shakedown.

Washington and the Gulf want none of it. The line out of every capital is the same, international waterways stay toll-free, full stop.

Marco Rubio: "I know of no country on the planet that supports tolling or fees for the use of the strait"

Here's the part that stings. The war was sold as a way to leave Iran weaker, and instead Tehran walked out of it trying to charge the planet rent on the same chokepoint it shut down for months and slammed closed again days after signing peace.

And the only service really on sale here is Iran agreeing not to attack your ship.
