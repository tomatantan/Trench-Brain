The way you trade should be based on size of your own port

You can’t copy the trading style the guy who has a port 10/20/50x bigger than yours uses

They can afford to hold coins longer and risk the roundtrip, maybe take a chance on coins with more uncertainty 

You probably can’t afford to do that

Keep stacking smaller, more certain wins, gradually increasing your port and get to the point where you can afford to do that
