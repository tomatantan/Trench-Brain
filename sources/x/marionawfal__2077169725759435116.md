🇺🇸🇮🇷 Trump clashes with a Fox reporter over Iran's nuclear intentions:

"It just never stops. They wanted a nuclear weapon. We knew they wanted a nuclear weapon."

"Everything they say is a lie. They lie. I've never seen anything like it."

"Everything they say is a lie" is convenient but double-edged. 

If Iran's word is worthless, then no deal with Iran is enforceable either, which undercuts the very negotiations Trump says he still wants. 

You can't call a country categorically dishonest and also sell the public on a signed agreement with it. 

The line plays as strength, but it also torches the credibility of any paper the two sides produce.
