PlayStation players are planning a week-long boycott of Sony from August 23–30, urging gamers to avoid logging into PSN, playing online, or buying games in protest of Sony's plan to stop producing physical discs for new PlayStation games beginning in 2028.

The #PSBlackout boycott, organized by the game preservation group @doesitplay1, comes as more than 345,000 people have signed a petition launched by Jade Pearce, CEO of Canadian game retailer @pnpgames, urging Sony to keep releasing physical PlayStation games. Supporters argue physical copies protect ownership, game preservation, and players' ability to buy, sell, and lend games.
