Dmitry Berenzon says why RWAs need a two-category framework instead of one:

"Two different types of RWAs. Financial assets have one property profile, private credit, debt, equities, funds all behave similarly."
"Physical assets have a different property profile, commodities, real estate, collectibles, GPUs. Different nuances for issuers and holders. Different impact on how the asset behaves."

"A tokenized Pokemon card is an RWA. Most coverage stops at financial assets, the broader framing matters."
