"It's usually the Fed that breaks it, that causes earnings degradation and puts us into a recession"

Franklin Templeton strategist Chris Galipeau on why the market signals recessions before the data

"The only thing that really puts earnings under pressure is the Fed raising rates and breaking it and putting us into a recession. That's the tech bubble, that's the GFC. A little different with COVID but same concept, it's usually the Fed that breaks it, that causes earnings degradation and puts us into a recession"

"And you know what, the stock market moves before that. It knows the earnings degradation is coming, and the stock market rallies six months prior, before the end of the actual recession as dated by the NBER"

"These are all examples of why I don't rely too much on economists or economic data or what the Fed says. The stock market tells you if you're paying attention and know where to look"
