Andy asked Hanson Birringer about the specific US regulatory path for Hyperliquid:

"The world is big. Different regulators, different countries, different rules on how financial markets get approached. Hyperliquid is an open source permissionless L1, it operates across jurisdictions by design."

"US specifically: the Hyperliquid Policy Center and Phantom co-sponsored a comment to the CFTC pushing for decentralized venues to qualify for the new US regulatory clarity framework."

"If it lands: regulated broker front-ends can execute through Hyperliquid's back-end. That's the AWS-of-finance thesis reaching US retail through the compliant surface."
