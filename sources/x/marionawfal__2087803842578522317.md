🇨🇳 The Chinese yuan is extending its rally against the dollar, hitting its strongest level since February 2023.

A weaker dollar and cooling U.S. inflation are doing a lot of the work, with markets betting the Fed holds off on rate hikes.

Solid Chinese trade data is helping too, along with Beijing's steady push to get the world using the yuan and leaning on the dollar less.

All those tariffs and export bans meant to box China out, and the yuan just hit a 3.5 year high.
