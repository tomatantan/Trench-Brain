🇧🇬 A drone carrying a "solid amount of explosives" just entered Bulgarian airspace from Romania before crashing just 100 metres from the border.

The alarming part is that Bulgaria says the drone wasn't detected by Bulgarian or Romanian radar, or even NATO's combined air operations centre.

It crashed in a sunflower field near a compressor station on the Trans-Balkan gas pipeline, causing no casualties or damage.

Romania says its radar detected no aircraft crossing into Bulgaria, leaving the drone's origin, and how it apparently slipped through NATO air defences, unknown.
