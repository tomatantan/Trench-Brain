Iberia’s first Starlink plane went live.

Madrid to São Paulo with WiFi that actually works.

Free for everyone on board.
No cabin tiers.
No hourly meter.
No watching one email load like it is crossing the Atlantic by canoe.

Speeds can hit around 450 to 500 Mbps.
Basically home broadband at cruising altitude.

This is only the first plane.

Iberia wants Starlink on 35% of its long-haul fleet by year-end, while IAG plans to fit more than 500 aircraft across British Airways, Aer Lingus, and Vueling.

Airline WiFi finally stopped being a prank.
