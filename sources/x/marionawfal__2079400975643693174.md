🇳🇮 Nicaragua's Ortega declares elections ABOLISHED: "never again" after nearly two decades in power.

-Ortega announced the country will no longer hold elections, closing off any electoral challenge when his term ends next year: "There won't be any more elections here for them to try to seize the government and seize power"

-His framing, delivered at the Sandinista revolution anniversary in his first public appearance in TWO MONTHS: "The days when parties backed by the Yankees and the Somocistas would return to power are over, never again"

-The 80-year-old has ruled continuously since 2007, naming his wife Rosario Murillo "co-president" through constitutional reforms that already extended terms to six years and built a one-party state

-The last election in 2021 ran with opponents jailed and dissent criminalized, and the 2018 protest crackdown killed more than 300

-Exiled opposition figure Felix Maradiaga's read: less a show of force than "a confession of fear"

-The Trump administration has sanctioned more than 2,350 Nicaraguan officials and relatives, calling the government a dictatorship

Ortega spent four terms rigging elections rather than canceling them, because even hollow votes provided legitimacy theater, and ending them entirely means the couple concluded the theater itself had become a risk.
