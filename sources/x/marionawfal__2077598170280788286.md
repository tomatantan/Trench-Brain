One of the biggest shifts in this market is flying under most investors’ radar.

While the S&P 500 posts red days, a record number of individual stocks are still closing green. That’s a massive departure from real bear markets. 

In 2022, selling was brutal and indiscriminate: liquidity vanished, correlations spiked, and almost everything crashed together.

Today’s market tells a different story.

Instead of broad liquidation, smart capital is rotating. Investors are dumping a handful of mega-cap heavyweights while quietly accumulating strength across the rest of the market.

This explains why the headline index can look weak even as underlying participation strengthens.

If this rotation continues, the next leg of the bull market could be powered by broader, more sustainable leadership, a classic sign of a healthier and far more durable advance.
