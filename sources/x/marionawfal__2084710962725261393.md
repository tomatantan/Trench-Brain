🇺🇸🇵🇾 Marco Rubio signed a civil nuclear cooperation deal with Paraguay.

Doesn’t sound flashy until you zoom out.

Paraguay runs almost entirely on hydropower and has been looking at small modular reactors to diversify its grid.

The U.S. locked in a formal framework to help them explore that path.

It’s another quiet deepening of ties with one of the most pro-U.S. governments in South America. Security, minerals, and now nuclear energy are all on the table.
