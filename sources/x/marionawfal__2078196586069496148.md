🇶🇦🇮🇷 Iran is now bombing the one Gulf state that defended it.

Qatar has condemned Iran's attacks on its territory, along with Jordan, the UAE, Bahrain, Oman, and Kuwait, calling them a flagrant violation of sovereignty and international law.

Doha says it holds Iran fully responsible and reserves the right to respond under Article 51 of the UN Charter, the self-defense clause.

Qatar has long been Iran's closest partner in the Gulf, sharing the world's largest gas field with it and keeping channels open when others wanted Tehran isolated. 

Doha hardening its language this far, and openly talking self-defense, shows how much even that relationship has soured.
