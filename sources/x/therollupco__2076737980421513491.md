Michael Terpin (@michaelterpin) unpacks the four-seasons Bitcoin cycle framework in under 4 minutes.

→ Spring at the halving day (miner rewards cut in half but prices stay flat because forward buyers absorb sellers).

→ Summer at the first new all-time high after the halving.

→ Fall at the day the bubble pops (the read that separates buy-and-hold from active positioning). 

→ Winter from capitulation through to the next halving (the longest season). 

Current cycle: nearing capitulation, possibly already hit at $57K or one more leg down like 2022/2018.
