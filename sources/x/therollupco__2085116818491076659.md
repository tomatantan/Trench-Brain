$6 billion in daily DEX volume sounds like retail flooding onchain.

Arca Head of Research Katie Talati says it's not:

"From what we've seen looking at retail flows, it's definitely not coming from retail at this point. A lot of retail investors are still using centralized exchanges."

"Even though DEXs got popularized and Uniswap and MetaMask made onchain trading easy, that's still a step a lot of folks aren't taking. It's mostly professionals and arbitrage."
