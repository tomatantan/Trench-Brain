🇷🇴🇷🇺 Romanian army divers blew up 2 drones drifting in the Black Sea near the massive Neptun Deep gas project.

A civilian ship spotted the Gerbera-type drones yesterday, and the military destroyed them in a controlled blast to keep the shipping lane safe.

Romania’s defense minister didn’t say where they came from, but said Ukraine confirmed they weren’t Ukrainian.

Russian drones have crossed into Romanian airspace repeatedly since the war began, and Romania shot down 3 in 3 days last month.

Neptun Deep is expected to start producing gas in 2027 and could make Romania the EU’s biggest gas producer.
