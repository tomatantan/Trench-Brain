🇦🇿🇺🇸 Azerbaijan is asking the U.S. for help in clearing one of the world’s WORST landmine problems...

Amb. Khazar Ibrahim says his country is “one of the most landmine-contaminated” per capita, after decades of conflict with Armenia and the legacy of the Soviet collapse. 

So Baku is requesting American tech, techniques, and better mapping. Especially after last year’s U.S.-brokered peace deal and a new partnership charter.

Clearing mines is slow, expensive, and deadly. But under Trump, the ambassador says: “Whatever you agree upon, you immediately start working on.”
