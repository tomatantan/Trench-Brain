Flock Safety's network of more than 100,000 cameras logs about 20 billion license plate scans a month across more than 6,000 communities. Officers can search the database without a warrant.

According to the Washington Post, a Georgia police chief used his department's Flock cameras to look up his ex-girlfriend's license plate roughly 600 times, along with her teenage daughter's, tracking her movements to the grocery store, doctor's appointments and dates. He was arrested on stalking and harassment charges.
