🇨🇳🇩🇪 China cracked open the door to its currency system for the 1st time.

Deutsche Bank is now the first non-Chinese bank in Europe cleared to settle renminbi payments straight from Frankfurt.

For years only Beijing’s own banks controlled those pipes.

European firms can finally move money with China without bouncing through dollar intermediaries or Chinese middlemen.

This is bigger than it looks.

The yuan still owns less than 4% of global payments, but China already settles over half its own trade in its own currency.

Every new clearing bank like this one chips away at the dollar’s chokehold.

German exporters get faster, cleaner deals, and Beijing gets another lever against sanctions and dollar weaponization.

Quiet plumbing today.
