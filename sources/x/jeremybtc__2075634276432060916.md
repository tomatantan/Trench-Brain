A robot hand can now zip up a jacket, sort grapes by color, and plug in a USB-C cable first try.

This is the new hand from 1X, unveiled yesterday for their Neo home robot.

It has 25 points of movement, close to a real human hand.

The hand can also feel. Push on a finger and it reacts, knowing exactly how hard you pushed.

Sensors in the fingertips catch when something slips and fix the grip instantly.

In the demo it built LEGO, picked up single coins, poured tea, and held a wine glass without shattering it.

It’s even waterproof, so it can work at a sink and rinse itself clean.

Robots have struggled with hands for decades. I think that time is over.
