🇷🇺🇩🇪 RUSSIAN SABOTAGE NETWORK linked to DRONE BOMB PLOT in Germany

Investigators say a suspected Russian sabotage network hid drone parts beneath plastic flowers in a Lithuanian cemetery. 

A recruited courier retrieved the cache, moved it through Poland, and delivered it to a hostel in Düsseldorf. Other dead drops reportedly contained explosives concealed inside food cans.

German authorities are investigating whether the operation was linked to an explosive-laden drone discovered at Leipzig Airport. 

Security experts believe the goal wasn't simply destruction, but a warning: Russia can carry out covert attacks deep inside Europe against countries supporting Ukraine.
