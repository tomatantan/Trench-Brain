🇮🇷🇺🇸 There may be a much darker story behind the downed F-15 and the rescued pilots than anyone has said publicly. 

Trita Parsi, Iran expert and president of the Quincy Institute, joined the show with a theory he said he was raising for the first time on air: 

"One theory, in the absence of a credible explanation by the government, is that there was some sort of effort to go at the nuclear sites, which required taking over an airway in Iran, or going in with forces to build one... and that this whole thing was that attempt, and it failed miserably. 

The cover story, which may not be false in and of itself, is that there was an F-15 shot down and a rescue effort to save those two pilots, but that's just a marginal part of the story compared to what actually happened." 

He tied the timing directly to what came next: 

"Keep in mind, it is about two days after all of this that Trump comes out with that threat of saying he's going to end an entire civilization, and 12 hours later he agrees to a ceasefire." 

On the deeper cause of the collapse, he pointed to the Tomahawk numbers: 

"The U.S. lost more aircraft that weekend than it has since Vietnam, 12 in total. Was there ever a U.S. interest that warranted this war? The answer is absolutely clear, there never was." 

He wasn't buying the headline numbers on the Hormuz fee reports rattling markets: 

"I've seen a lot of reporting coming out of Reuters that's been highly questionable. I've not come across those numbers in any conversations I've had."
