The scary thing about high mcap trades is

When it’s going up hard, you’re sidelined cuz you know a lot of people having crazy uPNL so u don’t top blast

But your FOMO kicks in so you mistime it and top tick the chart 

Now you’re inclined to hold but when it goes down you don’t wanna sell for a loss, but u also don’t want to wait to 0 but you know a lot of people still have TPed and now there’s 6-7 figure bags underwater that haven’t capitulated yet

You’re not trying to sell the bottom but “selling the bottom” could be the difference between a $50k roundtrip and a $300k loss 

One of the issues people tend to have especially when it comes to hitting their first big trade is this idea of “I gotta make this one worth it I never know when I’ll get another chance” so you end up overholding for that extra milestone (7 fig, 100k, 250k, 2000 SOL, whatever pretty number) the thought of roundtripping is humiliating (mainly to one’s ego, nobody on the timeline actually cares), but the thought of “fuck I had 500k Pnl at the top I can’t just leave with 100k” then that thought becomes “fuck I knew I shoulda exit at 100k profit, now I’m down, I’ll sell if it recovers to 100k profit” then “fuck now that it’s back to 100k I feel like we can get 300k profit again” until you eventually miss that final exit and you’re down multiple 6 figs when you shoulda profited. I always warn people about the spiral chasing numbers

Anyways recent price action across the board has been short of impressive. Don’t get me wrong, fk load of cooks still, still a great time to be trenching/trading. But genny results don’t come from just holding, it comes from securing those profits for another time.

Bullish overall, but cautious.
