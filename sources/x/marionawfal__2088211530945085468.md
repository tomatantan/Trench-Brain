🇮🇷 Iran's parliament has reportedly approved the outline of a plan to control the Strait of Hormuz, including a ban on U.S. and Israeli ships.

A parliamentary committee signed off on the general principles, a committee spokesman told Iranian state media. 

One resolution would block vessels owned by the U.S., Israel and other "hostile countries" from passing through.

The reason he gave is that those countries have used the strait to act against Iran.

For now it's just an approved outline, not a law or an actual blockade. 

Trump says the U.S. has "total control" of the strait and plans to keep it. Tehran keeps insisting it's theirs.

It can’t get much messier than that…
