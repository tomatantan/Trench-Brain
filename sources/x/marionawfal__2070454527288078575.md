🇲🇪🇮🇷🇺🇸 An accused IRGC hacker who targeted 150+ American universities just got arrested, at a beach resort in Montenegro.

The numbers are staggering: $3.4 billion in damage. Stolen credentials allegedly funneled straight to Iran's Revolutionary Guard.

Montenegro police picked up Amir Barati, 39, in the coastal town of Kotor, on a U.S. warrant out of NY.

Here's the strange part: 

He's not one of the 9 men on the FBI's 2018 wanted poster for the Mabna Institute case, the operation that hit 144 U.S. universities and 100,000 professor accounts worldwide.

But his case matches Mabna almost detail for detail.

Same start year. Same university targets. Same IRGC link. The exact same $3.4 billion damage figure.

U.S. authorities haven't confirmed a connection. But the overlap is hard to ignore.

In 2018, the FBI's own deputy director warned: "We will keep at it, because the FBI and our partners at the Department of Justice have a very long memory."

Turns out the FBI wasn't bluffing.
