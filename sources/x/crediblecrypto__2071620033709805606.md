With today's move the can has been kicked down the road.

Cash reserves have been padded and 1.25B dollars worth $BTC has been approved for sale if needed to maintain cash reserves specifically for the payment of $STRC dividends and debt interest.

This alleviates the immediate concern of not having enough runway to fulfill the $STRC dividends for more than 5-6 months, that runway has now been extended out to 15+ months and up to 25+ months if you include the potential sale of 1.25B worth of $BTC.

However, this also means that Saylor has confirmed that $MSTR will now not only be a net buyer of $BTC but, if necessary, will sell billions of dollars of $BTC if/when needed.

Ultimately, this is a good development for the immediate short/mid term but that will change if/when $BTC enters a prolonged/secular bear market in which case a $MSTR related Bitcoin sell-off has become an even more realistic probability.
