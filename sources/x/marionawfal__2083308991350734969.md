🇺🇸🇮🇱🇮🇷 Iran’s power plants and refineries may be next on the target list

CBS sources say the U.S. and Israel are preparing strikes on Iranian energy infrastructure, with possible action this weekend.

Trump has not given the final green light. An Israeli official says they have not been formally asked to join.

Hitting energy targets is about more than explosions. It aims to degrade the regime’s ability to power military operations, keep cities functioning, and maintain internal control.

The U.S. has already struck dual-use bridges.

Expanding to the power grid and refining capacity would raise the cost of continuing the fight without a ground invasion.

Trump has linked previous threats to Iranian attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz: hit a ship, lose a bridge or a plant.

The logic is clear, make the economic and practical pain exceed the benefit of further escalation.

Markets are already watching. Oil, electricity, and the wider regional energy balance all sit downstream of whatever decision lands this weekend.

Whether it forces a shift or just prolongs a grinding air campaign is the open question.

The order still hasn’t been signed.
