An AI can find you the cheapest flight in seconds but It still can't buy the ticket.

That's the wall CZ keeps pointing at, and it's the most convincing crypto argument I've heard in a while.

Every payment system we have was built for humans. Cards need someone to swipe them. Banks need someone to authenticate.

The moment an agent hits two factor authentication or a KYC check, it stops dead.

A wallet doesn't care. It never asks who you are.

CZ reckons AI agents will end up doing 10,000 times more transactions than humans ever could, and none of it touching Visa or Swift.

Circle's CEO says the same. Billions of agents online, transacting around the clock, no human in the loop.

Nobody had to invent that use case. It's just the only rail that works for software.

Everyone keeps treating AI and crypto like rivals for the same money.

One of them is going to end up being the payment layer for the other.
