This one Hermes workflow has been a complete game-changer.

Literally everyone should set this up:

Connecting Granola to Hermes Agent.

It turns Hermes agent into your personal knowledge base - trained on all your meetings, notes, and even random brain dumps.

If you're unfamiliar, Granola is an AI tool that sits in the background of every call and automatically transcribes and summarises it.

By connecting it to Hermes, that data flows straight into your agent's memory with no manual filing required.

You don't even need to be in a team meeting to use it.

I'll literally start a solo Google Meet with Granola running and just talk through whatever's on my mind for 30 minutes.

That entire brain dump becomes searchable context Hermes can pull from forever (and can even create skills from).

To get started: install Granola and let it run during your calls. Then, just tell Hermes to scan to your Granola account and ingest every meeting into its memory.

Once it's connected, you can start asking things like:

→ "What did we actually decide on the call with [client] last week?"
→ "What are the recurring blockers coming up across my recent meetings?"

The possibilities with this are endless.

- Create a company knowledge base for your team
- Create skills for other AI tools (like Claude)
- Build SOPs based on notes/meetings

Such a high ROI setup that literally takes 5 minutes max.
