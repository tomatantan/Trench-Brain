🇨🇴 A 7.4 earthquake tore through western Colombia this morning, and the toll is climbing fast.

Local officials now put it as high as 70 dead, 40 of those in the Pereira metro area, per Mayor Mauricio Salazar. The major wires are still catching up around 47.

It hit at 7:34 a.m. local time, the strongest Colombia's seen in a decade, with shaking felt all the way to Bogotá.

Cali got hammered too. At least 25 buildings collapsed with people trapped inside, and rescue teams are rushing in from Bogotá and Medellín.

Seven airports are shut. Crews are digging through rubble as aftershocks keep hitting.

Praying for everyone still trapped and the families waiting on news tonight. 🙏
