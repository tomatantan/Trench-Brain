🇺🇸 Six months after Nancy Guthrie was taken from her bed, the loudest fight in the case is between the people meant to solve it.

Savannah Guthrie's 84-year-old mother was taken from her Tucson home on February 1. 

A masked figure showed up on her doorbell camera, but that video nearly got lost to auto-delete before investigators recovered it.

Half a year on, still no arrest. One ransom note claims she died soon after being taken and is "buried in nature," though that's never been confirmed.

Instead of a break, there's a public feud. FBI Director Kash Patel says the Pima County Sheriff's office shut the Bureau out for the first 4 days. 

Sheriff Chris Nanos denies it and says the FBI was in from day one.

Now sources close to the case want the FBI to take over completely, saying a killer is walking free while the two sides keep bickering.
