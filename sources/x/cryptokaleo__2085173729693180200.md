One of the issues crypto twitter has is a fixation for instant gratification.

So many reactions like this one:

"You said xyz was going to happen, and it didn't within 24 hours or even a week so you're dead wrong!"

So instead of holding, or even stacking - they sell. And then they FUD.

And then, after the move finally happens, the only thing they can chalk it up to is a conspiracy theory that the cabal didn't want them to win.

So then there's no accountability.

And when there's no accountability, the cycle repeats.

The majority of people will win more, when they learn to trade less, and believe in something instead.

And that doesn't mean you have to hold everything!

You should cut losers. If you don't have conviction in a position, you have no reason to hold it.

But if you do, and it's just not playing out exactly with the timing you were hoping for, then don't rotate and chase after greener pastures.

Quit thinking too hard about it. Or maybe think more.

Either way, hmm it over, and learn to believe again.
