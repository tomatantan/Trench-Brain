🇺🇸🇯🇵 The Fed is being dragged into Japan’s currency crisis.

Treasury Sec. Scott Bessent called for the Fed’s FIMA repo facility to be “upsized.” 

That obscure pandemic-era tool lets foreign central banks temporarily swap their U.S. Treasuries for dollars, without dumping the bonds on the open market.

Japan holds over $1.1 trillion in Treasuries, the largest foreign stockpile, but the yen just hit 40-year lows. 

Washington and Tokyo already staged a rare joint intervention last week, now Bessent wants a bigger Fed backstop ready so Japan can keep defending the yen without lighting a fire under U.S. bond yields.

This goes way beyond helping an ally, persistent yen weakness threatens global capital flows, Treasury market stability, and financial conditions everywhere. 

One weak currency in Tokyo can force policy decisions in Washington almost overnight. 

The quiet expansion of the Fed’s balance sheet into currency defense is starting to look inevitable.
