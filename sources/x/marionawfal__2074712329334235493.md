🇺🇸🇮🇷 The oil waivers just got revoked, and the ceasefire now exists on paper alone.

Analyst Stefano Ritondale draws a line most coverage is missing. 

Militaries can trade tit-for-tat strikes and still stumble into a fragile equilibrium, he observes. 

Economies work differently.

The formal removal of the key oil waivers, in his read, destroys the baseline that kept diplomacy alive. 

Those waivers were the critical economic conduits underpinning the entire Memorandum of Understanding.

With restrictions tightening and maritime traffic disrupted, Stefano says the agreement everyone keeps referencing now essentially functions on paper alone, a document without a living deal behind it.
