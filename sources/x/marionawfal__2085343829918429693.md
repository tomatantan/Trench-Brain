🚨🇮🇷🇴🇲 A deal between Iran and Oman is expected to be announced TODAY, establishing a "transit corridor" in the Strait of Hormuz.

Under the proposed 60-day interim framework, inbound vessels entering the Persian Gulf will use a northern lane under Iranian coordination. 

Outbound vessels exiting toward the Arabian Sea will use a southern route managed by Oman

Iran has emphasized talks with Oman are strictly bilateral, and the agreement isn't necessarily a guarantee that the waterway has become "safe."

Still, it's an important step towards resuming normal flows of oil in the world's most vital shipping lane.

And hopefully a stepping stone towards a broader deal.
