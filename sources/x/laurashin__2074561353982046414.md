"The misconception is that institutions want DeFi to become TradFi. But that's not actually true. They just want DeFi to be safe"

TuongVy Le on what institutions actually want from DeFi

"Institutions really want access to crypto and DeFi. The power of DeFi is that it can be all the things we were talking about, programmable and transparent and interoperable and composable. Institutions see the power of that"

"But they need to understand things like where does control sit, what is the strategy, what the risks are, how do the assets move, what happens in a stress event, what diligence should they be doing, and how do they explain this to all of their internal stakeholders"

"The lesson for us at Veda has been DeFi's powerful, people want it, everyday users want it. But the market needs to build products that are more usable, have better risk controls. We need more legal clarity, better operational infrastructure, and better interfaces between onchain systems and the real world"
