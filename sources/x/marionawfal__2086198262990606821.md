🇺🇸 Young Dems are all-in on DSA foreign policy radicalism.

The numbers tell the story: ending all immigrant detentions and deportations excites 71% of under50 Democrats, ending U.S. support for Israel to free Palestine gets 56%, dropping sanctions on Cuba, Venezuela, and Iran lands at 52%, and slashing the military budget while closing overseas bases also hits 52%.

The party’s old guard is still wedded to the establishment foreign policy consensus, while the rising young left wants a sharp break.

This internal tug-of-war is only getting messier.
