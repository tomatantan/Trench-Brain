Here's how to think about @Morpho Midnight

Midnight turns borrower trustworthiness into something competing curators can assess and price. 

It turns creditworthiness into a market, which creates a path toward undercollateralized lending onchain. 

If it works, onchain identity finally gets a direct economic use case.

A borrower specifies the loan, terms, and evidence of trust – Bitcoin collateral, identity, credit history, or another signal – then curators compete to price that risk.
