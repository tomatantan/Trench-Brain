Vivek Raman on why crypto is fine with or without the CLARITY Act:

"The CLARITY Act is important, it encodes how tokens are treated into law. But stablecoins are already in law. And that puts public blockchains into law."

"You can't put stablecoins back in the box. Too many institutions, every fintech company is integrating them."

"It's like undoing email, no one goes from email back to paper mail. No one goes from stablecoins back to physical wires."

"CLARITY would pour fuel on the fire. But tokenization is happening regardless. Stablecoins are happening regardless. Ethereum is now irreversible."
