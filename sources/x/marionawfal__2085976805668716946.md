FIFA boss Gianni Infantino is denying claims that UEFA paid off an alleged lover while he ran the organisation.

A Telegraph report says European football’s governing body handed a 6-figure sum to a woman said to have been in a relationship with him during his time as general secretary.

UEFA confirmed the payment happened and matched the rules back then.

Infantino’s side calls the whole thing categorically untrue and says no one ever complained about his behaviour.

This drops right as he’s fighting to keep his job after the World Cup sell-off fiasco blew up in his face. Not looking good.
