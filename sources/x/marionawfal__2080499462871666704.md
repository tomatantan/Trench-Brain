🇮🇷🇺🇸 Araghchi warns Trump's plan to spend Iran's frozen money on ship damages sets a precedent that will outlive this war:

"Seizing another nation's assets to pay for unrelated future claims is an incendiary precedent. 

Those who celebrate or profit from such funds should remember: once governments normalize confiscation, no one's assets are safe. 

Ensuing chaos will not be pretty or peaceful."

He's aiming past Washington here. 

The audience is every central bank holding reserves in dollars, and the argument writes itself: if your money can be seized and spent by whoever controls it, the dollar stops being a neutral store of value and just becomes a lever.

Beijing and Moscow have made that case for years. 

Trump just handed them a live example.

Iran's frozen assets run somewhere between $124 billion and $167 billion, and unfreezing them was the core of what Tehran got in June. 

Now they're a damage fund administered by the country Iran is fighting, and every nation watching does the math on what happens if it ever ends up on the wrong side of Washington.
