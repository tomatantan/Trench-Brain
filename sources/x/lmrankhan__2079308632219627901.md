A lot of great young talent with no crypto background is entering the space, raising from non crypto investors and assuming they will figure out PMF along the way.

My advice is to have at least one crypto native firm with deep knowledge on the cap table that have went through at least two cycles. 

Otherwise, it takes time to learn the culture, how crypto users behave, what worked in the early defidays, which experiments failed and why.

There is a lot of nuance yield farming, lending models, AMM design, attempts to solve impermanent loss, crypto options, token incentives, socialfi experiments etc

Without that teams will burn through capital repeating mistakes the industry has already made.

The good news is that a lot of talented founders are entering crypto.

The good / bad news is that a lot of investors with no crypto background are funding primitives they do not fully understand.
