Andy asks Kubi why market maker pricing beats Uniswap and Aerodrome's AMM model.

Kubi says it comes down to real time information:

"The most relevant price gets reflected at the top of the block. Everything that interacts with Ethereum after that has the freshest price available."

"Tighter quotes means routers and aggregators look onchain. And then you have the DeFi Lego money network effect."

"It's just a matter of making that liquidity available to everyone onchain. And you naturally have that in a permissionless blockchain ecosystem."
