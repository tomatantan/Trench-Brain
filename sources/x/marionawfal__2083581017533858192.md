🇸🇦🇾🇪 New satellite images reveal the damage the Houthis did to Aramco.

Sentinel-2 imagery from yesterday gives the clearest look yet at the damage from last week's Houthi drone and missile strike on Saudi Aramco's Jazan oil refinery.

The imagery reportedly shows at least three storage tanks completely burned out, one Horton sphere destroyed, and two more tanks badly damaged.

This is the first time the Houthis have hit Aramco since 2022, and they didn't stop at Jazan. They went after facilities in Yanbu too, all as payback for Saudi strikes on Hodeidah.

For a group everyone keeps writing off, reaching this deep into Saudi Arabia is not a small flex.
