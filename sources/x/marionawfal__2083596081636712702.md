It's false. Spain's Interior Ministry confirms it directly, zero deaths by stabbing in Ceuta between July 30 and August 1. Two knife injuries, both migrants attacking migrants, one after having an argument in the street. 

One suspect's already been arrested.

A fake stabbing video doesn't need to compete with reality here. Reality was already the story.
