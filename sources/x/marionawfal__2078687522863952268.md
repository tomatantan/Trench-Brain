🇰🇼🇵🇰 Kuwait is shopping for a new protector, and the shortlist doesn't start with Washington...

Reporting indicates Kuwait is negotiating an expanded defense pact with Pakistan, reportedly asking for aircraft, drones, air defenses, and thousands of troops, building on a limited 2023 arrangement. 

Saudi Arabia signed its own mutual defense treaty with Islamabad last September.

The talks are early, but the direction is unmistakable.

Understand what that represents. 

American bases on Kuwaiti soil are now the reason Kuwait gets hit, its interceptors are draining, and its power and water plants are taking fire. 

So the calculation shifts from whether the American shield holds to whether hosting it is worth the cost.

That is the deeper stake of this whole war.

The postwar order runs on the belief that American protection is the best available. 

Every capital that starts pricing alternatives is a payment the guarantee stops collecting.
