🇺🇸🇮🇷 Trump said Iran and other Middle East countries "asked" him to hold off this weekend's strikes. That's not the full story.

CNN reports the U.S. has burned through nearly 80% of its THAAD interceptors and roughly half its Patriots since this war started in February. Senior commanders are calling the stockpile "dangerously low."

This isn't new pressure. The Pentagon's been warning Trump for weeks. Gen. Dan Caine reportedly told him directly: the military could run a bigger campaign, but it would burn through the interceptors meant to protect U.S. troops and allies across the region.

A CSIS think tank report backs it up with numbers: Patriot interceptors down to fewer than 830, from 2,330 before the war. THAAD down to fewer than 280.

Rebuilding that stockpile takes years, not months.

So when Trump posted that Iran "begged" for a pause, Tehran's state media didn't even bother arguing the diplomacy... they went straight for the ammo story, gleefully repeating CNN's own reporting on the shortage as the real reason.

Diplomacy and depleted stockpiles aren't mutually exclusive here. But only one of those explanations makes Washington look like it's negotiating from strength.

Which version actually explains why the attack got called off, the deal, or the calculation?
