"An alliance needs to be based on the fundamental principle of good neighborly relations. My country is still faced with an open threat of war by Türkiye. 

The sensitivities of all NATO allies need to be taken into consideration. I am sure the issues can be resolved in the spirit of cooperation."

With Greece just securing a deal for its own advanced F-35s, the last thing they want is for Turkey to get them too.
