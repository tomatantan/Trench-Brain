Crypto traders are now leveraging on a Chinese chip IPO they legally aren't allowed to buy.

The company is CXMT, China's biggest memory chip maker and the fourth largest on earth.

It's about to have Asia's largest IPO of the year, listing in Shanghai on July 27.

But foreign investors are mostly locked out. China requires a huge minimum balance and years of experience to buy in.

So Hyperliquid built a synthetic version. A contract that tracks CXMT's price, tradeable by anyone, with leverage.

The contract implies CXMT is worth around $535 BILLION.

Its actual IPO values it at about $85 BILLION.

That's a 526% premium. Traders are pricing this chipmaker at six times what it's listing for.

CXMT is China's shot at breaking the memory chip monopoly held by Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron.

The stock isn't even public yet, and the world is already trading it onchain.
