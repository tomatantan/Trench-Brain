Bitcoin has a CPI-day playbook, and it's asymmetric. 📊

We went back through the last 8 US inflation prints. The pattern is hard to miss:

When CPI comes in cooler than expected, BTC has averaged +4% on the day — including +4.5% (Jan 13), +4.0% (Feb 13), and +4.4% (Jul 14, the biggest CPI-day pop of the run).
When it comes in hotter? The one clean hot miss (May 12) cost just −1.5% on the day… but bled −4.6% over the week.

In-line prints barely register: ±1%, even when the headline number looked scary. March's 3.3% — driven by a record gasoline spike, still saw BTC close +1.7%, because it landed below consensus. The market trades the surprise, not the level.

Today's test: July CPI drops at 8:30am ET. Consensus is 3.4% YoY (down from 3.5%), with BTC sitting near $63.6K going in.

Based on the last 8 prints:
🟢 Cooler (≤3.3%) → historically +3–4.5%, tests $65.5–66.5K

⚪ In line (3.4%) → range holds

🔴 Hotter (≥3.5%) → historically −1.5–2.5%, risks $62K
Historical averages, not predictions. But 3 of the last 4 cooler prints were +4% days.
