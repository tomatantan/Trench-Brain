🇺🇸 NYC Mayor Mamdani is handing home daycare providers emergency cash to stay open

Mamdani's putting up $3 million to help home daycare providers when something suddenly breaks. About 500 can apply this year for $1,000 to $10,000 to cover a burst pipe or storm damage, the kind of thing that shuts a daycare overnight.

What makes it matter is who they are. The city's got 6,000-plus, mostly women of color, watching over 100,000 kids on about $6 an hour. When you're earning that little, one bad week is enough to close the doors.

They're also the backbone of Mamdani's universal childcare plan, so keeping them going is the point. It should be up and running by year's end.

Small program, right instinct: government actually showing up for the people holding the childcare system together.
