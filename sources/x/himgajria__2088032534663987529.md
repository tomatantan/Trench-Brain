Ending the plumber debate.

TL;DR: Difficulty in entering the game is inversely correlated with difficulty in playing the game.

The game’s easiest to play when no one wants to play it, in its infancy, but that in itself makes it hard: having conviction in the game when no one believes in it.

The game becomes harder as more players enter, but loses its reward multiplier as it gets easier in terms of conviction: it’s no longer asymmetric.

I.e. entering the game when it’s hard (playing the game when it’s easy) > playing the game when it’s hard (entering the game when it’s easy)
