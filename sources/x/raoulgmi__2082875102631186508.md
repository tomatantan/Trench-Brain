That's the mistake many people make… lose money on a token, decide the whole thing is a scam, and walk away angry. All while the story keeps playing out around them.

It's how AI agents make payments at machine speed. It's how identity, privacy, and value move across the internet when humans aren't the primary users anymore.

AI and blockchain are married at the hip. Every agent, autonomous transaction, piece of the machine economy that's arriving now needs a programmable layer to run on. That's what the layer 1s actually are.
