🇺🇸🇸🇦 U.S. Middle East power rested on a grand bargain with Saudi for decades.

American military protection, bases, and security guarantees in exchange for Gulf oil priced in dollars, petrodollar recycling into U.S. Treasuries, Wall Street, and defense industries.

This anchored the post-1971 dollar system after Nixon ended gold convertibility.

Then the Iran War cracked it.

Riyadh reportedly refused full support for Washington's "Project Freedom" Hormuz counter-blockade, limiting U.S. options and forcing a diplomatic pivot.

With China as its top oil buyer and tech partner, plus the 2023 Chinese-brokered Saudi-Iran rapprochement, Riyadh is hedging for more autonomy.

The 50-year strategic-financial order that sustained U.S. hegemony in the Gulf is starting to fracture.
