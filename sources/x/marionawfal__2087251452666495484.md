🇺🇸🇬🇧 Mystery drones just showed up over one of the U.S.'s most secretive bases in the middle of nowhere.

Diego Garcia sits alone in the Indian Ocean, 1,000 miles from anywhere, a hub for U.S. bombers, nuclear subs and Space Force ops. The Pentagon's fallback fortress in the region.

Now drones of unknown origin have reportedly been spotted over it. The UK MoD confirmed it's aware but won't say a thing. U.S. Pacific Command was just as tight-lipped.

Iran already threw ballistic missiles at this island once during the war. Sea-launched drones are the threat everyone's quietly worried about next.
