🇬🇧 Andy Burnham is looking for something the UK has never had: a single written constitution.

His plan takes serious control over transport, housing, skills, policing, health and a share of income tax away from Whitehall and hands it to English mayors.

Burnham says shifting this much power from the centre strengthens the case for “a new clear set of principles” about how the country should be run.

Britain has always operated with an uncodified constitution, a mix of Acts of Parliament, political conventions, and common law (rules built up by judges over centuries through precedent) rather than one supreme document.

Burnham is now arguing that real devolution on this scale eventually means writing those rules down for the first time.

That’s a fundamental change.
