Just enabled monetization on X after 2 years and it looks like I got paid for all my engagement over the last two years while I didn't have it enabled... 

Doesn't really amount to much- just a little over $300 a month, but more importantly it means I can now start experimenting with X subscriptions.

One of the challenges I've had over my 9 years on this platform is finding a balance between sharing free content and actively engaging/interacting with my growing audience.

When I had a smaller account I would respond/reply to almost every single comment and DM. If you were an early follower, you'll remember this clearly. As my audience grew, this slowly began to become increasingly difficult- not only did I have 10x the message requests and comments coming in, but a number of them were from people who, unfortunately, weren't mindful of my time and would ask repeated, unnecessary questions without taking the time to watch/consume my free content first. 

Ultimately, this meant I was forced to cut down the amount of time and attention I could give to replying to comments/questions from my genuine followers/supporters and I had to change my approach (for my own sanity). 

With subscriptions, I have a third option- that is to charge a small subscription fee for those who want to actively support me while at the same time standing out amongst the crowd via the "subscription badge" they would get whenever commenting on a post of mine or interacting with my page on X- increasing the odds that I will respond/reply to them if/when they have a question or comment.

I don't want this to be viewed as a "service" (the sub fee would be too small to confuse it with one anyways) but rather a simple way to separate my "serious" followers vs those that are not respecting my time and taking it away from someone else who really values and deserves it.

Aside from the focused attention on subs, there are some other ideas I have that I may implement down the road (1 personal chart request per month for subs?), but in the end this will all just be one evolving experiment to see what makes the most sense for me and my audience (if at all).

I haven't turned subs on yet, but I am curious- does this sound like something you guys would be interested in/support?
