🇺🇸🇮🇷 Iran can't get oil out of its own ports past the U.S. blockade, so it stopped using them.

Since July 14, U.S. forces have turned back 48 ships, disabled 2 that refused, and boarded 2 more.

Not one crude tanker has slipped past enforcement out of the Gulf of Oman since July 12.

So Iran rerouted...

66 tankers hauled out roughly 80 million barrels since mid-June, over $6 billion for the IRGC.

Iranian tankers offload to Malaysia via ship-to-ship transfers off Johor, then sail home empty.

50+ ghost fleet tankers loiter openly near Singapore, signals on, without consequence.

Around 50 Iranian cargo ships still run the Indo-Pacific, Red Sea, and Baltic.

You can blockade the ports, but there's always a workaround...
