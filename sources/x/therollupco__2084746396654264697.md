Matthew Sigel reveals the market-regime change that explains the apathetic tape: CapEx winners became losers, and Bitcoin's just software.

"The first five months of the year, the companies spending the most on CapEx had the best-performing stocks."

"Since June 1st, that flipped, the CapEx spenders started getting punished."

"And Bitcoin fell into the software bucket. People were tracking the semi-to-software ratio, and in the end, Bitcoin is software, open-source software. So when software underperformed, crypto got weighed down with it."

"On top of that, AI tools like Claude Code and Codex are surfacing vulnerabilities in open-source projects whose upgrade cycles are slow, you can't just force an upgrade."

"Maybe it started with the Zcash issue. That software underperformance is a lot of what's weighed on crypto."
