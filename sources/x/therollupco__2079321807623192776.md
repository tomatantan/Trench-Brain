Salman Banaei explains why a single section of the bill could be the biggest unlock for banks onchain:

"Title IV, Section 401 permits banks to use digital asset technology for any financial activity they engage in. That in and of itself is an enormous unlock."

"Banks could get that guidance from regulators in the interim. But having a statute behind it makes it future-proof."

"Banks build on multi-year horizons. If there's any concern a future regulator makes them wind down digital asset operations, that raises the risk they assign to every new project."
