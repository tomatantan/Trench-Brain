Stani’s assessment is correct and lays out the penultimate bear case for most L1 tokens. The networks do not have strong pricing power wrt blockspace, ie fees (therefore burns) cannot go up. But staking yields are critical to the ecosystem so yields cannot go down

This makes the token inflationary (read: unprofitable) with no clear exit ramp to profitability

So the major PoS networks are trapped in circular economies they cannot escape barring some application going mega viral. Except that mega viral applications now have a preference to build their own chains (uniswap and many others)

brutal catch-22
