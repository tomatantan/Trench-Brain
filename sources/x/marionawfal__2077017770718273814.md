🇨🇳🇺🇸🇮🇷 China is urging the U.S. and Iran to avoid returning to war, stressing the need to "safeguard the hard-won ceasefire."

And "more importantly," China's FM spokesman said, "prevent fighting from spreading and hurting more innocent people."

China has been the largest purchaser of Iranian oil, buying roughly 90% of Iran's exported crude, a crucial lifeline to the isolated state.

So Beijing holds significant leverage in theory, but its influence is constrained by Iran's ideological goals and its own geopolitical interests.
