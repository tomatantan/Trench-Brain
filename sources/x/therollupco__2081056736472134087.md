"Everyone obsesses over pricing data for tokenized stocks. The real unsolved question is what you actually own."

"This is a share of stock, do I have dividends coming? Do I have the right to vote? We're not there yet."

"Pricing gets the lion's share of attention. But the key thing is: what do I actually own?"
