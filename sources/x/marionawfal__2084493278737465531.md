🇰🇷 Korea's market has dropped 38% in 27 trading days, and the AI boom that built it explains why it can fall that fast

Weeks ago Korean exports were setting records, clearing $100 billion in a single month on almost nothing but AI demand. 

That figure was never really about exports. 

It was a bet that the world's data centers cannot run without Korean memory, and the entire index was leaning on it.

Concentration feels wonderful going up. 

Samsung and SK Hynix jumped 27 and 30% Friday, then handed back 9% each on Monday, with foreign money buying a record amount one session and dumping nearly $700 MILLION the next.

What actually shifted is China closing ground on chips. 

No Korean factory got worse over the weekend. 

The assumption that nobody else can do this got shakier.

Markets built on being irreplaceable break hardest the moment that stops feeling obvious.
