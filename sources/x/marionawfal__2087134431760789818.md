🇺🇸🇮🇶🇦🇫 America’s post-9/11 wars have uprooted more people than ANY conflict since World War II.

A Brown University Costs of War study puts the conservative total at 38 million, displaced across 8 countries.

Iraq alone accounts for 9.2 million, followed by Syria at 7.1 million, and Afghanistan at 5.9 million.

Most of those forced from their homes remained inside the greater Middle East, though hundreds of thousands eventually reached Europe and North America.

Researchers say "the true number" could be closer to 60 million...
