🇮🇷 Speed is the new warhead, and Iran is turning it into a force multiplier.

A rocket motor that accelerates the re-entry vehicle in the final seconds can push impact velocity high enough for pure kinetic energy to rival or exceed a larger conventional explosive. The same missile suddenly hits harder without carrying extra weight.

Cheap drones compound the problem. Every multimillion-dollar interceptor that knocks one down still leaves the next drone free to be retargeted in real time. The cost exchange heavily favors the attacker.

Meanwhile, systems built for predictable ballistic paths struggle when the warhead starts maneuvering. Interceptors locked onto an outdated flight path arrive too late. The combination of higher kinetic damage, swarm economics, and agile terminal trajectories is rewriting the defensive equation.
