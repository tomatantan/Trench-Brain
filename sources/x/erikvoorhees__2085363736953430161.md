Sell someone an e-bike? 

No crime has been committed, and no person has been hurt. There is no victim from that action.

Making it illegal *creates* the victims: the retailer who has a right to sell a good and earn money, and the consumer who has a right to buy the device and use it.  Neither of them has committed any crime, yet are now victimized under the coercion of state. 

If you hit/hurt someone whether on an e-bike, or a car, or any other device, that is already illegal. If the state was ethical and effective, it would prosecute such crimes. Since it is neither ethical nor effective, it invents new crimes where no victim exists and punishes people for those.
