🇺🇸🇬🇧 The U.S. government wants in on Trump’s $10 billion lawsuit against the BBC over a documentary that spliced together bits of his January 6 speech. 

The broadcaster is calling it a clear and stark conflict of interest, and it's not hard to see why. 

The federal agencies looking to join the suit answer directly to the man bringing it.

Apparently when you're president, the line between "my personal lawsuit" and "the full weight of the U.S. government" gets a little blurry.

This is the same playbook the administration tried in the E. Jean Carroll defamation case, where it tried to swap itself in as defendant.

It didn’t go especially well then either.
