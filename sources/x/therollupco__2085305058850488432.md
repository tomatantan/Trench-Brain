Which stablecoins are actually most at risk of depegging?

@markoinether, founder of @Tapir_Protocol says the yield-bearing ones running debt strategies in the background.

"Any product giving you higher yield via a more sophisticated, and therefore riskier, strategy has a much higher chance of depegging."

"I'm not very worried about USDC these days. A short-term depeg in a big market dislocation, sure, that's possible."

"But a long-term depeg? Probably not. It's the yield and bond-strategy products that carry the real risk."
