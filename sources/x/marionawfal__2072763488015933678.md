🇻🇪 Fuel shortages are slowing earthquake rescue efforts in Venezuela

Fuel shortages have forced excavators and cranes to stop working at some rescue sites in La Guaira, delaying efforts to clear debris and search for survivors.

Families say heavy machinery has been sitting idle since the previous day because there wasn't enough diesel.

In response, state oil company PDVSA ramped up diesel production, while officials moved to address the fuel shortage.

The government says 1.5 million liters of diesel have now been delivered to keep rescue equipment running.
