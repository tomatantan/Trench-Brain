🇺🇸🇮🇷 The U.S. just stacked nine tankers over Hormuz

Six KC-135 Stratotankers and three KC-46A Pegasus, all running refueling loops around the Strait.

These planes don't drop anything, they keep fighters fueled and airborne, and you don't crowd this many of them over one narrow waterway unless you expect jets to be up there for a long stretch.

It's one of the heaviest tanker buildups the region has seen lately.

Fuel is the quiet part of a loud plan, and right now there's a lot of it circling Iran's doorstep.
