🇷🇺🇺🇦 Zelensky just admitted Ukraine has no undamaged power plants left, and Stanislav Krapivnik says that's the logic of how this war ends.

Krapivnik, a former U.S. Army officer originally from the Donbas, joined the show with the blunt math behind Russia's advance: 

"Any serious war is won before the first bullet is fired. The U.S. supply chain is destroyed. China has 97% of the world's processed rare earth minerals, Russia has 1%, and the rest of the world has 2%, which is nothing." 

Why Russia keeps pushing rather than stopping at the current line: 

"When Kharkov is being shelled, Russia has to defend its citizens, so it has to keep pushing back the boundary until it gets to somebody that doesn't want to shoot anymore. 

Whether that's western Ukraine, Poland, or Berlin, that's up to the other side." 

On claims that the Baltics are neutral bystanders, he pushed back hard: 

"Estonia is fully part of this war. It's not just airspace, drones are lifting off of Estonia and Latvia. Everybody knew that, they just didn't want to expand the war by admitting it."

And on the Wall Street Journal report that NATO expects Russia to test the alliance by 2029, he had a warning about the rhetoric itself: 

"When they say they're about to attack us, the translation is we need to attack them first. 

That's exactly what Hitler told the Reichstag to get the rubber stamp on Barbarossa."
