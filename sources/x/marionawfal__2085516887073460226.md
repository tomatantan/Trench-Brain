🇺🇸 Trump put public pressure on Congress to finish a bipartisan bill that would lock in protections for college athletics.

The measure targets the chaos that followed big court rulings and the NIL era; unlimited transfers, rising costs, and real risk to women’s programs and Olympic development sports.

College athletics still produce a huge share of America’s Olympic talent, and the White House is treating the system as something worth preserving rather than letting it drift into pure pro-style economics with no guardrails.

The aim is set clearer rules, protect scholarships and roster spots in non-revenue sports, and give schools tools to keep the broader ecosystem viable.

The push is straightforward: keep the college-to-Olympics pipeline intact and stop the drift that was already putting entire programs at risk.
