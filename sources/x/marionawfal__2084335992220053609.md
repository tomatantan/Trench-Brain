🇵🇸🇮🇱🇺🇸 Trump got Hamas to agree to disarm, and Israel answered by bombing anyway

Trump's Board of Peace called on Israel to halt operations fully and immediately. Hamas agreed Friday to hand its weapons to a new Palestinian committee. Trump called it a milestone and said Israel was "very happy" with it.

Netanyahu hasn't said a word about the deal. His office quietly went the other way, insisting the public version "does not reflect Israel's positions."

The strikes never paused. Gaza officials say 19 Palestinians were killed since early yesterday, women and children among them.

Netanyahu's condition is simple: no withdrawal until Hamas is fully, verifiably, irreversibly disarmed. That's the deadlock. Trump wants the guns down through diplomacy, Israel wants them gone by force before it moves an inch.

Now one of the plan's own architects, Mohammad Dahlan, is accusing Netanyahu of strangling the best shot anyone has had at ending this war.

Trump's name is on the peace plan. The bombs landing on it are Netanyahu's.
