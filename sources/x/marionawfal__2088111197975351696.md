🇮🇷 🇺🇸 The strikes killed the Iranians most willing to make a deal

Every senior security post Mojtaba Khamenei filled this week belonged to someone killed in February, and who he chose decides the next year. 

Mohsen Rezaei, the man who told the Wall Street Journal in 2012 that Iranian enrichment did not have to happen inside Iran, now runs the Supreme National Security Council and demands Washington surrender on Tehran's terms. 

Ahmad Vahidi took the Revolutionary Guard, Hossein Taeb took the Basij, and the technocrats arguing that a deal preserves what's left of the economy lost that fight decisively.

Tehran has also stopped needing the old deterrence. 

The New York Times reported it drained American Patriot stocks in Jordan before punching through, while Gulf producers now treat Iranian control of Hormuz as permanent, with crude transits collapsing from roughly 20 million barrels a day to 2.2 million.

A cornered government holding cheap leverage, with nobody left inside to argue for restraint...
