SpaceX stock climbed today even as the first big lockup expired and more than doubled the shares available to trade.

Employees and early investors can finally sell near 911M shares.

Markets usually panic at that kind of supply dump… but the stock rose anyway, with massive volume.

After the post-IPO rollercoaster (and yesterday’s drop on the AI spending numbers), that’s a pretty clear signal: a lot of people still want in.

Elon structured this IPO so tightly that only a tiny slice was public at 1st.

Now the float is expanding in stages, yet the core thesis hasn’t changed.

Same guy who made rockets reusable and Starlink global is now pouring that efficiency mindset into AI infrastructure.

Short-term volatility is noise. The long-term opportunity is still enormous.
