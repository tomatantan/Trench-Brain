A stablecoin cross-border transfer settled in ~7 minutes vs. 3-4 hours for traditional interbank.

Olivia Vande Woude on why enterprises adopting crypto rails don't actually care that it's crypto:

"We're focused on business outcomes, not the minutiae of whatever tech is underlying them."

"With this cross-border remittance, the relevant parties weren't interested in the fact that it was a stablecoin-based transfer on Tether. They cared about the cheaper fees and the time it took."

"The entire process took about seven minutes. Traditional interbank transfers take as much as three to four hours, or longer."
