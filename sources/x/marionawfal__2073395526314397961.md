🇮🇷 Mojtaba Khamenei, the new Supreme Leader, has not been seen in public since March. 

He wasn't at the memorial for his own wife and teenage son, killed on the war's first day when the family compound was bombed. Security officials are blocking his attendance at his father's funeral over fears Israel will assassinate him or track his location.

Iran is being run by a Supreme Leader in hiding.

The pragmatist faction, Ghalibaf, Pezeshkian, the IRGC's senior generals, pushed the deal through despite hardliner opposition, essentially forcing Mojtaba's hand.

The Supreme National Security Council voted 12-1 in favor. Mojtaba approved it while publicly stating he opposed it "on principle."

Meanwhile hardliners are calling negotiators traitors on state television, chanting "death to appeasers" at Araghchi in Iraq, and at least one lawmaker is publicly asking whether a coup is underway. The director of state broadcasting (appointed by the senior Khamenei from the ultra-hardline camp) cut Ghalibaf off mid-interview live on air.

Iran's vice president for executive affairs recently said the new Supreme Leader's opinion is like any other official's and should be debated. A statement that would have been unthinkable 6 months ago.

The appointments Mojtaba makes after the funeral (judiciary, state broadcasting, Basij, chief of staff) will tell you everything about which direction Iran actually goes from here. 

The pragmatists got the deal. The hardliners are fighting for the institutions.

This is the real story underneath the MOU.
