@retirewithfire built something a reward mechanic that pays you by how long you hold, not how big your bag is.

@sonder_crypto broke it down:

Most reflection tokens (Index and others) pay 3% on buys and sells, rewarding bag size. $FIRE flips that. The longer you hold, the more of the reward pool you earn, so a smaller trader with conviction can out-earn a whale who just parks size.

And the rewards come in tokenized stocks (Apple, Nvidia, Tesla), not in $FIRE itself. So you buy, hold, and get paid in stocks that aren't correlated to the coin.
