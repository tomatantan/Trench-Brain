🇺🇸🇮🇷 The truce that was supposed to calm the war may have handed Iran the fuel to fight the next round

Look at what Tehran did with the pause instead of what it said. 

During the roughly one-month agreement, Iran rushed out around 70 million barrels of stored oil, five to six billion dollars, per Wall Street Journal figures. 

The money matters, but the deeper win was operational.

Iran's tankers and Kharg Island were near capacity, its wells inching toward a shutdown that would have crippled output for years. 

The truce drained that pressure.

So a deal meant to stabilize energy markets did the opposite of contain Iran. 

It refilled the war chest, relieved the infrastructure, and bought time to rebuild the drones and missiles now falling on Gulf bases.

Meanwhile the American ledger ran the other way. 

Every interceptor fired at those drones is one closer to a stockpile the U.S. cannot refill for years. Iran restocked during the calm. 

Washington spent through it. 

That asymmetry is the war's quiet engine, and the pause FED IT.
