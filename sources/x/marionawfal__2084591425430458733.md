🇷🇺🇺🇦 Russian drones have struck the Holy Spirit Cathedral in Kherson.

The church sustained damage, windows were broken, and nearby residential areas were also hit.

No casualties reported thus far.

The cathedral has been repeatedly damaged by Russian shelling:

-⁠In January 2026 (on Christmas Day), shells hit the main dome and the grounds.
-In February 2026, further strikes damaged the roof, bell tower, and a chapel.
-In April 2026, two shells struck the roof, causing a fire, destroying parts of ceilings, and heavily damaging the central altar, walls, murals, and surrounding buildings.

The cultural destruction of this war is secondary to the human lives lost, but it's yet another casualty...
