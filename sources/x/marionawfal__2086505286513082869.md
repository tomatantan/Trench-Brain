🇺🇸🇮🇷 Trump says the U.S. is deliberately “low keying it” with Iran for now.

Instead of launching a new military campaign, he is letting economic pressure do the work.

In a short interview he pointed to Iran’s soaring inflation, empty coffers and inability to pay its own troops.

The naval blockade is intensifying that crisis while oil prices have eased enough to reduce pain at the American pump.

Trump described the standoff as a chess game that “always works out.”
