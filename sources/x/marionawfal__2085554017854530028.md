🇺🇸 Someone erased an entire town’s surveillance network overnight.

Every single Flock license plate camera in Winona, Minnesota is gone. 

Poles were cut down, cameras taken, and nothing was left but weeds and empty mounts.

The 8 devices would cost roughly $24,000 to replace. 

Police say it happened between late July and early August, but this isn't simply random vandalism. 

Flock Safety is at the center of a fight that’s already spreading across the country: cities canceling contracts, states looking at bans, critics calling the cameras a privacy nightmare, yet police calling them essential for catching criminals.

The debate used to stay in meetings and courtrooms, but now it’s happening on the side of the road with a saw.
