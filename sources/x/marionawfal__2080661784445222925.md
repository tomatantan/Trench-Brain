🇺🇦 Ukraine’s fertility collapse did not begin with the full-scale invasion, the downward trend started in 2014.

The first half of 2026 has produced the worst population figures yet...

Deaths now outnumber births by a ratio of 3.55 to 1, and the birth rate itself has plunged more than 15% compared with last year.

Even regions far from the front lines are recording steep drops. Western oblasts such as Vinnytsia, Lviv, and Ternopil are among the hardest hit.

Natural population decline is accelerating, with Kyiv posting a particularly dramatic annual surge. The numbers paint a grim picture that long predates the current war and only continues to deepen.
