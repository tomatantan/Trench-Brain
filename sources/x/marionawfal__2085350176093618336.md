🇺🇸🇨🇳 The Pentagon’s top policy official can’t even get an invite to Beijing...

Elbridge Colby, a longtime China hawk, has been pushing for months to visit and speak at China’s National Defense University. 

Thus far, the Chinese are giving him the cold shoulder.

Anger over the record $11 billion U.S. arms sale to Taiwan appears to be a big factor. 

Beijing's in no rush to host the man who once wrote that China is preparing for war and that America needs to prioritize the fight.

Colby has softened his tone lately, talking about strategic stability and insisting the U.S. doesn’t seek regime change. 

But an RSVP is unlikely to arrive in the mailbox.
