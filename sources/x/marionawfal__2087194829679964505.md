🇷🇺 Russia just opened the door to public crypto trading.

The Bank of Russia has officially added Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Tether to its list of cryptocurrencies approved for trading on domestic exchanges.

Ordinary (non-qualified) investors will be limited to buying no more than $3,632 worth of crypto per year.

The central bank said these 3 assets met its requirements for market size, trading volume, and long-term pricing history.
