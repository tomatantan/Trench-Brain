AMD took a shot at NVIDIA’s robot brain

At its AI event the company launched an all in one board that combines the main processor, graphics, AI chips and real time controllers.

One chip runs the brain, others handle movement and sensors instantly.

AMD’s own numbers claim it reacts up to 3.4× faster and handles more tasks than NVIDIA’s top edge board for robots.

They’re also backing an Italian humanoid startup. Insane.
