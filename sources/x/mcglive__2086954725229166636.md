Umbra is trying to make privacy the default, not a separate step people have to seek out...

The team explained their approach to distribution:

Privacy only becomes useful if it shows up where people already are. Asking someone to move capital to a separate platform just to get privacy isn't exciting or realistic for average day-to-day use.

Payroll became the first real proof of that thesis, and it worked. The plan is more integrations like it, embedding privacy into existing workflows rather than building a separate destination for it. More similar products are on the table too, once they find strong pmf for the core offering.
