George Gammon says the AI industry may be building a business model where the endgame is failure and a government bailout.

"They're charging $20 a month, but the cost to deliver the service is far more than $20." 

His argument: AI companies know the economics may not work, so they're racing to become too big to fail, fueled by massive spending and circular financing.

“If we win, great. If we don’t, we’re going to get bailed out by the government. 

So, what’s my downside? Just keep selling the dream.”
