🇱🇧🇮🇱 Lebanese PM Salam hit back hard at a visitor who accused the state of favoring Israel over national sovereignty.

Salam argued that the real damage to Lebanon came from those who pulled the country into costly “support” wars, handing Israel repeated pretexts to strike cities, kill civilians, and displace hundreds of thousands.

He insisted true sovereignty exists only when a single independent state decides and a single army controls the entire territory.

Calls for dialogue and unity, he said, ring hollow unless they start with honesty about past reckless choices and the heavy price Lebanese families are still paying.

The government remains focused on ending the occupation, freeing prisoners, and enabling the safe return and rebuilding of destroyed communities.
