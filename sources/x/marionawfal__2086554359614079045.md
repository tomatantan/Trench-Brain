🇮🇱🇺🇸🇵🇸 The director general of the Gaza Board of Peace Nickolay Mladenov pushed back after Netanyahu rejected the 15-point plan.

Mladenov said the roadmap’s whole point is to make sure Gaza can never threaten Israel again like it did on October 7. 

That means complete disarmament of every weapon in the territory first, including personal arms, heavy weapons and production sites.

Israel would only withdraw step by step, sector by sector, after those weapons are verified as collected and rendered unusable by the Board of Peace and an international security force. 

Governance of Gaza would also shift to a National Committee under the Board.

He stressed the entire process is built on verification, not trust. 

If the commitments aren’t met at any stage, everything stops. 

Mladenov called this the only realistic path to prevent another October 7.
