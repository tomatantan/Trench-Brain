🇺🇸 A billionaire who bankrolled New York Republicans for decades just shut the checkbook.

Ronald Lauder, the 82-year-old heir to the Estée Lauder cosmetics fortune, has gone quiet. Nothing to his home-state GOP this year, and his super PAC is just sitting there in a midterm year.

He's also walking away from the World Jewish Congress after running and bankrolling it for 20 years.

Quietly, he's cut loose a whole crew of advisers and consultants, some who'd been with him since his 1989 mayor run.

Word is his own kids stepped in to slow down his spending, and the family money took a beating. His stake in the cosmetics giant is down at least $1.2 billion, with the stock off 77% from its 2021 high.

So the GOP is losing one of its biggest wallets right when it needs the cash most.
