🇨🇳 China is going all-in on food independence, and it could reshape global markets.

For decades, China has been a massive buyer of U.S., Brazilian, and Argentine ag products, driving demand for soybeans, beef, and more.

Now Beijing is treating food self-sufficiency as a national security priority, using the same playbook that made it dominant in EVs and solar.

They’re pushing alternative proteins, changing livestock feed, and investing heavily in domestic production.

Analysts say efficiency gains could wipe out up to a quarter of soy demand by 2030, roughly the entire volume of U.S. soybeans China imported in recent years.

By 2040-2050, China could flip from top importer to competitor or even net exporter in some animal proteins.

Exporters that built entire sectors around feeding China are watching closely, there’s no easy replacement market at that scale.
