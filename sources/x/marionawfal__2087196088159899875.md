🇸🇦🇨🇴🇮🇱🇸🇾 Saudi Arabia joins Syria in STRONGLY condemning Colombia’s decision to recognize Israeli sovereignty over the Golan Heights.

The FM called the move a clear violation of international law and UN Security Council Resolution 497 from 1981, which declared Israel’s annexation of the occupied Syrian territory 'null and void.'

Riyadh said such recognition only entrenches the logic of force, undermines the established international position on Golan, and damages efforts to achieve lasting peace in the region.

Saudi reaffirmed that Golan remains occupied Syrian Arab land, and urged Colombia to reverse its decision.
