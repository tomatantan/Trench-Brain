🇮🇷 Iran's parliament just moved to lock in its grip on the Strait of Hormuz.

Ebrahim Azizi, who chairs the parliament's national security committee, says the bill formalizing Iran's control of the strait was introduced overnight, timed to what he called the downing of American drones.

He called the strait a red line Iran won't budge on, and warned this is just the first step, with more to come that "will rob sleep from the eyes of our enemies."

The plan asserts Iran's sovereignty over the waterway, bars hostile and Israeli-linked ships, and puts the military in charge of enforcement.
