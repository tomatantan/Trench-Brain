Matthew Sigel from VanEck on the AI-vs-railroad-bubble comparison:

"On scale: US put 3% of GDP into rails for almost two decades. Year five post-GPT and we're only hitting 3% of GDP once this year."

"Current equity valuations aren't discounting two-decade continuation, most analysts see peak by 2030."

"On financing: railroads were government-engineered. 1862 Railway Act granted hundreds of millions of acres of federal land to railways."

"Titles didn't transfer until the whole network was built. US Treasury floated construction bonds subordinated to private capital. Largest railways sold bonds overseas marketed as safe, dependent on land transfers, companies didn't hold title yet."

"On utility and backlog: rail networks didn't provide true utility until East Coast and West Coast connected. AI factories generate utility immediately."
