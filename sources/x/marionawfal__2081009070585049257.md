🇮🇷 The IRGC says it stopped 4 ships in Hormuz over 24 hours with warning shots, forcing them to alter course. 

The vessels were using the southern corridor Iran calls an "unsafe route"; only Tehran-designated lanes are permitted.

Strip the language and the mechanism is the new checkpoint. Iran has been hitting tankers that hug the Omani coast to push traffic into its own waters, where "safe passage" is available, the unstated price being a fee the MOU never provided for.

Not a single drop passes without permission, the IRGC says. Permission is the whole business model.
