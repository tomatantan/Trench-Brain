🚨🇺🇸 Justice Alito has confirmed to WSJ that he will NOT be retiring from SCOTUS... yet.

Near the end of the Court's term in June, NPR posted and later retracted a story announcing his retirement.

He's since batted down those rumors, saying he intends to continue serving, even if he runs the risk of Trump having a Democrat succeed him.

This is the challenge with the Supreme Court being a lifetime appointment. You can stay on until you die or are unable to continue in the role...

Or you can bow out during a favorable administration to ensure your ideological continuity on the bench.

For now, Alito is staying put.
