🇱🇹 Lithuania could soon drop its constitutional ban on hosting nuclear weapons.

The country's political leaders have agreed to start the process, saying the security situation on NATO's eastern border isn't what it used to be.

If parliament signs off, Lithuania could eventually host French nuclear weapons under Macron, France's biggest fan of aviator sunglasses, or even U.S. nuclear assets.

It's not a done deal yet. The amendment still has to pass two parliamentary votes with a two-thirds majority.

A few years ago, this would've sounded far-fetched. Now it's a serious political discussion.
