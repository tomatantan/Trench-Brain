While everyone waits on CLARITY, Dinari co-founder Gabriel Otte says they didn't, they put tokenized US stocks onchain legally today:

"For so long, people said there's only one way to get tokenized stocks in the US: the issuer-sponsored way, going to each company and asking them to tokenize their shares separately, outside the existing capital-market system."

"We just demonstrated that's not needed. You can be legal within Reg NMS, the national market system, and launch these tokens."

"The CLARITY Act will only solidify and clarify that further, which is not a bad thing. But we're still in the first part of the first inning."
