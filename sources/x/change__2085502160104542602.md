"Nobody ever wants to find out that their son's body parts were sold."

Andrea Mauk was out of the country when her 18-year-old son Damon died in a car accident. By the time she made it home, she learned his heart tissue, skin, eyes and ligaments had already been removed as "anatomical gifts," without her consent. "A simple 'yes' from his estranged father let his organs be removed before an autopsy," she said.

Now, Andrea is calling on Congress to pass "Damon's Law" to require real informed consent and stop donated tissue from being shipped to and sold by for-profit companies.
