🇦🇪🇮🇷 Iran keeps hitting ADNOC's tankers, and the company keeps buying more of them

ADNOC says 15 of its vessels have been struck by Iranian missiles or drones since the war began, three just this week. 

One crew member is dead and 20 injured.

Yet instead of pulling back, ADNOC just announced $1.3 billion for 11 new supertankers. 

Getting shot at hasn't slowed the UAE down, it's betting the oil keeps flowing long enough to make the ships pay off.
