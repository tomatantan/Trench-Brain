🇺🇦🇺🇸 Zelensky can't find anyone willing to be Ukraine's ambassador to Trump's Washington.

It should be an easy task, but everyone keeps turning it down at the worst possible moment in the war. 

He offered it to outgoing PM Yulia Svyrydenko, who passed, and he's been hunting for a heavyweight ever since without any luck.

The problem is the gig itself. 

Trump's White House has flipped on weapons and peace terms so many times the last envoy called it "diplomatic whiplash." 

So whoever takes it inherits that chaos just as talks may reopen and Kyiv fights to build its own Patriots.

The most important seat Ukraine has abroad could sit empty until after the U.S. midterms.

Everything Kyiv needs comes through Washington, and no one wants to be the one asking for it.
