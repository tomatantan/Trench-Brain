Bloomberg says the SEC has major crypto plans coming.

@Bitwise CIO @Matt_Hougan on what they actually are, and why they're bigger than people think:

"This is the bypass road on the CLARITY Act."

"CLARITY is legislation, slow. The SEC can act through regulation. In about a week, we expect the first draft of the Reg Crypto rules."

"They're intended to let new projects raise capital without triggering SEC registration, then evolve into a centralized platform."

"That's a dream we've had since 2018 and the ICO boom, but now with regulatory teeth and certainty."

"People are dramatically underestimating the entrepreneurial energy this creates."
