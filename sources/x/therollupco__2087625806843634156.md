Guillaume Lambert says Uniswap's "5 basis point" fee switch is really a 15-25% cut to what liquidity providers earn.

"It's five basis points out of 30. In a 30bps pool, every swap pays 30 to the LP, and the fee switch redirects five of those away from them."

"So the LP goes from 30 to 25. That's a 15% reduction in their rewards."

"And it scales. In a 5bps pool, the redirected portion is a bigger proportion, that's when you get to a 25% cut."

The takeaway: "5bps" is misleading. What matters is 5bps out of the pool's total, and for LPs, that's 15-25% of their earnings.
