🇺🇸🇮🇷 U.S. spies are quietly admitting the war on Iran isn't working.

Intelligence analysts have concluded that Washington and Tehran are stuck in an indefinite limbo between peace and war.

Iran has lost commanders, air defences, and a huge chunk of its hardware. The regime is still standing. Analysts specifically flagged Tehran's staying power.

The White House theory is that enough pressure eventually makes Iran flexible. Jonathan Panikoff, the former deputy national intelligence officer for the Near East, says that assessment is almost certainly incorrect and sees no good options left for the U.S.

Every round of strikes was sold as the one that would force a deal. Months later the deal is no closer and the flare-ups keep coming.
