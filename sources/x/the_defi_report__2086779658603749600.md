Does World Chain's economics matter if the chain isn't the product?

For @world_chain_ , the chain itself is not the core product. It's infrastructure for distributing and using the identity layer.

Roughly 60% of World Chain transactions are account-abstraction transactions, meaning users can simply tap the app rather than interact with a wallet and browser.

That means the identity layer's value may not show up in a chain-fee chart.

World generated just $58.5k in fees over the last 90 days, but the question is whether the identity layer can become valuable enough for applications to pay for verification.
