🇸🇦🇹🇷🇵🇰 A new military bloc just formed in the Islamic world, and it's got the nukes Iran wishes it had...

The Makkah (Mecca) Joint Defense Agreement, signed today by MBS, Erdogan, and Shehbaz Sharif, carries one line that does all the work:

An armed attack on any one of them counts as an attack on all three.

That's Article 5 language, the same principle that binds NATO, now stretched across two continents and a nuclear-armed Pakistan.

It's not just the shared trigger. The three are also stitching their defense sectors closer together, weapons, training, the whole lot.

Three militaries now tied to one serious red line. Anyone weighing a move against one of them now has to plan for all three.
