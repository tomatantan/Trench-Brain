🇪🇺🇪🇸 Ursula von der Leyen joins in the criticism of Sánchez' government, over its handling of Ceuta:

"The images coming from Ceuta are unacceptable. We cannot allow ANYONE to come to our Union without abiding by our rules...

Dangerous crossings must STOP immediately. Smuggling networks must be dismantled. And returns must be swift, as our rules allow."

She says she's tasked two commissioners with supporting control of the situation, and that they are already "working closely with Spain."

She also noted the Union has a close partnership with Morocco, that she hopes will help to deliver results.
