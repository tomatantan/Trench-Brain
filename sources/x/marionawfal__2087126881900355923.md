🇦🇺🇯🇵 Australian PM Anthony Albanese made 'a boob joke' about a pair of melons he received from Japanese PM Sanae Takaichi, and is now under fire.

A former diplomat went so far as to call it a blunder "of nuclear proportions."

Here's what was said:

Albanese: "The Japanese Prime Minister brought a melon."

Interviewer: "Did she smuggle it?"

Albanese: "I don't know. Got a couple of melons, she brought two, as you do."

Interviewer: "She just came in looking like Pamela Anderson!"

Albanese: "And they're beautiful."

For Japan, Crown Melons are luxury symbols of profound respect, perfection, and gratitude.

But while the joke might have been a bit offensive, does it really rise to the level of "nuclear blunder"?
