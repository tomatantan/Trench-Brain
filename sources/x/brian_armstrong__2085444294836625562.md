I often get asked about Coinbase's area of focus, since we build products for multiple customer segments. The answer is that we build better financial services powered by crypto technology.

Crypto is updating every aspect of the financial system from trading to payments to tokenization to agentic finance (AiFi). We have a common platform we've built (custody, licenses, exchange liquidity, stablecoin integrations, fiat integrations, etc) that power all our products and give us economies of scale, and some network effects.

By improving financial services for retail, advanced traders, businesses, institutions, and developers, we can leverage these common foundations, and make progress toward our mission of increasing economic freedom.
