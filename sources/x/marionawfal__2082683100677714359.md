🇺🇸 America is now paying $1.3 trillion a year just in interest on its debt.

Lawrence Lepard says the endgame is yield curve control, which is a polite way of describing a government printing money to buy what nobody else will:

"When a government can no longer sell its debt or pay the interest on its debt, it becomes a buyer of its own debt."

The last time America went down this road was after World War Two, and bondholders got crushed while inflation raged for a decade.
