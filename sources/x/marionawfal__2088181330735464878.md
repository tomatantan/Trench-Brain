🇮🇷 Iran is reportedly ready to shift the war from defense to offense if its demands aren't met.

Mohammad Mokhber, a senior adviser to Khamenei, said the leader's strategy is for the war to turn offensive if Iran's conditions go unfulfilled.

There’s already speculation that the order could extend to U.S. warships that have so far been left alone, though that goes beyond what Iranian officials have said publicly.

The warning lands as Trump's approach has been to wait and let economic pressure do the work. 

Tehran seems to be betting that patience runs out.

Mokhber's real pitch is a Hormuz security setup that runs without the U.S. 

Any American failure to protect its Gulf allies, he argues, just proves Washington can't deliver.
