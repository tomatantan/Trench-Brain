🇮🇱🇵🇸 An Israeli strike leveled critical medicine warehouses in the heart of Gaza.

Two of the four central storage sites at Al-Aqsa Martyrs Hospital in Deir al-Balah are gone. 

The supplies inside were keeping dialysis patients alive.

Hospital director Raed Hussein says dialysis services could shut down completely if replacements don’t arrive. 

He called it a disaster.

Gaza’s Health Ministry now reports more than half of all medicines and over half of medical supplies are simply unavailable.

Patients who need these treatments to survive are watching the clock run out.
