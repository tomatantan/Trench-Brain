🇺🇸🇪🇺 Washington sold Europe's currency to rescue Japan's, and told the ECB after it was done

To prop up a collapsing yen last week, the U.S. sold euros instead of dollars, and the European Central Bank only found out once the trade was already done.

That's what stings in Frankfurt. Selling a European asset to defend an Asian currency, with no heads-up, hit some senior ECB officials as a real breach of how western central banks cooperate.

Why euros? Dumping dollars would've cut against Bessent's strong-dollar line. Euros were the clean workaround, clean for Washington anyway.
