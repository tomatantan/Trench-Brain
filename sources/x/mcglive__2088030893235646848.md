@DLMMonSOL  skipped the Uniswap arms race and built its own AMM instead...

The founder walked through why:

Most Robinhood launchpads compete for share while sitting on top of Uniswap infrastructure. @DLMMonSOL took a different path, incubating Block, a native AMM, DEX, liquidity book, and launchpad built specifically for Robinhood, framed around Raydium's compounding liquidity model with @MeteoraAG -style configurations layered in.

The core mechanic: 10% of all fees automatically compound back into locked, burned liquidity, permanently, constantly growing the pool and generating more fees for developers, paired with a solid creator split for anyone launching.

Launched from stealth roughly three weeks ago, with poolstrade releasing a similar compounding AMM about a week and a half later. The tradeoff is that building independently means no automatic indexing on trading terminals like Axiom or GMGN, keeping current volume lower by design.
