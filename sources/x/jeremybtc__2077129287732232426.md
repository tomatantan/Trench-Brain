On June 1, Larry Ellison was the second richest person on earth, worth over $300 BILLION.

Today he's eighth. His fortune has dropped $125 BILLION in about five weeks.

The reason is Oracle, and Oracle bet everything on AI.

Ellison owns roughly 40% of the company, so his wealth moves with the stock which has crashed about ~49% since June 1.

Oracle is spending up to $70 BILLION building AI infrastructure.

Wall Street cheered it at first, pointing to a massive backlog of future orders as proof.

Now analysts are whether it was all real demand, or just contracts on paper.

The moment the market stopped believing the story, $125 BILLION evaporated.

Oracle was supposed to be the "safe" AI play.

If this is what happens to the "safe" one, think about the ones running on pure hype.
