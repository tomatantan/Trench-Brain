🇨🇦 A tiny mining company just signed up to spend $8.5 million digging holes next to one of Canada's biggest copper mines.

Here's the deal NovaRed Mining made. It doesn't own the land. It rented the right to buy it.

To actually own 70% of it, they have to pay the landowner $250,000 in cash, hand over 3 million shares of the company, and spend $8.5 million on drilling and exploration work over the next few years.

Miss those payments and they walk away with nothing.

Why bother: the land sits 10 km from Hudbay's Copper Mountain Mine, which is already producing copper and making money.

Nobody has found anything yet. It's a bet that good rock has good neighbours, placed at a moment when the world is desperate for copper.
