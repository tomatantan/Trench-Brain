Rob asked @SecuritizeBilly why @Securitize went public through a SPAC:

"The past few years have been mega-IPO territory. Companies stay private as long as humanly possible. Wealth creation happens on the venture and private side." 

"When they finally debut, valuations are massive, retail upside is limited. Even big flashy crypto IPOs have barely gotten back to their initial offering price."

"Securitize is eight years old, regulated, already operating like a public company. Going public is the logical next step, control our own destiny."

"$400M raise, tokenized our own equity. That's the playbook for other issuers: take a company public, tokenize the equity, move the tokenization movement forward."
