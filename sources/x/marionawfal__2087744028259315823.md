🇺🇸 Hedy Lamarr wasn’t just one of Hollywood’s biggest stars, between takes she was in her trailer working on a secret invention with composer George Antheil.

The invention? A frequency-hopping system that could keep enemy forces from jamming Allied torpedo signals during World War II.

They got a patent, but the Navy sat on it. Decades later those exact principles became the foundation for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and the wireless world we live in now.

She conquered the silver screen and quietly helped invent the future.
