🇮🇷🇾🇪 An Iranian Mahan Air flight touched down in Sanaa this week, the first direct civilian flight from Iran into Houthi-held Yemen in around 11 years.

It brought back more than 200 wounded and stranded Yemenis who'd been stuck abroad, then flew a high-level Houthi delegation out to Tehran for Khamenei's funeral.

By Friday evening, crowds packed Al-Sabeen Square in Sanaa to celebrate what the Houthis are calling the moment they broke the years-long air blockade. 

Their military says Saudi jets tried to stop the flight and were driven off, though that account comes from the Houthi side and isn't independently confirmed. 

Yemen's internationally recognized government condemned the whole thing as a violation of sovereignty and UN resolutions.

One plane, and both sides read it as a win. Tehran gets its allies at the funeral, the Houthis get their blockade-breaking headline and a rally to match.
