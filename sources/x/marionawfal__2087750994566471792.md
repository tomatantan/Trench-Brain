🇸🇦🇴🇲 A road through the world's largest sand desert is now the Gulf's answer to a shut strait

Saudi Arabia's 564 km stretch across the Empty Quarter runs from the Batha Haradh intersection to the Omani border crossing, and it cost roughly $533 MILLION to build.

Crews logged over 3.3 million work hours, ran 750 machines chosen specifically for the conditions, and hauled out 150 million cubic meters of sand while the dunes kept moving under them.

It opened in late 2021 with trade and pilgrimage in mind, cutting an 18 hour trip through the UAE down to about six.

Then the war turned it into strategic infrastructure.

Dubai Customs and Oman opened a bonded corridor through Omani ports in March, and declarations exploded from about 12,000 that month to nearly 100,000 in April.

Sharjah signed its own version in May.

Scott Bessent said the strait would become just another body of water within two years.

Every kilometer of that asphalt is a bet he's right.
