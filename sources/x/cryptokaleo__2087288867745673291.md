I've talked about this so many times.

People used to make fun of you if you shared a chart of anything other than Bitcoin that was denominated in USD.

"lmfao you're an idiot BTC is the only thing that matters, nobody cares about how many dollars something is"

That was the case until FTX came around.

FTX was the first exchange to standardize pairing alts with USD at a large scale.

From there, it really didn't take long until people started laughing at you for sharing the BTC pair, and any exchange that DIDN'T adjust lost customers and volume as a result.

Point being - a lot of people are resistant to changing narratives.

And because of that, they sideline themselves.

Don't be that person next bull market.
