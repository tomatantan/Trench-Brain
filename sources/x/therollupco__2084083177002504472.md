Binance US CEO Steve Gregory says memes drained the liquidity that should have pushed the majors to new highs last cycle.

"Last bull market, the meme-coin craze ate up a lot of the liquidity for everybody else."

"That's why we didn't get the higher highs we thought we'd get on the mega caps."

"Now ETF flows have dampened the volatility on Bitcoin and Ether, they're harder to move."

"So people are seeking volatility outside that, in memes and lower-cap tokens. It's kind of back to the crypto original days."
