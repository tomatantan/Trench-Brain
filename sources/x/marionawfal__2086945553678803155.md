🚨🇱🇾 A bomb planted in his own car killed the intelligence chief of Libya's most powerful warlord as he sat outside his home in Benghazi

-Fawzi al-Mansouri was killed Monday evening when an explosive device in his vehicle detonated in the Hawari district of Benghazi, two security sources told Reuters

-He had run military intelligence for the Libyan National Army since MAY 2024, which put him at the center of everything Khalifa Haftar's forces knew about their enemies

-The LNA's General Command called the bombing a terrorist attack and vowed to bring whoever ordered it to justice

-No group has claimed responsibility and the sources offered nothing on who planted the device

-Libya has been carved in two since 2014, with a UN recognized government in Tripoli on one side and Haftar's eastern administration on the other, and the LNA holding most of the east and south

The country has never been whole since the 2011 uprising that ended Muammar Gaddafi's 42 year rule, and the 2020 ceasefire that stopped the big battles left the militias armed and in place.
