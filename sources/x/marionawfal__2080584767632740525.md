🚨🇮🇷🇺🇸 Iranian officials are reporting a U.S. air attack on the city of Piranshahr, in the West Azerbaijan province.

The region's crisis management organization says a site in the city, near the border with Iraq, was hit by a U.S. air attack this morning.

Several vehicles were reportedly damaged in the attack, but no info yet on any casualties.

During the 12-Day-War last year, Israel targeted Piranshahr because it hosts military infrastructure, including an IRGC base and radar installations.
