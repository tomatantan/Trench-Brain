One of the most interesting teams I worked with last batch was @GreaseAI.

They are using AI to modernize how used kitchen grease is collected and converted into biodiesel. When they first pitched the idea, I thought it sounded insane. But the more they explained it, the more it made sense. After doing some research, I realized this could be a big market.

Restaurants produce a ton of used grease, which is eventually sold to refineries and converted into biodiesel. The problem is that collections are unreliable. Grease piles up, becomes a nuisance for kitchens, and the operators collecting it still manage their fleets through outdated systems. And payouts happen irregularly or not transparently enough.

GreaseAI gives each restaurant a smart bin that automatically signals when it is nearly full. Their AI dispatches trucks, optimizes collection routes, picks up the grease, sells it to refineries, and pays the restaurant on time!

They started four weeks ago and have already onboarded 60 kitchens, are growing 100% week over week, and have reached a $300K ARR run rate.

A lot of old service businesses are going to be disrupted by a few hungry founders with AI.
