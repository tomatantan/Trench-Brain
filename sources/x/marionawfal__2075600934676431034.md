🇬🇷🇩🇪 New plane fear unlocked 😱 A Ryanair passenger was partially sucked out of a Boeing 737 at 16,000 feet after his cabin window shattered mid-climb.

His wife held onto him until the plane landed.

Flight FR1879 left Thessaloniki for Memmingen early Friday when the window blew out, triggering a rapid decompression and dropping the oxygen masks. 

Suspected engine debris is being blamed, though the cause isn't confirmed.

The 61-year-old Serbian man was pulled halfway into the opening before others grabbed him. He landed back in Thessaloniki with friction burns.

Ryanair's version was calmer, describing a window that simply "dislodged."
