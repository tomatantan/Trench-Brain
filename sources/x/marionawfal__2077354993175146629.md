🚨🇺🇸🇮🇷 The cost of the Iran war is MUCH higher for the U.S. than you may think... as much as $100 billion.

The Pentagon has maintained an estimated cost of roughly $30 billion for military operations.

But U.S. officials and internal assessments indicate the actual financial impact is FAR greater.

That's because the initial public estimates provided by Pentagon officials primarily accounted for MUNITIONS used (such as Tomahawks) and day-to-day operations.

When you factor in EXTENSIVE U.S. base damage and destroyed aircraft (more than 40+ damaged or destroyed), that number balloons RAPIDLY.

Iranian strikes heavily damaged crucial U.S. installations, particularly the Navy's Fifth Fleet base in Manama, Bahrain, and U.S. bases in Kuwait. 

Repairing these alone is estimated to cost at least $5 billion... so that $30 billion total doesn't quite add up.
