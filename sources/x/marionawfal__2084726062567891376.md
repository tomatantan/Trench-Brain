🇨🇳 China's nuclear bomber goes public 

China just showed off the missile it usually keeps hidden.

New PLA documentary footage shows an H-6N bomber flying with a large ballistic missile slung under its belly (not the usual wing-mounted type) escorted tight by two J-20 stealth fighters.

It's almost certainly the JL-1: nuclear-capable, air-launched, roughly 8,000km range, hypersonic reentry, flexible enough for anti-ship or land targets too.

This missile is the last piece of China's nuclear triad: land, sea, and now a mobile, hard-to-track airborne leg that no one can predict the launch position of.

China debuted the JL-1 publicly for the first time last year at its Victory Day parade. This is the first time they've shown it actually flying.

A "documentary" airing this on state TV isn't showing off. It's a message, and it's addressed to someone specific.
