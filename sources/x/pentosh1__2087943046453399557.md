Everyone has a different trading style, and not everyones trading style works well with person to person. 

I typically have two trades I take and I stick to them. Those trades are also heavily context dependent. For example, not really having traded crypto for the past year. Just like staying out in prev bear markets is bc of the context/macro trends. 

One is a defensive trade, other is reclaim at what I call historical supports. But the reclaim itself isnt as important to me as is the history of the spot. The defensive trade is at a htf spot with a sharp sell off that isn't looking for a trend reversal but a lower high. While the reclaim is typically an end to end trade. 

Core postions are a bit different in the fact that something i have conviction in. The core doesnt get touched, but I will trade around the core on a different brokerage in order to. Llower the effective cost average, or accumulate more shares. The core itself is a longer term belief where as 90% of what I trade means nothing to me at all. So there are both low time frame, and high time frames trades and beliefs. 

I will post some examples of the actual style with context. But again there are some things you just cant really describe. Like gut. There's a lot of thoughts and feelings that go into buying and selling that you can't put on paper.
