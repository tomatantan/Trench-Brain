🇪🇬🇺🇸🇮🇷 Fmr Egyptian senator handed the U.S. a win on Hormuz.

Dr. Abdel Monem Said told Al Arabiya that the U.S. already has the stronger position: Iranian ports are effectively under siege, while Gulf exporters from Kuwait to Fujairah keep the oil flowing.

Iran can claim control of the Strait, but global energy flows do not wait for Tehran’s permission.

The waterway belongs to neither side. International law treats it as a transit passage, and Gulf producers have more to lose from a shutdown than Iran does.

Said’s regional maritime council would take Hormuz out of the bilateral shouting match and put the countries that depend on it in the room.

The real question is simple, do the tankers keep moving?
