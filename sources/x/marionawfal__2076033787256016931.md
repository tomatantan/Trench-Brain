🇺🇸 Meta discovers people don't love being turned into AI art

So Meta had this bright idea: let anyone generate AI images off your public Instagram just by tagging you. What could possibly go wrong? Everything, as it turns out, and days later they quietly walked it back.

The official word is the feature "missed the mark." Cute. What actually happened is the backlash landed hard and the actors' union told its members to run for the opt-out button.

And the timing was almost too perfect. One researcher watched scammers stick his AI-generated face on fake dementia cures aimed at the elderly, ads he says Meta left up even after he flagged them. Nothing builds trust like that.

The tag-anyone tool is gone now, though the rest of Meta's shiny AI photo gadgets are still very much switched on. Baby steps, apparently.
