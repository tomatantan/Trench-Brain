🇺🇸 One of the fastest men on El Capitan is gone at 30.

Jake Whisenant, the Mammoth Lakes climber who tore up Yosemite's biggest wall, died August 3 in an accident in the Sierra Nevada. 

Officials haven't said yet exactly what happened.

Back in October 2024 he raced up El Cap's Lurking Fear route in 2 hours, 55 minutes and 32 seconds, smashing a record that had stood for 21 years.

What makes it wilder is he did it as a full-time 9-to-5 guy, no sponsors, squeezing world-class climbing around his job.

He kept pushing after that. In May he climbed the Nose twice in a single day, a 3,000-foot vertical wall that takes most climbers days to finish once.

A lifelong friend said Jake died doing what he loved in the mountains he called home.

Rest easy, Jake.
