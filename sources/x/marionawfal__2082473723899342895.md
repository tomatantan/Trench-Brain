🇺🇸 A $5,000 drone is giving Marines a safer way to check suspicious ships without risking an entire helicopter crew.

Instead of flying close enough for a visual inspection, teams can hang back several miles and launch a disposable FPV drone to get a clear look at the vessel.

If the drone is shot down or simply ditched afterward, the loss is minor compared with putting people and aircraft in range of potential fire.

The system doesn't replace high-end surveillance platforms.

It simply adds a low-cost, expendable set of eyes that boarding teams can carry and use on short notice, cutting human risk while still delivering the intel needed.
