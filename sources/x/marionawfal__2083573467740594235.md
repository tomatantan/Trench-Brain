🇻🇪🇺🇸 Venezuela's acting president just said what she really thinks of her old boss.

Delcy Rodríguez says Maduro's biggest mistake was isolating Venezuela internationally. Too closed off, too little diversification, especially with the U.S.

She's spent months undoing that: new oil deals, Halliburton back in the country, commercial flights running, Trump calling personally to greenlight them.

On Maduro himself, sitting in a U.S. federal prison since January: barely any contact. A few messages, only through his lawyers.

His read on it all, relayed secondhand: he thinks what's happening now is "very positive."
