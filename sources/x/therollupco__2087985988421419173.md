"The L1 thesis is dead, and the data supports it." 

Jeff Dorman's chart, unpacked by @robbieklages, plus the caveat that makes it a real debate:

"Blockchains captured 90%+ of monthly crypto revenue from 2021 to 2023. By mid-2026, that's fallen to ~25%. Value is shifting from base chains to apps."

"This is fat protocol vs. fat application. The old view: value accrues to the L1 through gas. The new one: value flows up to whoever owns the customer relationship. Hyperliquid, Pumpfun and the like are proving the app thesis true."

The honest caveat: "Most of that app revenue comes from basically two apps, Hyperliquid and Pumpfun."

"It mirrors the AI-compute bet: we thought demand would outpace supply forever, so the infra would accrue value. Then supply caught up. Now L1s have a value-accrual problem to sort out."
