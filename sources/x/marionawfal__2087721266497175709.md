🇮🇷 Iran’s cyber strategy is evolving from disruption to battlefield integration

In July they exploited SS7: the backbone of global phone networks, to pinpoint the real-time locations of U.S. troops across the Middle East. 

That data didn’t stay digital, it went straight into the targeting process for kinetic strikes that left American service members injured.

Since the conflict intensified, Iran has deliberately recalibrated its cyber operations to serve specific wartime goals: locating forces, guiding drones and missiles, assessing damage, and shaping the information space.

Compromised telecom networks and even security cameras are now feeding the kill chain. Digital access is being converted into physical effects in near real time.

This is the new face of hybrid warfare, an integrated tool of modern conflict, and Iran is treating it that way.
