🇬🇷 Along with Spain and France, Greece is now fighting major wildfires too.

Strong winds are driving flames through forests near Athens, forcing evacuations, some by sea.

In France’s Var region a big blaze that had been partly controlled has flared up again, while Spain continues to battle multiple fronts even after its worst fires of the summer.

This is the latest chapter in an extreme European fire season already running well above average and surpassing last year’s records.

A wetter winter grew extra vegetation that successive heatwaves then turned into perfect tinder.

The same heat has also driven thousands of excess deaths across the UK, France, Germany and Austria.

The fires keep jumping borders. The underlying conditions are the same.
