be prepared to go through periods of red and sideways when your trading approach no longer works like it used to. the ‘best’ traders are the ones with the style and framework best suited to the current meta. metas can last weeks, months, years but they don’t last forever.

me and @kiko_ftw made 7 figures by just sitting in bottomed out shitters that had even the most minuscule chance of a catalyst sending them multiples higher. this was a viable strategy in 2024 and beginning of 2025 but won’t give you the same R/R today (even though kiko who is “retired” still occasionally pulls off these monster cooks turning 1 sol to 400+).

when the market shifted i had to study and learn from a lot of great traders coming up and realign my trading framework. i couldn’t sit on my ego and pretend i was a better trader than them when i wasn’t. it has been a constant learning process to get where i am today and i look forward to learning a lot more to get to where i want to be in the future.
