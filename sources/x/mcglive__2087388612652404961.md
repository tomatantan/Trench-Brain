Delta's building the first Robinhood AMM in house, could this be the Meteora of Robinhood?

The reasoning behind it:

An AMM (automated market maker) is what powers on-chain swaps and liquidity routing. Right now, @deltaliquidity  gives users an advanced experience layered on top of Uniswap, but all the underlying fees and routing still flow through Uniswap's infrastructure, not Delta's.

Building their own AMM changes that entirely. Liquidity, pools, and routing all get hosted and managed directly through Delta, meaning the team controls fee generation instead of relying on someone else's protocol. 

The stated goal: if Delta's TVL for a given range outweighs what's sitting on Uniswap for the same pair, most swap activity  and most fees route through Delta instead, effectively competing directly with Uniswap on its own turf rather than building on top of it.

Full-stack instead of borrowed infrastructure.
