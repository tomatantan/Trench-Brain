"The thing that the largest fintechs are doing today is mostly onchain lending"

Veda CEO Sun Raghupathi explains why vaults won't stay just an onchain lending product

"That's what Robinhood, Coinbase, Kraken, MetaMask, a number of these other companies are building today with vaults, getting exposure to protocols like Morpho, Aave, on the Solana side Kamino, Jupiter"

"But increasingly, as more assets move onchain through things like tokenized funds, tokenized equities, that increases the universe of products that can be built"

"Vaults are really this very flexible primitive that today have found significant PMF in the onchain lending world, but my view is long term that this will evolve"
