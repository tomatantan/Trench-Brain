I turned Claude into my Chief of Investments.

One of my favorite things about this system is the personal context layer.

Most people prompt Claude for stock advice and get generic answers because Claude knows nothing about them.

This fixes that entirely - here's how:

I built a folder with 5 files that give Claude full context on me as an investor:

→ Investor-profile.md: my experience, goals, time horizon, risk tolerance, portfolio size
→ Strategy.md: my max drawdown tolerance, rebalancing rules, position sizing, entry/exit criteria
→ Portfolio.csv: my current positions and weightings
→ Watchlist.md: sectors, tickers, and potential setups I'm tracking
→ Prompts.md: my saved prompts so I'm not rewriting the same thing every session

Once this folder is attached inside a Cowork project, connected to live market data through the FMP connector, every single response Claude gives is filtered through my actual thesis and goals, not generic advice.

If I include my ideal trading setups in Strategy.md, Claude will scan live price data and tell me if a trade is actually forming based on my own criteria, and exactly how I'd size into it.

Setup takes under 5 minutes once you have the files ready.
