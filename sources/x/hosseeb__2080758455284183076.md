"We should ban Chinese models" is so stupid I don't even know where to begin.

It's nothing like banning Chinese EVs, or even a commodity like oil.

It's like saying "we should ban Chinese-discovered molecules" or "we should ban Chinese battery architectures."

It's a raw input into innovation. It makes humanity collectively richer and more capable. You can use these models HOWEVER YOU WANT TO. If you don't like that they don't answer questions about Tiananmen Square, guess what, you can fine-tune them! You have the weights!

There is a sensible debate to be had if you want to ban Chinese inference providers (whether for protectionism or to prevent data ending in the hands of the Chinese)--look, reasonable people can disagree, but at least there's a strategic angle to that.

Banning the MODELS? 

A self-own of the highest order.
