🇺🇸🇮🇷 America is running out of the weapons it needs to fight Iran, and that could make Trump take even more dangerous gambles.

Geopolitical analyst Brandon Weichert says Trump has invested too much politically to walk away without extracting major concessions from Iran.

Iran, meanwhile, believes time is on its side.

It sees rising energy prices, pressure on U.S weapons stockpiles and growing regional instability as leverage.

Neither side wants to blink first, and that's where the real danger begins.

Brandon argues that if America can no longer sustain a prolonged long-range missile campaign, the Pentagon's answer won't necessarily be restraint; it may be escalation.

Instead of striking safely from a distance, U.S aircraft could be forced to operate closer to Iranian territory in an effort to destroy missile launchers, underground facilities and command networks, but there's a problem.

Nobody can say with confidence they know where all of Iran's launch sites are, and no one can guarantee they can eliminate them.

Every mile American aircraft move closer increases the danger to U.S pilots.

That's what makes China's reported shipment of hundreds of portable air-defence systems to Iran so significant.

Those weapons matter far less when American aircraft are launching from long range, but they matter a whole lot more if U.S jets are forced to fly lower and closer to their targets.

Brandon's warning is that Washington may soon find itself trapped between two options:

Accept a strategic setback...

Or escalate into a much larger war using fewer long-range weapons, more exposed aircraft and no clear path to eliminating Iran's ability to fight back.

That's why he believes the most dangerous phase of this conflict may still lie ahead.
