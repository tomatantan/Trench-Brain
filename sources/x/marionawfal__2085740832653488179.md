🇮🇱🇨🇴 Israeli Minister Ben-Gvir just congratulated Colombia’s new president and offered a very specific invitation.

In a public message to Abelardo De La Espriella, he praised the incoming leader as a “friend of Israel” and highlighted his campaign promise to build a “megaprison” for terrorists.

He invited De La Espriella to visit Israeli prisons to see the changes he has implemented and learn "how to take away from [terrorists] the desire to commit terrorism.”

Colombia is currently building a mega-prison in Medellín designed for more than 1,300 inmates, in part inspired by El Salvador's CECOT. It's set to open next year.
