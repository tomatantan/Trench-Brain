🇭🇺 A heatwave is about to shut down Hungary's only nuclear plant.

The Paks plant covers a third of the country's power and needs Danube water to stay cool. Problem is, the river just hit record lows after months of drought and brutal heat since May.

PM Peter Magyar says engineers already throttled it down to a quarter of normal overnight, and the whole thing could go dark this weekend, the first time ever.

And the timing is rough. Highs above 40C are rolling in this week, so everyone cranks the AC right as the country's biggest power source taps out.

Magyar's scrambling to keep the lights on: fines for big industrial users who hog power, old backup plants getting fired back up, freight trains parked during peak hours from Monday.

A record heatwave knocking out a third of a country's power is how a hot week turns into full-blown blackouts.
