Traditional NYSE listings serve one investor profile. 

Billy Miller explains why tokenized equity opens the same asset to a second profile:

"The tokenization industry is still young. We're at $30-40 billion on any given day. Crypto as a whole is $2.5-3 trillion. The entire investable market is $300-400 trillion. So we have a long way to go." 

"We're at the one percent of the one percent. By doing this now, we're really trying to enable choice for investors to engage with the asset how they want to."

"If you are the type of investor that we expect a lot of this next generation to adopt, where you do want to hold your own asset, use it however you want, with less intermediation, use it in DeFi, trade it when you want to, that's the huge unlock." 

"These are not wrappers or derivatives or synthetic assets. These are the actual shares, issued and traded in the United States in a crypto-native way."
