🇧🇩 A SINGLE cable failure at one of Bangladesh’s floating LNG terminals sent spot prices soaring 40% overnight... 

An accident at the Moheshkhali facility slashed regasification capacity by 500-600 million cubic ft per day, dropping gas supply from 2,640 to 2,114 MMcfd and wiping out around 2,000 MW of power generation. 

Two contracted cargoes priced near $16 per MMBtu could not be unloaded and were deferred, with one now facing a penalty. 

Authorities have responded by approving an emergency shipment from Vitol at $22.35 per MMBtu. 

LNG tightness often comes from constrained infrastructure rather than a shortage of the gas itself.
