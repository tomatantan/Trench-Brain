.@ProductEMP explained why @EmpulserTech transitioned from Soar's debt-backed model to an ACE Round:

- The goal is to give supporters real equity (not just exposure to a future liquidity event)
- Under the previous debt model, the team controlled when liquidity could occur
- ACE Rounds shift that dynamic by converting participation into equity ownership
- Investors gain more control over when they choose to exit
- The move aligns with the broader mission of democratizing access to early-stage startup investing

The team believes the new structure creates stronger long-term alignment between the company and its community.
