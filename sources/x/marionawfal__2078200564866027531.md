🇺🇸 A newly declassified file just exposed which world leaders wanted Trump gone in 2020.

An August 2020 U.S. intelligence assessment breaks down how different foreign governments felt about Trump winning a second term:

🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia: Crown Prince MBS was nervous about Trump losing. He saw him as the "most Saudi-friendly" president they'd ever had.

🇮🇷 Iran: wanted Trump gone, mainly because of his sanctions campaign and his talk of changing the regime in Tehran.

🇹🇷 Turkey: Erdogan tried to quietly sway U.S. candidates, convinced big parts of the U.S. government are anti-Turkey.

🇩🇪 Germany: Merkel "almost certainly" hoped Trump wouldn't win again.

The interesting part? It wasn't just enemies like Iran wanting him out. Even one of the U.S.'s closest allies was quietly hoping he'd lose, while the Saudis were basically the only ones rooting for him to stay.
