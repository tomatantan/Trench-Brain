🇺🇸🇮🇷 Iran is already pushing back on Trump and Vance’s version of the frozen-assets deal.

Iran’s Central Bank Governor Abdolnasser Hemmati says the signed documents do not force Tehran to buy American agricultural products with the released money.

He claims if U.S. food is cheaper and better quality, Iran can buy it. But Iran says it can also use the second tranche of frozen assets to purchase any goods that are not under sanctions.

That matters because Trump and Vance have framed the deal as money that would flow back to U.S. farmers through corn, soy, and wheat purchases.

Iran is now making the opposite point: the funds are Iranian, and Tehran says it will spend them based on price, quality, and sanctions rules.
