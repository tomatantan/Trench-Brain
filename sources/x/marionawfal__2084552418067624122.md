🇸🇦 Saudi Aramco just posted a 44% jump in second-quarter profit despite the chaos in the Strait of Hormuz and Bab al-Mandeb.

Net profit hit $32.69 billion between April and June, driven mainly by higher prices for crude, refined products, and chemicals.

Here’s the part that stings: the disruptions tighten supply and jack up prices, so you pay more at the pump. 

In theory, lower volumes should squeeze the producers too, but when the price spike is big enough (and you’re a low-cost giant with pipeline workarounds), the extra dollars per barrel more than make up for shipping less, so profits rise anyway.

Turns out weapons makers aren’t the only ones who do well when the region catches fire.
