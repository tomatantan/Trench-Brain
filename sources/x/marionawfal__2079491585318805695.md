There's something very funny about Optimus explaining first principles.

A humanoid robot calmly describing the mindset that helped create rockets, EVs, factories, and eventually itself.

First principles thinking is basically Elon refusing to accept the “because that’s how it’s always done” answer.

At Tesla and SpaceX, the question is not only how to make the old thing better. It is whether the old thing should exist in that form at all.

Why throw away a rocket booster if the physics says it can come back?

Why build an EV like a compliance car if batteries, software, motors, and factories can be redesigned around performance and scale?

They strip the problem down to physics, materials, cost, and function, then rebuild upward from what is actually true.

Very useful for building things that did not exist before.
