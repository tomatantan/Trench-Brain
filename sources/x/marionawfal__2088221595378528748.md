🇦🇩🇻🇦 Andorra is moving to decriminalize abortion...

PM Espot and his team met Cardinal Pietro Parolin in Rome to continue talks that have run for years.

The change would remove criminal penalties for women who end a pregnancy, while still banning abortions from being performed inside Andorra itself. 

The government plans to bring a Penal Code reform to parliament next month.

Because one of Andorra’s two heads of state is the Bishop of Urgell, any reform requires careful handling with the Holy See. So far Rome has kept a low profile and has not moved to stop the process.

Would the Vatican actually be willing to sign off on a law decriminalizing abortion?
