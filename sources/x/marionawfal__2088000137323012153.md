🇮🇱🇮🇷 Iran is rebuilding its missile capabilities much faster than Israeli officials ever expected.

After the war earlier this year, Israel thought it had hit Iran’s military industry hard enough to set it back for years.

Four months later, Iran is already rebuilding faster than expected, especially its ballistic missile production.
