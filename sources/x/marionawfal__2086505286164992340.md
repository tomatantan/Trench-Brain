🇨🇳🇩🇪 Germany’s trade deficit with China just blew out to €55 billion in the first half of 2026.

Exports to China crashed over 12% to under €37 billion, now only Germany’s 9th-biggest market (it was #2 in 2021). Meanwhile, Chinese imports into Germany jumped 8.9% to €91.8 billion. 

Beijing is relying less on European industry, focusing on its own supply chains, and catching up technologically. The overall quality of Chinese goods is also improving. China remains Germany’s top overall trading partner… but the imbalance is growing fast.
