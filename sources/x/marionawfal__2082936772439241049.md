🇺🇸🇹🇷 One Russian missile system is all that's stopping Trump from arming Turkey

At the NATO summit in Ankara this month, Trump said he'd lift sanctions on Turkey and sell them the F-35, calling Erdogan more loyal than most allies. 

The catch is U.S. law: Turkey got booted from the program in 2019 for buying Russia's S-400, and a State Department letter just confirmed it still hasn't ditched the system, which it has to before any sale is legal.

The fix being floated is Turkey handing the S-400 to a country like the UAE, clearing the bar without giving it back to Russia.

Then there's Israel, leaning on Trump to kill the deal over fears it dents their military edge. He waved it off: "Nobody tells me what we should be selling."

Bought a Russian answer to a problem and created a much bigger American one.
