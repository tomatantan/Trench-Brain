I think the next generation of L1s should actively build first party apps instead of copying the playbook of earlier chains.

No one understands a chain’s technical advantages better than the team that built it. First party apps can showcase what is technically possible while proving the team is willing to put its money where its mouth is by building real products with real users. 

That traction becomes the wedge. Once other builders see real users and demand, it attracts more founders to build on the chain.

At this point, any chain copying the old founder acquisition playbook is fighting over the leftovers.

So what I am saying is L1s should pivot from trying to build a developer ecosystem to building real products that attract users. Almost nothing is under your control when you rely entirely on third party founders. Once they realize your chain has no users  they will do what is best for their company and move to one that does.

Your job as an L1 founder is to figure out how to get users first then founders will follow.
