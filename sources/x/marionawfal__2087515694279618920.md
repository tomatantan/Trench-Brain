🚨🇹🇷🇸🇦🇵🇰 Erdogan, responding to written questions regarding the Mecca Agreement, says it "does not target any country." 

Other key takeaways:

He says "the matter should not be viewed solely through a military lens; the primary goal of this agreement is to enhance its deterrent dimension, fortify the security incentive of the parties by strengthening the spirit of solidarity among them, and protect regional stability."

"Our vision is not limited to 3 countries; rather, we aspire for it to grow, and perhaps appropriate conditions will emerge one day for all countries of the region to gather under its umbrella, which would be the optimal step toward achieving lasting stability throughout the region."

The Turkish leader stressed that his country's relations with Saudi are a "strategic relationship," and said the coming phase would "elevate relations between the two countries to much higher levels."

"Our hope and expectation is that the current tension will end as soon as possible, and that Hormuz will return to performing its role in serving international trade in a safe, stable, and uninterrupted manner."

He also made clear that "Israeli attacks on Lebanon must stop immediately" and that Turkey "will not allow the presence of any organizations or entities in northern Syria, or any other part of it, that would threaten the security of Turkey."
