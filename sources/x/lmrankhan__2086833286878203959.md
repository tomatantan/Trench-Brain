What I think Solana's leadership has done really well vs. all of the other chains is support all founders and ideas, no matter how cringe the experiment may be.

It enables founders to be weird and more expressive, similar to what Ethereum felt like in the early days. This leads to more experiments onchain and brings in net new ideas, because eventually one of those weird experiments can turn into a giant onchain game or an entirely new category.

At the end of the day, you don't want to support some experiments and not others. It just alienates people. You want the maximum number of experiments happening because that creates a hotbed for new onchain ideas -> liquidity -> users
