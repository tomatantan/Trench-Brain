Something I (and many others) have been saying. It’s not about “pre and post quantum”, it’s about exiting the golden era of ECC and moving into the new crypto agile era thanks to AI + Q. 

Rapid progress in math means rapid progress destroying cryptographic puzzles and one way functions. Quantum is accelerated by AI. No surprise that Q day and AGI will probably happen in the same year. 

Blockchains will need to adapt to this new world and become agile; that is, not depending on the security of any given function, but rather able to shuffle between them.
