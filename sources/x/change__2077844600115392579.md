New York City's Council Health Committee held a public hearing this week on legislation to end the horse carriage industry in Central Park, following the death of 18-year-old tourist Romanch Mahajan, who was killed last month when a carriage horse bolted and threw him from the vehicle, according to police and local news reports.

Now, thousands have signed a petition backing the bill, known as Romanch's Law, calling on the City Council to pass it. The legislation would stop new carriage licenses immediately and phase out the industry entirely by 2028, while funding a transition program for the roughly 150 drivers currently working in the trade.
