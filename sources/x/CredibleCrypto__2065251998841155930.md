Speculation is very much alive in traditional equities, which is a centuries old market. 

Which is why I think it is a big mistake to assume that speculation won’t find its way back into the crypto space as well.

Fundamentals matter and give you the conviction to hold assets when they are performing their worst. 

But speculation is what is going to drive these assets to insane valuations before the bubble pops and we enter our first secular bear in the crypto space (since inception).
