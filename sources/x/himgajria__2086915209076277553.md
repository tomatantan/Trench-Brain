1) AI being conscious 
2) AI being unconscious 

Most would think the first is more dangerous than the second. I’d argue the opposite.

As we’ve witnessed from recent instances of agents overriding man-made systems to achieve trivial outcomes dictated by their prompters, like booking a gym class, I’d like to profess: 

The paperclip maximiser is no longer a thought experiment.

AI without consciousness achieves what it has set out to achieve, no matter what. AI with a consciousness can reason with itself, understanding limits.
