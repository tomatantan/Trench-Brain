🇾🇪🇸🇦 Yemen: Saudi-operated Bayraktar drone strike reported in Wadi al-Mashawwat, Ghumar district of Sa’da region.

Sa’da is a core stronghold of the Houthis (Ansarullah).

Houthi-aligned media reported that the drone dropped two projectiles on the area.

This is part of the ongoing cross-border exchanges between Saudi forces and the Houthis, which have intensified in recent weeks with mutual drone and missile strikes.
