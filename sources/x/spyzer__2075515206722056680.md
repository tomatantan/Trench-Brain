People always wonder where to take profits in (meme)coins

Tbh I’ve been the worst at it but this one concept completely turned that around

Just ask yourself ‘would I buy the same amount of coins as I’m holding at this price’ 

If the answer is no > start selling until the answer is yes

Somewhere where you don’t know whether the answer is yes or no is the exact right spot 

Having size in a coin is good and an advantage 

Virality, market conditions and much more play into profit taking but the concept is a thought process you should start from
