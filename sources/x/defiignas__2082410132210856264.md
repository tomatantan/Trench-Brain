Coinbase Ventures, Animoca, a16z and Tether are the four most active crypto VCs in 2026.

Animoca is a fun one:

- Did 80 NFT deals in their history, ZERO this year

- About 230 of its ~600 portfolio companies are gaming, and gaming went from 141 rounds in 2024 to FIVE in 2026.

Animoca is doing small checks now while pivoting to stablecoins and AI before a Nasdaq listing (via reverse merger).

------

Coinbase Ventures did 26 deals this year, almost all early stage and almost all pre-token.

They lead almost none.

Seems that Coinbase deals buys them relationship with teams that will build on Base, use Coinbase custody and list on their CEXs.

Maybe not a conviction VC to copy trade.

----

a16z closed a $2.2B crypto fund in May, they invest in institutional infra instead of new L1s: Digital Asset, Talos, Stitch, Arc.

Although a16z did less deals, they deployed more USD than the other three combined.

Median round they join is $25M. Coinbase is $10.2M.

Two most interesting deals they did are:

1. Morpho: $175M in June together with Paradigm and Ribbit.

They bought $MORPHO at average monthly market price. So no VC discount.

2. Arc: Circle sold 740M $ARC for $222M at $0.30

a16z took $75M of it. BlackRock, Apollo, ARK, ICE and Standard Chartered took the rest.

Two pure token plays!

-----

Tether did 13 deals and went alone in 6!

With ~$190B in reserves earning yield, it doesn't need VC returns but it needs USDT distribution.

So they invest in companies and countries that already have some USDT distribution. e.g.,

- @MercadoBitcoin ,  Brazil, $20M
- @bello_app , Argentina, $14M
- @UseLemfi , Africa and Asia remittances
