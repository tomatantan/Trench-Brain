Lindsay Clancy, a Massachusetts mother on trial for killing her three children in 2023, is now several days into testimony in her murder trial at Plymouth Superior Court.
Prosecutors argue Clancy acted "deliberately and meticulously" when she killed her children. Her attorneys, however, argue she was suffering from severe postpartum psychosis at the time. They point to the four months before the killings, when she was prescribed 13 different psychiatric medications by multiple providers and was discharged from a postpartum treatment program after a single day, according to Boston Globe reporting.

The case has sparked a petition calling for Clancy to receive mental health treatment instead of prison.

"I do not support the actions that Lindsay took while in crisis, but also believe that she should be in a mental health facility over incarceration," the petition reads.
