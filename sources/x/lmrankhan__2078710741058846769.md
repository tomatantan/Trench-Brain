I’ve noticed some startups pivot away from markets that feel overly hot because they think the contrarian move is to go somewhere less crowded.

But the real issue is not the market. It is that the team lacks the domain expertise needed to break through.

That quickly turns into negative self talk 

The market is too hot. Every competitor is already doing it. There is no margin left. It is not technically possible.

Then an extremely domain specific team enters the same market and figures out every problem the other teams could not solve.

This is why domain expertise or passion matters so much when choosing a startup idea.
