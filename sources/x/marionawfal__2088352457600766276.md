🇺🇸🇮🇱 Trump won't endorse Netanyahu, and he's not exactly hiding why.

"Bibi is his own worst enemy" 

That's what he's reportedly told people privately about the guy who's supposed to be one of his closest allies.

The backdrop: Netanyahu's coalition is polling at 49-53 seats, down from the 68 he holds now, nowhere near the 61 needed to govern. His rival, former IDF chief Gadi Eizenkot, is polling ahead of him. 

When Trump actually asked Netanyahu how the polls looked, in the Oval Office 2 weeks ago, Netanyahu just paused. 

An aide had to jump in and claim, "Mr. President, he is winning." (He wasn't)

The rift isn't just about polling. Netanyahu publicly rejected Trump's Gaza disarmament plan days after promising Trump he'd give it a real shot. Ad the two have been drifting apart over Iran and Lebanon strategy for months. 

Trump could still come around (there's time before October 27) but right now, one of the most Israel-friendly presidents in decades is watching his closest regional ally struggle and just... not saying anything.
