🇪🇺 Claude is getting a hidden watermark in everything it writes, and you can thank the EU for that.

To comply with the EU’s AI Act, Anthropic has started baking invisible, machine-readable signals into text generated by its newer Claude models.

The watermark lives in the wording itself, meaning it can survive copy-pasting and even some light edits.

Brussels wrote the rule, but Anthropic is applying it worldwide, so users everywhere get the watermark whether their country asked for it or not.

For now it only applies to Claude models launched from August 2 onward, with older models coming later.

It’s also far from bulletproof. Heavy rewriting, paraphrasing or translation can wipe the signal out.

One bloc's transparency law just became the default setting for the entire planet.
