🇺🇸 America's biggest loss may not have been missiles or money. 

Prof. Glenn Diesen thinks it was something much harder to replace: credibility.

Washington built its global alliance system on the perception that it could always protect its partners. Once that aura of overwhelming power begins to crack, countries from the Gulf to East Asia inevitably start asking a different question: Can the U.S. actually deliver?

According to Diesen, that's why this conflict reaches far beyond Iran. He believes Washington has exhausted resources in the Middle East while remaining deeply committed in Europe and still talking about confronting China, leaving allies and adversaries alike to reassess the global balance of power.

"Future historians will look back at this and scratch their heads how they could possibly do something this foolish."
