🚨🇾🇪 The Houthis say they hit a Saudi oil tanker in the Gulf of Aden and forced it to turn back.

-The group claims a ballistic missile scored a direct hit on the tanker Daisy, carried out "successfully" and sending the vessel back the way it came, though the claim cannot be independently verified

-They framed it as enforcing their blockade under a "siege for siege" equation, their stated policy of answering the blockade on Yemen with one of their own

-The warning attached: they are watching Saudi tanker movements and will not let those ships sail the southern Red Sea or the northern approaches until Yemen's siege is lifted

-UKMTO separately reported a loud explosion near a tanker 95 nautical miles southeast of Aden, with all crew safe and no environmental impact, and it is unclear whether that is the same vessel

-Saudi authorities have said nothing
