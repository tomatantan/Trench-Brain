🇺🇸 The U.S. federal budget deficit hit $432 billion in July.

That’s a record for the month and the largest monthly shortfall since the COVID peak in March 2021. 

It jumped 48% from July last year.

The fiscal year-to-date gap is already at $1.8 trillion, bigger than the entire deficit for fiscal 2025 with two months still left. 

Higher spending and weak tariff receipts are driving the numbers higher... oh yeah, and that little war in the Middle East isn't exactly helping
