🇺🇸🇨🇳🇷🇺 The Pentagon is rewriting America’s nuclear strategy, with a sharper focus on limited warfare against China or Russia.

Officials are examining a greater reliance on shorter-range "tactical" nuclear weapons for crises that fall short of an all-out global war.

The classified guidance would shape the options available to a president if conflict erupted in the Indo-Pacific or with Russia over NATO.

Current doctrine already treats China’s expanding arsenal and Russia’s threat to Europe as top priorities.

The new draft appears to go further, preparing specific tactical nuclear responses for more limited wars.
