🇨🇳 China is pushing harder to complete its Iron Triangle in the South China Sea: the Paracel Islands, the Spratlys, and Scarborough Shoal.

It already controls the Paracels and has a major air base there. In the Spratlys, China built seven bases years ago, but other countries still hold their own outposts.

At Second Thomas Shoal, Chinese Coast Guard ships recently clashed with Philippine sailors near the grounded warship Manila uses to keep troops there. 

The confrontation got so physical that both sides were swinging poles and oars.

Scarborough Shoal is the remaining weak point: China patrols it, but it still doesn’t have a base there.

Obama once warned Xi Jinping that building one would cross a red line. Whether that warning still means anything under Trump is unclear.

These skirmishes are becoming a live test of how far the U.S. is willing to go when one of its allies gets pushed.
