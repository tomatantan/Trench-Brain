🇮🇷 Iran’s supreme leader is reportedly swapping out his top security chief for a harder line loyalist.

Mojtaba Khamenei plans to replace Mohammad Bagher Zolghadr as head of the Supreme National Security Council with Mohsen Rezaei, according to a senior cleric and family in law.

Rezaei is a longtime IRGC heavyweight and already serves as Mojtaba’s top military aide.

If true, this is not a technocratic shuffle at all.

Putting Rezaei in the SNSC chair would tighten the grip of the old guard revolutionary commanders at the center of national security decision-making while the country is still at war and under blockade.
