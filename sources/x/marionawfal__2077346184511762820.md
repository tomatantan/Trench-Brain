This may indicate the ships are seeking a safe harbor to wait out the renewed U.S. naval blockade.

Effectively, they are stranded in operational and financial limbo, unable to legally complete sales or return home.

The Rani and the Amil alone are carrying a combined 1 million barrels of Iranian oil.

The broader halt amounts to tens of millions of barrels.
