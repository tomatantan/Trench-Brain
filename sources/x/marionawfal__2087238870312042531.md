🇨🇴 Colombia just got a new finance minister, and his in-box says everything: worst fiscal picture the country's seen in a generation.

The deficit went from 5% of GDP in 2022 to over 6% last year. Take out interest payments and it's the widest primary deficit in 3 decades. Pandemic not included. Public debt just topped 1,200 trillion pesos, a first.

The guy taking over, Miguel Gómez Martínez, is already breaking hard from outgoing president Petro, promising the central bank real independence and a market-friendly turn. Investors have basically been begging to hear that.

Latin America's 4th-biggest economy doesn't need anyone to predict a crisis. It's already sitting on his desk.
