Spent Saturday analyzing my genome using an LLM.

The stack:

* Genome sequencing: @nucleusgenomics (cheek swab kit via mail)
    - Takes about 3 weeks turnaround to get your results. They produce a health report but it's very high-level. You should feed that into your AI as well, but the prize is: Files > Download > VCF & CRAM formats (~25 GB)
* Fable to draft a plan.md in /plan mode (but isn't allowed to read any files)
* For genetic analysis, @AskVenice for private inference via Kimi-K3, acts on Fable's plan. (Anthropic models are great, but not giving them my DNA if I can help it)
* @pidotdev as harness (FOSS, no telemetry, very cache efficient)

Cost: $538 for genome sequencing, ~$2 of Fable planning, $14.53 of inference on Venice via Kimi-K3, including multiple subagents to review the final analysis and asking follow-up questions

Total cost: $554

Main learnings: My DNA is in pretty good shape. Apparently I need to start getting eye exams after 50. Looks like I'm high risk for macular degeneration in my 70s+, but I'm assuming by then I'll be a cyborg, so whatever. Apparently I have great alcohol genes (high tolerance, low addiction), which is wasted on me since I don't drink.

I have like 500 risk factors that would be much worse with smoking, and apparently I have nicotine-addiction genes. So thank god I don't smoke. It underscored that if I ever do, it's over for me.

But despite a history of CVD in my family, my DNA actually looks pretty good. 😤
