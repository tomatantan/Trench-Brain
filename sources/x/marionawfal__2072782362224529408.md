🇺🇸 And if you think the white women's workforce participation number is concerning, this FRED chart of labor force participation for Americans 55 and over should keep economists up at night.

It was holding steady around 40% for years, took the expected COVID hit, partially recovered... and then in early 2026 fell off a cliff, dropping from 38% to just above 37% in what looks like a near-vertical line on the chart.

That's a sudden exit and something specific triggered it. Whether it's early retirement waves, health concerns, layoffs hitting older workers hardest, or something structural happening in the labor market right now.
