🚨🇸🇦🇾🇪 The Saudis are reportedly preparing a MAJOR offensive against the Houthis by sea AND possibly by land in central Yemen...

The goal would be to break the current chokehold on its oil exports through the southern Red Sea and Bab al-Mandab strait.

Yemeni sources indicate Saudi forces are pulling back from the east and concentrating troops for a possible push into al-Bayda, a governorate the Houthis took over in 2020-21.

At the same time, Riyadh is building a naval coalition. 14 countries, including Turkey, Pakistan, Egypt, Sudan, and Djibouti have already backed a multinational maritime defense effort. 

Saudis have also asked the U.S. and several European nations to join. This could get real, fast...
