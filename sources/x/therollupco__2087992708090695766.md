The SEC just took the training wheels off tokenized money market funds.

Rob on the Franklin Templeton no-action letter and why it matters:

"The SEC's Division of Investment Management cleared Franklin Templeton's onchain money market fund to be used as collateral for securities lending, effectively as margin."

"Money market funds can normally be margined, that's why your brokerage cash earns interest. But because these funds were tokenized and lived on-chain, legacy custody rules designed for physical securities had blocked it."

"This relief lets Franklin custody and record ownership through its blockchain system instead. So now you can extend credit against these funds, and Franklin says the structure supports intraday trading, hourly NAV, and faster processing. The training wheels are off."
