🇷🇺 Russian tanker Caruzo (~1M barrels) was seen with makeshift anti-drone protections as it was passing through the Bosphorus.

Caruzo is a 28-year-old Suezmax crude tanker built in 1998, with a deadweight of about 146,600 tonnes and a length of 274 metres. 

It currently sails under the Sierra Leone flag.

The vessel is sanctioned by the UK, EU, Canada, Switzerland, Australia, New Zealand, and Ukraine for transporting Russian-origin crude and employing high-risk shadow-fleet practices such as AIS manipulation.

It is NOT under U.S. sanctions.
