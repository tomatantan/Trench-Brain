The Buffalo Bills have decided O.J. Simpson will not be included in the Family Circle, the honor space at the team's new Highmark Stadium. Simpson's name has been on the Bills' Wall of Fame since 1980, the year he became the first player inducted.

Former Erie County Legislator Betty Jean Grant has launched a petition asking the team to reconsider. “It's important to remember that history, in all its forms, must be preserved. Regardless of later personal controversies, the impact O.J. Simpson had on the sport and the team is undeniable,” the petition reads.
