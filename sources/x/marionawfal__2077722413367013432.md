🇪🇸 Thierry Henry just explained why Spanish teams keep dominating: they build TEAMS, not individuals.

Henry says Spanish football development focuses on players who understand space, movement, and passing from a young age, rather than chasing the tallest or fastest kids early on.

They keep talented players together, training with the best, and prioritize collective intelligence over raw physical attributes.

That philosophy is producing consistent, high-level team play across clubs and the national side.
