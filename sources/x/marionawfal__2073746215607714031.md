🇮🇶 Iraq is making a major move in global energy.

Baghdad just approved preliminary deals to study new strategic oil export pipelines, a significant step that could reshape how one of the world's top producers gets its crude to market.

With existing routes under pressure from regional tensions, new pipelines would boost Iraq's leverage and reduce its vulnerability to disruption.

In a region where who controls the flow of oil increasingly controls the conversation, Iraq just signaled it wants more of a say.
