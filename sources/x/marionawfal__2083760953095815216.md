🇺🇸 🇮🇷 The hacks on American drinking water have spread to at least seven states, and the evidence points DIRECTLY at Iran.

-The FBI and EPA say at least seven states have reported incidents since Monday, and "some of that activity degraded water operations," with CISA warning hackers are "targeting water entities of all sizes" and telling facilities to unplug vulnerable controllers from the internet

-Minnesota reported hackers targeting roughly three dozen municipalities, and Michigan has now confirmed nine municipal water systems reporting issues

-CISA says the intrusions have already "resulted in boil water notices and sustained manual operations," though officials stress no supply has been altered or made unsafe to drink

-One threat researcher called it a real escalation: "What's unprecedented here is that we're seeing direct, tangible effects to live industrial control systems inside U.S. critical infrastructure"

Trump publicly rejected the Iran link, saying "I think Minnesota is behind it" and "I don't think there was an Iranian cyberattack," while officials familiar with the assessment say his own agencies believe Iran highly likely did it.

Gov. Tim Walz answered: this "is what modern warfare looks like".
