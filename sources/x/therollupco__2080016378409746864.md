Johann Kerbrat, Robinhood's GM of Crypto, on why their chain lets memes, RWAs, and yield all live in the same place:

"It's important that we make the chain permissionless and available for everything. Memes, RWAs, stablecoin yield, so many more products. Everything is going to coexist."

"Each does a job. Memes bring market makers and DeFi customers. RWAs are for customers across the world who can't easily access US stocks and ETFs. And Robinhood Earn brings yield on stablecoins, and a lot of liquidity."

"Partnering with multiple DEXs from the get-go, Uniswap and others, meant we had the infrastructure to support trading on day one."
