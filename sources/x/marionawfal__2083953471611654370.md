🇨🇳🇮🇳 A Himalayan trade route connecting Tibet with India has just reopened after 6 years.

India and China have revived commerce at Nathu La Pass, a remote 14,140-ft border crossing between Sikkim and Tibet, closed since the 2020 Galwan clashes and the pandemic.

For ~600 local traders, it’s an absolute lifeline: rice, tea, wool, silk, and more can now flow again on an ancient yak-caravan route.

Trade volumes are tiny compared to the $151B bilateral total, but the move signals a cautious thaw. 

Small step. Big symbolism high in the mountains.
