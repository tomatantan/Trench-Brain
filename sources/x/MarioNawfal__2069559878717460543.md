🚨🇮🇷 The Strait of Hormuz is roaring back to life...

Ship crossings nearly tripled in a week, jumping from 32 vessels between June 12 and 14 to 93 between June 19 and 21, per MarineTraffic.

The single day swing tells the story best.

Saturday transits leapt from just 3 a week earlier to 42, a near vertical recovery the moment the diplomacy took hold and a temporary US Treasury license eased the legal risk of sailing through.

This is the deal showing up as hard data instead of headlines.

The waterway the entire war was fought over is refilling with traffic in real time, the clearest sign yet that the peace is functioning where it counts most.
