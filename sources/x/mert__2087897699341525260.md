this will continue to happen across all software, some basic tips:

- always use an email alias via protonmail (use 1pass and save each new email automatically)

- you almost never need to give your full name on anything

- never use a single-signer setup for your money. just do not do this. it does not matter if it's a hardware wallet. almost all websites requires 2FA/MFA but your crypto money somehow doesn't lol

- never reuse the same password for sites. once they have your email or phone, they can fish on the dark web to see your past exposed passwords and start trying to use those

- have a hardware MFA method like yubikey wherever possible. never SMS auth.

- using a password app, you also don't need to enter your same birthday on every site. most of them save these together for you so you don't have to remember.

- wherever possible ship to a shared space like your team's office

- depending on who you are, do not disclose where you are until after you've left. and importantly make sure your girlfriend/wife does the same thing.

- obviously double check the sender from all emails, you can ask chatgpt whether the domain is legit

- it's favorable to have different devices for money, personal, and work

- do an audit. if one of your passwords or emails or whatever is hacked, how much damage can be done? ideally, reduce scope as much as possible 

- some of these are overkill, depending on who you are, you don't need to do all of them, but do the basics
