Companies are getting tokenized without permission and their own executives could trade the tokens anonymously

"Coinbase has not tokenized their equity, or Robinhood. But there are five different versions of derivatives of Robinhood equity or Coinbase equity that have been issued completely without Coinbase permission, completely without Robinhood permission"

"They're all different derivatives issued by different companies. They are using the name of the company without their permission, and in some cases they list the CUSIP, like if this was a real security. CUSIP is this legal number that every security has. They're offering them as tokenized stocks even though they're not stocks. If I was an issuer, I would not want that to happen"

"Tom Farley, CEO of Bullish, posted the other day something very interesting. There's somebody that has created a derivative of Bullish equity that trades offshore without KYC in a permissionless environment. So as a CEO, my CFO could go there and buy or sell those derivatives completely anonymously the day before the earnings announcements"
