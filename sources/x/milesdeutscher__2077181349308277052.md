Everything I've learned building to 700K+ followers:

1. Pick the right topic first

The topic you pick determines the audience you build.

Doesn't have to be the biggest topic - just has to be the right one for what you eventually want to sell.

If you want to sell AI consulting, create AI content. 

If you want to sell YouTube coaching, create content about growing on YouTube.

The topic you pick determines the audience you build. 

The audience you build determines what you can monetise.

2. Master the hook

First sentence. That's it. That's the whole game.

Your hook has one job: make someone stop scrolling. 

The hook isn't to inform them or impress them, it's to get them to stop.

Tip: Build your own hook library. 

3. Give people a reason to follow

This is the one most people miss.

Going viral and getting followers are not the same thing.

You can hit 2 million impressions and gain 50 followers if you don't give people a reason to follow you.

Your CTA doesn't have to be "follow me." It can be:

→ "I'll be posting a full breakdown of this next week"
→ "Bookmark this and come back when you're ready to build"
→ "I've been doing this for 5 years, here's what I learned"

Anything that signals authority, builds anticipation, or creates a reason to stay.

The formula is simple: 

The right topic + a scroll-stopping hook + a reason to follow.

Most people get one of the three right, but if you get all three right consistently, the growth compounds fast.
