🇺🇸 You’ve been hearing “Patriot” nonstop this year, so here’s what it actually is.

It’s the U.S. system that knocks incoming missiles out of the sky before they hit. Ukraine leans on it every night against Russia. The Gulf used it hard against Iran. 

Right now it’s the most wanted piece of military gear on the planet.

Two wars are burning through the interceptors faster than anyone can build them. The numbers are brutal:

U.S. burned about 65% of its stock from February to July, from 2,330 down to under 850.

Saudi Arabia used 86% of its 2,800 in the first 38 days against Iran, left with just 400.

Six to eight countries are now lined up fighting for more.

You can’t just make more overnight. One interceptor runs $4–5 million. A full battery costs over a billion. These take years to build, not weeks.

So here’s the reality: everyone wants Patriots at once, the shelves are nearly empty, and the big $58.6 billion Lockheed deal won’t deliver for years. 

Air defense is the one thing you really can’t run out of, and the West is getting close.
