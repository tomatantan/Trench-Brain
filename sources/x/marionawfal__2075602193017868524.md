🇺🇸🇶🇦 Trump's new Air Force One is missing its missile defenses

The Qatari-donated 747 Trump rushed into service doesn't have the anti-missile gear the old jet carries, multiple officials say.

It's a big enough gap that leaving Turkey, the Secret Service just put him back on the older plane.

These countermeasures are the systems that throw incoming heat-seeking missiles off track. You can spot them on the old jet's wings and tail, and they're simply not there in photos of the new one.

It comes down to the rush. A proper secure retrofit takes well over a year, but Trump pushed to fast-track it, reportedly moaning the old jet wasn't flashy enough for foreign trips.

Flying a half-protected president into Turkey, right next to an Iran the U.S. is bombing, is the exact thing that gear is for.
