🚨🇮🇷 Iran's foreign minister spent Saturday calling the region to warn what happens if America strikes...

-Araghchi held separate calls with Turkey's Hakan Fidan and Pakistan's Field Marshal Asim Munir, warning that Iran would respond in kind to any "adventurous action" by the United States

-In a call with Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan, he said Iran would answer "any aggression" from the U.S., Israel or other countries in the region decisively and proportionally

-Fidan confirmed the call publicly, saying the two discussed the state of the ongoing negotiations

-It builds on Araghchi's "eye for an eye" line from last week, promising retaliation for any attack on Iranian infrastructure

The warnings land with U.S. and Israeli strikes on Iran's power plants and refineries possible across the weekend, and American embassies telling citizens to be ready to leave.
