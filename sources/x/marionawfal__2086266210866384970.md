🇺🇸 The S&P 500 is busting out new all-time highs again, and the historical pattern is hard to ignore.

Over the past 30 years, after hitting new records, the index finished higher 6 months later 13/17 times. 

Average gain: roughly 6.3%. The misses? 2000, 2007, 2018, and 2019. 

The first two opened the door to major bear markets, and the most recent comparable breakout in 2025 was followed by a clean +12.3% over the following 6 months.

This year alone, the S&P has already notched 26 all-time highs, after 39 in 2025, and 57 in 2024. 

That’s 3 straight years of relentless record-setting…

Momentum after new highs tends to be on the upside… but the exceptions are an all-too-important reminder not to count on it.
