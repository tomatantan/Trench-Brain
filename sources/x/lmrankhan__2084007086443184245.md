Life is far better when you create more friends than enemies, especially when you’re still an upstart.

I really don’t understand the intense public hostility between Kalshi and Polymarket. The negative PR campaigns and constant attacks feel to me shortsighted and risk damaging public perception of the entire space.

I’ve even had startups building prediction market products tell me that incumbents reached out asking them to participate in taking shots at the other side.

Where is the camaraderie in competing? 

You can hate each other. You can talk shit about each other’s products. Steve Jobs openly mocked Bill Gates’s lack of product and design sense, but they still shook hands, shared a stage from time to time and still went on to build two of the most important companies in tech. Microsoft even invested in Apple when Apple lost its ways. 

But recruiting smaller startups into proxy wars and running coordinated negative campaigns doesn’t grow the market. It just makes us all look like shit.

Get your shit together. Shake hands and compete like the people who came before you.
