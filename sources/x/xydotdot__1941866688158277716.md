Sunday Tip

One of the simplest, most overlooked tools for understanding your relationship to material things is this: Rent it before you buy it.

Rent the watch. Rent the car. Rent the house.

Not for an afternoon. Not for a weekend.

Rent it long enough for the novelty to wear
