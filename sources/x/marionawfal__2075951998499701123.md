🇪🇸🇫🇷 Barcelona set to lose Torres to PSG

The 26-year-old forward has said yes to the move and wants it sorted once the World Cup wraps up.

On his end it's a five-year contract with a pay rise of around 25 percent, so the player side of this is basically done.

The problem is the two clubs. PSG and Barcelona still haven't agreed on a price, and until they do, nothing is final.

Barcelona seem fine letting him go. They've strengthened their attack this summer, so losing him doesn't hurt as much as it would have.

And there's history here. Luis Enrique coached Torres with Spain and rates him, so you can see why PSG want him.
