🇫🇷🇸🇾 This abandoned mansion outside Paris once belonged to Rifaat al-Assad, the man they called the Butcher of Hama.

He was Assad's uncle and Hafez’s brother. Yes, uncle of the fmr. Syrian president.

After Syria pushed him out in the 1980s he turned this sprawling estate into his private playground of luxury while thousands back home paid the price.

French courts eventually seized the whole place after convicting him of laundering money stolen from the Syrian people.

Now the indoor pool sits cracked and dry, the stables are rotting, and the rooms are trashed.

Explorers just walked through the ruins of one of the clearest symbols of how the Assad family turned public funds into private palaces.
