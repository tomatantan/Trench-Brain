"The DAO should not be voting on what brand of toilet paper to use at the Arbitrum meeting"

Offchain CEO Steven Goldfeder on the over-decentralization mistake they made early on

"One of the mistakes we made early on in the Arbitrum ecosystem is when we overemphasized decentralization. There's a difference between decentralization of the root of power versus operational logistics. The DAO should not be voting on what brand of toilet paper to use at the Arbitrum meeting"

"People go to the DAO saying, can I get $5,000 for this conference, and what you get is apathy, where the smart investors are like, I can't handle this volume, that's not my problem. You have these professional delegates that are like, oh, I'll answer this. So what we did was get rid of these professional delegate programs, and put in procedures where votes go live on Thursday, so you know when new votes will go live"

"We also put in charge, via a vote, certain operations, the opco, you are the operating company, so we don't need to bring decisions like toilet paper to the DAO. By the way, if there was a revolt and it was a terrible brand of toilet paper, you can come back and take that away"
