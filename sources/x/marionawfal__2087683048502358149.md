🇺🇸 Scott Greer described how campus activists shifted Charlie Kirk’s approach to immigration.

In the late 2010s Groyper students showed up at TPUSA events and asked direct questions on restriction and demographics that the standard free-market talking points didn’t fully address.

Kirk’s earlier support for policies like stapling green cards to diplomas gave way over time to a harder line on overall immigration levels as those interactions highlighted what many college audiences prioritized.

The back-and-forth helped reshape the conversation.
