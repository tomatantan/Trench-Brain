🇮🇷🇸🇦🇾🇪 As if the problems in the Strait of Hormuz weren't bad enough, the Houthis are now threatening to close the Bab al-Mandeb, too…

These two straits form the critical corridor for Middle East oil reaching Europe and Asia, and you cannot bomb the Bab al-Mandeb open any more than you can bomb Hormuz open.

The Saudi East-West pipeline routes crude to the Yanbu terminal on the Red Sea specifically to bypass Hormuz, and a Houthi blockade of the Bab al-Mandeb would cut that workaround off entirely.

Global reserves have been depleted since February, strategic reserves are running low, and China is back buying at full pace.

If both routes close simultaneously, what does that do to a global economy already running on empty?
