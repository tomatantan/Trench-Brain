In September 2020, Paris Hilton launched a petition to shut down Provo Canyon School, a youth residential treatment center in Utah where she says she endured abuse as a teenager.

Now, the school is closing for good.

Hilton spent the last six years turning her allegations of abuse at Provo Canyon into a broader reform movement. From Utah’s Capitol to Congress, she helped push for stronger oversight of youth treatment programs, especially in Utah.

On Friday, the Utah Department of Health and Human Services determined that both the boys’ and girls’ campuses were not safe for the children in their care, marking the end of a years-long push for accountability from Hilton and fellow survivors.
