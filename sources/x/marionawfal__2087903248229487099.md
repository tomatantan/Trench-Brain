🚨🇫🇷 The man who commanded France's only nuclear carrier just fell off a mountain...

Vice Admiral Olivier Lebas fell to his death yesterday while climbing in the Pyrenees, the mountain range along the France-Spain border.

He flew combat missions over the Balkans, Iraq, Afghanistan, and Libya. Once brought a badly shot-up jet back onto a carrier deck in one piece.

From the Charles de Gaulle, France's flagship and the only nuclear-powered carrier that isn't American, he ran the naval side of the coalition's air war against ISIS in the eastern Mediterranean.

Later, he became maritime prefect of the Atlantic, then joined Airbus.

What a tragic way to go out...
