"The primary risk here is credit risk. We call these instruments digital credit because they're not risk free. They have one primary risk"

Matt Cole on the real risk behind digital credit and why the critics contradict each other

"What's interesting about a lot of the critiques is that from the perspective of the non-Bitcoiner, the critiques are almost the exact opposite of what you hear from the Bitcoin community. You could almost just have the critics debate each other because they say things that are actually the exact opposite"

"What you'll find from us is we're trying to actually get out there and talk about the real risks we see. The primary risk for me is credit risk. We call these instruments digital credit because they're not risk free"

"Some people in the Bitcoin community will say it shouldn't be called digital credit. But then when they talk about the risks themselves, most of the risks are actually credit risks. I'm more interested in having the more intellectual debate about the risks and the opportunities"
