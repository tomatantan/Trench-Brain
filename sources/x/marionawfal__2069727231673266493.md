🇮🇷🇱🇧 Lebanon matters to Tehran for one big reason: Hezbollah.

The group was born out of Israel's 1982 invasion of Lebanon, but it was Iran that built it. Revolutionary Guard officers poured into the Bekaa Valley with money, weapons, training and ideology, transforming a local Shiite resistance into Tehran's most successful foreign project.

Four decades later, Hezbollah is far more than a militia; it's a political party and a military force.

For Iran, Hezbollah isn't just an ally. It's the crown jewel of its regional strategy and its most potent proxy against Israel.

That's why Tehran wants any deal with Washington to protect Hezbollah as much as it protects Iran itself.
