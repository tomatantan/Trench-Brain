🇪🇺 Eurovision rewrote the hosting rules. 

If your country is in an armed conflict or any “sensitive geopolitical situation,” you automatically lose the right to host the next contest even if you win.

They say it’s for clarity and to keep artists and crews safe.

This comes right after Spain, the Netherlands, Ireland and Slovenia sat out this year over the Gaza war and Israel’s participation. 

The message is pretty clear: win the song contest, stay out of wars, or someone else gets the party.
