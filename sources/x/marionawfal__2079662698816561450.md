🇺🇸🇱🇧  Trump’s Hezbollah strategy takes shape as Aoun pushes Lebanon disarmament plan

Lebanese President has presented Trump with a plan to place all weapons under state control, dismantle armed groups, and make Hezbollah the top priority for disarmament efforts.

The proposal also calls for strengthening the Lebanese Armed Forces and urges Trump to pressure Israel into a gradual withdrawal from southern Lebanon.

The move comes as Trump has suggested Syrian President Ahmad al-Sharaa could play a role in countering Hezbollah, adding another potential regional pressure point against the group.

The challenge remains the same. 

Hezbollah has spent decades building its military and political influence, making any attempt to restore the Lebanese state’s monopoly on force a major test for the region’s new balance of power.
