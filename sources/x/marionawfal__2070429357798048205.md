🇺🇸 New satellite imagery shows Iran did far more damage to America's only naval base in the Middle East than the Pentagon has ever admitted.

The WSJ used satellite images, social-media footage, and interviews to map the hits on Naval Support Activity Bahrain, struck repeatedly between late February and June. 

The command headquarters, at least a dozen other buildings, and 2 satellite communications terminals took direct hits.

No one was killed and the military says operations weren't significantly affected, but the damage didn't stop in Bahrain. 

At least 20 U.S. sites across the region were hit, and officials say it now has the U.S. rethinking its entire footprint in the Middle East.

On the table: scaling back in Kuwait and Saudi Arabia, moving command nodes underground, and pushing some bases west, out of Iranian missile range. Israel is reportedly one of the options.

CENTCOM says the strategy worked, noting Iran fired more than 8,000 missiles and drones with only 2 hits causing U.S. fatalities. 

CSIS still puts the war's total cost near $40 billion, with up to $5.1 billion of that in base damage the Pentagon won't quantify for Congress.
