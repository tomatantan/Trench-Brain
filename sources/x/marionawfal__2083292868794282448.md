🇺🇸 U.S. emergency oil reserves just dropped to their lowest level since 1983.

The Strategic Petroleum Reserve fell 3.8 million barrels last week to 308 million, an 18th straight weekly decline.

It's down 108 million barrels over that stretch, a 26% drop

Commercial crude outside the reserve fell 7.2 million barrels to 405 million

That's the lowest since October 2018, roughly 7% under the 5-year average

How it got this low: two big releases. 

Biden pulled 180 million barrels after Russia invaded Ukraine, and Trump released 172 million during the Strait of Hormuz shutdown this year. 

Once Trump's is fully out, the reserve drops to around 243 million.

The cushion keeps shrinking while the Iran conflict is live, which is about the worst time to be running the tank down.
