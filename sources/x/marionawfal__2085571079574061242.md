🚨🇮🇷 Iran's own negotiators are warning mediators they cannot guarantee a deal will hold, and one of them is facing impeachment for trying...

-Arab mediators say Araghchi and Ghalibaf embraced the plan to split Hormuz into inbound and outbound lanes, then the Revolutionary Guard demanded explicit recognition of Iran's role, the right to charge transit fees, and firm assurances the blockade and oil sanctions come off

-Mediators say Iranian negotiators now speak openly about the limits of their own authority and the pressure to produce immediate money

-The Guard is being praised internally for keeping America off balance, and mediators were told it could resume attacking ships if a deal is not lucrative enough

-The KNIVES are out at home: the editor of Kayhan, the hardline daily whose supervisor is appointed directly by the supreme leader and which is widely read as a barometer of what the top wants, wrote that reopening the strait would be "opening an escape route for the enemy"

-Lawmaker Hossein-Ali Haji Deligani says he is gathering documentation to IMPEACH Araghchi for not putting a Hormuz pact to a parliamentary vote

-A relative of the supreme leader by marriage called on social media for a march on Tehran to finish off Pezeshkian's government, and Khamenei's office had to publicly disown it

-The vacuum at the top explains a lot, since Mojtaba Khamenei has not appeared in public since March, Araghchi says he has never met him, and Pezeshkian reportedly threatened to resign just to secure a meeting

The impeachment threat against Araghchi is the part to sit with.

Iran's hardliners aren't just undermining the negotiators, they're moving to remove them for negotiating at all.

Kayhan calling a reopened strait "an escape route for the enemy" tells you how the Guard sees the chokepoint: not a bargaining chip to be traded away, but the weapon itself.
