🇪🇺 Europe is heading into winter with its gas tanks dangerously low and no real way to fill them

EU storage sat at 58.1% on August 5, about 15.6 points below where it normally is this time of year. Run the last 15 years of refill seasons against today's starting point and not one of them gets the bloc to its 90% target by November. Even a best-case year falls short.

It's really a two-speed Europe. Poland and Hungary are fine. The problem kids are Germany, sitting at 47.6% against a normal 75%, and the Netherlands at 38.5% against 77%. 

Together they're the biggest hole on the continent, and neither has any history of closing a gap this size in three months.

Here's the part that stings. Europe swears it's banning Russian gas by 2027, yet it keeps quietly buying it to plug the gaps. Belgium is now fully reliant on Russian LNG after the Gulf war choked off Qatar's supply.

The wildcard is weather, same as last winter. A mild autumn lands the EU somewhere in the 70s. 

An early freeze or another price shock tips Germany and the Netherlands from "problem" into full crisis before winter even starts.
