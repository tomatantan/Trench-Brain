🇺🇸🇮🇷 Trump says Iran's got some military left, and he wants it gone.

At a Camp David cabinet meeting today he said the US has "obliterated" Iran's military capacity over five months of war, and they've got some left "but soon they won't have some left."

The plan is real. CENTCOM chief Adm. Brad Cooper has drafted a 10 to 14 day campaign to wipe out Tehran's remaining missiles, the biggest US operation since the April ceasefire. Officials say Trump is "in an escalatory mood" but still weighing how far to go.

His own top general isn't sold. Joint Chiefs chairman Gen. Dan Caine warns that going big would drain the US stockpile of air-defense interceptors, the same missiles protecting American troops and allies everywhere else.

Tehran's split too. Its own negotiators are reportedly on a completely different page than the IRGC, which keeps hitting US forces even while the diplomats talk.

Both sides have a faction that wants off-ramps, and both keep getting overruled.
