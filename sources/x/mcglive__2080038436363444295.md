.@Natan_benish broke down a "side effect" of @longdotxyz's stock pairings that has been popping up.

Here's the setup:

Every stock trade on Robinhood references an oracle price, a Chainlink-Robinhood feed pulling the real-time price from the stock exchange. That sets the reference for what you should get when you trade, say, $100 of USDG into a stock.

Normally your price impact is slightly negative (-1 to -2). But Nate says he spotted a positive one for the first time, people buying the stock slightly below the official oracle price. It's a capital-efficiency/market-structure effect from prop market makers competing on the order flow.

Talented stock traders can now hedge and pull liquidity from their pairs.
