🇺🇸🇮🇷 How solid is U.S. energy independence right now with Iran tensions flaring again?

The Strategic Petroleum Reserve is flashing warning signs.

It held steady around 410 to 415 million barrels early this year, giving Trump serious leverage to release oil and keep gas prices from exploding during the first shock.

But it's plummeted since, down to roughly 319 to 330 million barrels as of early July, the lowest level since the 1980s.

Both sides want leverage over the Strait of Hormuz, but only one can truly control it at the end.

That thinner cushion leaves Washington with far less room to flood the market if Tehran disrupts flows again.

Strategic energy autonomy looks shaky when it matters most.
