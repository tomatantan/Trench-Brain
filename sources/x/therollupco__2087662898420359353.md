LPing used to be the best thing to do with your ETH. Now it's dying.

Guillaume on what comes after passive liquidity provision:

"Generally you felt like you made money. Some activity, sometimes you lost, but most of the time, whether incentives or real fees, this was one of the best things to do with your ETH or whatever airdrop you got."

"You can borrow against your position. You can LP on leverage. You can buy puts, buy protection for your LP position, manage impermanent loss instead of wait and see."

"More diversity of strategies than being an LP and hoping the price stays in range."
