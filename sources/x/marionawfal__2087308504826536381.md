🇲🇦🇪🇸 Morocco's Justice Minister went on Arab TV and said Ceuta and Melilla are still MOROCCAN TERRITORY.

Abdellatif Ouahbi confirmed the claim remains open, they raise it in every meeting with Spain, and they plan to settle it through negotiation and patience while staying attached to those cities.

Washington is in this now too. 

A House Appropriations Committee report in April placed the cities in Moroccan territory and pushed the State Department toward brokering talks, though State said on August 6 it unequivocally supports Spanish sovereignty.

Morocco has held this position since independence in 1956, but a sitting minister saying it this directly on air right after the recent migrant surge into Ceuta is still striking.
