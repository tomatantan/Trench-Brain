Interesting idea I heard recently: 

"Oil prices aren't dependent on Trumps actions. Trumps actions are dependent on Oil prices"

We often markets aren't just a speculative instrument, they are powerful coordination mechanics and their price movements have an incredibly potent affect on how the world functions. 

This is why I believe Conditionals will the most significant new market structure to emerge this decade. 

They create a surface for pricing how actions and events affect markets, affect the world, while allowing people to better position around each possible outcome.
