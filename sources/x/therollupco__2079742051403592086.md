Keone Hon says the most valuable protocols focus on users before revenue.

“The companies or ideas that ultimately are really valuable start with a desire to deliver a lot of value to users.”

“Then they find a way to ultimately make money from that.”

“We’ve seen that whether it was Google, Facebook, or Nvidia.”

“These are teams that care a lot about a really high-quality product.”

“We should care about revenues, and we should go toward where there is value.”

“But one could start worrying too much about revenue from day one.”

“Then you start locally optimizing rather than globally optimizing.”
