🇺🇸🇦🇪 U.S. loosens tech and military export rules for the UAE

These are the controls that decide what sensitive American tech can and can't be sold abroad, and the UAE just got a big chunk of them lifted.

And it's not small stuff. What's now cleared to go:

       Certain military gear
       Commercial satellites and spacecraft
       Advanced computing tech, the high-end chips that run AI

Basically the U.S. is pulling the UAE in tighter as a tech and defense buddy, and giving the Emiratis' big AI dreams a real leg up while it's at it.
