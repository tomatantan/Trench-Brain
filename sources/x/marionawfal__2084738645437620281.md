🇺🇸 The UFC lost $30 million on its White House fight night, deliberately.

Parent company TKO laid out the numbers on its earnings call. The June event, Freedom 250, cost more than $60 million to pull off with zero public tickets, and sponsors only covered about half.

Here's what the $30 million actually bought. The first combat sports event ever held on the South Lawn, an average of 34 million viewers worldwide, and one of the most-watched MMA cards in history.

And it barely dented the business. Even with the loss, UFC's quarterly revenue jumped 29% to $535.7 million, lifted by its new $7.7 billion Paramount streaming deal.

Call it a loss on paper. In reach and prestige, it's one of the best deals the UFC has ever made.
