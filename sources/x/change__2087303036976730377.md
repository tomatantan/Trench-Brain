Timothy Wedlock, 58, of Australia, was killed Aug. 1 after police said a driver ran a red light and struck pedestrians who were legally crossing Lower Broadway.

His death has renewed concerns about pedestrian safety on the busy downtown stretch. Over the past five years, there have been 34 pedestrian-involved injury crashes along Lower Broadway, according to Metro Nashville Police crash data.

Lower Broadway is one of Nashville’s busiest entertainment districts, drawing large crowds of pedestrians to its bars, restaurants and live music venues while remaining open to vehicle traffic.

A petition launched after Wedlock’s death is urging city officials to close the pedestrian-packed stretch from First through Fifth avenues to vehicle traffic.
