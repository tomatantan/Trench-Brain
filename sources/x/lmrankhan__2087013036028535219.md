It’s funny many of the best ideas I’ve seen work best when paired with a product that already has dominance, because it’s much easier to extend existing distribution into similar  products / features than build that distribution from zero

e.g we’ve funded startups building clans because thesis was that fnf groups will become the modernday esports teams. Same with livestream prediction markets 1.5 years ago, or creator/startup token platforms.

But given Pump / Fomo’s focus on social and the distribution, liquidity and user graph they already have, it’s probably easier for them to enable these products as features than for a standalone startup to build the entire network from zero.

It’s basically Ben Thompson’s aggregation theory applied to crypto once you aggregate demand side, a number of standalone products can become features.

You could argue that a lot of the crypto consumer ideas that are out there could be mostly collapsed into what Pump and Fomo are building
