🇮🇷🇾🇪 Iran is publicly distancing itself from the Houthis, insisting the group acts on its own.

In a letter to UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres, Iran's UN envoy Amir-Saeid Iravani called the claim that they act on Tehran's behalf "completely baseless."

He argued the Sanaa authorities represent a large share of the Yemeni people and make their own decisions based on what they see as Yemen's interests.

The Iran-aligned group has long backed Tehran and the Palestinians, carrying out attacks on Israel throughout the recent regional conflicts.
