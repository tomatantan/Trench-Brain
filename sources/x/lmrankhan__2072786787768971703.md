One of the biggest mistakes founders make is keeping unnecessary friction around in the early days.

Your startup is already working against you. Why add more?

If you're constantly picking up your cofounder's slack because you've known them forever or keep hoping they'll change, it's time to move on.

Your only job is to remove anything that slows you down and double down on everything that creates momentum.
