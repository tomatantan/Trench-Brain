🇵🇸 Gaza’s next government could end up covering $400 million in unpaid bills from the Hamas era.

The plan is pretty simple. Go through old debts to workers, suppliers and contractors, and pay the ones needed to keep basic services running.

Officials say this isn’t a $400 million check for Hamas. Every payment would be reviewed, and anything that could benefit the group gets blocked.

Ted Cruz isn’t buying it. His argument is that paying bills left behind during Hamas rule could still indirectly help the group.

Officials say the $400 million is just a cap, not a spending commitment.

So nobody’s getting a blank check. Each old bill gets looked at one by one.
