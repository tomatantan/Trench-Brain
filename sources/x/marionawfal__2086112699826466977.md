🇮🇷🇺🇸 Iran's president just made his sales pitch for ending the war 

From strength, not surrender.

Pezeshkian says now is the best moment to reach an agreement precisely because Iran feels united, strong, and, in his framing, victorious after this war. 

Not a weak hand forced to the table. A winning one cashing in.

It's the same message he's repeated all week: national cohesion held, Iran wasn't toppled, the new supreme leader kept the country together.

The catch is that "victorious" is Tehran's own scorecard. Depleted U.S. interceptor stockpiles and a strait still under negotiation over transit fees don't obviously read like anyone's decisive win.

Every side of this war is telling its own people the same thing right now: we're the ones who came out ahead. 

Whose version will survive contact with the actual deal once it gets signed?
