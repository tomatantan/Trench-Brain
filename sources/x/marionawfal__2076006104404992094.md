🇦🇷🇪🇬 Hackers claiming to be Egyptian breached an email account belonging to Argentina’s national football federation after the team’s 3-2 World Cup comeback win over Egypt.

The message, sent to journalists under the subject “SYSTEM HACKED: UNFAIR DECISION,” accused the referee of stealing the match from Egypt, claimed Argentina and Messi were being protected for marketing, and warned: “If there is no justice on the field, do not expect peace on your networks.”

Argentina’s Football Association, known as the AFA, said the emails were unauthorized and may have come from an account compromised by hackers. It told recipients to ignore unusual messages, links, attachments, or requests for personal information while it investigates the breach.

Egypt’s coach and football federation had already accused FIFA and the referee of influencing the result. FIFA defended the officiating.
