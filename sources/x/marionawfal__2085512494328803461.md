🇺🇸 Trump's new battleships just got 50% more expensive and not one of them exists yet.

The Congressional Budget Office now puts the lead ship, USS Defiant, at $23.4 billion, about $8 billion over the Navy's first estimate.

The other 14? Just over $18 billion each. That's $275 billion for the fleet, Ford-class supercarrier money for every hull.

What sent it through the roof? Trump swapping the ships from gas turbines to nuclear reactors.

And money might be the easy part. The CBO warns U.S. shipyards may not have the capacity to build them, with the job likely falling to a yard already years behind on carriers and subs.

First ship could take 8 years or longer, when Trump wanted the first two done in under 3.

Trump wanted them fast and cheap. He's getting neither.
