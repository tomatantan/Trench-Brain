🇮🇷🇺🇸 Iran spent the ceasefire quietly wiring its proxies for round two.

The IRGC reportedly used the recent pause to get its whole proxy network on the same page, coordinating with Hezbollah, the Houthis, and Iran-backed militias in Iraq.

Quds Force commanders ran virtual meetings with militia leaders and pushed advisers out to allied groups on the ground.

The goal is straightforward: expand coordinated attacks across the region to jack up the strategic and economic cost of staying in the fight for Washington.

Tehran is also tightening its grip on Hezbollah directly, slotting senior Iranian commanders onto the group's governing council to fill leadership gaps and steer its operations.

A quiet stretch that looks like peace is often just both sides reloading, and Iran clearly used the calm to get its network ready for round two.
