Andy asks why Story rebranded to The DATA Foundation.

“It’s not really a pivot away from IP.”

“Ever since we launched Story, we’ve always considered data to be a form of IP.”

“Not all forms of IP are best suited for permissionless licensing.”

“The most valuable IP in the world is the least likely to want to open up for anyone to use permissionlessly.”

“We saw this massive demand around data and training data, and also how opaque it was.”

“Having transparency, provenance, and attribution around who contributed what data for what dataset is very important.”
