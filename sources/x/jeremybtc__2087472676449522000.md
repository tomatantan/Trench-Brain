Japanese bond yields have been climbing all year and almost nobody in crypto is watching

The 2 year bond yield sits near its highest level in three decades

That matters because of the carry trade, where traders borrow cheap yen and bet it on everything from stocks to crypto

Rising Japanese yields are what unwinds that trade, and the last time it happened in 2024 it crashed markets worldwide

The pressure is building again while everyone stares at CPI
