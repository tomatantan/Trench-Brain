🇺🇸 Local police sent 102 radio warnings about Thomas Crooks before he shot at Trump in Butler. The Secret Service received zero of them.

The counter-drone system, the one piece of equipment that could have detected Crooks' 9-minute drone flight over the rally site, was inoperable. 

It was being operated by a single undertrained agent who had never tested it before the event. When a call finally came in about the rooftop, that agent Googled the building's location to figure out where it was.

There was also classified intelligence about a long-range threat to Trump that was never shared with the Pittsburgh field office or the agents planning security. 

One bystander died. Trump was grazed by a bullet that came a few centimeters from killing the President of the United States.

6 staffers received suspensions of 10 days to 6 weeks. The Secret Service agreed with all 7 recommendations.

Does that feel proportionate to you?
