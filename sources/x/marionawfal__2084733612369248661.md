🇮🇷🇵🇰 Iran has found a way around the U.S. blockade, and it runs straight through Pakistan.

With Washington choking off Iran's ports, Tehran now wants to use Pakistan's ports at Karachi and Gwadar as hubs to re-export its goods to other countries. 

A senior Iranian trade official pitched the plan in Islamabad this week, alongside talks to push trade toward $10 billion.

The Strait of Hormuz is still choked, with just 6 vessels slipping through yesterday, all hugging Iranian waters. So Iran is routing around the sea entirely.

Here's the twist: The Telegraph reports the U.S. and Israel are now weighing a land blockade too, after Trump reportedly decided the naval one has done all it can.

Good luck with that. Iran shares land borders with 7 countries, and sealing every road and crossing around it is a very different job than parking warships off one coast.
