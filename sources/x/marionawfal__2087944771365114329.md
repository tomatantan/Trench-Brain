🇺🇦🇷🇺 Ukraine wants a Black Sea ceasefire, but only for the ships.

Kyiv is asking both sides to stop hitting commercial boats, and sent the offer to Russia through Turkey. Moscow hasn't said anything back yet.

The fighting has spread from warships to regular cargo ships, some of them Turkish, which is why Turkey jumped in to help.

Ukraine says over 218 ships have been hit since July. Russian strikes near Odesa have basically shut down its exports by sea, which is a big part of how the country still makes money.

Ukraine hasn't gone soft on Russia here. It's still hammering Russian warships, and just wants the cargo lane that feeds its economy left out of it.
