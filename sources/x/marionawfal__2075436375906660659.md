🇺🇸🇮🇱 The era of American military aid to Israel is ending, by mutual decision

-Israel and the U.S. have both decided to end traditional military aid and transform the relationship into a "reciprocal partnership"

-The current $38 BILLION aid agreement expires in 2028, and official talks began last month on replacing the memorandum of understanding with a "memorandum of partnership"

-Netanyahu backs the shift, saying Israel must move "from an aid recipient to a strategic partner"

-The driver: bipartisan American demands to end the subsidies, with Israel concluding "charity" framing makes the alliance politically fragile when U.S. moods shift

-The new model trades visible grants for deep defense-industrial integration, U.S. access to Israeli battlefield data on missile defense, cyber, and AI, and enhanced congressional oversight

Buried in the fine print is the real hedge: Israel is building its own production capacity because it expects future American conditions it can't accept.
