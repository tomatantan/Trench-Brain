Salman Banaei lays out the two most likely paths for the crypto bill's ethics language:

"The White House has been heavily involved in the evolution of this ethics language, so I don't think it gets to a standoff at the president's desk."

"More likely: either no ethics language comes out and the bill languishes, or language comes out that isn't enough for Democrats to get to yes."

"I'd put it around 50-50 between those two."
