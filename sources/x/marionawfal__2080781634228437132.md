The $110 billion Paramount-Warner Bros. deal just got a lot messier. 

Paramount agreed to suspend the merger until potentially July 2027 after a federal judge paused it, siding with 12 Democratic AGs who say the consolidation raises serious antitrust questions.

Beyond the money, the flashpoint is media power: the deal would put CNN and CBS newsrooms under David Ellison, an executive with close Trump ties. 

Analysts say the path forward, whichever way it goes, just got longer, messier, and more expensive. 

Hollywood's biggest power play is officially stalled.
