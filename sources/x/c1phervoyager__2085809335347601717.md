no fees and faster fills are obviously good features

but people saying pump “destroyed” fomo are comparing a network effect to a fee schedule. fomo’s edge was never the buy button. it was seeing several traders you actually trust independently converge on the same trade in real time. that social confluence is unmatched.

pump can copy the ux 1:1 and pay profitable traders to switch, but that buys activity, not trust. it’s renting the graph instead of building one. once people are paid to be there, every thesis comes with an asterisk. 

i genuinely don’t care about saving 50bps if the feed is full of paid grifters and rented conviction. bad signal costs far more than 0.5%.

in a social trading app, the social layer is the product. everyone obsessing over the fucking fees is missing the entire point.
