🇮🇷 Iran's FM Spox. Esmail Baghaei:

"Even without relying on Article 5 of the MOU, Iran, as the coastal state bordering the Strait of Hormuz, possesses legal and sovereign rights over the strait.

The experience of military attacks by the U.S. and Israeli regime, along with the cooperation of some countries in the region, has demonstrated that Iran mustn't allow this vital waterway to be exploited against the country's security and national interests.

Iran is determined, in cooperation with Oman, to adopt the necessary measures and arrangements to fully exercise its sovereignty and prevent any security threats in the Strait of Hormuz."
