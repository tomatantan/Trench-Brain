🇮🇷🇺🇸 Tucker and Darryl Cooper revisit the American strike that killed roughly 168 people at a girls' school in Minab on the war's first morning

The Shajareh Tayyebeh elementary school was hit on February 28 while classes were still in session, and most of the dead were girls between seven and twelve years old.

Israel said its forces were not operating in the area. 

A preliminary US assessment concluded American forces were likely responsible, and Amnesty has called the strike unlawful and demanded accountability.

Cooper walks through the part that keeps pulling Tucker back to it, that the site was struck again roughly 40 minutes later while people were digging through the rubble.

What lands hardest is everything that followed.

NO APOLOGY anyone remembers, no accountability.

Cooper says it belongs on the front page of every history book.

Tucker's take is blunter, that a country capable of shrugging at the deaths of its enemy's children has given up something it will not get back easily. 

He's right.
