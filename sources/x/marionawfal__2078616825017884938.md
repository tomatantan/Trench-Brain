🚨🇺🇸 A body was found at a Boston home owned by the husband of Rep. Ayanna Pressley, with the homicide unit now investigating...

-Boston police were called to 25 Malta Street in Mattapan Saturday afternoon "to investigate a person" and found them deceased

-City property records list Conan Harris, Pressley's husband of more than a decade, as the owner of the $919,000-assessed home, currently for sale at $1.15 million

-Police released no details on the deceased or cause of death, with the homicide unit handling the case and crime scene tape sealing the property

-The discovery lands in a rough stretch for Harris: a firm where he's a partner is part of the team awarded a nearly $2 BILLION Massachusetts courthouse contract now being challenged in court over alleged conflicts of interest

-Pressley has not been accused of wrongdoing in either matter, and her office hasn't responded to comment requests

The unanswered questions carry the story for now: who the deceased is, how they died, and why they were at a vacant house on the market.
