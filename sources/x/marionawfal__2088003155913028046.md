🇺🇸 Why can’t the U.S. Navy simply get fresh food to its ships?

Matt Bracken explains that the problem isn’t technology but geography. 

With traditional ports in the region unavailable or too dangerous for shore leave, ships operating around the Gulf and Indian Ocean can be weeks from the nearest viable resupply point.

The result: sailors spending extended deployments with little more than canned food and nowhere to go ashore.

“If American sailors were let loose in Karachi, there’s a good chance some of them wouldn’t make it back to the ship.”
