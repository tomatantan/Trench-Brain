A former missile engineer just built a tiny drone that hunts mosquitoes mid-air.

The 40-gram autonomous drone uses ultrasonic pulses and microphones to detect the unique wingbeat signature of mosquitoes, tells them apart from bees, then intercepts them with its propellers.

Malaria still kills over 600,000 people a year and mostly kids under five. The team behind it (YC-backed) thinks a handful of these could clear a whole square kilometre and slash control costs by 100x.

Still early days (public demo was a moth indoors), but this is the kind of wild engineering that could actually matter.
