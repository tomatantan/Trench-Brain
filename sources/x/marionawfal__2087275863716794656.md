🇮🇷 Iran’s IRGC general claimed their missile factories outrun the battlefield.

Mohammad Reza Naqdi says ballistic missiles and drones are rolling off the lines faster than they can fire them, and the stockpile will keep raining down even if fighting lasts years.

Tehran has said versions of this before while rebuilding after heavy losses.

Open source and intelligence estimates put Iran’s recovery phase production at roughly 50-150 ballistic missiles a month after wartime losses and strikes on facilities, with simpler drones higher.

Pre-war stockpiles were already heavily depleted.
