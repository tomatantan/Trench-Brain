🇮🇷 Iran got its military hammered, but the real crack runs straight through the top.

For decades Ali Khamenei was the one who kept the regime's rival factions in line. 

They took him out, and that didn't just cost the regime a figurehead, it cost them the referee.

His son Mojtaba was supposed to fill that hole. 

Instead, nobody outside a tiny circle can even confirm he's alive and actually running things. 

He's never once shown his face or used his voice since taking the job, ducked his own father's funeral, and "speaks" only through written notes that someone else puts out.

The knives are out in the open now. 

The Assembly of Experts, a body that almost never makes a peep, went public slamming the security council over the talks with Washington, basically accusing officials of blowing past the leader's red lines. 

Even Mojtaba hedged, letting it be known he'd been against the deal before he signed off on it, which pretty much everyone read as lining up someone else to blame if it falls apart.

Then the mess spilled right into the war. 

U.S. officials say the ship attacks that reignited the fighting were pulled off by an "errant" hardline faction trying to blow up the deal with Washington, and Tehran quietly admitted the strikes were a screwup.

Back in 1988, Khomeini drank what he called the poisoned chalice and ended the Iran-Iraq war. 

Right now there's nobody with the standing to make a call like that.

You can rebuild a missile site. A leadership that can't even agree with itself is a whole lot harder to fix.
