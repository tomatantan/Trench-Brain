🇺🇦🇷🇺 Ukraine has reportedly offered Russia a Black Sea truce, and Moscow's answer is already looking like no.

Kyiv sent the proposal through a third party, suggesting both sides stop hitting civilian targets in the Black Sea. Russia says nothing formal ever reached it.

The offer comes with grain piling up on the docks. Ukraine's exports have cratered 76% this month as the blockade chokes Odesa. 

Though it cuts both ways, Kyiv hit Russia's Novorossiysk port this week and shut all 3 terminals.

Russian FM Sergey Lavrov said an immediate ceasefire isn't possible and vowed to step up efforts to destroy everything the West supplies to Kyiv. 

A truce, he said, only comes once the war's basically already settled.

Two of the planet's biggest grain suppliers, torching each other's harvest.
