🇦🇷🇬🇧 Argentina players celebrated their World Cup semi-final win over England by holding up a banner reading “The Falklands are Argentine.”

The move has triggered a major diplomatic row. Argentina’s foreign minister lodged a formal protest against a British warship the same night, timing it perfectly after the match.

FIFA now faces calls for disciplinary action. Precedent exists for bans over similar political gestures, though Argentina is unlikely to be thrown out of the final.

The incident highlights how the Falklands dispute still poisons relations decades after the 1982 war.
