🇯🇵 Japanese long-term borrowing costs are climbing fast once more...

The 40-year government bond yield jumped another 10 basis points to 4.01%, closing in on the record set earlier this year. 

30 yields are also near their highest point since those bonds first appeared in 1999.

Higher oil prices and rising U.S. Treasury yields are driving the move, while persistent yen weakness is keeping inflation risks alive.

Bank of Japan officials are now being described as more willing to raise rates faster than markets had expected.

The combination of inflation pressure, potential tighter policy, and long-standing fiscal worries is putting serious strain on the world’s third-largest bond market.
