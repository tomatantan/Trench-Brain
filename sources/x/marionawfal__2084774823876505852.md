🚨🇮🇷 🇺🇸Tehran is signaling real progress on the Hormuz talks, with a deal possibly landing by Wednesday.

-Diplomatic sources in Tehran describe positive indications, though the situation remains fragile and has collapsed at this exact stage before

-The Foreign Ministry spokesman told Iranian state TV that talks are under way to determine a safe trajectory and separate entry and exit lanes for ships to reopen the strait

-His condition is the sticking point in plain language: those routes must fulfill "the sovereign rights and security concerns" of both the Iranian and Omani sides

-Tehran assesses the diplomatic engagement positively on both the technical and political tracks, with talk of an agreement possibly reached by the end of Wednesday

-Diplomatic sources are also flagging "disruptive actors," a nod to the forces that have wrecked every previous version of this

-The framework reported so far gives Iran control of the inbound lane near its coast for a temporary window of up to three months

Notice what the sticking point actually is.

Not the routes, not the timing, not even the money. 

Iran wants the arrangement to formally honor its "sovereign rights" over the water. 

Six months of war, and the fight has come down to whether America signs something that concedes Iran has a legitimate say over who passes.

That's why "temporary" is the word making this possible.
