🇩🇪 Germany staged a huge tunnel fire rescue drill, and thank God it wasn't real, because every single passenger would be DEAD.

More than 250 responders staged a fake ICE train fire inside a 1,320-meter tunnel in Thuringia, with about 30 actors playing passengers who needed rescuing.

The first crews showed up 2 hours and 7 minutes AFTER the emergency call. 

The plan was 10 to 25 minutes.

Turns out the alarm chain broke down badly, with the rescue center told a full 30 minutes late, then the fire brigades another 30 minutes after that.

The public warning apps also failed to reach everyone, all while the state interior minister watched the fiasco unfold in person.

This is definitely NOT what Germany used to be like…
