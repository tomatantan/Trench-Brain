🇮🇷🇺🇸 Iran just declared its deal with the U.S. basically dead.

IRGC commander Mohsen Rezaei told state TV the memorandum with Washington has collapsed, and he laid the blame entirely on the American side.

His list of U.S. violations:

- Israel never pulled out of southern Lebanon
- An illegal shipping route was carved through the Strait of Hormuz next to Iran's legal one
- Iranian territory kept getting hit
- Iran's frozen assets were never released

Then came the accusation that matters. Rezaei says the U.S. only signed to buy time, running war and negotiation at once, and used Iran's two-week ceasefire to quietly finish its naval blockade while both sides talked.

To Iran, the ceasefire was just breathing room for the U.S. to finish the job.
