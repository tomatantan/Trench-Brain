🇺🇸 51% of American adults read zero books last year.

Americans who read for pleasure on any given day fell from 28% in 2004 to 16% in 2023. 

The decline cuts across every age group, gender, and education level. Even retirees, women, and college graduates, historically the heaviest readers, have seen a collapse.

Books themselves are changing to match. New York Times bestsellers today have sentences roughly a third shorter than they were a century ago.

The share of 13-year-olds who said they rarely or never read for fun rose from 8% in 1984 to 29% in 2025.

Fourth and eighth grade reading scores have been sliding for a decade.

For most of history the reading problem was illiteracy. People couldn't read.

The new problem is post-literacy. People can read. They just don't.
