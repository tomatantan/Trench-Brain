Here is how I think about creator tokens.

The real question is not how much existing value a creator can move onchain e.g bringing my Youtube or X payouts on chain.  It is how much net new demand they can create onchain, excluding creator fees 

The two questions are 

How many new users, deposits and customers can creators bring onchain?

And can crypto create new onchain native celebs?

Youtube, Instagram and Tiktok made completely unknown people famous. Onchain platforms need to do the same.

But the token cannot be the entire product. The token should help finance, coordinate and grow a real business or community around the creator.

A streamer could launch a branded neobank built on crypto rails, bringing in new users and deposits, similar to what MrBeast is beginning to do with Step.

Gary Vee / Flyfish Club used NFTs to raise capital and bootstrap a real restaurant and membership club. The NFT was the starting point and now they have a full fledged restaurant and community in NC

A gaming creator could build their own branded game, bring their audience with them and use the token for membership, rewards or in game currency.

Creator tokens should be less like memes and more like infra for turning attention into new products, companies, deposits, customers and eventually new celebs created entirely onchain.

There is still severe execution risk. We have seen many people try and fail. The model probably take off after a few creators build real businesses people continue using e.g how many users/deposits did you bring on chain 

Once you have on chain volume then you can monetize onchain and show why the token should exist in the first place
