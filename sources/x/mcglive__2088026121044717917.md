.@Larpseidon  broke down the incentives to launch a market they're running on @Perpsdotfun .

His thesis: launching your own market flips the usual script, market makers are normally pitched by teams asking for liquidity, but here they can effectively launch their own token or market instead, with 45% of trading fees flowing back to them.

The appeal varies by who's launching. Market makers gain a house edge they don't get trading elsewhere, since they otherwise rely purely on a profitable trading strategy with no fee cut. ETF providers or proprietary data owners get a new distribution channel for their indices, earning fees directly. Crypto teams without a Hyperliquid listing get an alternative path to list their token. Conversations split fairly evenly across these groups, with smaller, niche market makers, often outside major hubs, showing the most interest in bringing new asset classes like equities and currencies on-chain.
