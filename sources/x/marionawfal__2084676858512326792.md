🇮🇷🇪🇺 Iran is seriously considering letting European countries help demine the Strait of Hormuz. A potential breakthrough after months of deadlock.

Bessent said it outright: "I think there is a chance we may have a deal today or tomorrow to open the Strait."

Qatar backs that up; a draft agreement is circulating between the parties right now, aimed at a short-term de-escalation before anything bigger.

The catch: Iran's negotiators reportedly want an arrangement where "Iran is dominant" 

Security, demining, and maritime services all run through Tehran, not shared with outsiders.

Shipping analysts aren't as optimistic as Bessent; they're counting fewer than 10 ships a day moving through, hardly "normalized."

Hormuz has become the gateway to any wider U.S.-Iran deal. If this moves forward, shipping could reopen and talks could restart. 

But nobody wants to say who actually controls the strait once it's signed.
