Why has the gacha model exploded across crypto? 

@allocateur explained the mechanics:

The appeal for players is that it's plus-EV. Pay $25 for a pull, and you're guaranteed a floor (the lowest gacha tier is ~$15 in value) with a real shot at rarer, higher-value cards. If you're an actual collector who keeps playing, you come out ahead on fair-market value over time. And it's transparent, you know exactly how much the house keeps.

The business side: the platform acquires inventory at a discount. If a player sells back at 90%, @ripcarsio gets the card 10% under. If players hold, even better, Rip Cars buys inventory in bulk on the open market at a 20-30% discount because it's early to Hot Wheels.
