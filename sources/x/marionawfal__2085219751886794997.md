🇱🇧 🇮🇱 Two Israeli soldiers were killed by a booby-trapped building in Lebanon, and Israel answered with strikes while peace talks ran in Rome.

-Maj. (res.) Harel Birenstock, 34, and Sgt. Maj. (res.) Tamir Vaknin, 33, died when an explosive detonated as their unit entered a structure in Majdal Zoun around noon Wednesday, with four more reservists seriously wounded

-Both served in the 2855th Battalion of the 55th Paratroopers Brigade, and the IDF says they were there to demolish Hezbollah infrastructure

-They are the first Israeli deaths in Lebanon since late June

-Israel launched what it called "precise strikes" across the south in response to a "blatant violation of the ceasefire" by Hezbollah, again without specifying what the violation was

-A strike on Tibnin hit the roof of a cemetery prayer hall, killing one person and wounding 12, per Lebanon's Health Ministry, which counts at least 4,333 killed and 12,250 wounded since March 2

-The Israeli military issued fresh displacement orders for al-Mansouri residents, telling them to head north

-All of it happened as the second day of U.S.-brokered Israel-Lebanon talks wrapped in Rome, with Hezbollah leader Naim Qassem saying Tuesday the negotiations bring "nothing but shame, humiliation"
