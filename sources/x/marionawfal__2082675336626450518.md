🇮🇱 🇵🇸 Netanyahu's full defense against the genocide accusation rests on the warnings Israel sent:

"We sent millions of text messages, phone calls, and leaflets telling Palestinian civilians to get out of harm's way.

Hamas stopped them from leaving at gunpoint.

Why did we do that if we had genocidal intent?

We value every civilian life.

We've incurred far fewer civilians to combatants lost than just about any urban warfare in modern times."

The warnings were real, and Hamas embedding in civilian areas is documented.

But the argument has a hole the evacuation orders themselves created: warning people to flee only clears you if somewhere safe existed to flee to.

Strikes repeatedly targeted the designated zones, and by the end, the orders were forcing the same displaced population to move back and forth across a territory that offered no exit, with progressively fewer places to seek refuge.
