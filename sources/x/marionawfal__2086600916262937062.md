🇺🇸 New Orleans is now letting a chatbot answer 911, and the dispatchers are losing it.

Since late July, an AI decides which emergency calls reach a human and which the bot handles alone. 

Officials swear it's limited and that nobody's being replaced. The people who've worked those phones still think it's insane.

A dispatcher of 11 years called it the worst idea imaginable, because the calls that matter most are the ones a bot can't read. 

The caller who can't speak. The thick accent it mangles. The domestic victim "ordering a pizza" because saying the real words gets them hurt. 

A human picks up on that. A bot just books the delivery.

Seattle quietly ran similar tech for two years and told nobody. New Orleans just handed the machine the phone.
