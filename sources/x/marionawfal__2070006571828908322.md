🇮🇷 Iran claims Hormuz as its own waters, and the U.S. has no real standing to argue.

Maritime analyst Sal Mercogliano breaks down the legal mess underneath the standoff. 

International law guarantees "transit passage" through straits like Hormuz, but Iran disputes that, claiming the waterway as its own territorial waters.

The catch? Iran never signed the convention, and neither did the United States, which leaves Washington in a weak spot to enforce international transit norms.

Everyone reaches for Suez or Panama as comparisons, but Mercogliano says the real parallel is the English Channel, a 21-mile international waterway where neither bordering nation holds exclusive control.

The open question: can this standoff even reach that point?
