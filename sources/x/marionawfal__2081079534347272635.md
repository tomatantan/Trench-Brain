🇺🇸 Chris Martenson tells Tucker the "energy independent" line doesn't check out. 

The U.S. is a net exporter of total petroleum, but strip out propane, butane and other gas liquids, and it still imports more crude oil than it sends out. 

U.S. refineries are built for heavier foreign crude, so the barrels keep coming in.

The “energy independent” talking point sounds stronger than the crude oil reality.
