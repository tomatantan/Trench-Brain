Gian Sacco explains why crypto exchanges stay regional, and how VALR beat the biggest exchange in the world in its own backyard:

"The similar tech isn't the hard part. In our part of the world, it's the ability to engage directly with regulators and build the trust to run a regulated exchange, and to operate stable fiat on and off ramps."

"When we started in South Africa, there was already a dominant player, and Binance entered right after us. But a big brand and a lot of capital doesn't mean you can set up the bank rails or actually serve customers' real needs."

"We have the best on and off ramps in sub-Saharan Africa. That's how we beat away the largest exchange in the world."
