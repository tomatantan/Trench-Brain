🇲🇱 The UN says dozens of Malian soldiers were killed after they had already surrendered.

OHCHR points to the Azawad Liberation Front and JNIM for what happened in the Gao region on 18 July. The video going around shows captured soldiers shot with their hands behind their heads.

Two very different agendas, and they keep turning up in the same operations.

Here's the part nobody talks about: the FLA is still not designated a terrorist organization anywhere.
