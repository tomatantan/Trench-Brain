🚨🇺🇸 🇮🇷 The U.S. military is buying 1990-era Patriot interceptors again, its first domestic order of them in more than THIRTY years...

-Raytheon says the Pentagon has ordered PAC-2 series GEM-T interceptors, the first American purchase of the type in over three decades, per its Q2 earnings call

-CEO Christopher Calio told investors the company booked nearly $20 billion in awards, including over $5 billion of GEM-T Patriot effectors driven by international buyers, plus "our first domestic GEM-T production order in over 30 years"

-The order appears to trace to a $441.6 million Army contract awarded in April, described as supporting Operation Epic Fury against Iran

-The PAC-2 debuted in 1990 and was long ago superseded by the hit-to-kill PAC-3, with GEM-T the only PAC-2 variant still in production

-Global Patriot demand is at a peak, driven by both the Middle East war and Ukraine

Reaching back to a Reagan-era design is a huge inventory admission.

Nobody bought this missile for thirty years because the PAC-3 is simply better, so restarting the line comes down to volume: interceptors are going out faster than the premium ones can be built, right as reports say the Pentagon is hiding missile shortages from the White House.

12 nights of strikes and the magazine is being restocked with WHATEVER the factories can still produce.
