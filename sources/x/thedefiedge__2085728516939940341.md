Most people have an emergency contact for their health, their bank, and even their home.

But almost nobody has one for their crypto.

The Seed Phrase Museum has documented more than $7.2B in crypto that’s been lost forever and very little of it was due to hacks. 

More often, it comes down to forgotten passwords, inaccessible devices, lost backups, or someone passing away without leaving a way for their family to recover their assets.

It’s something I think about more than I probably should.

You can spend years building a portfolio, but if something happens to you, the people you leave behind are often left trying to piece together wallets, devices, passwords, and seed phrases they were never meant to understand.

That’s why I like what @bronwallet is doing with Digital Inheritance.

It lets you nominate beneficiaries in advance while keeping your assets and portfolio private until access is actually needed.

Because securing your crypto is only part of the job.
Making sure it doesn’t disappear with you is the other half.

Glad to be partnering with @bronwallet on this.
