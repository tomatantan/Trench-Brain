🇲🇦🇪🇸 Morocco’s Interior Ministry has given its "official version" of the mass migrant crossings into Ceuta and Melilla.

According to them, *only* 40,000 people attempted to enter the Spanish enclave of Ceuta. Another 1,135 tried to reach Melilla.

The ministry is blaming two main factors: heavy misinformation spreading on social media and the work of human smugglers. 

It also says a recent Spanish court ruling that restricted rapid returns of people intercepted at sea encouraged more migrants to try their luck.
