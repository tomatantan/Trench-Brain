If you're building in a competitive market and aren't trying every product in your category, I don't know what to tell you.

You should ignore competitors, not their products. On a small scale it could be UX. Maybe your product takes five steps and five minutes to sign up while your competitor's product takes a minute.

On a larger scale, it could be Zuck copying Spiegel's Stories feature or X copying Clubhouse's Spaces feature. Those were likely medium sized lifts, but enough to prevent competitors from eating away at their market share.

Using competing products gives you a birds eye view of how others might see your own product. It can show blind spots and can help you with positioning

And if someone has already solved a problem then just copy it. It'll save you time and potentially save you from a future incumbent
