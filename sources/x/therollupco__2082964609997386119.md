Eleanor Terrett lays out the Clarity Act vote-count reality:

"It's everything happening all at once, but it feels like hurry up and wait."

"August recess starts next week, and there's very little to suggest they have the votes to get it across the finish line. Though it's changing by the minute."

"It comes down to 60 votes to invoke cloture. You need about 10 Democrats to get there. If you don't have those, you don't have a bill."

"And it's not one quick vote. Cloture, then motion to proceed, then amendments, with 30-hour windows in between. There's really very little time."
