🇬🇧 If you live in the UK, might want to make sure your doors are locked...

70,000+ prisoners in England and Wales were released from prison under the Labour government's early release scheme to tackle overcrowding.

New PM Andy Burnham THANKFULLY announced a pause to the plan, which was introduced in 2024 under Starmer. But many are already on the streets...

Under the plan, prisoners eligible for release after 50% of their sentence would be slashed to just 40% of it.

For certain violent and sexual offenders, the time-served would have been slashed to 50%, from 75%.

Burnham says no further prisoners will be released under this scheme until they've conducted a thorough review to "minimize risk to the public."

What's the point of a sentence if everyone just serves 40-50% of it??
