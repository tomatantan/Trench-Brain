🇨🇴 Colombia’s army just captured a FARC dissident commander known as “Menor” as armed groups intensify attacks across the country.

The 29th Brigade detained the mid-level leader of the Dagoberto Ramos structure during a raid earlier this month.

At the same time, one of the largest rebel organizations claimed a vehicle-mounted assault on police compounds in a border city that wounded more than ten officers and destroyed dormitories, vehicles and nearby businesses.

Security forces have responded by flooding the same region with extra troops, armor and checkpoints.

A separate operation farther south netted five members of another criminal network along with weapons and vehicles.

A fresh strike on a rural police station overnight shows the violence is still spreading.
