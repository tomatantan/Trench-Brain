🇬🇧🇺🇸 The new UK government is already facing pressure from Washington.

Trump is pushing the UK's new Prime Minister, Andy Burnham, to maintain U.S. access to RAF Fairford and the Diego Garcia military base as the war with Iran escalates.

The previous government initially refused to let the U.S. use Diego Garcia for B-2 bomber missions before later allowing its use for operations described as degrading Iran's military capabilities.

With Burnham being the 7th PM in a decade and promising to deliver the "biggest changes in 40 years," the U.S-UK relationship will be worth keeping a close eye on.

What happens to this arrangement will be very telling.
