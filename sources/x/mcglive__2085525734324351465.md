.@0xprogrammable's revenue model breaks down into three clean buckets...

@hazarxyz walked through the full structure:

The main token generates 1% swap fees, which funds ongoing development, team hiring, and legal setup and there is money already banked well enough to survive three months with zero volume.

The Programmable platform itself directs half of its fees toward buying back the main token ($V4), with the other half slated for holder dividends, weighted by amount held and possibly duration. Rather than burning bought-back tokens, they're adding proceeds into the liquidity pool instead therefore, deepening liquidity and improving sell-side stability rather than just reducing supply, since a thin pool makes any token overly sensitive regardless of burns.

The buyback contract was postedyesterday. Weekly snapshot-based dividends are the next mechanism being built out, with the goal of keeping everything fully onchain and verifiable rather than requiring trust in the team.
