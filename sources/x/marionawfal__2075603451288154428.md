🇺🇸 Meta's new AI image tool (Muse Image) could end up being remembered as more than just another AI feature. 

If you have a public Instagram account, your photos can be used to generate AI images of you by default unless you actively opt out, and most people probably don't even know that's now the case.

Right now, it's being sold as a fun creative tool. 

But AI is advancing so quickly that it's not hard to imagine a future where realistic, non-consensual deepfake pornography becomes cheap, instant, and almost impossible to contain, and this tool could be a huge part of that picture.  

When one of the world's biggest libraries of personal photos is opted in by default, that isn't just a tech story; it's a cultural one.

For years, we've seen aggressive attempts to change sexual norms, from hookup culture to the growing push of LGBTQ ideology, to even disputes over what kinds of sex education are appropriate for young children. 

Whatever your view on those debates, AI has the potential to add an entirely new dimension by making it far easier to create and spread fabricated sexualized images without the consent of those depicted. 

If that happens, we may look back on this as part of another sexual revolution: one driven not just by changing attitudes, but by technology that erodes privacy, weakens boundaries, and ultimately makes society even more sexually permissive and liberal.
