🇺🇸🇮🇷 New data suggests Iran has been tracking the phones of U.S. military personnel across the Middle East.

Researchers spotted a wave of signals flooding networks in Gulf nations like Bahrain at the start of the war in February, in what one called a coordinated attack campaign.

Iran reportedly exploited SS7, a clunky telecom protocol from the 1970s, to pull location data on phones connected to local networks, which U.S. troops sometimes use.

That's a real jump in Iran's cyber game, and a genuine danger for troops sitting within range of Iranian missiles.
