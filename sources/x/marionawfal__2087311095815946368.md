🇺🇸 Elon looked back at how SpaceX actually started

It was a small warehouse in El Segundo and a team that openly admitted they had no idea how to build rockets at first. 

They blew up three of them before finally reaching orbit.

Landing an orbital booster and flying it again was even harder. 

Experts told him it couldn’t be done. And even if it worked, no one would buy a used rocket.

His response: “I’d rather be on the thousandth flight of an aircraft than its first flight.”

They started calling them flight-proven boosters. Now they land and recover one every few days.
