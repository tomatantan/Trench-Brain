🇪🇸🇮🇹 Italy has reintroduced border checks on arrivals from Spain...

Meloni’s government ordered the move after tens of thousands of migrants flooded the Spanish enclave of Ceuta a few days ago.

Beginning yesterday, police at major Italian ports and airports began random checks on people coming in by air or sea. 

EU citizens only need to show ID. Non-EU travelers must ALSO produce valid residence permits or visas.

The controls are temporary, lasting a month for now, with focus on monitoring non-EU movement inside the Schengen area. 

Italy calls it a precautionary step allowed under existing Schengen rules, when public order or security is at risk.

The decision has sparked a sharp political row. 

Spain’s Sánchez has written to EU leaders asking Brussels to step in. Meloni and other EU leaders are pushing for tougher joint action on irregular migration and external borders.

The European Commission says most of the migrants who entered Ceuta have already been sent back, and that none have reached mainland Spain or other EU countries thus far.
