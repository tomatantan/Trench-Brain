→ Don't fake engagement. The algorithm is smarter than you think, and your real engagement rate drives distribution.

→ Do what others won't. Video, tutorials, threads, custom infographics, high-effort content is a bigger moat than short tweets.

→ Funnel from other platforms. Most people only push X to YouTube. Almost nobody pushes YouTube/TikTok/IG traffic back to X.

→ Spread your posts out. Let each tweet build its own momentum instead of competing with your last one.

→ Topic and hook are everything. No hook, no virality, no matter how good the value is.

→ You don't need to be an expert to start. Treat X as a public journal early on. Your beginner's perspective is an asset - not a weakness.

→ Use AI as a research assistant and editor, not a ghostwriter. Keep it things sounding like you and develop a unique brand voice.

→ Don't sweat the small stuff early. Post consistently, improve as you go.

→ Easy daily 30-minute starter routine: 10 min replying, 10 min short tweets, 10 min threads/long-form.
