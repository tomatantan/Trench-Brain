🇺🇸 Lindsey Graham's sudden death was the result of a fatal tear in his aorta stemming from chronic heart disease.

But in the golden age of conspiracies, this could become one of the biggest we've seen in years.

Given his foreign policy reputation, and the fact that he died just after returning from Kyiv, assassination theories have been running rampant.

Even if toxicology and forensic testing point to a natural death, that often doesn't settle the debate.

America, especially, has reached a point where official findings often end the science, but not the argument.

That is what makes this kind of tragedy so politically dangerous:

If people became convinced that Graham's death was the work of Russia, or Iran, or some other nefarious entity, they might demand a response.

I don't buy into this particular conspiracy, but I do worry about others who might...
