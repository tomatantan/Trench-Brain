🇮🇷 A well-known photojournalist just got 15 years in an Iranian prison, and nobody should be shocked.

Yalda Moaiery has spent 20 years shooting wars and protests, and Iran's jailed her before, during the 2022 Mahsa Amini demonstrations. This time the court reportedly sentenced her last month without her even in the room.

The charge? Acting in the interests of Israel and the United States.

Maybe she's tied to Israel, maybe not. Who's going to confirm it? This is a regime that jails and executes people on its own say-so, no real evidence needed.

She's not alone either. Rights groups say Iran went after at least 225 journalists or outlets in 2025, with 25 handed a combined 30-plus years. 

No way to prove those numbers either way, but given Iran's track record, they're not hard to believe.
