🇮🇷 Iran found two ways to make the same missile hit harder, and neither one needed a bigger warhead

Everyone ranks these weapons by explosive weight. That number means less than people think.

Start with speed, since kinetic energy scales with the SQUARE of velocity. 

A 500 kg warhead arriving at 2 km per second already carries energy worth about 239 kg of TNT before anything detonates. 

Push it to 4 km per second and the figure jumps to roughly 956 kg. 

Same mass, four times the arrival energy.

Then look at what Iran did in the war's opening weeks. 

An Israeli official said ABOUT HALF the ballistic missiles fired at Israel carried cluster warheads, splitting at 7 to 10 km and scattering as many as 80 bomblets across a radius of 8 km. 

Once they open, interception is over.

One missile becomes dozens of impact points.
