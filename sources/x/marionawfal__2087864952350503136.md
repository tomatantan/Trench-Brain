🚨🇷🇺🇯🇵 Japan is FURIOUS with Russia, after Putin just stepped foot in territory that they claim...

Putin just visited the disputed Kuril islands for the first time, a Russian-controlled Pacific island chain also claimed by Japan.

Japan asserts territorial rights to the four southernmost islands of the chain, which it calls the Northern Territories.

The USSR took them from Japan in the last days of WWII.

Moscow has stepped up its military presence in recent years on the islands, a clear indication of their stance.

Japan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs put out a statement calling the islands "an inherent part of the Japanese territory" and saying it "strongly protests" the visit.

Fmr. Russian President Dmitry Medvedev hit back, saying Japan's PM Takaichi "can prattle on all she likes about the islands being 'historically Japanese,' but this chatter won't change a thing.

They were, are, and will remain Russian land. Anyone who fails to understand this will face the monstrous consequences of their delusion."
