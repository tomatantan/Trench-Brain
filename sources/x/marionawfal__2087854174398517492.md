🇪🇺 Europe's gas storage is just 59% full heading into winter, the lowest for this time of year on records going back to 2009.

The culprit is the Iran war. With the Strait of Hormuz choked off, roughly a fifth of the world's LNG isn't going anywhere, and buyers are elbowing each other for whatever cargoes are left. 

Qatar, normally the world's second-biggest LNG supplier, has basically been benched.

Prices have already nearly doubled from a year ago, and it's not even cold yet.

Countries like Italy and France are paying to get their tanks filled. 

Germany, whose reserves are already lagging behind them, is refusing to step in and betting private traders sort it out in time.  

The country runs on gas heat, so if they’re wrong things could get ugly, and cold.
