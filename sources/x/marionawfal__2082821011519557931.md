🇬🇧 British police spent at least £631 million on diversity and inclusion since 2020, and an influential think tank says the obsession is getting in the way of actual policing.

Policy Exchange argues some forces have put DEI ideology ahead of the basic rule that everyone should be treated equally under the law.

Its report points to failures around the Nottingham stabbings, the Southport murders and the death of Henry Nowak (jury inquest due in 2027).

Half a billion pounds to teach officers about privilege, and somewhere along the way they forgot the main job was catching criminals.
