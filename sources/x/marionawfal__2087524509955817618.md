🇮🇷 Iran is set to join the BRICS New Development Bank.

Dr. Abdolnaser Hemmati, Governor of the Central Bank of Iran, said membership will be finalized "soon," ahead of a BRICS finance meeting in India.

The move would give Tehran a new channel for development funding outside the Western-dominated system. 

Iran has been under HEAVY U.S. and international sanctions, but became a full BRICS member in 2024.

It has since pushed for a shareholding stake in the bank, created by Brazil, Russia, India, China, and South Africa.
