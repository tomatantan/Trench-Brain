"I'm talking to founders like, things look really good for you, what's going on, and it's like, yeah we have users, we just don't make any money on them"

Offchain CEO Steven Goldfeder on why having users is no longer enough

"We have a strong ecosystem team tracking projects. There's a lot, so not every project, but certainly a bunch. We're seeing massive consolidation everywhere. What I track probably more than that is one level up, Arbitrum versus its competitors, where we've also seen massive consolidation. I think it's healthy, probably a good thing ultimately"

"There was so much capital in crypto, and the idea was you don't have to think about revenues, just put this thing out, make it open source, get people to use it, launch a token, and that's going to save everyone. I think we're coming very much back to reality. Some of the projects we're seeing shut down, I'm talking to founders like, things look really good for you, what's going on, and it's like, yeah we have users, we just don't make any money on them"

"Back in the day having users was sufficient for the token price, your users would buy the token, that was how they paid you. Those times are well gone. Now revenue is actually important, and ultimately I think it's healthy"
