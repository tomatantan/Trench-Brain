🇬🇧 Britain just tossed another £130 million at getting rid of petrol cars.

The government's putting up nearly £130 million ($175 million) to juice up electric vehicle tech. 

The mission's pretty simple: no new petrol or diesel motors on sale from 2030, and everything fully electric by 2035.

The only catch is whether Britain can build enough chargers to keep up. So far, not looking great.
