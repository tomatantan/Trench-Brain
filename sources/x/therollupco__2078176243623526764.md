Rob picked up Killa XBT's bearish SOL post and didn't soften it: the run from $7 to $300 wasn't organic demand, it was a supply story.

His read on the structural case: unconstrained supply, tokens that can be printed through inflation, and VCs sitting on unlocks they'll eventually sell into the market. 

On top of that, he thinks the Solana meme-coin trenches left a stain the ecosystem now has to overcome. 

Pump is printing revenue while its token languishes. Solana's next act is riding on tokenized equities working.

But that's where he flips bullish on the category, not the coin. Equities moving onchain is a one-way train with enormous room to run. 

He just thinks healthier, more fully-diluted supply models, and points to Hyperliquid and NEAR, are the better place to be.
