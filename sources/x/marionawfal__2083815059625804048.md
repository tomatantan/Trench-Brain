🇺🇦🇷🇺 Whatever Zelensky says about Wildberries distribution centers being dual-use, it's clear from the scale of this campaign that Kyiv is striking them to put pressure on ordinary Russians

At least 14 of the warehouses have been hit so far, and this morning saw another in Novosemeykino go up in smoke.

Wildberries is Russia's largest e-commerce business, and mostly ships clothing, cosmetics, electronics, home goods, etc. The kind of stuff you'd expect to see on Amazon.

Zelensky is justifying the strikes by saying that the logistics centers were used to supply the Russian military with sanctioned components for drone production, navigation equipment, and other military hardware.

That may be true, it's hard to tell in the fog of war, but if you want to hit military supply chains, Russia has far more useful targets than blowing up warehouses of pet food and cheap clothes.

If however, you want to make ordinary Russians feel the effects of the war, you hit the companies supplying them with essential goods.

The problem is that you can't hit civilian facilities without being accused of war crimes, so the logic is to find some reason you can call it a dual-use facility.

It's the same playbook that Israel used in Gaza, the U.S used in Iran, and Russia uses in Ukraine, so you can't blame them for doing the same.
