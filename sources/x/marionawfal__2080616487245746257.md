🇮🇶🇸🇾🇺🇸 The U.S. is now importing Iraqi fuel oil that traveled by truck through Syria and out the Mediterranean, the first shipments of their kind ever.

With Hormuz effectively shut for months, Baghdad built a workaround: roughly 900 tanker trucks a day rolling across the border to Syria's port of Baniyas, loading a tanker every 7 to 10 days.

3 Aframax tankers made the run between June and July, carrying about 1.4 million barrels combined toward the U.S. Gulf Coast.

A Baniyas engineer said Syria hasn't exported crude or petroleum products in years apart from a single 2025 shipment, and everything moving now is Iraqi.

Iraq says it plans to keep the Syrian corridor open even after Hormuz normalizes.

A route built out of desperation is turning into a permanent hedge.
