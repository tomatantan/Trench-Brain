🇮🇷🇴🇲🇺🇸 Iranian FM Araghchi says talks with Oman on a new transit route are close to an agreement, but stresses it does not mean the Strait of Hormuz will reopen.

He blames the current situation on the U.S. violating an MOU and demands full remedies.

“We will not tolerate violations... nor will we tolerate Iran’s sovereignty over the Strait of Hormuz being challenged.”

This move looks like a pragmatic hedge by the more diplomatic side of the regime, building a backup route with Oman while the IRGC and hardliners maintain their tough stance on Hormuz control and keep military pressure options open.

The hardliners have been vocal about not ceding any sovereignty in the strait and using it as leverage. That looks contradictory, but actually makes a solid foreign policy. It worked so far.
