"Sky functions more like a central bank, and the sub-DAOs like Spark function more like a commercial bank"

Spark CEO Sam Macpherson on how liquidity flows from Sky down to sub-DAOs like Spark

"The way things sit is that Sky functions more like a central bank and wholesale liquidity issuer. So the sub-DAOs, Spark being one, function more like a commercial bank that can draw on the liquidity reserves of Sky and deploy it into commercial lending activities"

"Primarily this is a mix between real world assets, to get the base yield usually from treasury bills, and crypto over collateralized lending, primarily like Bitcoin and ETH backed loans"
