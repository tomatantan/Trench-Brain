You can now describe a spaceship to Grok and watch it build in Blender.

Not just a rough shape. Grok writes the Python, creates the geometry, materials and rigging, checks its own viewport renders, then keeps fixing what looks wrong.

One demo ended with a fully rigged 86,000-triangle spaceship from text alone.

That turns 3D modeling from “learn every tool first” into “start with the idea and refine from there.”

The default cube should be nervous.
