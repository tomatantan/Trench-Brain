🇸🇦🇾🇪 Saudi hit the brakes hard on the Houthi fight.

After the Yemeni group blocked their ports and hit oil facilities, everyone expected bigger retaliation.

Instead Saudi paused the airstrikes after only two days and quietly restarted talks through Oman.

They’re walking a tightrope. Their economy already shrank almost 5% last quarter because of the wider Iran war and oil export chaos.

Another full scale campaign against the Houthis would wreck what’s left of their Red Sea shipping routes and suck them deeper into a regional mess they’ve spent years trying to stay out of.

The Houthis know this, and that’s why they’re pushing for money and open access as the price of calm.

The U.S. is also telling Saudi to keep it cool, nobody wants Bab el-Mandeb closed on top of Hormuz.

Saudi is betting that limited pressure plus quiet diplomacy can contain the fire without burning their own house down.

It’s a high stakes gamble, and the whole energy market is watching to see if it holds.
