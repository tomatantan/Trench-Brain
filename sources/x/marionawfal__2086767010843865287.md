🇷🇸 Serbia's President Vučić just called out his fellow Europeans for enjoying life too much while America and China pull ahead.

"As Europeans, we are lagging behind. We are lagging behind because we work less than Chinese and Americans."

He pointed to Americans working longer hours for less, still hungry to "deliver the very best results," and said China runs much the same way.

Europe, by contrast, is busy enjoying life. 2 vacations a year, 15 city breaks, and a vague hope that somehow it won't fall behind.

His answer is simple: work harder.

Europeans will surely consider it when they go back to the office around September 😆
