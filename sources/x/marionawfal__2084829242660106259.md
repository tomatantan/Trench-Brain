🇱🇾 Another militia shootout lit up western Libya.

Rival armed groups, the Anti Security Threats Apparatus side and the 103rd Infantry Battalion, clashed hard along the coastal road between Zawiya and Surman early this morning.

At least 5 people are reported dead so far.

The main highway got shut down. Fighting spilled into residential neighborhoods, shells hit homes and a scrapyard, and prisoners even escaped from Surman’s jail in the chaos.

Local Red Crescent teams are on full alert.

Same old story in that stretch west of Tripoli when 2 government recognized militias turning the coastal road into a battlefield over turf.
