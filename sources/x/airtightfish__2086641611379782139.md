Fomo isn't a good example here because the social element. 1) strong network effect 2) Your following there is worth something, so you are invested in that way. 

That being said it surfaces some interesting ideas around tokens. 

In a world where proper tokens are expected (not there yet), and all else is equal, it probably becomes a headwind to not launch a token. 

The key part there is "all else equal" product and execution matter WAY more. 

If anything historically people have leaned to hard into tokens, and the games associated with them, instead of product. This is a result of where incentives have previously existed in this industry for founders. Tokens have allowed quicker growth and quicker exits, even when fundamentals weren't there. 

The market doesn't support this anymore. 

This also varies depending on where are in the cycle. For example the ability to raise capital matters for growth. An early token, or fixed plans for one may be a headwind in the current environment. 5 years ago that wasn't true. 

Just some musings, lots of nuance here. There are definitely some counterexamples.

As a crypto native I want every revenue generating protocol to have a token. As a founder I recognize why many teams don't prioritize it.
