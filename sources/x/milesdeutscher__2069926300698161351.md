6 years ago, I had my head buried in a textbook, studying a uni degree I didn’t really enjoy.

On a whim, I created a Twitter account and started posting about financial markets and technology I found interesting.

I didn't know it at the time, but that single decision would change my life forever.

Before I knew it, I was on a plane to South Africa to join one of the world's biggest media companies (CryptoBanter).

And just a couple of years later, I was in Dubai, building businesses with some of the most talented entrepreneurs on the planet.

Fast forward to 2026, and I'm now working on my ultimate passion project:

Teaching people how to use the most transformative technology ever created (AI).

I never could've planned any of this.

What started as a 20-year-old college kid tweeting in his parents' house eventually turned into something much bigger.

If I could give one piece of advice to aspiring entrepreneurs, it would be this:

Just move. Just start. Take action.

You'll never have the perfect plan.

And in fact, most great things are impossible to plan.

The only way to create a "perfect plan" is to just try things and stumble down a path.

Make it your mission to be someone who just takes action.

I guarantee you'll shock yourself with where you end up.
