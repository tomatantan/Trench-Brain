🇳🇿🇨🇳 Pacific nations are at each other’s throats over China’s missile splash in their backyard.

New Zealand is pushing hard for a collective statement condemning the July test, which sent a dummy warhead into waters near Tuvalu and Kiribati with almost no warning and landed inside the region’s long-standing nuclear-free zone.

Several other Forum members remain reluctant, wary of upsetting their relationships with Beijing.

The much-promoted “Ocean of Peace” vision is now facing its clearest test yet.

Some governments want a firm regional line against outside military activity, while others prefer to avoid confrontation.

The outcome in Suva (Fiji capital) will show whether the Pacific can still speak with one voice or if the cracks have already become permanent.
