🚨🇺🇸 🇮🇷 Washington says any ceasefire has to start with Iran, and warns there will be "a price to pay" for more attacks.

-A U.S. official tells the NY Post that Trump will give talks a chance but wants Tehran to move first: "That's the basis for any type of negotiation; it starts with a cease-fire, and it has to start with Iran"

-The official points at Iran's internal split as the real obstacle, with Pezeshkian and Araghchi working the diplomatic channels while the IRGC keeps launching strikes and demanding control of the strait: "It's incumbent on the Iranian leadership to get their house in order"

-The warning attached: if Iran keeps "attacking ships, attacking US bases, attacking Gulf allies, then there will be a price to pay for that"

-Talks are still running through technical teams and mediators, aiming at what the official describes as a Memorandum of Understanding 2.0, keeping some pieces of the first deal

-Trump's red line in those talks is money: the strait stays toll-free, which puts him directly against what Iran and Oman have been discussing

-The IRGC is not backing off, saying it "will punish the aggressor" and that countries helping the U.S. would also "receive a harsh response," per Iranian state media

Washington is asking Tehran to deliver something Tehran may not be able to deliver.

Telling the civilian leadership to get its house in order assumes the house has a landlord, and the IRGC has spent a month proving otherwise, launching strikes during pauses the negotiators arranged.

And MoU 2.0 runs straight back into the rock that broke MoU 1.0.

Trump says TOLL-FREE, as Iran and Oman are working out what to CHARGE, and neither side has found a word that lets both claim victory over the same water.
