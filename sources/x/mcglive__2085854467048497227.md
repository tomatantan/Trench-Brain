.@JustSparkIdeas built a real incentive layer so good ideas actually make it to market...

@Mathis_btc walked through the mechanics:

The trading fee structure directly rewards the person who brings an idea forward. Of a 2.5% fee, 10% goes to the "editor"...whoever surfaces and pushes the idea live, even if it started as just a tweet. If that idea gains traction, the originator earns a share of ongoing trading fees, with the remaining split feeding back into treasury for investors and builders. Notably, Spark takes no cut on the raise itself so if founders don't deliver, nobody gets paid, protecting investors by design.

The model is a small team: three people total, with roles split across technical build and marketing/community.

Beyond incentives, Spark stays hands-on post-launch working closely with founders to actually help projects succeed, not just deploying tokens and walking away.
