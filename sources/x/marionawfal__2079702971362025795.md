🇺🇸 🇳🇮 A resurfaced clip shows a young Bernie Sanders praising Ortega as "an impressive guy," the same day Nicaragua's dictator abolished elections entirely.

-In the vintage interview, Sanders describes his trip to Sandinista Nicaragua: "As a socialist, the word socialism does not frighten me, and I think it's probably fair to say that the Nicaraguan government is primarily a socialist government"

-His read on the regime's leaders: "I was impressed... Ortega is an impressive guy"

-Sanders recounts personally ADVISING Ortega on media strategy: "My point to Ortega is they are not getting their message of what they are trying to do out to the American people," noting the Sandinistas had hired a U.S. public relations firm

-He also defended Castro in the same breath: "He educated their kids, gave them health care, totally transformed the society"

-The clip recirculates as Ortega, now 80 and ruling since 2007 with his wife as "co-president," declares Nicaragua will never hold elections again: "never again"
