Does cope kill more traders than bad calls do?!

@sonder_crypto gave us his trader psychology thesis:

"The cope is really strong in a lot of people, and it was in me too. You have to train it out of you because you have to be able to buy things higher."

The Ansem example: he saw it at $80M and thought $100M was the top. But $100M is never the top when the volume is real. That's just where everyone SELLS.

His training exercise: when you feel that pit in your stomach on a red candle, write it down. Why do you feel this way? What do you do next?

Then bid the shit out of it...
