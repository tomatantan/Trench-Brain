🇺🇸🇮🇷 Oil jumped hard as markets closed

Brent crude finished the day at $91, up more than 4% in a sharp late move.

The chart shows the climb accelerating right into the close.

Talk of U.S. and Israeli strikes on Iranian energy infrastructure over the weekend is already circulating.

Any hit on power plants or refineries, or further disruption around the Strait of Hormuz, feeds straight into the price.

Shipping risk, supply fears, and the chance of a broader energy squeeze are all priced in real time.

The market is treating the next 48–72 hours as live.

If the strikes materialize, or even if the threat stays elevated, $91 may not be the top of the move.

Energy has become another front in the same conflict.
