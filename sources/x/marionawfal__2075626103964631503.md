🇺🇸🇮🇷 U.S. says talks are still on, but with strings

At an emergency meeting of the UN Security Council, the UN's main group for dealing with war and peace, deputy U.S. envoy Tammy Bruce said the door to talks with Iran is still open, it just comes with conditions now.

Her terms:

▪️ No talks while Iran keeps breaking the basic rules
▪️ Stop firing on civilian ships and targets
▪️ Shoot at those, and the U.S. shoots back

She said Trump would rather have peace, but accused Iran of breaking international law and the council's own orders, and had a go at the council too for stalling instead of doing something.

A carrot in one hand, a stick in the other, that's the whole U.S. approach to Iran right now.
