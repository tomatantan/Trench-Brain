🇺🇸 Two weeks from news story to federal subpoena, and back again.

The New York Times reports Trump left Turkey on the old Air Force One because the Qatari-donated jet lacked antimissile defences.

July 10: U.S. Attorney Jay Clayton, Trump's pick to run national intelligence, subpoenas the journalists to find out who talked.

The Times calls it harassment, and points out subpoenaing reporters is supposed to be rare and signed off at the very top.

Today: a judge is told the subpoenas are being withdrawn.

The reporting hasn't been retracted, corrected, or denied at any point in that sequence.
