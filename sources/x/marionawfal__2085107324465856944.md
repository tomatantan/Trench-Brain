🇦🇷 Lisandro Martínez just drew a hard line on Argentina’s land law reform.

The football defender posted a blunt message: Argentine lands are not for sale.

He is rejecting a government proposal that would scrap the current limits on how much rural land foreigners can own. 

Supporters say it will attract investment. Critics say it puts the country up for sale.

Martínez shared an Amnesty International video calling the defense of land a human rights issue. 

He is so far the only World Cup champion to speak out as artists and activists prepare a march on Congress.
