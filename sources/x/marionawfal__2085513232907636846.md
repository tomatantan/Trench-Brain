🇾🇪 Houthi missiles and drones struck government camps in Marib and Hadramout, killing at least 30 Yemeni troops.

This is the sharpest break in the informal ceasefire that has held since 2022.

The Houthis say they responded to a Saudi-aligned buildup.

The targets sit in oil-rich provinces that have long been strategic prizes.

The Houthis remain linked to Iran and have spent recent years hitting Red Sea shipping, forcing costly international responses.

Saudi has shifted toward containment but still backs the government side.

A return to open fighting risks pulling Riyadh back in while giving Tehran another pressure point.

Yemen’s war never fully ended. It froze.

Neither side can force a decisive win, so flare-ups like this one serve as signals.

With oil fields and key shipping lanes nearby, even limited strikes carry real weight.
