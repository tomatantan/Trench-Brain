🇺🇸 Markets flashed one of the rarest warning signs in nearly 20 years.

Traders have piled into a record 967,136 open contracts in the August federal funds futures, more than double the positioning from mid-June. 

That’s higher than the previous peak during the October 2024 uncertainty spike.

Right now the market is pricing only a 36% chance of a rate hike today.

Since the 2008 crisis, the Fed has held 135 FOMC meetings. Only 4 of them, just 3%, ever entered decision day with odds stuck in that murky 33–66% zone.

This level of division almost never happens.

It means investors are split down the middle on whether the Fed will hold or hike… and they believe whichever way it goes could instantly rewrite the entire outlook for inflation, growth, and the path of rates.

When positioning gets this extreme and the odds this evenly split, the room for surprise, and for violent market moves, is massive.
