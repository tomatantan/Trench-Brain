🇮🇷 Iran's President Pezeshkian: The country's diplomats "will never compromise on the rights of the Iranian nation."

He cast the war as an economic siege meant to trigger "internal pressure and social collapse," and gave the credit to Iran's producers for keeping the country from shortages and famine.

He also said negotiations are happening, despite state TV airing only the parts of the Supreme Leader's remarks about no talks. 

Pezeshkian says Khamenei privately pushed to end the no war, no peace limbo.

Iran's main ask at the table was reopening the Strait of Hormuz for oil exports, but "the other side violated it."
