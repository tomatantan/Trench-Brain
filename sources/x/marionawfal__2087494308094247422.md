🇮🇩 A ferry caught fire in rough seas near Bali, forcing rescuers to pull 172 people off the burning vessel.

A 19-year-old woman was found dead, while at least 2 others were injured.

The ferry manifest said only 131 people were onboard. Officials later realized there were dozens more passengers than listed, along with 44 vehicles and 53 motorcycles.

Waves reached up to 13 feet as navy ships, rescue boats and a helicopter joined the operation.
