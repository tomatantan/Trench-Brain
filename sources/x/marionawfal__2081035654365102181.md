🇺🇸🇨🇳 Tim Cook is lobbying the Trump administration to let Apple buy memory chips from China's CXMT and YMTC for devices sold outside the U.S. 

His pitch: it eases the shortage and keeps iPhones cheaper.

Both firms are designated Chinese military companies by the Pentagon. YMTC is on the Entity List.

These are the same suppliers Apple fled in 2022, when Rubio warned it was "playing with fire." Cook is now accusing Micron, the only large U.S. memory maker, of gouging at 80% margins.

But he also called the shortage a "100-year flood." If it's that severe, 80% margins aren't gouging. They're the price.

Micron is lobbying to kill it, warning that letting Chinese chips in could hollow out U.S. memory manufacturing the way China hollowed out American steel. Regardless of where the phones are sold.

So Trump picks: cheaper iPhones, or the last American memory maker.

Which one survives a president who ran on both?
