⚽ FIFA is hitting back hard for Gianni Infantino. The global soccer organization is slamming what they call “unsubstantiated” and “demonstrably false” claims that Infantino had an affair with a UEFA employee while he was secretary general. 

The Telegraph report also claimed that she got a six-figure payoff plus MBA tuition fees. 

UEFA confirms that the departure payment was made and was within the rules at the time (the regs have since been tightened). FIFA says it’s part of a “concerted effort” to undermine Infantino outside democratic processes.

It’s getting harder and harder to see a path forward where he remains the head of FIFA.
