Rebecca Rettig says you can tell which banks are serious about crypto by who they're hiring:

"This is just a new way to do old instruments, old financial transactions."

"Getting there takes time working with regulators, which all these traditional institutions are doing now."

"The real question is how fast they want to move and how much capital they're putting behind it. Morgan Stanley has a head of digital assets. Bank of America just hired someone."

"But other banks are waiting to see what happens with CLARITY before they put the full weight of their resources behind it."

"That's where the difference is. It's not going to stop the TradFi era crypto is in."
