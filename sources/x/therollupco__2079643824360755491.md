Meredith Hannon explains why @WisdomTreePrime sees tokenization as the ETF story repeating, not a new mega-trend:

"The way ETFs were the gateway to access for all investors through a brokerage account, tokenization is that access for global investors, all through your phone. That's our ethos: the democratization of finance."

"A CFO managing money across the world can move between a stablecoin and a money-market fund seamlessly, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, without going through a bank."

"Where we've really excelled is a money-market fund that moves and trades like a stablecoin, but with the yield you're not getting from a stablecoin."
