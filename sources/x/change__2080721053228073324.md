Just 13 days before move-in, Howard University unenrolled 502 incoming freshmen over unpaid account balances.

The university says the students missed a July 10 deadline to pay or set up a payment plan after months of reminders. But many affected students say their financial aid was still being processed, outside scholarships hadn't yet been applied, or their account balances were inaccurate.

Now, thousands have signed a petition started by incoming freshman Fatima Ahmed calling on Howard to reinstate the affected students and reform the university's financial aid and enrollment policies. "The disenrollment not only disrupts students' education but also causes undue stress, anxiety, and challenges that can impede our academic excellence," the petition reads.
