🇶🇦🇸🇦 The nation of Qatar is strongly condemning the Houthi attack on Saudi Arabia.

The Ministry of Foreign affairs described it as: “a flagrant violation of the Kingdom's sovereignty and territorial integrity, a breach of international law, and a threat to regional security and stability.”

“The Ministry of Foreign Affairs reaffirms the State of Qatar's full solidarity with the sisterly Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and its unwavering support for all measures it takes to preserve its security, stability, sovereignty, and the safety of its citizens and residents.”

The statement came a day after Houthis claimed responsibility for the strike in retaliation for Saudi-led coalition airstrikes on Sanaa airport that disrupted an approaching Iranian flight.
