🇮🇷🇴🇲 Iran's IRGC claims it turned back 2 oil tankers trying to cross the Strait of Hormuz via the southern route, which runs close to Omani waters.

One of the tankers reportedly caught fire during the incident.

The IRGC says the ships were being guided by U.S. aircraft, framing it as fresh proof of its grip on the waterway.

If the intercept happened where the southern lane cuts through Omani waters, it would mean Iran violated Oman's sovereignty to pull it off.

None of it has been independently confirmed, and there's no statement from Oman.
