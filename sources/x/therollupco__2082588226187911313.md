XRPLF (@XRPLF) CTO @angell_denissays Canton and Ripple are competing, but not for exactly the same institutions.

"I'm a permissioned-blockchain kind of guy, so I always kept Canton on my radar."

"I love their docs, smart contracts laid out in YAML, running in a trusted environment with attestations. It's permissioned, and for broker-dealers, that works."

"Ripple sits in the middle. It's not fully permissionless like Ethereum or Bitcoin. There's a UNL, a Unique Node List, a trusted set of about 35 validators that keep the network moving forward."

"So do they compete? In some categories. Canton attacks the fully-permissioned use cases, settlement, clearinghouse, trade desks, well. Ripple is more focused on integrating the broker-dealer onto the chain itself."
