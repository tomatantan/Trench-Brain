🇺🇸 Louisiana is on high alert after a terrifying spike in flesh-eating bacterial infections 💀

5 people have already died this year from Vibrio vulnificus, a brutal bug that’s hitting the state much harder than normal.

9 cases reported so far, all linked to wounds exposed to seawater, and every victim had underlying health issues.

The bacteria love warm summer conditions, and officials are begging people with open wounds to stay out of brackish or salt water.

If a cut starts looking red, swollen, or painful after water-exposure, get to a doctor fast.
