Damn, Stablecoin supply has been shrinking for 3 months straight.

Last time it started shrinking, it didn't stop for 17 straight months through the 2022-23 bear. So I get why people are nervous...

I don't read it the same way this time, and here's why.

Supply is still above $300B.

It peaked near $320B in mid May and sits around $307B today, roughly 4% off the high.

For context, the 2022 bear erased 26% of all stablecoin supply.

The dollars haven't left, they've just moved because of regulations.

The GENIUS Act restricted payment stablecoin issuers from offering yield, which changed how large holders think about idle stablecoins.

Why keep billions sitting in $USDC or $USDT earning nothing when tokenized Treasury products like $BUIDL or $USYC can offer yield?

Tokenized Treasuries went from $6.5B to nearly $16B in a year, up 56% in the last six months alone.

So instead of stablecoins being the place where capital waits, they are becoming the settlement layer.

Onchain stablecoin volume hit $1.8T in June, an ALL TIME HIGH, up 63% in a single month. The float is shrinking while usage sets records.

We are also seeing this shift on exchanges.

Binance and Bybit saw billions in stablecoin outflows recently, but some of that liquidity is moving onchain, into RWAs, or simply sitting outside exchanges waiting for opportunities.

One example: Binance added $USYC as off exchange collateral, and $1.84B of it now sits on BNB Chain. BNB Chain holds $4.7B in tokenized Treasuries, second only to Ethereum.

With the Clarity Act queued for a September Senate vote and companies like BlackRock, JPMorgan, Visa and Stripe building around stablecoin infrastructure, the bigger picture looks interesting.

Stablecoin outflows just aren't carrying the same bearish signal they did in previous cycles.

What would change my mind:

• Tokenized Treasury AUM flattening while stablecoin supply keeps falling
• Exchange stablecoin balances recovering without any RWA growth to match
• A fourth and fifth month of outflows with total supply breaking below $300B
