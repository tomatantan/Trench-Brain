Sam Callahan says central banks will probably be the last to adopt Bitcoin.

“Central banks are probably gonna be the last to adopt Bitcoin.”

“Bitcoin today is still viewed as a risk asset.”

“In periods where there is more volatility, it tends to fall with everything else.”

“That’s when central banks want their FX reserves to show stability.”

“Today Bitcoin is not really a good FX reserve.”

“But eventually every single balance sheet’s gonna hold Bitcoin.”

“I just think it’s a little early for central bank adoption.”
