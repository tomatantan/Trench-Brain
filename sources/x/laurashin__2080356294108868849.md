"When I think about building a trading product, there's no better than him to do that"

Jesse on why he handed the Base app to Cobie and refocused on the chain

"The big reflection for me over the last six months has been that building Base into the blockchain for global finance, really serving as the settlement layer for every economic transaction over the next decade or two, is just a massive undertaking. Having my full focus on that is required to achieve all that we want to achieve"

"At the same time, I built a really strong relationship with Jordan, Cobie, over the last year. I was actually really involved in the process of getting him to join Coinbase in the first place, and once he got here we've continued building a friendship and trust between us"

"It felt like a natural partnership, where I could double down on this blockchain for global finance, continuing to work towards decentralization, and he could focus in on that trading problem. I don't think I would be feeling as fired up if I didn't trust Jordan to be such a good shepherd of this product that we've built"
