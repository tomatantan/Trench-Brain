🇺🇸🇪🇺 Everyone keeps burying NATO way too early

The latest doom call says the alliance is finished. It's half right.

The old deal is dying, the one where the U.S. does the heavy lifting and Europe coasts.

But dead? Not close. What's happening is a rebalance, Washington pivoting hard to China and finally telling Europe to carry its own conventional defense.

Hegseth said it plainly in Brussels, the U.S. stays in NATO, just as a partner now instead of the boss.

Hegseth: "We're doubling down on our effort to make NATO what it always was supposed to be: a balanced alliance with Europe in the lead for its own defense…"

And Europe's stepping up, a 5% of GDP defense pledge at the Hague, filling most of the gaps the U.S. leaves behind, with the U.S. nuclear umbrella still overhead.

The panic misses the obvious. Washington has been pushing Europe to do exactly this since Obama.

Europe finally paying its own way is what NATO was always supposed to look like, about 20 years late.
