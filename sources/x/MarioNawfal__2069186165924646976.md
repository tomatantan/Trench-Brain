🇴🇲🇮🇷 Oman steps back into the picture, with its foreign minister meeting Iran's Ghalibaf and Araghchi to talk regional stability and the safety of the Strait of Hormuz

Per Al Jazeera, both sides stressed using the current diplomatic moment to support peace, de escalation, and stability, with the conversation explicitly covering maritime navigation through Hormuz and the wider waterways.

The Oman angle is worth tracking.

The MOU specifically tasks Iran with opening a dialogue with Oman to define how the strait is administered going forward, so this is not a courtesy visit, it is the early groundwork for who actually manages the waterway after the 60 day window. 

Muscat has been Iran's most trusted back channel for years, and putting Hormuz security on the agenda now is the quiet start of the governance fight that decides whose strait it really is.
