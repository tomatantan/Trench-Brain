Nic Cabana compares Solana to a digital Silicon Valley

"There's a beautiful composability of projects and startups on Solana"

"There's a huge community, essentially a digital Silicon Valley. Startups get built, everyone gets to know everyone, then the talent splits apart and builds other startups"

"What does that mean for entertainment projects like us? We always have dope tech and dope startups that come to us from people we know and trust"

"They're like, 'Hey, I'm also building this. Maybe you guys want to test this out.' You get this huge advantage of coming up with interesting ideas because all these startups are pitching you new things they're building"
