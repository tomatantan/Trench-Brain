"Ethereum's qualm has always been communication. Being a super smart talented developer and being a communicator is not necessarily synonymous"

Luca on why Ethereum's real problem has always been communication, not technology

"I always felt like Vitalik had a good finger on the pulse in terms of what Ethereum needed to succeed. The problems the core foundation has been working to solve are probably the right ones. This is a different group though"

"It's like the big treasury holders. I'm kind of with you Kain, I don't know what difference 15 minute finality makes. Clearly it makes a difference to somebody. I'd take the bet and trust the teams having conversations with institutions that it's a problem somehow. It's probably not arbitrary. But the fact that all three of us are like what the fuck is this thing is not amazing"

"Ethereum's qualm has always been communication, which is a byproduct of the gigabrains that work there. Being a super smart developer and being a communicator is not synonymous. I do my motivational inspirational thing and the developers in the group are just like oh my god no no no, this is not good"
