A proposed AI data center at the site of a former Crystal Geyser bottling plant just outside Mount Shasta, California, is drawing backlash. Thousands are backing a campaign calling on county officials to block the project. The campaign was started by a local resident who lives just miles from the proposed site.

"Data centers are notorious for their high water and electricity consumption," the petition reads. "The operation of a data center could lead to significant depletion of our vital resources, impacting both the residents, tourism, and the local ecosystem."
