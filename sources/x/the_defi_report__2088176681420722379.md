Is the yen still a liquidity signal?

A weaker yen has often coincided with rising risk assets, since cheap yen funding helps fuel carry trades. 
Rapid yen appreciation can do the opposite, forcing leverage to unwind.

That relationship has recently broken down: over the last three months, the Nasdaq rose even as the yen strengthened, turning the correlation slightly positive.

The 10-year spread that supported carry trades in 2023 was 3.4%; today it's 1.9%.

The carry-trade mechanism is still visible in the funding spread, but the price relationship is no longer moving with it.
