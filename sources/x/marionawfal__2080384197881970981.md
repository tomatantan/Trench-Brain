They want combat data on how modern warfare actually works; drone tactics, submarine stealth, missile defense, battlefield intelligence.

Western officials are watching this in real time and they're worried.

It gets lessons that used to take a full-scale war to learn.

Drone countermeasures. Sub-surface stealth upgrades. How Western missile defense systems respond under pressure. What breaks and when.

None of this is theoretical anymore. It's been stress-tested in the field for 3 years.

If China absorbed even half of what Russia has learned about fighting a modern, Western-armed adversary, that changes the calculation on every war game the Pentagon runs.

Ukraine was supposed to be Russia's problem.

It may end up being everyone's.

Is the West watching this closely enough? And if so, what can it even do about it?
