🇮🇷🇺🇸 America has burned through nearly 80% of its THAAD interceptors, with commanders warning stockpiles are "dangerously low."

-Two sources familiar with the latest inventory report tell CNN the U.S. has used close to four-fifths of its THAAD missiles and roughly half its Patriots since the war began, starting from about 452 THAADs and 2,200 modern Patriots

-The Army has also expended "virtually all" of its long-range surface-to-air weapons, with ATACMS and PrSM hardest hit, while nearly half the Tomahawk stockpile is gone

-Replenishment is nowhere close: the Pentagon is receiving roughly 15 Tomahawks and 20 Patriots a month, and CSIS estimates rebuilding THAAD inventories alone would take three or more years

-Gulf countries are raising alarms of their own, since they rely on American systems and fear they could not intercept Iranian retaliation if Trump escalates

-Hegseth actually received the final go-ahead order Friday for massive strikes, and Trump stood him down Saturday after allies warned about retaliation against their energy infrastructure

-The White House rejects all of it, with a spokesperson saying the military "has more than enough munitions, ammo, and stockpiles to serve all of President Trump's strategic goals and beyond"

One source familiar with the shortage put the strategic problem plainly: "The lack of interceptors means that conventional attacks can backfire if the suicide drones start making it through in-masse. 

Tit-for-tat will never end this." 

That is the real reason the strikes keep getting called off, and it is also the reason Iran keeps firing cheap drones at a shield that costs millions per shot.
