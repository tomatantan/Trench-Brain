🇨🇴 11,000 troops and police mobilized for Abelardo de la Espriella’s presidential swearing-in in Cali.

It's an unusual ceremony: for the first time in modern Colombian history, the presidential swearing-in is being held outside Bogotá, at the Arena USC of Santiago de Cali University.

De la Espriella, who has been called "Trumpian," chose Cali partly for symbolic and security reasons.

The inauguration marks the formal start of his term, and the beginning of a new age for Colombia.
