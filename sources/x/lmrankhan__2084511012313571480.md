I liked POAPs. It was a nights and weekends cultist tech idea, closer to the early PC builder hobbyist vibes than a venture backed company type idea 

Most of us knew it would not become a venture outcome, but that was never really the point. It brought indie ideologists into a new space where people could experiment almost every possible idea

The small nights and weekends tinkerers slowly disappeared, similar to how the PC hobbyists faded once companies like Dell and Apple took over the market.

I think crypto is now shedding the last remaining pieces of its old shell as we enter the next era. Fewer products built purely for culture and more products built for real world impact 

Crypto growing up is necessary, but something culturally important is being lost along the way which I’ll always remember
