🇺🇸 Lockheed Martin is rolling out a cheaper Patriot missile as militaries scramble for budget-friendly ways to swat down drones.

The new PAC-3 ACE will run less than half the cost of its current interceptors, which go for around 4 million dollars each.

Lockheed's pitching it as battle-tested and budget-smart, and plans to co-build it with U.S. and European partners.

The timing's no accident, with drone-heavy wars draining stockpiles and scrappy startups like Ukraine's Fire Point undercutting the old guard on price.

It's also a sign U.S. defense giants are shifting from just selling weapons to Europe toward building them there, as Trump pulls back from the continent.

Everything's got a budget tier these days, missiles included.
