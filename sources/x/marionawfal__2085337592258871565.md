21 suspects were detained for allegedly collecting information on military and air-defense sites. 

In separate operations, authorities also arrested 4 members of an armed gang and 5 people accused of involvement in a coup plot.

Iran frequently announces such arrests, especially amid heightened tensions with Israel. 

These claims are hard to independently verify, but they reflect ongoing internal security ops and the regime’s focus on addressing potential foreign espionage.

We do know, however, that Mossad maintains an active presence and deep operational capabilities inside Iran.
