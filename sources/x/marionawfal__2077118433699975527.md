🇮🇱 Netanyahu's coalition just shielded ultra-Orthodox draft dodgers from arrest

Israel's parliament passed a law protecting tens of thousands of ultra-Orthodox men who refuse to join the army.

In Israel military service is required, but this group has long fought to skip it, and now they can't be arrested for dodging it.

The catch is the timing, the protection lasts at least until after October's election.

Those men vote for the religious parties that keep Netanyahu in power, so the law quietly guards the exact voters he needs.

Handy timing, the shield stays up right until the votes are in.
