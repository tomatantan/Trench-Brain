Are we pivoting to RWAs and stocks at the top? 

@OxSimpleFarmer says crypto was always meant to be the replacement for the world's financial layer. We just got bored and drifted into "liquidity musical chairs." @RobinhoodCrypto Chain stock tokens (off-hours trading, a stock's price moving based on on-chain trading) are a step back toward that original vision.

Since equities are hot and crypto's been weak, he argues pairing tokens to stocks is actually a feature: stocks often move opposite to crypto, so the pairing is a built-in hedge that gives builders more strategy. 

His view: positive both ways, and bullish for the space overall.
