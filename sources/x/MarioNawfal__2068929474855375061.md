🇺🇸 Money-losing companies are beating the profitable ones

Look at the Russell 2000, the index of America's smaller companies.

The firms in it that actually lose money are up 60% since April 2025. The ones that turn a profit? Up just 38%.

So lately, bleeding cash has been the better bet than making it.

The whole index is up 48%, one of the hottest of any major market, ahead of the S&P 500 at 38% and just behind the Nasdaq 100 at 58%.

It really caught fire in April, after the April 7 U.S.-Iran ceasefire sent investors piling into small-cap tech.

When the cash-burners are outrunning the cash-makers, you're watching a market that cares more about an AI story than whether the company actually earns a dime.
