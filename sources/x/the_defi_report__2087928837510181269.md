Can QE exist without the Fed buying bonds?

The FIMA repo facility gives foreign central banks dollar liquidity against their Treasury holdings, reducing the need to sell those Treasuries into a stressed market.

But unlike QE, the Treasuries remain outstanding. Duration isn't removed from the market, and FIMA doesn't create a new buyer for long-dated Treasury supply.

That's why we think of these tools as "QE Lite": they can prevent forced selling without actually expanding Treasury demand.
