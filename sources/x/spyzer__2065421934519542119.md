What will the world look like in 10 years from now? 

The ChatGPT moment happened less than 4 years ago.

A basic LLM back then. The latest Claude model is being called too strong. The progress has been exponential.

Now what happens if we give it 5 more years. And add a ChatGPT moment for robotics.

Blue collar work gets automated in order of environment, not difficulty. Structured, repetitive jobs go first -> warehouses, assembly lines, sorting. The messy, unpredictable ones (plumbing in a 100-year-old house) come later. But they come. 

White collar is already mid-transition. Customer service, data entry, junior analysts, back-office roles -> AI is absorbing them right now. And the trend here is already compounding. 

The real bottleneck here for both isn’t hardware or the tech itself. It’s quality training data.

It’s hard to collect quality at scale. 

Imagine tracking every move of 100,000 warehouse workers for a year. One of them has the perfect throwing technique. Another figured out the most efficient picking route. A third has a trick for stacking that nobody taught him.

Train one robot on all their data -> and you’ve built the best warehouse worker that’s ever existed.

Now copy him a million times.

He doesn’t get tired. MagRes power transfer mean effectively 24/7 uptime. He’s stronger, faster and smarter than any human. And he’s still learning every shift.

Pass this logic into every sector where physical work lives. All of it is possible in the near future.

And the workers providing that data? Their expertise becomes the scarcest, most valuable asset in the economy -> before the handoff completes. This will take time and the right companies to do it.

In 5-10 years you’ll buy a humanoid robot for a couple grand that handles every chore in your house. Faster and better than you would.

All of this will lead to : 

More efficiency.
More productivity.
More innovation.

By a great margin.

And it all leads to way more free time for humans.

That’s why my bet for one of the biggest growth sectors is entertainment. Festivals, sports, travel, gaming -> everything people love doing with their time. Because for the first time in history, free time will be the thing we have most of.

Don’t believe me? History repeats. 

100 years ago: 12-hour days, brutal conditions. Today: fewer hours, MUCH better lives. 20 years from now? This trend doesn’t stop/slow down yet only accelerates.

Abundance isn’t a dream. @elonmusk 

And btw — this thesis doesn’t even need quantum computing. 

But if it comes into play… 🤷‍♂️
