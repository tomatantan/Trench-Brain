🇺🇸 Trump's border wall is supposed to be done, Pacific to Gulf, by this time next year. But the math disagrees.

To hit that deadline, crews would need to build over 13 miles of wall a week, every single week, for a full year.

The actual pace for most of 2026: 2.6 miles a week.

Even the fastest stretch on record, 4 miles in 5 days, only projects out to about 292 miles in a year. They need 698.

DHS says most contracts are now awarded, mostly to just 2 companies, and that should speed things up.

Trump already got border crossings to a 55-year low. 

But the wall is part of Trump's signature brand, a physical monument to secure his legacy.
