I actually think Duolingo is the right app model for post AI world. AI will commoditize features, but it won't commoditize products that understand the user's psyche.   

The goal is to build something so personalized and habit forming that people feel like they can't leave because they've invested so much time and energy into it or a better example is a pair of shoes that's molded to your feet only

Almost every iconic consumer app has a cult like following and the emotional connection is much harder to copy than any AI feature.  Some examples include Figma, Strava, Duolingo, Spotify etc
