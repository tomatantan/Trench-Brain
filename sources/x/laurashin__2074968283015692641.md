"Ethereum keeps doing these very long roadmaps and very big things, but you have to be on the ground floor and present today. Solana pretty consistently sees a user need, a problem, and figures out how to ship it"

Taylor on the difference between how Ethereum and Solana operate

"In order to be responsible you have to have the long term in mind. But the EF keeps doing these very long roadmaps and these very big things, and that's the most talked about thing. It's not always easy to execute on that, especially in the short term, especially with the increase in competition"

"It's very easy to say, well once we have this in five years then we'll be competitive again. No, you have to be on the ground floor and be present today. That worries me a little bit. I do wish we were seeing more focus on promoting Ethereum and new features today. What are we giving builders? What are we giving for experience now?"

"Solana specifically is one that perhaps they go too hard on this, there's a good argument there. But pretty consistently their builders are like, oh there's a user need here, oh there's a problem here, and then they figure out how to ship it"
