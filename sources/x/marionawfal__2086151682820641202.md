🇺🇸 America's oil cushion just hit levels not seen since before most of its own strategic reserve was built up in the first place.

Total U.S. crude inventories have now fallen for 17 straight weeks, the longest decline on record, breaking the previous streak set in 2021.

The numbers:

Total crude stocks: down 166 million barrels since early April, to 712 million, the lowest since March 1984.

The Strategic Petroleum Reserve alone: down 111 million barrels since March, to 305 million, the lowest since February 1983.

Commercial stocks excluding the SPR: down for 10 straight weeks, tying the 2018 record.

The reserve built specifically for a moment like a Gulf war getting drawn down during a Gulf war, at the same time commercial stocks are falling in lockstep instead of offsetting it.

The cushion America built for exactly this kind of crisis is the same cushion the crisis is now spending.
