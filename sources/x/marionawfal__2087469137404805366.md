A clean-energy car shouldn’t leave a polluting battery problem behind.

Tesla’s goal is for no battery to ever end up in landfill.

Across its U.S. operations, more than 90% of key materials like nickel and cobalt are already being recovered through recycling.

Meaning less battery waste, less risk of pollution and less need to mine and process fresh materials to replace what was thrown away.

In 2025, Tesla processed around 3,000 metric tons this way, enough material for roughly 9,000 Model Ys.

The battery gets another life. The planet gets one less thing dumped into it.
