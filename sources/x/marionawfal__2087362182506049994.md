🚨🇮🇷 🇺🇸 Iran has a plan to survive Trump's blockade... and it's to get POORER...

-Tehran is deliberately shrinking into a survival economy, rationing scarce goods, restricting access to hard currency, slashing investment and pushing the pain onto households while protecting strategic imports and the machinery of the state

-Iranian oil exports fell to nearly ZERO in July after the blockade was reimposed, down from $4.5 billion in June, according to Capital Economics

-Not a single tanker has loaded this month at Kharg Island, the country's main export terminal, per ship tracking firm Kpler

-The cushion is thinner than last time too, with Iranian oil stored at sea dropping from roughly 180 million barrels to about 60 million by early July

-Annual inflation is running above 80%, with chicken up 190% and milk up 150% from a year ago

-The IMF expects the economy to contract 5.4% this year, and former Iranian social security official Hadi Kahalzadeh estimates the war could destroy 2 to 3 million jobs

-Rolling blackouts are already in place, some factories now run on Fridays to recover lost output, and a subsidized exchange rate covers up to $3.5 billion of wheat, medicine and baby formula imports

-Welfare households can buy essentials on credit, with the debt taken out of future payments

-Mediators have warned Washington that a country living under sanctions for years will not fold over money, and will instead escalate attacks to raise the cost

Here is the trap in this strategy.

Cuba and Venezuela both proved a government can preside over economic ruin for decades and never lose its grip.

Making Iran poorer is the easy part, and Washington is doing it faster than expected.

Turning that poverty into a signature is another matter entirely, especially with hardliners now holding every security post and the technocrats who wanted a deal losing the argument.
