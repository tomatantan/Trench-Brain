🇸🇦 🇮🇷 Saudi built a coalition to face Iran, and not one member wants to fight.

Trita Parsi, Quincy Institute EVP and Middle East analyst, looks at the Multinational Maritime Defense Coalition, Pakistan, Turkey, Nigeria, several Gulf states, and sees countries trying to replace a balancer that no longer works, because American military threats have lost their bite after two rounds of war ended badly:

"Does Pakistan want to go to war with Iran? Does Turkey? None of them do."

His read is that this is positioning for a future negotiation rather than a war front, a way to face Tehran together before the bargaining starts. 

And the UAE staying out points to the other target nobody names publicly: Israel.
