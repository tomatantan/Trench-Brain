🇺🇸 Trump has launched “Trump Accounts,” giving eligible newborns a government-funded $1,000 investment account that families can add to over time. 

It sounds generous, but it risks becoming another glossy band-aid over a much deeper economic wound.

America’s real problem is that wealth is increasingly concentrated in the hands of a few giant companies, especially in tech, while younger people struggle to buy homes, start businesses, or build assets of their own. 

And because AI is now treated as a national arms race, Washington faces a powerful temptation to keep subsidizing those same firms in the name of staying ahead.

That balance has to be handled carefully. 

Support AI leadership too little and America could fall behind; support it too aggressively and public money may simply deepen monopolies, widen wealth gaps, and leave ordinary Americans further outside the economy.

That is why pouring taxpayer-funded accounts into index funds feels so circular. 

The government says it is helping children build wealth, while much of that money may flow straight back into the corporate giants already holding an outsized share of it.

America does not need another financial patch. 

It needs an economy where young people can afford homes, build businesses, and create wealth without depending on stock-market gains from the very companies dominating the system.
