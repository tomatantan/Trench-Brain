3F Labs Co-founder Sonya Kim on the Meta Oracle approach to handling price deviations in lending protocols

"This reminds me of when I was at Steakhouse, we implemented this thing called Meta Oracle. In lending protocols, when you integrate an asset, there's a question of do you use the fair market value, which can whiplash around like we've seen here, or do you use the primary redemption value, or do you use both and try to figure out when there are deviations?"

"In different lending protocols, stablecoins have been hard-coded at $1. There are so many different opinions around this"

"I think it's quite prudent to have a Meta Oracle like the one Steakhouse implemented on Morpho, where you get notified when there's a deviation, and when there's a great enough deviation, you can switch between the two"
