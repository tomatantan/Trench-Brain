🇺🇸 Supreme Court just revived a 1979 murder case

The court ruled 6 to 3 to put Pedro Hernandez's conviction back in the disappearance of 6-year-old Etan Patz, and the three liberal justices wanted no part of it.

A lower court had thrown the whole thing out over how the trial judge answered one question from the jury, and the top court said that was nowhere near enough to undo everything.

"The Second Circuit exceeded its authority in holding that Hernandez is entitled to relief," the justices wrote.

So Hernandez, now 64, stays behind bars on 25 years to life, while his lawyers swear he's innocent and only confessed because a mental illness made him hallucinate.

Here's the part that still gets you. Etan walked off to his school bus stop one morning in 1979 and was never seen again, his face ended up on one of the first milk cartons ever to carry a missing kid, and the day he vanished is the reason we even have a National Missing Children's Day.

Hernandez didn't land on anyone's radar until 2012.

47 years on, there's still no body and no grave, just a man in a cell and a case the courts can't stop reopening.
