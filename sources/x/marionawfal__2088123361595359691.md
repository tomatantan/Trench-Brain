🇦🇺🇺🇸 Albanese spoke with Trump and confirmed two things: AUKUS stays on track, and the tariff exemption request is now on the table.

The nuclear-sub deal stays on track, Virginia-class boats still heading Australia’s way, and both leaders called the progress real.

At the same time Albanese pushed hard for a full exemption from the new U.S. tariffs, arguing Australia runs a trade deficit and should get the best treatment as an ally.

Trump said he would look at it.

This sits against a busier Pacific map: Australia has locked in new defence arrangements with Papua New Guinea and Fiji, China keeps expanding its presence and influence across the region, and AUKUS was always designed as the long-term counterweight.

The alliance is holding; the industrial timeline remains the hard part, and the tariff ask is now officially on the table.
