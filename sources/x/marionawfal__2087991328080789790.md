🇵🇱🇺🇦 A Russian missile landing on Polish soil is doing what months of Ukrainian pleas couldn't.

Warsaw will decide within days whether to hand Ukraine another batch of Patriot interceptors. 

The defense minister is leaning yes, and his reasoning is blunt: better one of these gets shot down over Ukraine than over Poland.

That comes straight from July 30, when a Russian Kh-101 cruise missile flew nearly 100 kilometers into Polish territory and crashed in a field.

Here's the catch. The PAC-3 is reportedly the only interceptor Poland has that can stop the Russian ballistic missiles now creeping toward its border, and it holds only around 200. Every one sent to Kyiv is one less over Polish cities.

The government's bet is simple. Ukraine losing this war is a bigger threat to Poland than a thinner missile stockpile.
