🇺🇸🇮🇷 Rubio threads the needle on regime change:

"The regime has to change. You may not have a regime change, but the regime has to change. 

The Iranian regime traditionally has been expeditionary. They don't want to just govern Iran, they want to govern the region. 

That does have to change. 

And the only way you're going to change that is if you make the price too high for them to pay."

"Not regime change, but the regime has to change" is a distinction with real consequences, because behavioral change has no finish line. 

A nuclear deal can be signed and verified. 

Convincing a government to abandon its regional strategy is open-ended by design, and it means the pressure never has a natural stopping point.

Netanyahu made the same move a few days ago, folding regime change inside the nuclear goal with the phrase "Iran, with this regime."
