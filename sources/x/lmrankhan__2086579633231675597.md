To promote more onchain building, I’ve now fully automated @onchainatlasorg as a free resource for founders.

The goal is to turn Onchain Atlas from a static wiki into a semi autonomous research engine for figuring out what should be built next.

Every day it will build and launch a new experimental onchain product, track the fastest growing products, and analyze governance, GitHub activity, and protocol changes to surface opportunities before they become obvious.

It will also generate new requests for products worth building, automatically discover and add new projects like Stonkbroker, and surface emerging primitives, experiments, and entirely new categories.

The broader goal is to bring back some of the energy of the early 2020s, when onchain builders were constantly experimenting and creating categories that did not exist before

Follow @onchainatlasorg  It will publish daily on what is growing, what is emerging, and what should be built next.
