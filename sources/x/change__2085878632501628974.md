Should people be able to bet on clinical trial outcomes?

David Tsai spends his days running clinical trials at a Bay Area biotech company, work he says depends on reliable data. Now he's warning that betting platforms like Kalshi and Polymarket let users wager on whether those same kinds of trials succeed.

Tsai is petitioning federal regulators to ban such betting, arguing it could give someone involved in a trial, like a pharmacist, a financial reason to alter the results. Former FDA Commissioner Robert Califf has endorsed the petition, calling the practice "a breach of scientific conduct," according to the New York Times.
