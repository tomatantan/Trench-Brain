🇺🇸🇩🇪 The U.S. is telling Americans to exercise "increased caution" when traveling to Germany.

The State Department advisory cites ongoing terrorism risks. 

Attacks could happen with little or no warning and may target tourist spots, transport hubs, markets, restaurants or large events.

This isn't a brand-new warning. Germany has been at Level 2 for terrorism since at least mid-2025, and the latest update simply keeps that status in place. 

The country remains open for travel, but officials want visitors to stay alert.
