🇮🇷 Iran's Foreign Ministry spokesperson talks nuclear: 

There will be NO new IAEA commitments, NO changes to existing inspection procedures, and NO nuclear talks under the MoU until Clause 13 of the MoU is implemented.

Clause 13 says paragraphs 1, 4, 5, 10 and 11 of the MoU must be implemented first before full negotiations can begin.

- Paragraph 1: Permanent ceasefire and end to military operations, including in Lebanon
- Paragraph 4/5: Reopening of the Strait of Hormuz to commercial shipping and lifting of naval blockade
- Paragraph 10/11: Sanctions waivers for Iranian oil exports, release of frozen assets/funds

So they demand those implemented first, then and only then may deeper talks begin.
