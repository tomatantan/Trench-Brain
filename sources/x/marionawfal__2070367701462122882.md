One of the best parts of the World Cup? Hearing what fans think of the host country.

Visitors in Atlanta from Haiti, Morocco, and Canada had some very American observations:

-  "The food portions are about three elephants."
-  "Your pizza is so cheesy."
-  "Everything is BIG... the roads, bridges, cars."
And they couldn't get over how you can find food from almost every culture on the same block.

The World Cup really is one giant cultural exchange.
