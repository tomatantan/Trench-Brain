Michael Egorov says everyone wants the stablecoin revenue model but Tether and Circle make it nearly impossible to crack:

"In OG times there was a lot of experimentation with algorithmic and CDP stablecoins. That undeniably works, there are quite a few today."

"But now the push is mostly toward redeemable stablecoins, backed by deposits. Tether, USDC, and the newer ones."

"Many players realized they want to participate in this space. But it's not easy, Tether and Circle are the existing players."
