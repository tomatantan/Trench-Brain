🇻🇦 Pope Leo XIV just excommunicated the entire Society of Saint Pius X 

Not just the 4 bishops ordained without papal approval on Wednesday, but the 2 bishops who performed the ceremony, all SSPX priests, and any lay members who formally adhere to the group.

The SSPX has been a thorn in Rome's side since Archbishop Lefebvre founded it in 1970 in opposition to the Second Vatican Council's reforms. 

The shift to Mass in local languages, the embrace of religious freedom, ecumenism, and crucially the condemnation of antisemitism. They never accepted any of it and have been operating in a kind of canonical grey zone for decades until Leo decided the grey zone was over.

One of the newly ordained American bishops called the mainstream Catholic Church "a modernist desert that kills everything it touches" hours after the ceremony.

Leo hasn't commented publicly but the decree really says everything.
