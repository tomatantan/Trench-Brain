🇪🇬🇹🇷🇸🇦🇵🇰 Egypt deliberately stayed out of the new Turkey-Saudi-Pakistan defence pact...

Ankara spent 2 years lobbying to bring Cairo into the alliance, which includes a collective-defence clause similar to NATO’s Article 5. 

Egyptian officials ultimately declined the offer.

The main reason was the need to protect Egypt’s delicate relationship with Israel and the UAE. 

Joining would have exposed Cairo to heavy Israeli pressure and forced it to choose sides between rival regional blocs.

Turkish FM Fidan still insists Egypt will join later, saying only “technical issues” remain. 

But for now, Cairo prefers to keep its options open and avoid being locked into any one camp.
