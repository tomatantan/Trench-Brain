🇨🇳 China’s commercial space industry crossed a line most people still refuse to see.

A fresh CSIS report shows it exploded from under a dozen companies in 2014 to roughly 600 today. 

They’re not chasing profits either, launch startups are building reusable rockets. 

Two mega-constellations, Guowang and Thousand Sails, are each racing toward more than 10,000 satellites, going head-to-head with Starlink while carrying clear dual-use military value.

Behind the scenes, satellite “gigafactories” are already capable of pumping out 4,100–5,000 birds a year. Aerospace cities, new launch pads, and vertically integrated clusters are shortening the path from lab to orbit at a speed the West isn’t matching.

The critical part: these firms sit inside an ecosystem where government direction, military needs, and private capital are deliberately blurred. 

Civilian labels mask state-backed muscle as well, so that makes it nearly impossible to draw clean lines between commercial progress and strategic power.

Track the government program all you want, ignore this commercial machine and you’re already behind.
