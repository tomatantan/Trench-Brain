🇷🇺🇺🇸🇺🇦 Trump may have made a Ukraine peace deal much harder to sell

The Kremlin has reportedly decided it won't give back any of the Ukrainian territory it occupies, and instead plans to keep parts of Sumy and Kharkiv as permanent buffer zones.

Moscow believes the informal understandings reached after last year's Trump-Putin summit in Alaska are effectively dead.

Increasingly confrontational statements from the U.S, along with tougher sanctions threats and continued Ukrainian strikes inside Russia, have convinced the Kremlin there's no reason to compromise.

Putin is now reportedly focused on capturing the rest of Donetsk before even thinking about serious negotiations.

That leaves Zelensky with an even tougher choice: accept a deal that gives up even more territory than before, or keep fighting.
