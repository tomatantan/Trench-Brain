Tushar Jain says the next buyers of Zcash (ZEC) are the same people who bought Bitcoin in 2015-2020:

"People, often outside the US, in countries with more oppressive governments, who want to be able to flee with their wealth."

"The Netherlands just passed an unrealized capital gains tax. People protect themselves from things like that, and they look to a private, censorship-resistant store of value like ZEC to do it."

"You saw wealthy Chinese entrepreneurs get into crypto back then. They're quieter now, but they moved a lot of money in. That politically exposed buyer is the next set of buyers for Zcash, just like they were for Bitcoin."
