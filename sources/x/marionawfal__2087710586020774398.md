🇺🇸🇮🇷 Democracies face a built-in challenge that authoritarian systems simply don’t.

The U.S.-Iran fight over the Strait of Hormuz has turned into a pure concession contest, and the election calendar is the pressure point.

Trump needs a visible win before the midterms so voters don’t punish him for high oil prices and a grinding conflict, which pushes Washington toward deals that can be sold as progress even if they hand Iran more control over the waterway.

Tehran faces no such deadline.

Behind the president and parliament sits the Supreme Leader and the IRGC, the permanent structure that actually calls the shots and can absorb military pressure while holding out for real sanctions relief.

This isn’t a pitch for authoritarianism, but a clear problem Western systems have to solve if they want to keep long-term leverage in prolonged standoffs.
