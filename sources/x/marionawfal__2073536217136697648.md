🇵🇱🇩🇪 A man who stood at the gates of Auschwitz and denied the Holocaust on camera is now facing criminal charges in Poland.

Reza B., a 55-year-old Iranian-born German citizen, was indicted this week for statements he made during a 2022 tour of Auschwitz-Birkenau, where around 1.1 million people were killed, 1 million of them Jews.

Standing at the entrance to the part of the camp where 90% of victims died in gas chambers, he questioned the criminal use of those gas chambers and crematoriums on video.

When questioned as a suspect, he refused to respond to the charges.

Holocaust denial carries up to 3 years in prison under Polish law.
