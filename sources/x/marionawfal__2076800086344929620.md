🇮🇷 What is Pickaxe Mountain? A look at the target Trump just promised to "take out."

The mountain complex near Natanz is believed to be Iran's newest and DEEPEST suspected underground nuclear facility, and expert assessments paint a sobering picture. 

Analysts fear Iran may attempt to recover surviving highly enriched uranium from its bombed facilities and transfer it to the site, moving the 'nuclear dust' Washington demands into its hardest shell.

The engineering is the problem. 

The facility's depth and scale are assessed to exceed Fordow, the fortified site America's bunker-busters famously cracked in 2025, making Pickaxe significantly more difficult to destroy with conventional munitions. 

If fully operational, analysts believe it could match or even surpass Fordow's enrichment capacity.

That's what makes Trump's telegraphed strike so audacious.

He's naming the one site built specifically to survive American bombs, with the uranium possibly already inside, and daring Iran to watch. 

Either the U.S. has munitions and targeting the analysts haven't accounted for, or the mountain calls the bluff.
