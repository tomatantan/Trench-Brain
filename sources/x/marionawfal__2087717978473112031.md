🚨🇮🇷🇺🇸 Text messages from a sailor aboard the USS Abraham Lincoln reveal no milk in three months and ROTTEN food arriving at sea...

He reportedly wrote that almost nothing fresh makes it out except meat, because only frozen goods survive the supply run, and called it the worst deployment he has been on, with sailors who have far more behind them agreeing it could still get worse.

Add that to the list of grievances, and they become almost too numerous to enumerate...

No hot water for long stretches, laundry down for weeks, hour-long lines at the ship's store, one sailor eating half a cup of rice and two tortillas.

Multiple sailors have reportedly attempted to jump overboard, and one wife said her husband sits on medical hold now, terrified a 13-year career ends in a dishonorable discharge because he burned out. 

The costs that don't make the gas sign are the ones Americans are actually paying: a thousand dollars a year gone from the average household, eighteen service members killed, and interceptor stockpiles run down to nothing. 

All of it rides on the bet that Iran breaks first, which raises a question nobody in Washington wants to answer about who breaks first back home.
