Daniel Stanford, a former middle school teacher in North Carolina, faces 24 felony charges for allegedly sexually abusing students at his own school between 2014 and 2015. One of his accusers, who says Stanford repeatedly abused him when he was just 14 years old, says the Union County District Attorney's Office has informed him it plans to resolve the case with a plea deal that wouldn't include prison time.

A petition is urging the DA's Office to reconsider any proposed plea agreement that doesn't include incarceration. The campaign has received the support of Monroe Mayor Robert Burns, who shared the campaign and wrote: "Crimes against children do not deserve mercy."
