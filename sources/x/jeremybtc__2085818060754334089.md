Someone put 10 of the top AI models in a physics simulator and told them to build the tallest tower they could without it falling over

Each one got 30 blocks to work with

The catch is that every block you add makes the tower taller but also more top heavy, so at some point one more piece is what brings the whole thing down

Nine of the models didn't get that so they kept stacking for height until their towers collapsed and they lost everything

Opus 5 was the only one that understood the risk and It built up a solid, balanced tower, then chose to stop early and keep its score rather than gamble it all on adding another block

It didn't win by building better, it won because it was the only one that knew when it was already ahead and didn't have to continue to win
