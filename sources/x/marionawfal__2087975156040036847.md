🇺🇸 Trump just won the legal fight over one of the most abused loopholes in American trade policy, and this one's been building since last summer.

"The Court ruled that the President had the Legal Authority to rescind this so-called 'privilege.'"

The loophole is "de minimis," which let foreign shippers send packages under $800 into the U.S. duty-free, no tariff, minimal scrutiny. 

Trump killed it by executive order last year. Importers, led by auto parts retailer Detroit Axle, sued at the Court of International Trade arguing the executive branch never had the authority to do that unilaterally. 

Today, they lost.

The loophole cost the U.S. an estimated $10.8 billion in foregone tariff revenue in 2024 alone, and had become a major channel for fentanyl and counterfeit goods slipping past customs. 

Congress had already voted to sunset de minimis by statute regardless, effective July 2027. This ruling just closes the door on reviving it early through the courts.
