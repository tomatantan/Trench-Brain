🇹🇷 Turkey may have sold its Russian-made S-400 air defense system to a Gulf country, and an official announcement is expected today.

This matters because the S-400 was the reason Turkey was removed from the U.S. F-35 fighter program. U.S. laws block Turkey from rejoining while it owns Russian military gear. 

By selling the S-400 to the Gulf, Turkey can claim it complies with U.S. demands, though Russia’s rules on transferring the equipment could still cause problems.
