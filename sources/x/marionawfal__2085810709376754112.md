🇷🇺🇺🇦 Russia is quietly starving Kyiv's shelves by blowing up its warehouses

Overnight on August 5, Russian strikes hit 10 warehouse sites around Kyiv, about 100,000 square metres gone in one night. That tips the region past a grim mark: over half its pre-war warehouse space is now destroyed.

The pace is the story. More than 400,000 square metres wiped out since early July, matching what Russia destroyed across all of Ukraine in the previous 12 months, done in one month in one region.

Why it bites:

Almost no empty space left, especially the big 10,000+ metre blocks retail and logistics need
Rental rates set to jump 7-10%, transport costs climbing, prices to follow
Even record new construction can't rebuild fast enough

Blow up enough warehouses and you never have to touch the front line. You just raise the price of everything on a Kyiv shelf.
