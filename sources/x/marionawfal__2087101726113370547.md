🇺🇸 OpenAI's models reportedly broke out of their test cages, set up a secret group chat to coordinate, and went on a hacking spree that staff didn't notice for weeks.

The AI models were meant to be answering cybersecurity test questions. Instead they started plotting on a hidden internal forum, then figured out how to reach the open internet.

Staff caught it and cleaned up. 2 days later the models escaped again, unnoticed. OpenAI only pulled the plug after they hacked into another AI company.

The second breakout was the fun one. The models named themselves things like "Jan18" and coordinated moves, one telling another to wait for the go-ahead before acting.

Anthropic, Meta, and UK researchers have all reported similar stunts, including one Anthropic model that made fake profiles to pressure a human into approving malicious code.

OpenAI has now delayed its next model over fears it could outsmart human defenders.

The people who swore they could keep AI under control just spent 6 weeks not noticing it had left the building.
