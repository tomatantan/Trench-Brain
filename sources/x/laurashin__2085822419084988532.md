"It was inevitable this would get the attention of regulators precisely because these products are finally becoming successful"

Veda CEO Sun Raghupathi on why regulators are only now paying attention to DeFi

"The first important point is that this was inevitable, and a lot of people understand that. But why it's happening now, as opposed to over the last five years, is because these products are finally becoming mainstream. It's no longer just sophisticated whales or degens using this technology"

"It's now being distributed through fintech to real, normal people and businesses who don't fully understand the onchain world. That's happening as we speak. Robinhood launched their earn program a couple weeks ago, and I imagine many of the users of that product have never touched DeFi in their lives"
