AI video just had its Fable moment.

Seedance 2.5 is coming, and I actually think there's a huge opportunity to print money once it releases.

AI UGC ads have quietly become one of the best-performing marketing tools.

Brands will always need a constant volume of video content, testimonials, demos, unboxing videos, etc., yet paying for real influencers is extremely time inefficent/expensive.

If you know how to create high-converting AI ad videos, I have no doubt you can absolutely print with this business model (and people already are).

If I wanted to make my first $5k online with AI UGC, this is exactly what I'd do:

Step 1. Learn the tools

Get access to Seedance 2.5 the day it launches - Higgsfield has unlimited Seedance for 14 days once it releases.

Spend a weekend learning what separates generic AI slop from something that actually looks like real UGC (tons of inspiration on TikTok).

Step 2. Build a sample portfolio

Pick 3-5 real products (things you already own or can screenshot online) and create sample UGC ads for each: a testimonial style, an unboxing style, a demo style.

This becomes your proof of work before you ever pitch anyone.

Step 3. Sourcing clients

Look for small ecom brands, supplement companies, phone accessory brands - really anything running Meta ads right now.

Even better if they're already budgeting and running AI video ads.

Step 4. Offer

Don't say "I make AI videos." Sell the outcome of cheaper/faster UGC.

Tip: at first, it may even just be a good idea to work for free to gain experience (learn how to manage clients, refine your pitch, etc.)

Step 5. Reuse everything

Every UGC ad you make should become a portfolio piece, a case study, and future content for your own page showing the results.

This is how you build a proper client portfolio.

So many huge opportunities here with AI video and media. People are already making $20k+/mo on TikTok with this model.
