🇺🇦🇷🇺 Is Ukraine the West’s last pawn blocking Russia's shipping?

Ukraine has flipped the naval script in dramatic fashion.

After losing much of its traditional fleet early in the war, Kyiv doubled down on cheap, innovative drones, building a booming domestic industry with hundreds of manufacturers, rapid iteration, and a resilient supply chain that now produces millions of units annually.

Long-range explosive boats like the Sea Baby can loiter for extended periods, use commercial satellite imagery to spot shadow fleet tankers (those turning off transponders to dodge sanctions), approach from behind, and disable engines with precision strikes, all without needing conventional warships.

In just weeks, these tactics have hammered Russian logistics: over 100 vessels hit in the Sea of Azov and expanding into the Black Sea, forcing route shutdowns, fuel shortages in Crimea, and major disruptions to oil, grain, and fertilizer exports.

For Europe, this matters hugely.

Ukraine is effectively acting as the EU’s forward maritime shield, choking off Russian energy and commodity flows that directly impact European markets and energy security.

Russia’s shadow fleet is taking heavy losses, exposing how poorly defended commercial vessels are.

If this campaign scales, the entire Black Sea could become too dangerous for Russian commerce.

Ukraine turned existential defense into asymmetric offense, and it’s reshaping the war at sea while buying Europe critical breathing room.
