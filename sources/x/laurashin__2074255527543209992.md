Parker White responds to the convertible puts cliff Strategy is facing and why he isn't worried

"This is why Strategy is migrating away from converts to preferreds. They don't want these bullet maturities where you have to address a situation all at once. "Even if they needed cash, Matt's analysis is like 10 to 15% of Strategy's balance sheet, and that assumes Bitcoin stays flat all the way until 2028. If that happens, the four year cycle would be completely broken. It also assumes Strategy doesn't do anything at all between now and then"
