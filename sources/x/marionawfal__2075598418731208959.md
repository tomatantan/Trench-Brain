🇺🇸 Cambridge City Council voted 5-2 back in May to shut down ShotSpotter, the acoustic gunshot detection system that pinpoints gunfire.

A system that gets help there quickly, even when nobody calls 911.

Their big reason? "Protecting Black residents and migrants" from "overpolicing" and "racist surveillance." 

In sum, it was performative empathy at its finest.

Fast forward 2 months: Xavier Bautista, a 32-year-old Cambridge DPW worker and dad, gets shot around 4:30 AM on July 4th. 

His body lies there for about an ENTIRE HOUR before a passerby finds him. 

No shots-fired calls came in. 

The area had ShotSpotter coverage before they killed the program.

How the hell you end up judging public safety tech by luxury beliefs is beyond me...

This is textbook "positive" racism, the soft bigotry of low expectations. 

Pretending minority communities need less enforcement instead of more protection from the bad actors in their midst. 

It doesn't help the law-abiding Black and brown residents getting shot or traumatized; it hurts them most. 

Public safety shouldn't be a virtue-signaling experiment. 

The real questions should only be: 

Does the tech work? What's the opportunity cost? Who bears the risk when it's gone? (Spoiler: Not the councilors in their safe bubbles.) 

Time to drop the ideology and prioritize REAL evidence, before more preventable tragedies hit the "welcome sanctuary city."
