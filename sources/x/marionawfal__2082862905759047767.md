🇱🇧🇹🇷 Lebanon's president stood at Atatürk's tomb and described the exact state Lebanon doesn't have

Joseph Aoun landed in Ankara today and went straight to Atatürk's mausoleum, laid a wreath, and wrote in the memorial's golden book.

He praised Atatürk for turning a land "reclaimed through sacrifice into a republic founded on law and institutions," and said Lebanon sees that as a model to emulate.

Coming from the leader of a country that's spent years sharing control with an armed group, that's more than diplomatic flattery. 

Rebuilding a real Lebanese state has been Aoun's whole pitch, and he's in Turkey hunting the backing to pull it off.
