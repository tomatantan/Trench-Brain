ICYMI: All the Alpha from the VVV call with @JonShapeShift from @AskVenice 

Kimi K3 dropped, integrated in Venice within hours. 
How a model is integrated in Venice depends on the provider. K3 is closed-source for now, so Venice has a direct relationship with the Kimi Lab. Since K3 is closed source, you can use Kimi anonymously through Venice, but not privately. When K3 releases open weights, Venice will run the model directly, and K3 will become private and anonymous. 

Venice maintains 20+ vendor relationships for models/inference. Jon says relationship management is arguably more work than the technical integration. 

Inference economics. 
Kimi K3 is now #1 in Code Arena, but at ~90% less inference cost than Fable/Opus 4.6, reigniting the token margins debate. US AI labs charge 40-100x more than Chinese labs, which produce ~90% of the quality. 

Venice has a structural advantage here. Venice doesn't train models, but it still nonetheless sells inference to them. AI labs bear the cost of training runs, and Venice gets to sell them all the same. 

Jon flagged a risk here: Labs pushing for regulatory capture is potentially constraining for the 'open-source squeeze' that Venice represents to leading AI labs. 

VVV Tokenomics: API credit burn

Live on July 17: $5 of every $100 in API/credit purchases now buys and burns VVV, closing the last major inference surface that wasn't touching $VVV Subscription burn was running ~$3k/day from signups. Credit Burn added ~$6k/day, so $VVV burn is now at ~$9–10k/day combined. Extrapolating from today's burn levels: ~$10m $VVV/yr burn rate. 
Jon hinted at the possibility at subscription renewals also adding to the $VVV burn, but emphasized just a possibility. 

We had a cool discussion about how every time Venice intertwines its business with VVV/DIEM, we get a real-time data feed revealing Venice economics and revenue.

Tease: Minds

Jon, Erik, and the Venice team has hinted about a new product called 'Minds'.
Most people suck at prompting, but a few people have incredible fluency about which models are best for which roles. Mind's allow these savvy devs to build 'Minds' - useful, packaged constructions for easy, powerful outputs. 
This opens up Venice to being a developer ecosystem with distribution - like an app-store, that Devs can build experiences on. 

I ask about rev-share for Devs who build highly-used minds... he gave me a wink but nothing more

The Lumara Film Festival from @AskVenice and @moonpay is inspiring more creative use of LLMs. There's a $100,000 competition for the best AI-generated films to be submitted and shown at a NYC screening in October.
