🇻🇪🇺🇸 Trump wants Venezuelan oil pumping now, and Exxon and Chevron aren't biting...

Seven months after Maduro was deposed, Exxon and Chevron still have not committed real money. 

Washington wants barrels flowing before the end of Trump's term, with Hormuz choking off roughly a fifth of the world's oil and gas. 

The prized fields sit in the Orinoco Belt, where heavy crude projects cost billions and run on timelines closer to deepwater development than a quick restart.

Memory is the other obstacle. 

Venezuela seized Exxon's assets in 1976 and again in 2007, and Conoco is still seeking $12 billion while Exxon waits on $1 billion. 

One consultant compared it to a house that draws a hundred people to the open house and then gets no calls. So Washington is turning to smaller private drillers who move faster. 

Output sits near 1.07 million barrels a day, against 3.4 million in 1998.

A supply shock happening this year cannot be answered by fields that pay out next decade.
