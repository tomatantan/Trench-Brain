Glenn Diesen: Washington is still negotiating on paper while everyone else is changing reality on the ground.

The U.S. still believes it can end a war with a signed agreement, then spend the following months renegotiating the parts it doesn't like.

The debate now isn't whether Tehran will have leverage over the world's most important shipping lane, it's how much everyone will end up paying.

Finland joined NATO, Lithuania opened the door to nuclear weapons, while Poland is preparing for a future confrontation.

European leaders call it deterrence, Russia calls it preparation for war.

Countries aren't waiting for diplomacy anymore; they're reshaping the strategic map first and arguing about it afterwards.

That's the thread connecting every conflict we discussed.

Everyone is trying to create facts on the ground that the other side will eventually be forced to accept.
