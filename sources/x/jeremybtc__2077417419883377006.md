Trump's 20% toll on the Strait of Hormuz lasted about 24 hours.

He announced it Monday, demanding 20% of the value of every cargo passing through.

The UN's shipping agency said there was no legal basis for it and shippers called it "fundamentally wrong."

Even his own Secretary of State had called tolls like this a recipe for "total chaos" just weeks earlier.

By Tuesday he scrapped it, replacing the toll with "trade and investment deals" from Gulf states instead.

Same outcome either way. Gulf money still flows to the US. He just found a version that isn't illegal.
