🇮🇷 Iran is teaching its missiles to beat U.S. air defenses.

It's leaning harder on the Kheibar Shekan and getting clever about it, throwing missiles and drones into the same wave and messing with the flight paths and speeds so U.S. defenses have more to sort through.

U.S. officials say they're still catching most of them, but a few are slipping through. That's the part that matters. Every one that lands shows Iran what works, and they keep tweaking from there.

A cheap missile that every now and then beats a multimillion-dollar defense system is exactly the math Iran's hoping for.
