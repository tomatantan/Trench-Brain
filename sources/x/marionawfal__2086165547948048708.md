🇺🇸 Trump told UFO whistleblowers to come forward but the best evidence stays locked up.

Two classified videos allegedly show a 100-foot black triangular craft over a Colorado military base, filmed on a soldier's personal phone on two straight nights in 2023. The footage reportedly shows it zipping over mountains and hiding itself in the clouds.

Both clips are said to be sitting with the Air Force Office of Special Investigations, still classified, even after Trump's new order waiving nondisclosure deals to get more people talking.

The whistleblowers say it's basically for show. "Nothing has changed," said Air Force vet Dylan Borland, who told Congress in 2024 he watched a silent triangular craft hover over a NASA hangar before shooting up to jet altitude in seconds.

Both Borland and another former contractor say they got burned for coming forward, with wrecked careers and smear jobs.

Not everyone's cynical though. David Grusch, the first big UAP whistleblower, welcomed the order and hopes it gives others the nerve to talk.

For now, the footage stays behind the wall. So much for the great UFO reveal.
