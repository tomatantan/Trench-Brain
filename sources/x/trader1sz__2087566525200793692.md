i completely get that...im the same i think we all are.

but then we need to step back and think...is this costing me

if conviction is strong long term...then need to realise what our natureis

for example me personally...when it comes to swing trades...what puts less pressure on me is having clear invalidation to start with. then by default if im correct it shouldnt matter if it goes against me a little..i know what i will lose and i accept the reality that market will move when it wants..not when i want it to

so then i can either start small knowing where my cut off is and scale into set range or scale in and then add as it confirms thesis and bottoms out

either way if  long term target then will make plenty...i tell myself to be content with not being perfect

the only thing we can control is our risk and have solid trade plan for thesis...the rest we have  no control over and just submit to discipline and time

and if it doesnt work thats fine...im losing what im okay with risking and theres always next trade

removing stress and pressure enabled me to make clearer and rational trade decisions instead of having the emotion of being stressed over perfection

just my thouights
