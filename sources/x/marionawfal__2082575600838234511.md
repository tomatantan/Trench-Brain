🇺🇸 The First Circuit lifted the last court block keeping South Sudan's TPS protections in place, following the Supreme Court's June ruling.

DHS had already decided last year that conditions in South Sudan no longer met the legal requirements for TPS. A 3-judge panel, including 2 Biden appointees, allowed the decision to take effect.

South Sudan has been under TPS since 2011, shortly after the country became independent. The program covered only a couple hundred people by this point.

The focus now shifts to how quickly DHS moves to end the remaining protections and what options, if any, remain for those affected.
