🇺🇸🇪🇸🇲🇦 Massie says the House "greenlighted the transfer" of Spanish territory to Morocco 2 weeks before the Ceuta crisis

The vote is real. H.R. 8595 passed 217-209, Republicans 215-1, Massie the lone no.

But the actual text he's citing is committee report language, not the bill itself: the House Appropriations Committee "notes" Ceuta and Melilla sit in Moroccan territory and "supports" diplomatic talks between Morocco and Spain on their status.

That's not a transfer. It's non-binding guidance. No legal force, no change in sovereignty, and the U.S. has no authority to hand over land it doesn't own in the first place.

The real hypocrisy point survives without the exaggeration: Republicans voted for language acknowledging Morocco's claim, then acted blindsided when migrants used that exact claim to storm the border.

Why inflate a real story when the honest version already lands?
