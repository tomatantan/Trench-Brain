🇺🇦🇮🇷 Michael Yon says global conflicts only make sense if viewed through what he calls a broader "depopulation war."

The former war correspondent argues that wars, food systems, public health, and regional instability are not isolated events but part of a single overarching strategy. Drawing on his past research into cults, Yon claims ideological belief systems help explain why, in his view, actors would pursue policies that appear self-destructive.

His framework also predicts expanding conflicts across the Middle East, with flashpoints such as Iraq, Saudi Arabia, and critical infrastructure serving, he argues, a larger agenda rather than isolated geopolitical objectives.

According to Yon, individual wars are just chapters in what he believes is one much bigger conflict.
