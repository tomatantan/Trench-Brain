🇺🇸🇮🇷 Is there any real international legal system left to hold war criminals accountable?

Professor Seyed Mohammad Marandi says the answer is no.

When the United States creates a jungle and imposes the law of the jungle, nations will build their own frameworks for justice to protect themselves.

From Iran’s perspective, people whose children were killed will not accept the perpetrators simply walking away. The state has to reflect that demand.

Marandi points to the daily killing of children in Gaza by pilots and snipers and asks whether they should just get away with it. 

He argues the West blocks any real accountability because it supports the actions, while the rest of the world demands justice by any means necessary.

He reminds that the United States destroyed Iraq, killed a million people, and later called it a “policy mistake.” That, he says, was mass murder.
