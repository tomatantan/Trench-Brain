🇺🇸🇲🇦🇪🇸 Just 2 weeks before 60,000+ migrants crossed from Morocco into Ceuta, Spain, House Republicans passed a bill directing at least $40 million to Morocco.

The accompanying committee report even described Ceuta and Melilla as Spanish-administered cities “located in Moroccan territory” and backing talks over their future status.

The money included at least $20 million in security assistance and another $20 million in military financing. 

Nearly every House Republican voted in favor of it (save for Thomas Massie).

Then Morocco relaxed border controls, and Ceuta was overwhelmed in the blink of an eye.

Was the timing a coincidence, or something else? 🤔
