Gian Sacco lays out VALR's 10x growth bet, and why Africa's open markets are harder to win than Korea's walled one:

"The opportunities global exchanges haven't cracked are in Africa. Korea has a regulatory moat that keeps new entrants out."

"Africa doesn't, but the problems are much harder to solve. You have to know what you're doing."

"Our thesis: Africa is dominated by stablecoin flows. It's well established here that stablecoins are a better way to move money than any other network available." 

"We're doubling down, and that's where we see 10x growth in volumes."

"The second part: access to the most interesting assets in the world is hard here. It's genuinely difficult to invest in Tesla, SpaceX, even raw commodities, in a lot of these markets."
