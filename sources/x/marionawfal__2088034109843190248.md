🇹🇷 Turkey's most famous fictional cop just managed to fail a real drug test.

Erdal Beşik Anadolu mayor of Ankara's Etimesgut district, got himself arrested in a massive corruption sweep. Naturally, his blood, urine, and hair samples all came back positive for weed.

If the name rings a bell, that's because he spent the 2010s playing Behzat Ç., the hard-nosed, rule-breaking Ankara police chief who was basically Turkey's answer to every gritty cop drama.

Here is the best part. Back in 2014, he conveniently told prosecutors on the record that he deeply regretted smoking weed and would never touch it again. Twelve years later, surprise surprise, the lab results say otherwise.

Around 40 suspects got hauled in for the exact same case, and he is officially suspended from office.
