🇪🇺🇷🇺🇺🇦 The EU has transferred another $1.6 billion in profits from frozen Russian assets to support Ukraine.

The money comes from interest earned on immobilized Russian central bank holdings and is the fifth such payment. Combined with earlier transfers, the total now stands at $9.3 billion.

Most of the funds will help Ukraine repay loans, while a smaller portion will go toward military needs. 

EU Chief von der Leyen said the goal is simple: Russia "must pay" for the destruction it has caused.
