🇮🇷 Iran is making clear the Strait of Hormuz stays shut until the U.S. lifts its naval blockade, no matter how the Oman talks go.

FM spokesman Baghaei blames the closure on U.S. and Israeli "aggression" and the use of regional bases against Iran.

He frames the Oman talks as purely technical, just mapping out a safe shipping lane, with nothing to do with the wider political standoff.

Baghaei even cites a UN resolution to brand the ongoing U.S. blockade an "act of aggression."

Bottom line from Tehran: no reopening until the blockade ends, the hostilities stop, and the U.S. pays for the damage.
