We asked @retrimentum if @upshot_cards solves the trust problem with tokenized cards.

First he explained the problem most people are ignoring...

When you buy a card "vaulted" on-chain, you're still trusting a company to actually hold it. You don't know if they're re-hypothecating it, you don't even know for certain the card exists in their vault, and he thinks it spirals as more copycats spawn.

The posed solution: Don't put Pokemon cards on-chain at all. They're reimagining prediction markets as their own trading card game, their own platform, own cards, own predictions.

It takes a different path then relying on a physical vault
