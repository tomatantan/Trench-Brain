🇮🇱 The myth that Israel can stand on its own militarily is getting a fresh round of scrutiny.

For all the talk of a self-sufficient tech powerhouse, Israel can't build an F-35 or anything like it, and more than 30 years of limits have kept it from a fully independent military industry.

It's the biggest cumulative recipient of U.S. aid since WWII, over $300 billion, and still pulls in $3.8 billion a year under the current agreement.

Even former IDF spokesman Jonathan Conricus has now called it "reckless" for Israeli politicians to campaign on breaking away from the U.S., admitting the economy just can't make the heavy gear modern defense needs.

All of this lands as Congress weighs a measure to tie the U.S. and Israeli defense-industrial bases even closer together.
