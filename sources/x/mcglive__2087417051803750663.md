@ColbySaysHi dropped his approach to why builders keep flocking to Prism.

"If I get hit by a bus, anyone can literally take the open source kit and spin up a thousand hydraheads of Spectrum."

Everything's open source, giving builders a head start on both tech and UI, a genuinely good user experience is something Prism treats as a real priority, not an afterthought.

The deeper hook is the shared-venture structure. Prism operates as a mothership of apps ,only one needs to hit it big for the entire flywheel to work, generating fees for everyone building on top rather than forcing each project to fight alone for attention and liquidity.

Layer in V4 hooks still being early, with most of the design space unexplored, plus AI lowering the barrier to actually ship an idea fast and Prism sits right at the center of both trends at once.
