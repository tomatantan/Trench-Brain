"Look at the scoreboard. Ethereum has more than 50% of all stablecoin settlement, over 55% of tokenization. In capital markets, Ethereum is a clear winner"

Joseph Chalom on why Ethereum is the clear winner in capital markets

"I'm the most optimistic person in this industry. I won't bad-mouth other people, but I'll share facts. Ethereum has more than 50% of all stablecoin settlement and activity, I think 10x Solana. Tokenization is over 55% and my guess is it will grow"

"In DeFi land, DeFi was built on Ethereum largely in the early days. High quality DeFi, borrowing, lending, and swaps, is dominated by Ethereum with the Aaves and Morphos of the world. Each chain is gonna offer something unique, and Solana will have a swim lane"

"But in capital markets where you want a decentralized player that's never gone down, no vendor lock-in, and real liquidity, real transactions, not transactions hopping off chain that you then represent on chain, Ethereum is a clear winner. We just need to get our narrative right. It's inertia and storytelling"
