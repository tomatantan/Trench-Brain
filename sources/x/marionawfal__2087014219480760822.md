🚨🇮🇷 🇺🇸Iran's unseen new supreme leader just handed the war to his hardest men...

-The February strikes killed Ali Khamenei along with nearly his entire team of security chiefs, erasing a generation of Iranian military decisionmakers in one stroke

-His son Mojtaba took over and has stayed invisible ever since. U.S. intelligence assesses he is alive but severely injured, top Iranian cabinet members say they have never met him, and Iranian officials insist he was hurt but is in good health

-Sunday and Monday brought the biggest reshuffle of his rule. Mohsen Rezaei, the longest serving commander the Revolutionary Guard ever had, now runs the Supreme National Security Council and was also named Khamenei's personal representative on it

-Brigadier General Ahmad Vahidi takes over the Revolutionary Guard, and he opposes giving Washington anything

-Hossein Taeb was handed the Basij, the volunteer force that crushes dissent, which he already led through the bloody 2009 protest crackdown

-Four more commanders were named, including the head of the Guard's navy, the branch running the attacks on shipping in Hormuz

-Rezaei said over the weekend that Iran will not accept any corridor through the strait it does not control, and he has taken to describing Trump as unhinged

-Argentine authorities have alleged Rezaei was involved in the 1994 bombing of a Jewish cultural center in Buenos Aires that killed 85 people

Rezaei is the one to watch here.

Making him Khamenei's personal representative means a leader almost nobody can reach just picked his voice in the room, and that voice has already ruled out any corridor Iran doesn't control.
