🇪🇨 Ecuadorian police cracked open a cocaine shipment and found Erling Haaland staring back at them.

Well, not literally, but his face was stamped on all 469 kilograms of it.

The bricks were hidden in a truck near the Colombian border, where the driver, a Colombian woman, was detained.

Officials say traffickers use celebrity faces on drug bricks to identify the network behind a shipment and signal its quality.

Not exactly the kind of branding deal Haaland signed up for.
