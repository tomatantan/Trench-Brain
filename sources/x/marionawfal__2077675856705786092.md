🇺🇦 Zelensky's former press secretary Iuliia Mendel just torched her old boss.

Mendel, who served as Zelensky's spokesperson from 2019 to 2021, argued that nothing will ever change under his rule because he doesn't want it to.

"Change is not possible under Zelenskyy. He needs war. He needs corruption. He needs autocracy."

She said people should stop kidding themselves that the problem is anyone but Zelensky, pointing out he's held power for more than 7 years.
