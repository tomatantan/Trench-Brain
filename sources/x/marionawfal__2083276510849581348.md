🇺🇸🇰🇼🇮🇷 The U.S. is eyeing a pullback from Kuwait.

For decades it was the Army’s biggest logistics hub in the region: tanks, artillery, thousands of troops.

It was the ultimate security blanket for Kuwait after Saddam.

The Iran war flipped the script... bases became targets and presence now looks like both a shield and a liability.

Pentagon was already talking drawdowns. Now it’s accelerating. 

Kuwait still wants the U.S. commitment, but the old permanent footprint may not survive.
