🇰🇷 Korean stocks just had a brutal day, with the Kospi closing down 10% and the exchange slamming on a 20-minute trading halt to stop the bleeding.

When a rally gets that hot, it doesn't take much to spook people.

Basically, the market got overheated, sentiment cracked, and the leverage did the rest.
