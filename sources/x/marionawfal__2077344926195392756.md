🇮🇷🇺🇸 An advisor to Iran's parliament speaker is warning that the region "will turn to hell" if the U.S. strikes Pickaxe Mountain.

Mehdi Mohammadi, advisor to Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, called the site "the most fortified nuclear facility in the world" and said it simply can't be touched.

Pickaxe Mountain sits near Natanz and is reportedly buried more than 100 meters deep in granite, one of Iran's most protected nuclear-related sites and believed to have survived earlier strikes.

The warning comes after Trump publicly floated hitting it, saying he'd give it "a nice, big, fat shot" unless Iran opens up fully on its nuclear program.
