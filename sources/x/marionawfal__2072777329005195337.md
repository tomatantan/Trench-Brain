🇺🇸 Olympian charged after touching Trump's $14 million Reflecting Pool

U.S. Olympic canoeist David "Davey" Hearn has been indicted on a felony destruction of property charge after his arrest at the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool.

Hearn says he was on a bike ride and only touched a loose piece of the peeling blue coating out of curiosity.

He denies damaging anything, saying the pool looked the same when he left.

The pool's new $14 million coating began peeling soon after it was installed, with algae also turning the water green.
