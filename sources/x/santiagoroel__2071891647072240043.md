“Errors of omission, lost opportunities, are generally more critical than errors of commission. Organizations fail or decline more frequently because of what they did not do than because of what they did.”

An error of commission is only an error if it is catastrophic. Minimize the tail risk when you set something in motion and an error of commission is valuable as it produces information to iterate and improve the process of selection.
