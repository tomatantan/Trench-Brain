🚨🇺🇸 Tyler Boebert, the 21-year-old son of GOP Rep. Lauren Boebert, was arrested Sunday in Colorado on multiple felony counts of child sexual exploitation.

The charges include inducing or enticing, possession with intent, material involving video, and 2 counts of contributing to the delinquency of a minor. 

The alleged conduct dates back to early 2024. 

He was released the same day after posting a $10,000 bond, and is due back in court August 19. This comes as he prepares for sentencing next month in 2 prior cases involving both child abuse and vehicle-related thefts. 

Boebert said she loves her son, takes the allegations seriously, and is praying for everyone affected. 

The timing adds immense pressure to her reelection campaign in a competitive district, but if anyone can overcome a controversy... it’s Boebert.
