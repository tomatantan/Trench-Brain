🇮🇷 Khamenei's funeral procession has reached Qom, the beating heart of Shiite Islam in Iran.

The body of the slain supreme leader and his family members arrived at Jamkaran Mosque ahead of Tuesday morning's funeral prayers, per Iranian state TV, after thousands packed Tehran's streets Monday to honor the leader killed in the joint U.S.-Israeli strikes of February 28.

From here the procession crosses into Iraq for Najaf and Karbala, Shiite Islam's two holiest cities, before the final burial in his birthplace of Mashhad. 

A six-day, two-nation farewell built to sanctify the man whose death began the war, and to bind his memory to the faith's holiest ground.
