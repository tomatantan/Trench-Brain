AI is the largest deflationary force in human history.

If it plays out the way I think it will, we get something we haven't felt in a very long time... people feeling richer for the first time in decades.

You can hand out subsidies, cut taxes, and print money but none of it lifts real living standards.

The only thing that has ever moved that needle is a productivity boom big enough to collapse debasement into abundance.

Now, I've been through enough cycles to know this won't be clean... there’ll be political fights, sectors slowing while others rip higher, and industries stuck on the wrong side of the cycle. But the setup is real and once productivity kicks in the outcome takes care of itself.

This is exactly what the Exponential Age is. It isn't about AI being better than us. It's about the productivity curve going vertical and the whole economy finally getting to breathe.
