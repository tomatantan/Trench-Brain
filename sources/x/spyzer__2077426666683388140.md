Don’t fall asleep in your comfort zone in life. I know people that have a mentality of ‘it will come by its own means’. Like finding a future girlfriend. She’ll be perfect and will approach him herself at the perfect occasion. Or they wanna get rich yet do nothing in order to achieve that. ‘’It will happen naturally’’

It’s so anoying to see happen because what they’re essentially doing is going fishing on their boat with 0 lines out. Hoping that the fish will jump in the boat themselves. But what are the chances right?

The people who are rich, successful, more happy in life are the ones that have the most fishing lines out. One is a sidehustle, another is working on networking, another is trying new things, another is grabbing an opportunity heads on when it arises, another is taking risk. 

A moving man will surely meet his luck. Life is a game of probabilities. Why are you scared to start that business? Someone told me it was because he didn’t know what to do. Well, noone does at the beginning. The winning formula is about starting the process, staying consistent with it & working towards improvement. That’s literally it. 

Don’t be scared to try and risk. One life is all we got. You are capable of so much more than you think. 

In the game of life it’s you vs you.
