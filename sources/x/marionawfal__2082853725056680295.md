🇷🇺 Russia put Telegram's founder on its terrorist list for refusing to hand YOU over...

Rosfinmonitoring, Russia's financial watchdog, added Pavel Durov to its terrorist list, a day after the FSB issued an arrest warrant. 

His accounts are frozen, though Moscow left him 10,000 rubles a month, roughly $120, for food and taxes. Terrifying punishment for a guy worth billions.

Telegram is where about 90 million Russians talk without the Kremlin reading over their shoulder, and Durov wouldn't hand that over. 

The charge is "aiding terrorism," tied to channels the FSB says Ukraine used for sabotage inside Russia.

Russia's so rattled by an app it reached for its terrorism statute...
