🇺🇸 America's emergency oil reserve has been drained to its lowest level since 1983, and the numbers leave little room left.

Former CIA analyst Larry Johnson lays out a scenario where the U.S. burns through what's left and hits a wall: keep the warplanes fueled, or keep diesel flowing to the trucks that stock grocery shelves.

"The United States is going to have to make a choice. Is it going to be guns or butter?"

The reserve has fallen to roughly 340 million barrels, draining close to 9 million a week, with the Strait of Hormuz still choking off the heavy crude U.S. refineries need to make diesel.
