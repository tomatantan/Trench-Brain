The CLARITY act represents a ton of bi-partisan work to finally establish clear rules for crypto in America, which 70% of Americans say we should already have.

Whether people love crypto or hate it, there is broad consensus that we need clear rules to protect consumers, give new tools to law enforcement to stop bad actors, and to bring crypto within the U.S. regulatory perimeter so it stops going offshore where we have no control.

The bill even contains many pages of new authorities for banks to integrate stablecoins and crypto and grow their business, which we strongly support. This is about America getting back to winning, and remaining strong as a financial and technology hub, to create economic growth, jobs, and tax revenue.

This week we get to see who in the Senate represents the will of the people, and will step up to vote YES on this common sense legislation.
