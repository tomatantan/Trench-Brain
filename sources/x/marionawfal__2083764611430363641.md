🇺🇸 Big Oil posted record Q2 profits.

But the real risk is U.S. export policy changing.

For years American shale producers have relied on the world to soak up their record output. 

That export outlet kept domestic production roaring and turned the U.S. into one of the biggest energy exporters on the planet.

Limit those exports and the math flips fast:

- Domestic supply floods the market
- U.S. crude prices get crushed
- Producers are forced to slash drilling and investment plans

The fallout wouldn’t stop at the oil companies; Refinery margins, gasoline and diesel prices, global supply chains, and the geopolitical leverage America gained through energy exports would all take a hit.

Markets used to be driven by supply and demand, now they’re being driven by government decisions.

Whether the policy shift actually happens or not, the conversation itself shows how fragile the current setup has become.
