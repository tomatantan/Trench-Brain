Many people believe harbor seals are responsible for declining salmon populations in Washington. But the rescue organization treating a 3-week-old seal pup shot in the head near Bellingham says that belief is based on misinformation and may be contributing to violence against seals.

According to Sealife Response, Rehabilitation, and Research (SR3), this is the third harbor seal shot in Washington in the past 11 months. The first two did not survive. While this pup is showing signs of recovery, he remains in critical condition.

A petition is calling on the NOAA and U.S. Fish and Wildlife to open a formal investigation and prosecute whoever is responsible.
