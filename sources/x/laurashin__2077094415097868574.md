Prophet, Co-founder of MetaDAO, on why voting doesn't work and markets make better decisions

"MetaDAO has a pretty opinionated take on this, which is that voting doesn't really work and you need an alternative, and we use decision markets. Voter turnout is not intrinsically an issue. The main thing we want from a governance system is making good decisions, and people showing up is actually a cost. The best scenario is we make all the right decisions and just one person shows up and spends the least amount of effort"

"The reason we want turnout in a voter-based system is because otherwise you can get capture. Capture is best illustrated by the 51% attack. If I have 51% of a token supply, I may benefit from voting the whole treasury to myself even if it's not in the interest of the DAO. If it's a $10 million treasury and I have 5 million tokens worth $5 million, even if I completely nuke the DAO, I'd benefit $10 million by voting the whole treasury to myself"

"What we use instead is markets to make decisions. Instead of people voting, they trade on what would be good for the value of the token. It means you have a direct incentive to participate, which is that you can make money, the same incentive to participate in any market. If other people are wrong, you can make money by trading against them"
