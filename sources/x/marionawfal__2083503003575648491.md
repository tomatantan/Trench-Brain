🇨🇳🇺🇸 China is quietly turning space into America's biggest military headache

For decades, the U.S assumed it would always dominate the ultimate high ground. China has spent the last decade building a sprawling orbital network designed to watch U.S forces, track carriers, sharpen missile targeting, and potentially disable the systems America depends on to fight.

Beijing now has more than 500 intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance satellites available to its military, a BeiDou navigation network that can rival GPS, and increasingly sophisticated radar, infrared, laser-communication, and anti-satellite capabilities.

The uncomfortable part is that China does not need to defeat the U.S military everywhere. It just needs to find the ships, feed the coordinates into long-range weapons, and disrupt the satellites guiding America’s response.

Even Chinese commercial firms are reportedly publishing analyses of U.S carrier and bomber movements, with one company sanctioned for allegedly supplying imagery that helped Iran target American forces.

For decades, U.S power projection depended on seeing everything while assuming the enemy could not do the same.

China appears determined to make that assumption age about as well as a Pentagon procurement schedule.
