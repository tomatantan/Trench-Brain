🇺🇸 ICE is planning to spend up to $20 million to equip its officers with gloves that deliver painful electric shocks straight to a person's skin.

The devices, actually acronymed GLOVE (Generated Low Output Voltage Emitter), work like normal patrol gloves until the officer hits a button to switch on the electrical mode. 

Unlike Tasers, they leave no burn or contact marks.

New York AG Letitia James said she's "outraged," and a former ICE acting director warned it's "too easy for this to get misused against a population that doesn't pose a threat."

The manufacturer itself says the gloves shouldn't be used on the elderly, small children, or pregnant women.
