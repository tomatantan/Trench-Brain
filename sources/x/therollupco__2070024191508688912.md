Andy (@andyyy) asks Chainlink’s Andrew (@AndrewMcMarkets) how institutional crypto adoption has changed.

“Two years ago, a lot of firms were asking, ‘Should we do something in this space?’”

“A year ago, it was, ‘What should we do?’ And now it’s, ‘How do we do it?’”

“Everybody has realized this is where this is going.”

“This is not about throwing away the existing system. It’s about making it better, stronger, more accessible, faster, and more efficient.”

“Whether you’re the biggest bank or a couple college guys in a dorm room, this is the technology people have been looking for.”
