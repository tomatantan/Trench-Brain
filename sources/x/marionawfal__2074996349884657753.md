🇮🇷🇺🇸 "We Will Kill Trump" banner appears in Mashhad

As Khamenei is set to be buried in his hometown of Mashhad today, a "We Will Kill Trump" banner has gone up on Imam Reza Street, the road running past the shrine where he'll be laid to rest, according to Mehr News.

It follows days of the same messaging along the Tehran funeral route, where mourners carried placards calling for Trump's death, a bullet drawn through his name, and at one point an effigy of him hanged.

The anger traces back to the February strike that killed the Supreme Leader, carried out by the U.S. and Israel.
