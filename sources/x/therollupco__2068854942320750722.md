Andy asked Alexander how important programmatic buybacks and token economics really are for a project like Aerodrome.

Alexander: "the bar is still shockingly low."

“About 90% of the top 100 tokens don’t redistribute any value.”

That is why he thinks the top 100 could get actively remade by tokens with real utility, real revenue, and real value flows.

“Anybody experimenting in the space of redistributing value, you’re already ahead of the curve.”
