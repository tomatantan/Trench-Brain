🇺🇸 Back in April 2020, Zuckerberg went on GMA and laid out how Facebook was turning its billions of users into a real-time COVID tracking network.

They partnered with Carnegie Mellon to run symptom surveys, then turned the aggregate data into daily county-by-county maps so officials could see where the virus might be heating up.

Individual answers never went to Facebook, just the big-picture signals.

It was one of the first major attempts to weaponize social media scale against a pandemic while everyone was still stuck at home.
