🇨🇺 Trump admin is turning up the heat on Cuba and quietly looking for someone new to run Havana.

Washington wants the island to open its economy, let in more U.S. businesses, buy American oil, and dial back the Russian and Chinese intelligence presence.

In exchange, they’re offering more humanitarian aid and some sanctions relief.

The catch? U.S. intelligence says there aren’t many pragmatists available. A lot of the possible replacements are even more hard-line.
