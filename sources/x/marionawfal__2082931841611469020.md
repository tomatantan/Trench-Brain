🇺🇸 Infantino's cash grab is getting rejected so fast even the hosts noped out

CONCACAF runs soccer across North America and the Caribbean, and that includes the U.S., Canada, and Mexico, the three World Cup hosts. 

They just told Infantino no on his plan to sell off chunks of the World Cup to private investors.

They didn't go full boycott like UEFA did hours earlier, when all 55 European members voted to walk away from every FIFA tournament. 

But CONCACAF's 41 members still ripped the deal apart, no proper process, a deadline rushed to hell, and nobody in FIFA's own governance even signing off on it.

So that's two big blocs killing it in a single day, 96 votes against already, and Asia looks ready to pile on. Infantino needs all six regions with him, and his $40 million-per-country sweetener isn't buying the room.

This is the same guy who wore FIFA-branded sneakers with a suit to the final, so subtlety was never really his thing.
