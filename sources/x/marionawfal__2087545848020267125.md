🇺🇸🇨🇴 The U.S. is sending urban search-and-rescue teams to Colombia, after this week’s devastating 7.4 magnitude earthquake.

At the request of President Abelardo de la Espriella’s administration, the State Department has mobilized specialized units from Fairfax County, VA and Los Angeles, California.

The teams will support ongoing search and rescue operations, as thousands are still reported missing.

Washington says it remains ready to provide additional assistance as Colombia assesses its full needs.
