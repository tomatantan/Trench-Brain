🇸🇴 Somali police opened fire on their own people in Mogadishu, wounding a woman.

Residents in the Waaberi district were protesting forced evictions and land grabs tied to the regime of Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, the corrupt and illegitimate leader who clings to power after illegally extending his mandate and stalling the elections.

Footage shows the Turkey-trained Haramcad unit firing into the crowd.

At least one woman was hit and rushed to hospital.

These are the same forces accused again and again this year of turning their guns on protesters instead of Al-Shabaab, the al-Qaeda-linked terrorist group they were meant to be fighting.

The land grabs are the story underneath the story. This is a government that seizes the ground its people live on, then shoots them when they object.

And it keeps happening. In May, police killed a civilian at eviction protests in the same city.

This is what a regime does when it fears its own people more than the terrorists it was meant to fight.
