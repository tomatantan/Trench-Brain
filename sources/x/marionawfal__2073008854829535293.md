🇵🇾 Paraguay just teamed up with Starlink to connect 1,600 schools to high-speed internet.

The deal brings fast internet to more than 50,000 students and teachers, especially in the country's most remote regions where reliable service has never really existed. 

President Peña called it a step toward a Paraguay where the place you are born does not decide the opportunities you get.

The 1,600 connectivity kits will also reach health centers in isolated communities, and this builds on a 2025 agreement targeting up to 500 rural schools and health centers, with the rollout already underway.

One nice detail: the metal structures for the satellite antennas will be built by prison inmates, which officials framed as a chance for rehabilitation and giving people a way to contribute.

Starlink now connects more than 12 million customers across 160+ countries. @elonmusk keeps quietly closing the digital divide while everyone argues about him online.
