🇺🇸 Trump's interim Air Force One, donated by Qatar, isn't as free or as owned by the U.S as he would like you to believe.

The $400 million jet was a free gift, but the cost of retrofitting it for use by Trump after it arrived in the U.S cost taxpayers at least another $400 million, with some estimates as high as $1 billion.

While it was a gift to the U.S, Trump has said that after the new Air Force One is delivered by Boeing (the Qatar plane is a temporary stopgap until the new one arrives in 2028) the Qatar plane will be transferred to his Presidential Library foundation.

So while the smoke and mirrors make people think it's a free gift to the U.S, it's actually an expensive one that Trump gets to keep when he leaves office.
