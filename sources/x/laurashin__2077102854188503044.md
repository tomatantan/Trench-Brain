"We're not pro governance, we're pro oversight. The goal isn't to get communities deeply involved in operations, it's to provide a check"

Prophet, Co-founder of MetaDAO, on what actually makes a token investable again

"Tokens as they existed are losing their trust. There's a few specific attack vectors. We have scenarios where people raise money and just disappear with it, that's the base case for an ICO. Then we have the token equity split issue, where you as a token holder want to know that if the business becomes valuable, you actually have some stake in that"

"You want to know it's not just siphoned off by some labs entity that gets to take all the revenue and internally distribute the profit. So you have this token structure, legal structure, and treasury structure to help mitigate some of those risks"

"We're definitely not pro governance, we have the saying that we're pro oversight. The goal is not to get communities deeply involved in the operations of these companies, it's mainly to provide a check and to provide trust and confidence in the tokens, because that's what makes them investable and allows people to align themselves with companies in this way"
