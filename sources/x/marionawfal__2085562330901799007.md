🇨🇳 China opened a real school for robots, and the first class of 30 is already enrolled.

The Hangzhou facility puts humanoid “students” through physical check-ups, specialized coursework, and a final skills exam before handing them official certificates.

Tracks cover industrial work, security, customer service, entertainment, and even medicine and the arts.

Once they pass, the robots are sent straight into real jobs.

This is more than a gimmick. China is racing to move humanoids out of demo halls and into the actual workforce.

The school standardizes training so the same software and skills can be copied across machines, speeding up deployment.

In a country already pushing hard on embodied AI, teaching robots how to hold a job is the next logical step.
