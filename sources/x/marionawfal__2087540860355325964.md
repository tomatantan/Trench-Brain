🚨🇺🇸 CPI “cooled” to 3.4%, but rates aren’t budging and the real story is still the money supply.

Core ticked up 0.2%. Shelter still carried a big chunk of the increase. 

Energy dropped on the month but remains way higher than a year ago.

Reality check? 

3.4% is still nearly double the Fed’s 2% target, and after years of price levels that never came back down, most households are still feeling the cumulative hit. 

Real wages have been under pressure for stretches this year, and the Fed has shown zero appetite for cutting anytime soon. 

Most economists and the futures market are pricing a hold through the rest of 2026, with some even floating the possibility of another hike if data stays sticky.

True Money Supply has been expanding again after earlier contractions, hitting multi-year highs earlier in 2026. 

The fact that rates are stuck elevated while official CPI “improves” just means the Fed is still managing the symptoms of its own earlier money creation.

CPI is a lagging, manipulated average of selected prices, not the green light the optimists want it to be...
