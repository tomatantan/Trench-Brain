"Bitcoin got so much bigger that it even became bigger than crypto really"

Kain on how Bitcoin outgrew the people who once set its culture

"There are people who care about Bitcoin that don't care about crypto at all, don't care about any of this stuff, don't care about Taproot Wizards or fucking Ordinals or any of these things. They just see it as an asset class"

"Bitcoin keeps working because it is a good asset and people keep mining it, and all the nonsense purity tests have literally no impact. It's too big for the people who once, I wouldn't say controlled it, but at least set the vibes of the place. They're irrelevant to the thing now"

"That's a bit different sociologically, because usually the people who started the movement become so powerful at the end. But Bitcoin has actually done the thing, it's so decentralized and so resistant to capture that you can have a bunch of crackheads running around doing crackhead things, and no matter how much the OG Bitcoiners like them or don't like them, it doesn't matter, it's irrelevant"
