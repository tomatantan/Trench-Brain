We asked @NiceGuyShri how @crediblefin turns profit...

Credible keeps about 0.20% of every transaction (20 basis points). Sounds tiny, but it's 4x what his old company made, they ran on card payments, where the bank, the card network, and the acquirer each skim a cut until almost nothing's left.

Credible skips all that by using cheaper rails like Pix (instant bank transfers) instead of cards. 

As Credible gets its own licenses, it removes the regulated partners who currently take a share, keeping more of that 0.20%.
