🇵🇪 A tourist plane crashed near Peru’s Nazca Lines today, killing all 13 people on board.

The Cessna Caravan, operated by Aerodiana, was on a low-altitude scenic flight over the famous geoglyphs with 11 passengers and 2 pilots. No survivors.

These overflights are popular because they give the best views of the ancient desert drawings, but they have a troubled safety record, previous fatal crashes in 2010 and 2022 already forced tighter rules.

The cause of today’s accident is still under investigation.
