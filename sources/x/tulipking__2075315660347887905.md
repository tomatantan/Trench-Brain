Crypto must save AI: the same guys who wrote AI 2027 just shared their policy wishlist to keep us on the "good path", it's as dystopian as you can imagine: export controls, hardware spying, tracking large pools of compute, and building an AI government

I see all this as like very likely but obviously not good. The key insight here is that they can't really monitor software or stop you from downloading open-weight models, the field is too vast. The practical aim is to both: stop open-weight models from being developed outside the purview of the government and to ration compute such that even if an OW model was made & you got your hands on it, you couldn't run it

Despite prices and a much longer journey still ahead, i'd argue Bitcoin and crypto has succeed in creating outside money. If your government moves to hyper inflate or repress you, you should be able to get your hands on Bitcoin or Private Coins or alternative fiat Stablecoins (centralized or decentralized). When the time comes, you have to trust the technology is here ready to receive you

Now it's time to do the same for AI. We need to find a way accumulate and coordinate a gargantuan pool of MatMul (or other) to secure our access to intelligence in the same way the accumulated pool of SHA256 ensures the security of and access to our money. Without exaggeration, this is the next trillion dollar coin

I don't know what it is yet, i'm trying to put a few coals in the fire, but i'm committing to this as a focus. There is another Bitcoin-Like opportunity out there to both make spectacular money & improve the human condition, you just have to find (or built) it
