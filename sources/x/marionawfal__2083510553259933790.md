🇺🇸🇵🇸 A 45-year-old Turkish resident has been arrested in the UK on U.S. charges alleging he used a charity to funnel tens of millions of dollars to Hamas since 2023.

According to U.S. prosecutors, this wasn't just about moving money. The alleged operation involved arranging cash deliveries to Hamas, importing supplies into Gaza, trucking materials in from Egypt and elsewhere, delivering them to warehouses allegedly controlled by Hamas, distributing aid according to Hamas-provided lists, and even disguising the true destination of the shipments by concealing who controlled the warehouses.

If the allegations are proven, it raises an uncomfortable question: how many "humanitarian" networks were actually functioning as Hamas' logistics department?
