The U.S. and Iran signed the same document, read it completely differently, and now the Middle East is on fire again...

Former JCPOA negotiator Robert Malley broke it all down for me:

Paragraph five, on Hormuz administration, was the fault line. Iran read it as confirmation of their control over the strait, the U.S. read it as guaranteeing free flow of commerce.

The moment either side tested that ambiguity, the deal was ALWAYS going to unravel.

"You could drive an aircraft carrier through the ambiguities, every paragraph could be read in a thousand different ways."
