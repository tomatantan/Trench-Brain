🇮🇷  Iran's highest military alert may be less about preparing for war than preparing its response

Middle East analyst Nima Rostami says Tehran was expecting a U.S. strike and believes any future attack on Iran's energy infrastructure would likely trigger a response focused on Israel rather than Gulf states.

He argues Gulf energy facilities remain highly vulnerable, warning that oil infrastructure presents a more likely target than civilian assets such as desalination plants, which he believes Tehran would avoid crossing as a red line.

"The fragility of the situation for the Gulf countries is enormous."
