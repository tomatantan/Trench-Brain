🇺🇸 Hundreds of passengers got stuck on a NJ Transit train in the Hudson River tunnel around 2 a.m.

A burning smell hit right before the power went out, leaving the packed train without AC in the humid heat for nearly 2 hours.

Many were coming back from the Chris Brown and Usher concert at MetLife Stadium.

Firefighters responded, and officials ditched the idea of walking people out on the tracks because it was too complicated.

Rough end to a big night, but everyone was eventually rescued safely.
