🇫🇮🇪🇸 Ministers in Finland's government are backing Italian PM Giorgia Meloni's threat to SUSPEND the Schengen Agreement with Spain.

It comes after the unprecedented wave of migrants that poured into the Spanish enclave of Ceuta, yesterday.

Mari Rantanen and Riikka Purra said member states that do not adequately guard the external border to the EU should lose their place in the passport-free zone.

Meloni says stronger measures are needed, as arrivals in Spain continue to rise, and its government JUST granted legal status and work permits to around 500,000 undocumented migrants...

Other EU countries are fearful of "secondary movement" (migrants informally overstaying in other EU countries).
