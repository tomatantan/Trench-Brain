🇸🇾🇷🇺 Syria announced a deal to take control of Russia’s 2 main military bases in the country.

Damascus will run the transport facilities at the Hmeimim air base and Tartus naval base on the Mediterranean coast. 

Russian sites will turn into joint training centers after a 3-month transition.

This largely ends Moscow’s long military foothold in Syria. 

Russia had hoped to keep the bases after its ally Bashar al-Assad was overthrown in 2024.

The move comes as the U.S. is in the process of removing Syria from its state sponsors of terrorism list. Russia has not yet commented.
