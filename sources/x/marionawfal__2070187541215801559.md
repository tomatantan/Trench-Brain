🇱🇧🇸🇦 The first commercial shipment from Beirut to Riyadh in years just left port and it was roses

Lebanese roses heading to Saudi Arabia while the ceasefire holds, the Geneva talks slowly inch forward in Muscat now, and the region tries to figure out what normal looks like after everything that just happened.

It's a small thing. But Saudi Arabia cutting off Lebanese imports was part of the broader regional freeze that came with Hezbollah's dominance of Lebanese politics and economics. 

Resuming them, starting with something as deliberate and symbolic as roses, is its own kind of signal about where Riyadh thinks things are headed.

Sometimes the most meaningful geopolitical statements arrive in a refrigerated truck.
