🇮🇶 Massive fire raging out of control on Baghdad’s Al-Zilal Street

A major commercial complex and surrounding warehouses are fully engulfed in flames. The blaze has now spread to adjacent buildings, forcing the evacuation of nearby residents as Civil Defense teams struggle to contain it. 

Al-Zilal Street, near Palestine Street, is a dense commercial hub packed with electrical goods, refrigeration equipment, and storage units, making it highly vulnerable to fast-spreading fires. 

The area has seen similar incidents in recent years, with massive financial losses but usually limited casualties. Thick smoke is billowing as firefighters battle on. No confirmed casualties yet, but the situation remains critical.

Another reminder of safety challenges in Baghdad’s busy commercial districts.
