🇩🇪🇦🇺 Deutsche Bank fined for misreporting 260,000 trades

That's 260,000-plus over-the-counter trades, the private ones done off the open market, all logged wrong.

Why does Australia get a say over a German bank? Deutsche runs an Australian arm, and any trade booked through it has to follow Australian rules, including reporting each one accurately.

The fine for getting that wrong? About $1.3 million, or 2 million in Australian dollars.

They messed up the 'direction' field, basically whether each trade was a buy or a sell, across currencies and commodities for about ten months.

Australia's regulator, ASIC, went further and called the failures systemic, a flaw baked right into the bank's reporting.

Deutsche says it's cooperating and cleaning it up, and stayed quiet when asked for comment.

For a bank this big, $1.3 million is couch-cushion money. The word that stings is "systemic."
