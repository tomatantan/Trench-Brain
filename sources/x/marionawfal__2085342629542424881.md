🚨🇩🇪🇷🇺 German authorities have foiled a suspected Russian plot to kill Stefan Thumann, boss of drone manufacturer Donaustahl.

Two suspects, a Ukrainian man and a Romanian woman, allegedly surveilled his home and company HQ from Dec. 2025-March 2026, on Moscow’s orders.

The pair were arrested in coordinated raids in Spain and Germany. Now charged as foreign agents.

Thumann is under heavy police protection, as Donaustahl remains a key drone supplier to Ukraine.
