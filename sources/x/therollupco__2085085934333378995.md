Rob says CLARITY is a catalyst whether it passes or fails, because the market just wants the uncertainty gone.

"The market likes certainty. Right now it's uncertain, we simply don't know if the Clarity Act goes through. But it doesn't matter whether it does or not."

"It can pass and the market pumps. It can fail and the market still pumps. Because what the market wants is to know, so it can re-rate and reprice." 

"Uncertainty is what keeps us in this low-liquidity, low-volatility, sidelines environment."

"Once this reaches consensus, either way, the trend emerges and liquidity returns. And it does feel like liquidity is starting to come back."
