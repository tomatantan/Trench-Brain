I see a lot of people who’ve been in crypto for years starting to lose at a faster pace than some newcomers. I think it comes down to one key factor:

The inability to pivot.

Evolution is part of growth - whether in relationships, your career, or as a part-time trader.

Imagine spending a lifetime with your spouse. They’re going to grow and change over the years. How successful will your relationship be if you refuse to grow alongside them?

The markets are no different.

Crypto isn’t the same market it was when you first entered. It’s not even the same market it was a year ago. Old playbooks stop working. Historical truths can become present-day fiction.

The traders who thrive the most aren’t necessarily the smartest or the ones with the highest IQ. They’re the ones willing to challenge their own assumptions, abandon strategies that no longer have an edge, and adapt before the market eats them alive.

Markets do not reward nostalgia. In many cases they punish it. 

Never become emotionally attached to a strategy simply because it worked in the past. Be loyal to the pursuit of truth - not to your own beliefs.
