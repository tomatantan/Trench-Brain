🇪🇺 More than 40 top EU legal scholars want the bloc to ban imports from Israeli settlements.

In a legal opinion to von der Leyen, Kaja Kallas, and the trade commissioner, they say the settlements are unlawful under international law.

Their argument is that trade policy, not the EU's slower foreign policy track, is the right way to restrict commerce with them.

They also reject the idea that a ban needs every member state's approval, calling that a political reading, not a legal one, and point to the EU's own curbs on Russian gas as precedent.

The settlements have been illegal for decades. They are now asking to finally put it in the customs paperwork.
