One thing a bear market does that nobody talks about enough is f with the size of your thinking.

In a bull market your brain gets used to seeing 7 8 figures.

Then the market turns.

8 figures become 7.
7 becomes 6.
Then 5.

Eventually you're fighting for amounts you wouldn't even have looked at before.

Your wallet got smaller, but the real danger is when your mind gets smaller with it.

I'm not saying spend like you're still rich. That's fucking stupid.

I'm saying don't let your circumstances convince your brain that this is your new ceiling.

Keep people around you who still talk in millions. Who build in millions. Who think bigger than the situation they're currently in.

Because once survival becomes your normal state, you start making survival-sized decisions.

I'd rather sit alone and think big than spend every day around people teaching my brain to think small.

Money comes and goes.

Don't let your mind go with it.
