🇮🇱 Ben-Gvir holds barely 10% of parliament, but a third of Israel loves his message... That's the point.

Alexander Christoforou flags a revealing gap in Israeli politics.

Itamar Ben-Gvir controls only about 7 to 10% of the seats in parliament, yet roughly 30 to 35% of Israelis view him favorably.

To Christoforou, that disconnect explains everything. 

Ben-Gvir isn't some rogue element going off-script, he's filling a deliberate role, delivering the hardline message that lands with a big chunk of the electorate.

And here's the part that matters: both the Netanyahu and Trump administrations are the ones handing him that platform, effectively letting him serve a strategic purpose in the political arena.
