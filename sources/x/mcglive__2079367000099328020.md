Most people don't know Hot Wheels has a rarity system as deep as Pokemon...

@allocateur broke it down:

Every retail box of 72 cars usually hides one "super treasure hunt," a $1 car you can flip for $100-300. There are also regular treasure hunts ($20-50), which is why you'll find people at Target on restock day hunting them.

Above those: chases (premium cars that hit $500+, heavier, more detailed), and Red Line Club (RLC) cars, limited runs of 3,000-10,000 with published quantities.

With RLC you know exactly how many exist (say, 7,000). With Pokemon, the print runs are often a mystery.

Known scarcity is the whole game.
