🇺🇸 The FAA has ordered inspections of hundreds of Boeing 737 MAX aircraft after structural cracks found on older 737s raised concerns that the issue could also affect the newer fleet.

No cracks have been found on any 737 MAX jets so far, but the directive covers 471 aircraft in the U.S. and 1,429 worldwide as a precaution.

Boeing's 737 program has already been defined by the 2018–2019 MAX crashes that killed 346 people and the 2024 Alaska Airlines door-plug blowout, making every new safety directive closely watched. 

The aircraft haven't been grounded for now.
