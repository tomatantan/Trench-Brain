Tesla took Cybercab to the National Federation of the Blind’s convention.

Honestly, probably the best place in the world to show off a driverless car.

These are the people who could gain real independence from it.

Braille controls.
Room for service animals.
Space for assistive devices.
Seats at a better height for wheelchair transfers.

A car that drives itself is impressive.

A car that helps someone go where they want, when they want, without needing a driver?

That is freedom.
