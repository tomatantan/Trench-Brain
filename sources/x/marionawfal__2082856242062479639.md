🚨🇪🇸🇲🇦 The crisis in Ceuta, Spain has been declared an “absolute humanitarian and social emergency.”

Or at least, it was by Ceuta's regional president, Juan Jesús Vivas.

Under Sánchez's Spain, cross the Med and you're basically an "EU citizen to be" (and, most importantly, one of his voters).
