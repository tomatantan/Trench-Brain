🇺🇸 The U.S. is loosening its radiation safety rules.

The Nuclear Regulatory Commission wants to scrap ALARA, the rule that pushes plants to keep radiation exposure as low as physically possible, not just under a legal limit.

It's part of Trump's drive to build reactors faster and cheaper. The regulator says it's about clarity, not weaker safety.

The Union of Concerned Scientists sees it differently, warning the change lets plants expose workers and the public to more cancer-causing radiation just to save the industry money.

The rule isn't final yet, the public gets 45 days to push back before it locks in.
