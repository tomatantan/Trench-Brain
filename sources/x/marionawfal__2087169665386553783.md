🚨🇩🇪 Germany is preparing to give its domestic intelligence service real operational teeth...

Interior Minister Dobrindt wants to transform the Verfassungsschutz from a largely passive monitoring agency into one that can *actively* disrupt threats.

Under the planned changes, the service would be allowed to interfere with data traffic, deploy spyware, infiltrate networks, and carry out offensive cyber ops against foreign infrastructure.

The goal is to move beyond surveillance and start shutting down extremist and foreign interference activities at the source.

Dobrindt has described it as turning Germany’s intelligence services into “real secret services” capable of competing with partner agencies and responding more effectively to hybrid threats, sabotage, espionage, and extremism.

The reform is part of a broader overhaul of intelligence laws, which have raised constitutional and privacy concerns among Germany's citizens.
