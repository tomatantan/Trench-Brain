Guillaume Lambert says LPs are the real risk takers behind every DEX trade.

“There’s always someone in the background taking some risk.”

“The LPs that provide liquidity put their tokens at risk.”

“They’re providing a service and they get paid whatever the swappers pay them in fees.”

“On the LP side, you are providing this service that is continuous, perpetual, never stops.”

“LPs are the main carrier of whether liquidity exists.”

“Unless you are careful with how you reward them or take revenues away from them, it can get more difficult for LPs.”
