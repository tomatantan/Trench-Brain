🇺🇸 AI is flooding the feeds… and people can smell it a mile away.

A new study dropped hard numbers: 41% of long-form posts on LinkedIn are now fully AI-generated. 

Reddit sits at 13%, Substack at 10%, and even short-form is getting hit: 30% on LinkedIn, 12% on Substack.

However, AI posts on LinkedIn get 45% less engagement than human ones.

The machines are pumping out volume at a rate we’ve never seen, and readers aren’t stupid. They scroll past the polished, soulless paragraphs. 

They stop for the messy, specific, human voice that actually lived something.

We’re entering the era where sounding real is the ultimate competitive advantage. 

The people who keep writing like humans; imperfect, opinionated, lived-in, are the ones still getting read.

Everyone else is just adding to the noise.
