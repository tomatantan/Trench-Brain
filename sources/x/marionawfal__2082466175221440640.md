🇺🇸🇮🇷 The Pentagon never told the public how badly U.S. troops got hit in the Iran war...

A FOIA request just spilled it.

New Defense Department records show more than 400 casualties in the first 6 weeks alone, including 17 life-threatening injuries. 

The Army took the worst of it, over 270 wounded, with head trauma the most common injury by far.

Some of the ugliest days never made it to the public. 

CENTCOM, which only releases monthly totals, quietly sat on the 70 casualties logged on a single day in March.

And since Trump ended the truce this month, 200-plus more casualties and 4 more deaths have piled up, right as the official death toll got quietly revised down from 18 to 14.

Accounting gymnastics can shrink the toll, but there are real bodies on the ground.
