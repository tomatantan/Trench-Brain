🇩🇪 Germany's plan to block a pro-Russia party is basically an ad for them

The AfD is polling strong in eastern Germany and could win big in September. Berlin is worried, because those states control local policing and administration, and an AfD government could slow down NATO troops moving east if Russia ever attacks.

So Merz's government is quietly working on a fix. One of his own party members admitted they're figuring out how to go around a state that won't cooperate without technically breaking the law.

That means planning to overrule a vote before it's even been cast. The AfD leans pro-Russia and anti-NATO, so the fear isn't made up. 

But secretly prepping to override your own voters is the exact thing that proves the AfD's whole pitch, that the people in charge will do anything to keep them out.
