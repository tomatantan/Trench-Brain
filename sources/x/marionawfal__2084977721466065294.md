🇺🇸 The U.S. Navy just handed out $76.6 BILLION in contracts for Virginia-class attack submarines and Columbia-class ballistic missile submarines. 

These deals go to General Dynamics Electric Boat and HII’s Newport News Shipbuilding, covering new builds plus major shipyard upgrades. 

The awards will create ripple effects for the AUKUS pact and America’s nuclear arsenal. 

The companies have already delivered 26 Virginia-class subs, and the first Columbia-class boat is expected in a few years. 

A long-awaited boost arrives for a shipbuilding industry that's been plagued by delays and workforce issues.
