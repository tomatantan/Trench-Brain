SEC Commissioner @HesterPeirce gives the definitive three-category breakdown of tokenized securities:

"Category one: natively tokenized, the actual share onchain."

"Category two: entitlement/custodied, the token represents a share custodied on your behalf."

"Category three: synthetic, economic exposure to the asset but not the actual security. In the US, you have to clearly explain to investors what they're holding. They're getting economics, not the asset."
