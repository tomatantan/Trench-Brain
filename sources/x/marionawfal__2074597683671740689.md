🇺🇸🇲🇽 ICE officer shoots dead a man in Houston

Yesterday morning ICE moved to stop a vehicle in a targeted arrest operation.

The driver, Lorenzo Salgado Araujo, from Mexico and here illegally, tried to run.

ICE says he rammed their vehicle, ignored every command, then tried to run an officer over, and the officer fired in self-defense.

The shot hit Araujo, and he died in hospital. The FBI now leads the investigation.

For now it's ICE's account, and the one man who could tell it differently is dead.
