🇺🇸 Someone just declared war on local water plants

Investigators think Iranian hackers might be behind a cyberattack that hit 36 municipal water systems in Minnesota. 

At least one plant had to temporarily shut down, and others had to switch to manual operations, though thankfully no water was made unsafe to drink.

Here is the twist: officials are warning that the hackers might just be pretending to be from Iran to stir up trouble.

Sleep tight knowing our entire basic water supply is apparently just one rogue click away from total chaos.
