.@Backpack and xStocks are producing very different liquidity structures on Solana.

Last week, prop AMMs handled ~61% of Backpack-issued equity volume, while ~86% of xStocks volume cleared through pools.

One possible explanation is operational fungibility. Backpack gives market makers a direct 1:1 conversion path between the onchain token and the corresponding security entitlement within the same stack. That can make inventory easier to hedge, recycle and quote continuously.

The takeaway imo is that prop AMMs will concentrate where inventory management and hedging are most frictionless.
