🇺🇸 Luigi Mangione is BACK in a Manhattan courtroom today, for what may be his last hearing before trial.

The 28-year-old charged with murdering UnitedHealthcare CEO Brian Thompson will appear in person as the case moves toward jury selection.

Last week, his lawyers asked the judge to livestream the trial and provide a public overflow feed inside the courthouse, a rare request in New York criminal cases.

Jury selection is scheduled to begin September 8.

Mangione has pleaded NOT guilty and also faces separate federal charges.
