Always think for yourself. And it doesn’t only apply to trading. A story from my own life; 

I play football and had an injury on both my knees. Frustrating. Even going down stairs hurt. For 2 years it lingered on. Football is my biggest passion and it had big impact on me mentally as well. I went to a physical therapist and did exercises for months. Didnt really feel improvement, first training back the pain returned after one mid intensity drill. More frustration.

Then I decided to switch to another physical therapist. Here I did better exercises and had some improvement but it wasn’t nearly enough to get me back to playing football. Months of 2h+ sessions, two to three times a week. 

Then I got so frustrated I decided to go full schizo mode. Scoured through the whole internet. Watched dozens of youtube videos, reddit posts, podcasts until I finally found a kinda low follow account that had an interview with a footballer. He had the same exact issues and a very very similar story (diff physical therapists- frustration etc) It was 1h30 and I watched the whole thing twice. The guy behind the account is called ‘the tendon guy’. Everything he said made sense. I listened to podcasts from him, followed him on insta and studied what he had to say about tendons.

Then, I went to another sessions at my physical therapist and explained in basic what concepts I learned and how I would like to apply it / change my exercise structure. At first, she reacted irritated because I’m the young guy telling a certificated physical on how to do her job. I noticed her change of body language and told her it wasn’t my intention etc etc. 

Next session, she was super happy and introduced the whole thing I told her into the whole exercise structure. The thing is, 2 months later I was subbed on in a game. And started games after. The improvement was incredible. I literally thought I’d never play football again a couple months before. 

I’m not saying I don’t believe in general healthcare but the lesson of the story; don’t be scared think for yourself, in any aspect of life. Build your own conviction in trades, nobody in this world is all-knowing or right every time- even if they claim they are.
