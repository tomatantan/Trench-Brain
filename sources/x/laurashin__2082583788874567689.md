"In Base's case they were faced with the decision, should we continue to pay OP, or should we just stop paying them"

Offchain CEO Steven Goldfeder on the licensing choice that made their ecosystem sustainable

"We architected our business very differently, and we got a lot of flak for this early on, particularly when it comes to licensing and the lock-in of the ecosystem. A core difference between our strategy and say Optimism's strategy is, do these people actually have to pay you? In Base's case they were faced with the decision, should we continue to pay OP, or should we just stop paying them? That was the decision"

"When there are big capital interests leeching off of that. That all goes down to the licensing model and the business model, and I think we built a sustainable one"

"It wasn't like that old age where the thought was give everything away, don't worry about revenue, the token is going to solve everything. This was very controversial, like the business source license, but today it's actually helped us have a sustainable ecosystem, where the large behemoths need to engage with us and can't just take that software and run it themselves and cut us out"
