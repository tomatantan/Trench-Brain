🇩🇪🇺🇦 Overnight operations at Leipzig/Halle Airport ground to a halt after reports of an unidentified flying object and the discovery of a suspicious item near one of the runways.

Incoming flights were diverted while police sent an explosives robot in to inspect the object.

The southern runway stayed closed into the morning even after other traffic resumed.

The disruption took place near cargo planes belonging to Ukraine’s Antonov Airlines, which now uses Leipzig as a major base.

Similar unexplained drone incidents have repeatedly forced temporary closures at other German airports this year.
