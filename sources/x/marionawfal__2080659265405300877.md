🇨🇳 Satellite images from a Shanghai shipyard may show China testing a new class of nuclear-powered attack submarine...

The vessel appears longer than existing Type 093 boats and features a notably smaller sail, a design change that could improve underwater speed, maneuverability, and acoustic performance.

X-form stern surfaces, usually linked to better handling, are also visible. 

Some experts believe the boat could be quieter than current Chinese nuclear submarines if paired with a pump-jet propulsor, though reactor noise remains a key unknown.

The sighting at Jiangnan, a yard not traditionally associated with nuclear boats, raises questions about whether China is expanding construction capacity or simply running a test hull.

Either way, a quieter, longer-range attack submarine would give Beijing greater ability to operate farther into the western Pacific.
