🇺🇸 DOGE reveals where USAID dollars were allegedly going

A list shared by DOGE claims USAID funded everything from social media influencers in Ukraine and climate activism in Brazil to a transgender-themed opera in Colombia and a "Trans hero" comic book in Peru.

The list also includes $20 million for a Sesame Street program in Iraq, millions for tourism initiatives in Egypt and Tunisia, funding for gender-related healthcare programs in Guatemala, and support for fashion industry travel tied to Ukrainian designers.

One of the biggest claims: more than $54 million to EcoHealth Alliance, along with funding for projects in Afghanistan, including agricultural assistance and reproductive health programs.

Is this what “foreign aid” looks like?
