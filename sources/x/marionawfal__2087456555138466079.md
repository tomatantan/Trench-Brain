🇸🇦🇪🇬 Saudi Arabia reportedly opposed Egypt joining the newly formed Mecca Joint Defence Agreement with Türkiye and Pakistan, despite Ankara publicly identifying Cairo as a future member.

The disagreement is particularly striking because the NATO-style pact was formed as Saudi faces an increasingly dangerous conflict with the Houthis. 

An alliance promising that an attack on one is an attack on all sounds formidable on paper.

But a dispute over membership within days of its creation shows just how much of the Mecca pact's unity and military credibility remains completely untested.

Bottom line: don't expect the pact to persuade the Middle East that there is a viable alternative for U.S. security overnight.
