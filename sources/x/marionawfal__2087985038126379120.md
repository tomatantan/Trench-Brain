🇯🇵🇷🇺🇨🇳 Japan PM Takaichi is calling Putin’s visit to the Northern Territories ABSOLUTELY UNACCEPTABLE.

Those islands are Japanese territory, period.

The trip was a clear middle finger from Putin, and it will only make Japanese people more hostile to Russia for a long time.

Japan is getting squeezed from 2 sides. 

Up north, Russia is digging in on the Kurils and teaming up with China on military flights and drills near Japan. 

Down south and out east, China keeps pushing harder around the Senkakus, Taiwan, and deeper into the Pacific. 

They’re coordinating more and more. 

That’s the real problem.

Tokyo needs to get serious. More defense spending, better missiles, higher readiness, and clear red lines. 

Soft talk won’t stop this.

Who’s actually going to help Japan if something happens, though? 

The U.S. is still the main one because of the security treaty, but let's just say the wars in Ukraine and Iran aren't doing any favors for U.S. defense support of allied nations.

Australia and India through the Quad bring real muscle and the same interest in keeping the region open. 

Those are the partners that matter. Everyone else can talk, but capability counts more.

Takaichi’s strong words were a good start. 

Now Japan has to back it up so neither China nor Russia thinks they can keep pushing for free.
