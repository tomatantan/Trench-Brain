important distinction on "vibecobing" vs engineers using AI: 

to non-mathematicians, all mathematicians are all the same. they're just elite at math. 

to the normal person, there are no bounds to even understand the gaps between the #1 ranked mathematician vs #25 vs #1000 

only when you are an elite mathematician can you easily spot what level other mathematicians are. #1 can easily place every single one of his peers. #25 can do the same, but mostly only for those that are worse than him.

similarly, the normal person using AI to vibecode cannot comprehend how far the gap is between them using AI on ChatGPT/Claude/etc. vs an actual elite engineer using AI. 

the app that you end up vibecoding can look very similar to a real app after some prompting, sure. 

but that would be just like looking at two basketballs going into the same hoop and ignoring the form, the shooting accuracy, the distance, the move into the actual shot, etc... (the guy at the very end of the NBA bench is closer to LeBron James than you are to him) 

maybe one day, this all gets democratized and you can "vibecode" the next Meta or the next Google but we are many orders of magnitude away from that even being comprehensible.
