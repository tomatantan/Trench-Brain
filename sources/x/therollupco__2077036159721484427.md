SEC Commissioner @HesterPeirce lays out the active crypto regulatory agenda before year-end:

"Clarity passing is the first watch item, big coordination job on rule-writing obligations if it goes through."

"Innovation exemption is on my priority list. Tokens in fundraising and capital-raising is another active workstream."

"Nuts and bolts: transfer agents interacting with blockchain, crypto asset custody on both investment advisor and broker-dealer sides, clearing agencies."

"DTCC got a no-action letter to run experimentation. Broker definitions and user interface regulation both under review."

"Vaults deserve attention. Activities could implicate securities laws, people need to be careful there. SEC-CFTC joint coordination on where products sit is also a big focus."
