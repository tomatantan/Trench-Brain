🇷🇺 Trump’s Air Force One switch got everyone talking. Meanwhile, journalists trying to track Putin are literally studying dying houseplants for clues.

Russian journalists noticed the same plants in Putin’s office appeared at different stages of wilting between broadcasts, sometimes seemingly getting healthier again.

That helped expose how the Kremlin records meetings in advance and airs them days or weeks later as if they just happened.

And that’s only part of it.

Putin reportedly has nearly identical offices across multiple residences, making it harder to tell where he actually is.

There’s also an armored train reportedly fitted with a gym and spa, hidden stations that don’t appear on public maps, and motorcades that may be used as decoys.

Officials who meet him are reportedly told to stay quiet for weeks until the footage finally airs.

A Russian reporter called the Air Force One trick “child’s play” compared with the lengths Russia goes to hide Putin’s movements.
