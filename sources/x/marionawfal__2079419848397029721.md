🇺🇸🇮🇷 America has the most powerful bunker buster ever built, and Pickaxe Mountain may still sit beyond its reach...

Trump calls it a target for "a nice big fat shot." The physics are less confident. 

The GBU-57, the 30,000-pound Massive Ordnance Penetrator, bores through about 200 feet of earth before detonating, and even under ideal geology tops out near 200 to 300. 

Experts place the tunnels inside Mount Kolang at 300 to 450 feet beneath solid rock. 

The deepest chambers may sit roughly twice as far down as the best American conventional weapon can reach.

The workaround is to drop MOPs in sequence, each digging into the last one's crater, the way Fordow was hit repeatedly last year. 

Against 450 feet of rock, that's a maybe, not a guarantee.

Which exposes the harder truth. 

Iran didn't dig that deep by accident. 

It built the one target America may not be able to destroy without crossing the line no one wants to cross.
