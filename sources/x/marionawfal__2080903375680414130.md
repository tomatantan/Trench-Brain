🇬🇧🇪🇪 UK is pulling its Challenger 2 tanks out of Estonia.

Official reason: switch to a bigger, more mobile anti-armour force (1,200 troops) packed with drones and modern weapons.

The more practical reason: those Challenger 2s are needed back home for the upgrade to Challenger 3.

Context: the UK has led NATO’s enhanced Forward Presence battlegroup in Estonia since 2017, regularly rotating Challenger 2s there as the heavy-armour backbone of deterrence on the eastern flank.

After years of that commitment, the tanks are now being withdrawn so the limited fleet can be modernised: new gun, new turret, full digitisation.

Heavy metal is leaving the Baltic. Lighter, drone-heavy units are taking its place.
