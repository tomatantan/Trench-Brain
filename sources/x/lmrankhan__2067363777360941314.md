I've seen a ton of "agents for X" ideas that help find creators, generate leads, or sell products. The problem is they all eventually flood inboxes and DMs with spam.

If everyone has an outbound agent, they will need a filtering agent just to manage the noise. At that point the system collapses under its own weight.

I'd much rather use agents to synthesize internal data, automate repetitive work, and make people dramatically more efficient. 

Let the agent handle the shit work while we focus on what machines cant do well like building trust, forming connections, and closing high value deals.
