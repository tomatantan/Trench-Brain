Last quarter, Ethereum's L2s accounted for 46% of all Ethereum DEX volume, up from 45% the quarter before. @base  alone represented 81% of all L2 DEX volume. 

@RobinhoodCrypto is joining an ecosystem where an increasing share of Ethereum's trading activity already lives and it will not be the last major platform to make this move.

So if more trading activity continues migrating to L2s, how should investors think about Ethereum's position in that ecosystem?
