🚨🇮🇶🇮🇷 Iraqi PM Ali Al-Zaidi and Iranian President Pezeshkian have signed four agreements and MOU for cooperation between the two countries. 

The agreements/MOU included: 

-An agreement on the transportation of goods by road. 

-An MOU for the construction of the Khosrowi-Khanqin-Baghdad railway.

-An MOU to make Tehran and Baghdad "twin cities."

-An MOU on public administration, training, and human resources.
