🇺🇸 Robert Barnes is watching a number most coverage has skipped: Republican party affiliation is falling, and fast.

Gallup has the swing at roughly 14 points toward Democrats since late 2024, from a 4-point Republican lead in Q4 2024 to a double-digit Democratic edge by early 2026. 

Unleaned Republican ID sits near its historic low, with a record 45% of Americans now calling themselves independents.

Barnes's read: the base didn't sign up for this war, and the affiliation numbers are where it's finally showing.
