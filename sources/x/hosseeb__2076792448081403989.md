Shower thought: here's how you fix education post-AI.

Instead of trying to ban AI, let's just acknowledge that students will CORRECTLY use AI for a plurality of cognitive tasks in their future.

So ban all AI except one: GPT-4o. Something just good enough to be a cognitive amplifier and that they'd be foolish not to use it, but the model must be stupid enough that it makes mistakes that a student will need to understand, catch, and fix.

Just like you were allowed to use a graphing calculator in your trig class but not Wolfram Alpha, students need dumb AI so they can learn how to use smart AI well.
