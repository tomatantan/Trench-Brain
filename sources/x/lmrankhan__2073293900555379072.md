Many times as an investor you're acting as a scout for a sports team. Your job is to find talent before everyone else discovers it, or before the founders even realize it themselves.

Back in 2018 at ETHDenver I saw Tanay Kothari (Wispr Flow) and Kenta Iwasaki pitch Canteen, a decentralized Kubernetes project. They ended up winning the hackathon and it was one of the best pitches I've ever seen.

I reached out to both soon after, but they were still juniors in college. Tanay went on to build Wispr Flow, now valued at around $2B 

You never know where great founders will come from, which is why you shouldn't ignore or brush off young, hungry talent. When someone catches your eye, build the relationship early.
