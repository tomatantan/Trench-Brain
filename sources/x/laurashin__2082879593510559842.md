"12 months out from every major conflict since 1929, the S&P is up 11% on average with a 76% hit rate of positive returns"

Franklin Templeton strategist Chris Galipeau on why investors shouldn't react to geopolitical shocks

"The playbook for any conflict generally goes as follows. I've mapped out every major geopolitical event globally going back to 1929, all of them. I got in business in 1991, I've seen a lot of them, the World War II data, the Great Depression, all that sort of thing"

"You pick the conflict, wars, acts of terrorism, bubbles, black swans like COVID, you pick the start date and then you measure equity returns three months out, six months, 12 months. 12 months out from every major conflict since 1929, the S&P is up 11% on average with a 76% hit rate of positive returns"

"That data set alone tells you investors should not react to some political or geopolitical event. Now that's really easy to say, and in practice that is not easy to do"
