🇮🇷🇺🇸 Iran says it isn't negotiating directly with the U.S and won't restart talks while it believes Washington is violating the June ceasefire memorandum; although, conveniently, messages are still flying back and forth through intermediaries.

The bigger problem is Hormuz. Tehran says its agreement with Oman on future shipping lanes is nearly finished, but the IRGC says the Strait stays restricted until Washington accepts all of Iran's conditions.

The IRGC isn't even dressing it up as maritime regulation anymore, calling Hormuz “a theatre of war for us and not just a waterway.”

So Washington wants freedom of navigation, Iran wants concessions before restoring it, and everyone else gets to watch one of the world's most important energy arteries remain a negotiating chip.

So much for the quick, decisive victory Trump promised.
