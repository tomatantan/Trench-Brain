🇮🇷🇺🇸🇯🇴 Is Iran's "kick the U.S out" strategy starting to pay off?

For years, Tehran has argued that hosting U.S troops makes America's allies less secure, not more. Now, hundreds of Jordanian politicians, lawyers, academics, and other public figures are openly demanding the removal of roughly 4,000 U.S troops from Jordan, saying the American presence has turned their country into a target.

Their open letter warns U.S bases expose Jordan to "security, political, and economic risks" and increase the chances of being dragged into a regional war that "it is not a party to." 

Jordan receives around $1.65 billion a year in U.S aid, making Amman's balancing act increasingly awkward: cash from Washington, missiles from Tehran, and growing domestic anger over being caught in the middle.
