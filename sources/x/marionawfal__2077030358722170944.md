🇺🇸 Trump is dead serious about slapping a 20% toll on cargo passing through the Strait of Hormuz.

Trump has wanted this for months and is now pushing forward despite internal pushback. He frames it as reimbursement for U.S. security costs in the volatile waterway.

This directly contradicts statements from his own VP and Sec. Rubio, who previously rejected any tolls or fees in the strait.

Details on how it would actually work, or whether Gulf states have been consulted, remain unclear. But Trump is tying it to a renewed U.S. naval presence.

The move would be a major escalation in how the U.S. treats one of the world's most critical shipping routes.
