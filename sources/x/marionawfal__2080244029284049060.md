🇷🇺🇾🇪🇮🇷 Energy warfare is shifting from oil fields to refineries and shipping chokepoints.

Attacks on Russian refining capacity and disruptions around Bab el-Mandeb are removing both crude and finished fuels from the market.

This creates shortages without destroying the reserves themselves.

The world can have plenty of crude oil and still run short of the fuel that keeps it moving.

Diesel, jet fuel, and other refined products are not easily replaced. Losing refinery capacity hits agriculture, freight, aviation, and military logistics much faster than a simple drop in global production.

The global economy remains critically reliant on oil, and this war is driving the cost of everything up as a result.
