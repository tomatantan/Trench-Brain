Agree 100% with this. There is nothing noble about blowing up.

Losing everything does not magically turn you into a better trader. It can destroy your confidence, damage your relationships, make you desperate to win it back and genuinely fuck up your life.

The whole “I lost everything and it was the best lesson of my life” narrative is mostly survivorship bias and cope. You only hear it from the few people who recovered (+ larps). The thousands who never came back are not posting inspirational threads about it.

And what exactly did it teach them? Position sizing? Not to overleverage? To take profits? To respect money? You can learn all of that from losing 10%. You do not need a 100% wipeout.

A lesson does not become more valuable because the tuition was catastrophic.

Protect your money. Stay in the game. You do not need to ruin your life to become wiser. The real edge is staying in the game long enough for time and experience to compound
