🇺🇦🇷🇺 Ukraine reached deep into Russia overnight, hitting one of Moscow's largest oil refineries some 1,400 km from the border.

Ukrainian drones struck Gazprom Neftekhim Salavat in Bashkortostan, touching off explosions and a fire. 

It's Russia's 10th-largest refinery, processing around 10 million tons of crude a year.

Drones are also reported to have hit the Afipsky refinery in Krasnodar Krai in the same wave.

Kyiv has been pounding Russia's oil sector for months now, and the distances keep growing.
