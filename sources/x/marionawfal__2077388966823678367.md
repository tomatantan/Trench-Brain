🇺🇸 Aduril’s Palmer Luckey on Facial Recognition:

“Facial recognition is probably the wrong tool to use as part of the kinda final stage targeting of kinetic weapons. It’s still a very fraught technology. There’s a lot of way to spoof it, there’s a lot of ways that it can be wrong. A lot of ways that people can go out of their way to affirmatively trick a system.

It’s a good tool, for let’s say law enforcement to perform investigative activity. It is not a good tool to use when you’re deciding who lives and dies in a split-second decision.”

At least he’s admitting it. How many of these tech companies are pushing products on civilian customers that aren’t refined enough to be effective, and end up causing more problems than they are intended to fix.
