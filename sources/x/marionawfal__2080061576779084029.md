The market thinks the energy shock is over. It's only the early stages of a long squeeze...

Raymond Zucaro, CIO of RVX Asset Management, says investors are badly underestimating how far the current supply shock ripples out. 

The rapid drain of petroleum reserves signals the beginning of a prolonged economic squeeze rather than the end of one.

The pain shows up in the essentials first. 

Rising costs on food and diesel work their way through the whole system.

And eventually, Raymond says, the market has no choice but to price in the recession it's currently ignoring.
