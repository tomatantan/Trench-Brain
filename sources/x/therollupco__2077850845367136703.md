Felix Leupold on where the moat actually sits when intent platforms get commoditized:

"For CoW Protocol the answer's clear. It's the network effects from coincidence of wants."

"Users trading at the same time get batched into the same execution problem, and they act almost as liquidity providers for one another. Even if the optimal route runs through 10 pools onchain, if someone's trading the opposite direction or has a resting limit order, we match you as a coincidence of wants at the midpoint, no spread, no fees."

"The more people use it, the higher the efficiency gains."
