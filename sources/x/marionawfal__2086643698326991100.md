🇬🇧 Prisons are packed, so Britain figured the easy fix is releasing the criminals.

PM Andy Burnham is pushing ahead with the early release scheme, letting prisoners out before they've served their full time to free up space. 

The Conservatives crunched the numbers and reckon it'll cost Britain nearly 90,000 extra crimes by 2029.

What they're expecting:

—About 500 more sex offences
—Nearly 1,800 violent crimes
—Just under 36,000 thefts
—Thousands more for drugs, weapons, and public order

It's a Tory estimate, and the government's waving it off as "highly speculative." Their logic: no early release, prisons overflow, system collapses.

Prisons so full they’re running a clearance sale on inmates.  😂
