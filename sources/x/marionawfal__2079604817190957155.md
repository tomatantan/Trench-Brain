🇮🇱🇮🇷🇺🇸 Israel believes Iran moved centrifuges and part of its highly enriched uranium stockpile to an underground facility, after the 12-Day War last June.

The site, known as Pickaxe Mountain, was closely monitored but left untouched due to its extreme depth and central location.

Iran has roughly 440 kg of near bomb-grade uranium, enough to produce 9-12 nuclear weapons.

Removing it remains a key goal for both Israel and the U.S.
