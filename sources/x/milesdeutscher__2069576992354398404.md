Frontier Labs have never had this much pressure.

GPT-5.6, Gemini 3.5Pro, and the next Opus models literally have no choice but to be huge leaps forward.

It's one thing for a model to catch up on 1-2 benchmarks/tests (can just rule them out as outliers).

But GLM-5.2 has consistently ranked in the top three for several independent benchmark rankings across coding, design, agentic work, long-horizon tasks, and more.

Of course, the practical application of GLM-5.2 is not the same as that of these frontier models (it's horrible at general conversation), but it's still a very impressive leap forward regardless.

Feels like the Mythos moment for open-sourced AI. I expect progress to rapidly accelerate from here for both open-weight and frontier models.

Anything less would be a disappointment.
