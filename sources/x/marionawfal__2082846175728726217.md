🚨🇷🇺 Russia has EXTENDED its temporary export ban on gasoline, diesel, and other fuels from August 1st through January 31st of next year.

Deputy PM Alexander Novak signaled a few days ago that limits would be extended, and the binding decree was signed today.

However, the restrictions on diesel and marine fuels will stop applying to direct producers starting September 1.

It's become quite apparent that Russia's domestic oil refining capacity has suffered deep, systemic damage that CANNOT be fixed quickly...
