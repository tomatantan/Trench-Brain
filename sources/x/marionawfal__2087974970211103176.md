Carney is reportedly ready to offer concessions on alcohol and dairy to get a deal done, but Ottawa wants real cuts on steel, aluminum, and auto tariffs in return.

That puts Carney in a tough spot: give too much and face backlash at home, give too little and the clock runs out.
