I think we're gonna see an insane bullmarket on-chain, not from optimism, but from people running out of options:

Mortgage rates are still hovering around 6.6–6.7%.  

A house that would’ve cost you $1,700 a month in 2020 now costs closer to $2,800–$3,000 for the same purchase price. 
And prices themselves are way higher than they were five years ago.  

This is the trap a whole generation is stuck in.  

You graduate into a job market that pays “okay” on paper but gets vaporized by rent that keeps climbing, groceries that keep rising in price, and student debt that follows you for a decade. 

Saving for a down payment feels like trying to fill a bucket with a hole in it. 

By the time you get close, rates or prices move against you again.  

Marriage, kids, any sense of stability, all delayed or abandoned because the math simply doesn't work.

When the traditional path (work hard, save steadily, buy a house, build equity) starts looking mathematically impossible in any reasonable timeframe, people start looking for exits that actually feel possible.  

That’s why so many are deep in the trenches or so active in sportsbooks and prediction markets. 

Not because they suddenly love gambling, but because the slow road is not realistic anymore.  

For all these people living paycheck to paycheck, a high-risk, high-reward shot starts looking like the only realistic way to change your life before you’re 40.

Some people will retire, some people will lose everything.

It's the sad reality, but true.
