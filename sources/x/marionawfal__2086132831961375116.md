🇺🇦🇷🇺 Zelensky just admitted Ukraine still can't get enough missiles to stop Russia's ballistics.

He says they've been scrambling for protection since day one, when Ukraine had nothing to shoot them down with.

The frustrating part for Kyiv:

South Korea and Japan won't help, blocked by their own laws

Kyiv's whole government is now basically one big missile hunt.
