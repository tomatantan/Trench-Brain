🇺🇸 🇮🇷 How Washington would sell Trump on nuking Iran...

Picture the meeting.

Someone walks into the Oval Office and explains that the B61 bomb has a dial on it.

Turn the yield down to 0.3 kilotons, against the 15 that leveled Hiroshima.

Contained blast, minimal fallout, tunnels under Pickaxe Mountain sealed forever.

Problem solved.

Matthew Hoh, a former Marine and State Department officer, has been dreading that conversation for months, and he can already hear the line that closes it: 

"Only Harry Truman was brave enough to use them."

Flattery aimed precisely at a president who prizes being called decisive.

What no pitch covers is the morning after.

Iran does not capitulate to a sealed tunnel.

Ninety two million people do not surrender because a mountain collapsed.

And the phrase "usable nuclear weapon" stops being a Pentagon abstraction for every other country on earth.

Eighty years of restraint, talked away with a dial and a compliment.
