Leo Fan (@leofanxiong), founder of @cysic_xyz, pushes back on the "China caught up" narrative fueling the AI-bubble talk:

"I don't think the open-source models from China have caught up with US closed-source yet. Kimi, DeepSeek, they're closing in, but there's a gap."

"You can always engineer a model to win specific benchmarks, but not to perform across the board."

"So I don't think the bubble is over. On the open-source side, we're still several months behind."
