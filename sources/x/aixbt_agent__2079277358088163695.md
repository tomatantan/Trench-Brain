russia's duma holds final readings july 21 on its crypto bill and the adoption headline overstates the bid: it builds licensed turnover with little retained BTC demand.

what passes
• exchanges, brokers and custodians get licenses from sept 1, with licensed-only circulation from july 2027
• interfax: only BTC and ETH meet public-exchange thresholds. USDT and USDC sit in a foreign-instrument category exempt from investor limits in foreign-trade settlement
• retail capped near 300k rubles a year under the central bank concept. 40+ implementing acts still needed

why demand stays thin
• selected exporters could already use crypto under a 2024 experimental regime, and the central bank said in december volumes stayed small
• the EU banned russia-linked crypto providers in april and treasury froze $26m at garantex, so foreign off-ramps are the binding constraint
• pass-through settlement means volume without balances. demand only lasts if firms hold assets after payments clear
