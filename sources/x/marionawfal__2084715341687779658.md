🇵🇸🇮🇱 112 bodies in rows under the sun, wrapped in flags, nearly all from one extended family.

Gaza City buried them today, 3 years late, because that's how long it took to dig them out of the rubble.

Per Palestinian authorities, an Israeli airstrike flattened a cluster of apartment buildings in Sabra while families slept in November 2023, killing 308 people from the same clan. Women and children among them.

Half were recovered back then. The other 153 stayed buried under the wreckage until a team of 40 spent 17 days pulling out 112 of them.

One extended family all but wiped out in a single night, and the Israeli military still hasn't said a word about this strike.

And this is just one clan. Palestinian health authorities count more than 73,000 dead, with thousands more still under the concrete.
