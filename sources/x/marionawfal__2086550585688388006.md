🇮🇷 Iran hit another oil tanker in the Strait of Hormuz.

An ADNOC vessel from Abu Dhabi was struck by a missile yesterday morning. No one was hurt and the situation is under control.

The UAE called it a hostile Iranian attack by the Revolutionary Guard and accused them of piracy. 

This is the 16th ADNOC ship targeted since the conflict started. Earlier attacks already left one dead and 20 injured.
