🇺🇸🇮🇷 Looks like nothing perks the dollar up quite like a war restarting.

The greenback had slumped to a 10-day low after the Fed held rates in a messy 9-3 vote, with Chair Warsh refusing to tip his hand on whether a hike is coming.

Then the U.S. resumed strikes on Iran, and the dollar clawed it all back, nudging up to 101.

The 30-year Treasury yield spiked to its highest in almost 20 years. 

The pound and euro slipped ahead of the Bank of England. Bitcoin held near $64,000.

Traders now see a 1-in-3 chance the Fed hikes in September.

The world's reserve currency, apparently allergic to peace.
