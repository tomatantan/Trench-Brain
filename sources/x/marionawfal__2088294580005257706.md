🇫🇷 France's Constitutional Council struck down a law banning under-15s from social media, just 3 weeks before it was set to take effect.

The ruling hinged on a fatal design flaw: enforcing the ban required age verification for ALL users, creating a mass personal data collection system the Council deemed unconstitutional.

Turns out building a surveillance apparatus to protect kids from surveillance is a hard sell.
