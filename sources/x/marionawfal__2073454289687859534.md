🇪🇺🇩🇪 The European Parliament is moving to formally deregister the AfD's EU coalition, the Europe of Sovereign Nations party, which would strip it of official status and cut off its EU funding entirely.

180 legislators signed the petition triggering the process, the EPP, Socialists and Renew Europe are all backing it, and a 294-page dossier has been compiled covering court rulings, official statements and social media activity from member parties documenting "antisemitic, anti-LGBT and anti-migrant rhetoric" alongside calls for repatriation of citizens with foreign roots.

The AfD says it's being punished for talking about real issues and calls it a free speech matter.

This is happening while the AfD is climbing in German polls, Merz is at 15% approval, and every mainstream European party is hemorrhaging voters to the right. 

The institutional response to that trend is apparently to try to defund and deregister the parties absorbing those voters rather than compete with the arguments driving the shift.

Whether that works or accelerates the problem is the only question left.
