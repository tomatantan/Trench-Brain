"It makes me think about how crypto performed in Q2, where as bad as it was, you had actual outperformance of small cap crypto relative to Bitcoin, Ether, Solana, XRP"

Andy Baehr, Managing Director of Asset Management at GSR, on what small caps outperformance actually revealed

"In my old job we built this index called the CoinDesk 80, a mid and small cap index that starts at sort of token number 21 and goes down to 100"

"In any kind of healthy or even stable neutral market condition, you can rely on large cap cryptos to pull away from small cap crypto. It just means there's a collective attention on those larger liquid names, and over time that's a pretty reliable indicator of a normal market. You might have situations in a sudden uptrend of small caps gaining a lot of energy and briefly outpacing lower volatility Bitcoin, Ether, Solana, but normally over time those mega caps pull away"

"It just showed how much evacuation there was from those majors, in ETFs, in perps markets, in spot markets, in digital asset treasuries like your own. To me, is that capitulation level towards the end of Q2? Perhaps. But it's certainly a sign that there was an evacuation that happened over that longer period of time"
