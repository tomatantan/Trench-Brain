🇨🇩 Congo’s latest Ebola outbreak has already killed more than 2,000 people, the fastest-growing on record.

Nearly 3 months after it was declared, confirmed cases stand at 4,381 with 2,011 deaths.

The second thousand fatalities arrived in just 3 weeks and medical teams are struggling to keep up.

Rebel fighting, poor roads, and unpaid workers on strike are slowing the response across 5 of the provinces.

Most new infections are appearing outside the circles of people already being monitored.

Clinical trials have only just begun in the epicenter.
