Travelling Merchant Update 🪙

The new Travelling Merchant system is rolling out with two separate ways to access gold:

Personal Gold
Players can now claim guaranteed daily gold based on their $KINS holdings. Complete your daily quests, then purchase your Personal Gold with materials. This gives players a steady daily gold option without competing against others.

Global Travelling Merchant
The competitive merchant will still have a 3,000 gold pool. Players can donate materials to earn hidden contribution points, which unlock how much gold they can claim from the Global Merchant, up to 10 gold per account.

Donate materials → earn contribution → unlock claim limit → purchase gold when the merchant opens.

A new way to stack gold begins now.
