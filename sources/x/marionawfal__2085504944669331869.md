🇵🇦🇺🇸 The U.S. just put its biggest military presence in Panama in over a decade on the ground.

It's for PANAMAX 2026, running August 3 to 13 with around 1,700 troops from roughly 18 nations, all built around one mission: defending the Panama Canal.

The Missouri National Guard, Panama's longtime state partner, is in Colón running medical readiness training with US Joint Task Force-Bravo and Panamanian officials, while task forces drill maritime patrols across the Pacific and Caribbean.

SOUTHCOM calls it the largest U.S. footprint in Panama in more than ten years. The timing is loud, with the canal caught in a tug-of-war over U.S. influence and China's role there.

Routine on paper. A pointed show of presence in practice.
