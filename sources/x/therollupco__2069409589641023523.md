Andy asks BCG’s Roy Choudhury what tokenized deposits mean for banks and the future of money.

Roy says the signal is clear:

“Tokenized money is here.”

“It’s going to play an important role in the future ecosystem of money and payments.”

“Programmable money is just going to be table stakes in the future.”

He says large corporates have billions sitting idle across bank accounts because settlement is slow, forecasting cash is hard, and money can’t move seamlessly 24/7.
