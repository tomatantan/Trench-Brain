🇮🇱 Mehdi Hasan pressed Bernie Sanders on whether the problem in Israel is just Netanyahu or something deeper.

Hasan pointed out that many in the pro-Palestine camp say focusing only on the current government misses the bigger picture, because even the opposition supports the war in Gaza, the campaign in Iran, and the settlements.

Sanders didn’t fully disagree. 

He said it would be too easy to claim that removing Netanyahu fixes everything. 

In his view, a significant part of the Israeli public has shifted right, the country has become more isolated internationally, and the media there plays a role.

This debate goes beyond one PM. 

Whether you see it as a broad political consensus or the result of a smaller group of powerful people steering policy for years, both sides of this conversation are treating the issue as bigger than a single election.
