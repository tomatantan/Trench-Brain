🇪🇺🇩🇪 Brussels is reaching for the AfD's off switch

Next Tuesday the European Parliament looks set to strip the far-right ESN bloc, home to Germany's AfD, of its official status and its EU money.

More than 180 lawmakers signed on. A watchdog now decides if the party respects "European values," and if not, the funding stops.

That's over €2 million a year, so this is no symbolic slap.

The three biggest groups, the center-right, center-left and liberals, are all behind it, so it passes.

And the timing says it all. AfD is rising in the polls while Germany's own courts still drag their feet, but Brussels already found its shortcut.

Cheaper to defund a rival than to beat one.
