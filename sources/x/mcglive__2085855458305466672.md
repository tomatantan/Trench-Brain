SpaceX and Nvidia are teaming up on "Star Mind," a satellite payload built for space-based data centers. 

@mynt_josh broke it down

The idea: a constellation in sun-synchronous orbit, always sunlit, so it sidesteps two constraints we hit on Earth, the scalability of energy and the political will to site data centers. Individual satellites carry less compute than a terrestrial campus, but linked by laser (satellite-to-satellite and satellite-to-Earth), the network could add up to something large.

Musk's stated goal from the IPO: 100 gigawatts within roughly five years. The longer arc is get mass to orbit, build the biggest data center off-planet, and scale compute with solar energy.
