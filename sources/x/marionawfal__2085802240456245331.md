🇺🇸🇮🇷 Prof. Glenn Diesen believes Washington is running out of options in the Hormuz standoff, and that every new round of pressure is strengthening Iran's negotiating position instead.

The latest U.S. strikes changed little on the battlefield while further depleting American munitions, leaving Tehran in a position to raise its demands. 

Diesen says Washington is increasingly being forced to accept painful concessions because it lacks a credible military alternative.

He also cautions against dismissing Iran as irrational. Tehran has generally behaved strategically, but after repeated attacks and widespread destruction, demands for tougher terms or even vengeance should not come as a surprise.

"I think there's a growing recognition that there's nothing they can do about the Strait of Hormuz."
