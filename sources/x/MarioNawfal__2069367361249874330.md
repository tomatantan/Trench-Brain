🇺🇸 Wikipedia banned its own founder. His crime: asking for more balance.

Larry Sanger, who literally coined the name "Wikipedia," launched a project last month pushing the site back toward its original commitment to intellectual diversity.

He was indefinitely blocked days later... his account was only reinstated after he made the ban public on X.

His core complaint: Wikipedia's real power sits with a tiny, mostly anonymous group of administrators, just 62 accounts by his count, 85% of whom have never revealed who they actually are.

One of those anonymous editors, central to pushing his ban through, was already barred weeks earlier from editing anything in the Israel-Palestine subject area for "problematic behavior."

In a past interview, Sanger said Wikipedia had adopted a "GASP point of view," which stands for Globalist, Academic, Secular, and Progressive.
