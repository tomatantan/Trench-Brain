🇮🇷 Iran's FM Abbas Araghchi: We choose death before dishonor

"Throughout history, the worth and courage of a nation have not been measured by domination, but by its readiness to sacrifice for freedom, justice, and human dignity.

We have learned from the school of Sayyed al-Shuhada (Imam Hussein) that an honorable death is preferable to a life of humiliation, because retreating and abandoning independence, collective identity, and national will destroys the very foundations of a nation.

With this deep ideological and strategic conviction, the Islamic Republic of Iran has stood firm in the face of all pressures and will remain committed to the path of resistance."
