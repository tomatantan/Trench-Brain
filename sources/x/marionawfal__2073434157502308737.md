🇬🇧 The U.K. wants to choose what you watch

YouTube is telling its UK creators to push back against a government plan that would force it to show some channels ahead of others.

The rules would park the big broadcasters, the BBC, ITV, Channel 4 and 5, at the top of your feed whether you asked for them or not.

YouTube: "This could severely limit your channel's ability to grow."

Space at the top is finite, so every slot they get is one taken from the smaller creators you actually came to watch.

Sold as fighting misinformation, it's really the state deciding whose videos reach you first.

Governments don't usually rearrange your feed to hand you more choice.
