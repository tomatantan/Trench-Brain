🚨🇺🇸🇮🇷 The MOU's two load bearing pillars cracked in the same 24 hours, and that separates tonight from everything since the signing...

Compare it to June to see the difference. 

When Iran hit the Ever Lovely on June 25, the U.S. answered with six aircraft striking four coastal radar sites, a calibrated tap designed to shape the Doha talks.

Both sides stood down within days and sent delegations back to the table. 

That was COERCIVE DIPLOMACY, violence in service of the negotiation.

Tonight was PUNISHMENT.

Over 80 targets hit, more than 60 Iranian small boats destroyed, air defenses, command networks, and antiship missile sites across Bandar Abbas, Qeshm, Sirik, and the strait islands, an operation officials call four to five times larger than anything since the signing.

The trigger tripled too, three tankers struck in a day, including Saudi and Qatari carriers.

The deeper rupture is structural.

The MOU rests on two mechanisms, paused STRIKES and eased SANCTIONS.

Tonight the strikes resumed at campaign scale and the oil waiver got revoked in the same news cycle.

Both pillars suspended at once, both sides formally accusing the other of breach, Ghalibaf declaring "we don't fold."

June tested the deal's flexibility.

Tonight tests whether it exists at all.
