Hanson Birringer reveals why he chose Grayscale to structure the Hyperliquid vehicle:

"An institutional investor wants to buy HYPE. They don't have a Coinbase account. They can't access Hyperliquid in the US. Maybe they're onboarded to an OTC desk, but getting that through all the risk controls is a lot of friction."

"Now, if they already have a brokerage account, they just one-click and buy the ETF."

"We chose Grayscale for a reason. Largest crypto asset manager in the world, close to 20 billion in AUM, and they won the battle with the SEC to get crypto ETPs approved."
