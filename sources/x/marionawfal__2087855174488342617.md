🇺🇦🇷🇺 Zelensky: Sell Ukraine 10% of the U.S. Patriot stockpile and "we will destroy all the Russians' ballistic missiles." 

Right now he says he's got 1%. 5% gets Ukraine through the winter, 10% knocks out every Russian ballistic missile, and he's spending his days on "millions" of phone calls trying to close the gap.

The shortage traces back to the U.S. war with Iran, which burned through interceptors that Ukraine desperately needs, leaving Kyiv defenseless on some of the worst nights. 

July was the deadliest month for Ukrainian civilians since April 2022.

Trump floated letting Ukraine build its own Patriots, then days later said he wasn't sure. Zelensky says he's still waiting.

For context, even if Ukraine had as many Patriot missiles as Zelensky wants, it still wouldn't be able to intercept every Russian missile, but it would certainly up its interception rates as Kyiv is currently rationing their use.
