🇮🇱 A departing Likud MK has confirmed what many suspected: a significant bloc inside Netanyahu's own party tried to remove him after October 7.

Dan Illouz told Ynet that at least a third of Likud's Knesset faction believed Israel's leadership had to change. The attempt failed due to internal power struggles over a successor.

"Asked whether there was an organised effort to replace Netanyahu: absolutely."

Illouz is leaving Likud over the Haredi draft exemption law passed this week, calling it a disgrace while the IDF warns of collapse under the burden.

"I simply cannot ask you to vote for a party I can no longer bring myself to vote for. This is no longer Likud. It has been hijacked."
