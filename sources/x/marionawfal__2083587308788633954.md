🇺🇸🇨🇳 China spent 7 years and £255 million fighting for one address in London. The U.S. now wants to take it before China ever moves in

The High Court just dismissed residents' legal challenge to China's planned mega-embassy at Royal Mint Court, set to be the largest embassy in Europe.

Residents are appealing. If they win, the site goes back up for grabs.

State Department officials and the U.S. Embassy in London reportedly have discussed buying it outright if that happens. Prime real estate, and a way to boot China out of central London in the same move.

No formal talks yet. No deal without the UK, and without China losing its last appeal.
