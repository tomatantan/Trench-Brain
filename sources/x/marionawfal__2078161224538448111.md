🇺🇸 Trump’s social media company is reportedly pitching traders a $100,000-a-month feed for faster access to his Truth Social posts.

One hedge fund executive put it bluntly:

“People will pay because they have to. If you’re behind on that news, you’ll get crushed.”

Trump’s posts have repeatedly moved stocks, oil, and the broader market. Now the company tied to his family may charge Wall Street for the fastest possible look.
