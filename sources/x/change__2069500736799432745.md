On June 14, 1-year-old Kohen Wiley was fatally shot by law enforcement outside a Walmart in Senatobia, Mississippi. Kohen, his mother Vellesiya, and her friend had just left the store and gotten into their vehicle when officers responding to a shoplifting call tried to stop them — though Vellesiya says store footage shows her friend had paid for her items.

Officers say the car drove toward them before an officer opened fire, but Kohen’s family has disputed authorities’ account of the incident, saying the car was moving away.

Now, a petition is calling for an independent review and the release of all footage from the incident, arguing that deadly force in response to a shoplifting call is unjustifiable and demands answers.
