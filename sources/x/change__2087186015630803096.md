Kristi Madore was once taking about 32 pills a day, including opioids, to manage chronic pain from multiple sclerosis. She says legally purchased THCA hemp products, including gummies and vapes, helped her get off those opioids entirely. On July 1, Tennessee began fully enforcing new restrictions on high-THCA hemp products, pulling most of them from store shelves.

Madore, a Tennessee veteran, started a petition calling on state leaders to reconsider the restrictions. More than 2,000 people have signed, including other veterans who say they rely on hemp products to manage PTSD symptoms.
