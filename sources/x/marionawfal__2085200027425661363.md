🇸🇴 The political situation in Somalia remains at an impasse, with corrupt and illegitimate leader Hassan Sheikh Mohamud (HSM) still resisting the calling for elections, and failing to cooperate in any fashion on U.S. counterterrorism. 

With the Red Sea heating up owing to conflict between Yemen’s Houthis and Saudi Arabia, stability and control in the Horn of Africa has become imperative. 

Somalia’s loose oversight of terrorist group Al-Shabaab is now becoming a problem.  An 11-member U.S. congressional delegation has been dealing head-on with Somali intransigence in their meetings in Kampala, Uganda. 

Last week, the U.S. delegation was led by Representative Ronny Jackson (Republican, Texas), chairman of the House Armed Services Subcommittee on Intelligence and Special Operations; Justin Davis - U.S. Chargé d’Affaires to Somalia; General Dagvin R. M. Anderson - Commander of U.S. Africa Command (AFRICOM); and 10 other members of Congress. Ultimatums were issued to Somali leadership that would lead to complete loss of fiscal and security support, unless HSM fully cooperated on U.S. counterterrorism and sorted the political impasse with the clans and regions.  

The U.S. is now leaning towards separate security deals directly with the Jubaland, Puntland & Somaliland regions, bypassing HSM and Mogadishu entirely for lack of convergence with U.S. interests.  In the next two weeks, further meetings are earmarked to finalise an agreement on elections.
