The four-year cycle is almost religion in crypto. @Bitwise's Head of Research @RasterlyRock says it's no longer mapping cleanly, and explains why:

"These cycles aren't perfect on timing. Historically it was three up years, one down year. Recently we had two up years, a slightly down year, and then a down year this year, 2025 was actually a down year for Bitcoin."

"The market is fundamentally changing, because the buyers are different. Four years ago it was early retail. Today it's the institutions we face off with at Bitwise, corporations, sovereign wealth funds, family offices."

"They take different exposure over different durations than early retail did. It's a much more liquid asset. The pattern still holds somewhat, but it's always going to look a little different now."
