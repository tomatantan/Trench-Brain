🇬🇧 A 2013 Lancaster University study found that domestic abuse rose by 26% when the English national team won or drew, and 38% when they lost. 

It also found that domestic abuse incidents increased in frequency with each new tournament.

So what's really behind this?

Major sporting events do not inherently generate violence; rather, they serve as explosive behavioral triggers. 

The toxic combination of heavy alcohol consumption, high-stakes emotional investment, and perhaps even gambling losses exacerbates violence within already abusive relationships.

And it's not just limited to the UK...

A study in Colombia tracked the 2014 and 2018 World Cups, finding that medical examinations related to violence against women increased by up to 43% on match days.

Experts in the U.S. have consistently identified their own surge in domestic abuse, immediately following major AMERICAN football games, like the Super Bowl.

Win or lose, there is absolutely no excuse for domestic violence... but how surprised are you by these stats?
