Zerohash CEO Edward Woodford says Visa's stablecoin move isn't a proof of concept, it's live to all eligible customers at full scale:

"Two or three years ago, these were companies dabbling their toes in. Now it's the core business."

"This is not a proof of concept with Visa, it's a true GA application to all eligible customers. Over 12 billion endpoints, 190 countries."

"Merchant pre-funding: instead of parking capital at a partner to do payouts, stablecoins enable just-in-time funding, which frees that capital to reinvest."

"And global payouts: paying a freelancer in the Philippines takes seconds, not days over SWIFT. You could go from being paid every two weeks to being paid every day, or after every service, so people don't need to take out loans."
