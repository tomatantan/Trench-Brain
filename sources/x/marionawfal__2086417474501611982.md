🇮🇷 Iran says President Pezeshkian just met Mojtaba Khamenei. The only problem? We still haven't actually seen Khamenei.

The official media account for news and statements from Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei claims they discussed the war, military developments, the collapsing economic picture, energy, foreign currency, and how to keep the government funded.

But there's a rather large Ayatollah-shaped elephant in the room: Khamenei hasn't been seen publicly for months, with no accompanying video or audio released to independently demonstrate he's actually still alive.

That doesn't prove he's dead, but when the supposedly most powerful man in Iran is communicating with the outside world largely through statements attributed to him, skepticism isn't exactly unreasonable.

At least a photo of the alleged meeting would certainly help.
