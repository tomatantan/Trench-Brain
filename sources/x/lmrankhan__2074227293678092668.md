I had my second customer launch this week. They decided to launch a few days early without telling me, and a lot of the product broke. I had to jump in and fix a bunch of last minute bugs.

One thing I've learned is that building with live customers makes you think you have every bug and scenario covered. Then a completely new scenario shows up and exposes an entirely new set of bugs.

And somehow it almost always happens while you're onboarding a brand new customer. 

What I learned is instead of trying to make the product  perfect, convince one customer to use it and go through the entire end to end end flow. Fix every issue you find, then repeat that exercise 5 to 10 times until you feel like you've covered most scenarios. 

Then you shoul  start onboarding bigger batches of customers, maybe 5 to 10 at a time, and slowly expand from there. This process will help you find bugs but mitigate customer impact when shit hits the fan.
