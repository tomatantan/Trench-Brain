🇨🇳 Deadly fire tears through a Chinese shoe factory

At least 28 people were reportedly killed when a fire tore through a shoe factory in Jinjiang, in China's Fujian province.

It started on the ground floor around noon and shot up the building, feeding on all the flammable shoe materials inside, with workers trapped on the roof as black smoke swallowed the sky.

Jinjiang is China's "shoe capital," thousands of factories cranking out a fifth of the world's shoes.

Xi called the losses "significant" and ordered an all-out rescue.
