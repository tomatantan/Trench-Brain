🇺🇸🇸🇾🇱🇧 Who’s the boss? I’ll give you a hint… It’s neither Tony Danza nor Zohran Mamdani.

NY City Hall claimed the NYPD "strongly recommended" a police escort for Mayor Mamdani’s wife Rama Duwaji, on her upcoming trip to Syria and Lebanon. 

The NYPD flatly DENIED it: “The NYPD does not send officers to countries with level 4 travel advisories for discretionary reasons not linked to investigative work.” 
 
Duwaji plans to visit family there in late September. 

A source claims she specifically requested the security detail after a previous unprotected overseas trip.
