🇮🇱🇵🇸🇺🇸 Netanyahu reportedly has "serious security concerns" about the proposed disarmament deal with Hamas, and has shared these concerns with the U.S.

Doron Spielman, spox. for Netanyahu's office, says Israel's intelligence indicates Hamas is not serious about demilitarizing, and that the IDF will not withdraw from Gaza.

“Our assessment is that if we redeploy before Hamas is truly disarmed, it will quickly expand its presence throughout Gaza, rebuild its terrorist infrastructure, once again threaten Israeli communities, and seek to carry out another October 7-style attack."

He also said that disarming means physically relinquishing weapons, and nothing less.

The Board of Peace plan that emerged in past days would have Hamas disarm and Israel pull out of Gaza, allowing a technocratic administration to take control of the territory.

Trump has been touting the deal, which was negotiated in Egypt, claiming Israel is "very happy" about it.

Between these comments and the ones we saw earlier from Ben-Gvir, Trump would seem to be incorrect.
