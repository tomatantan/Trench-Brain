While every category of crypto bled out this year, one sector went up 127.3%. 

Chamath Palihapitiya's research team at Social Capital says it's privacy tokens, the best-performing sector in crypto.

His argument traces financial privacy from first principles. Every Bitcoin transaction is public, which decentralized verification but made every coin traceable. And traceability quietly breaks one of the most important properties of money: fungibility. 

Chamath's piece argues Bitcoin fails four fundamental tests of money that gold and cash still pass.

Rob's read on where that points: if you want money that's actually fungible, you need privacy at the base layer. 

That's the case for zero-knowledge proofs, the cryptographic primitive Zcash is built on with Starks and Snarks, work the cypherpunk community has been grinding on for years.
