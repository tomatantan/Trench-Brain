"We were warned that we would face prosecution, and we were told that Animal Control would not help her unless she was returned to the property where she had been found."

That's what Ash and Amanda Marcum-Kamp, a Kentucky couple, say Animal Control told them after they rescued a dog they believed was suffering heat stroke. According to Hardin County officials, the dog later died in Animal Control's care.

The dog's owner, which had four other dogs on property, now faces 15 charges, including animal cruelty.

The couple has launched a petition for a Kentucky law that would protect people who rescue animals in imminent danger and prevent agencies from requiring them to return those animals to unsafe conditions.
