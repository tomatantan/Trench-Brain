"Bitcoin is very clearly a commodity, and ETH is very similar in that regard. But a lot of the things built on top of Ethereum and Solana stand to benefit"

Cole Kennelly, CEO of Volmex Labs, on who actually benefits from the Clarity Act

"Bitcoin is bonafide and clearly under the CFTC's remit based on previous administrations and the regulatory seats and things they've said"

"Maybe things like Hyperliquid and Lighter and others stand to benefit from the increased clarity in the ecosystem. It's nebulous how certain things are regulated, what is a security, what is a broker, and the Clarity Act brings transparency to those areas"

"On a seven day time frame Ethereum is up almost a percent and Bitcoin is roughly up half a percent, so this might be indicative of the fact that Ethereum and other smart contract platforms, as well as decentralized applications and exchanges, have a lot to gain from the Clarity Act going through"
