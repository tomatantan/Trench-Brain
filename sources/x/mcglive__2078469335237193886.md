.@DonJohnsonSays broke down @virtuals_io's ACF (Automatic Capital Formation), the clearest explanation of how founders raise as their token climbs.

The problem it solves: when a token runs, say it jumps from 6M to 16M in 12 hours, that's the most exciting and most stressful moment for a founder. Are you in Telegram scrambling to cut OTC deals? At what valuation? ACF automates it. 

Teams can opt in to set aside 25% of tokens, split into five 5% tranches that unlock at rising market-cap milestones up to $160M. As the token hits each range, those tokens are baked into the liquidity pool, so it never shows as a sell dragging down the chart.

Ie. it 10M FDV and the team gets ~330-350K. 20M is ~1.2M. 40M is ~2.6M.
