🇺🇦🇷🇺 Ukraine just closed one of Russia’s most vital export arteries, the Sea of Azov.

Agricultural, industrial, and military shipments that once moved through the Volga-Don canal system into the Azov and then the Black Sea now have nowhere to go.

Russia’s rail network is already overloaded and struggling with shortages of basic parts, so it cannot easily absorb the extra volume.

The port city of Rostov-on-Don has lost much of its economic purpose and is left mainly as a military logistics hub.

With the Azov closed, Ukraine is positioned to push the same pressure campaign into the wider Black Sea, targeting what remains of the shadow-fleet.
