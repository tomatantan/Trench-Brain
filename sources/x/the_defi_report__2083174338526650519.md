The Fed held rates steady this week. But the bond market is still pricing more tightening ahead.

The 2-year Treasury is trading 55 bps above the Fed's target range. Historically, when those two diverge, the Fed eventually follows the bond market not the other way around.

So one pause doesn't necessarily change the path the bond market is pricing.

If higher rates arrive while credit markets still price a benign outcome, one of those assumptions has to give.
