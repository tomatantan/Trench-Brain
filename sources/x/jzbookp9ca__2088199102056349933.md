Yaju Senpai is a character born from Japanese internet culture, based on an anonymous actor who appeared in a gay adult film in the early 2000s.
 
People started using his unique delivery, facial expressions, voice, and awkward acting to make reaction images, music videos, edited clips, and all kinds of absurd parodies.

That weird phrasing caught on, and the track was named “Ikisugiru Parade” (“Coming Too Much Parade”).
