🇰🇼🇮🇷 Kuwait says it downed 32 Iranian drones over its skies today

He says the Iranian aggression was going after vital facilities across the country.

The catch is the intercepts had a cost on the ground.

The intercepts held, even if the debris didn't stay in the sky.
