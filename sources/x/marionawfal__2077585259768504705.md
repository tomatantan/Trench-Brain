Deep in China's Taklamakan desert sit full replicas of US warships, fighter jets, the Yokosuka base housing tens of thousands of American troops, and Taiwan's government district in Taipei.

The warships aren't even static, they're mounted on rail cars along a 23-mile track to simulate vessels moving at sea, then targeted with live missiles. 

Nations don't build this kind of target practice for fun.
