Aztec Labs CEO Joe Andrews on why Canton's privacy is just trusting an intermediary, not real cryptographic privacy

"It's what I touched on earlier, privacy through intermediaries versus cryptographic privacy. The way they achieve their private stablecoins or private transactions is exactly the trust me bro, there's a sequencer who has a reputation, and you send all your transactions to that sequencer and trust, with a non-credible commitment, that they're not going to monetize that data or sell it to anyone"

"So while it jumps on the privacy bandwagon, it's basically a bunch of banks trying to adopt a different tech stack"

"I think it should come out in the wash, at least for these use cases that require strong credible commitments and actual privacy that's backed by cryptographic guarantees"
