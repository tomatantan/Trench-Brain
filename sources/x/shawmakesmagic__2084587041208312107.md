I get why people hate AI

They don’t see what’s in it for them— in fact it’s all marketing the opposite. AI to take their job. Take their passions.

It doesn’t have to be this way. When you own your data, your inference, your hardware and its kept honest by an open source culture, you can reap the benefits of the technology without paying that cost.

We don’t want to take people’s jobs. We don’t want to make their lifelong passions pointless. The opposite— we want to give them their time back so they can enjoy more of what they love, and give them their tools to stay competitive while not having to work as long or as hard.

We can bring everyone along. We have to bring everyone along.

It’s the most important thing one can work on right now, I think.
