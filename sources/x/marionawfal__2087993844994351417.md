🇺🇸 Bill Clinton once got smuggled onto Air Force One in a catering truck because he couldn't manage the front steps. 

Nobody ever reported it. Not then, not for decades.

That detail comes from Robert "Buzz" Patterson, the guy who carried Clinton's nuclear football, defending Trump's own catering-truck switcheroo out of Turkey last month. 

Trump boarded the real plane in public, then got quietly moved into a smaller jet while the big 747 flew off as a decoy. With fighter escorts, senior officials, and reporters still on board with their window shades closed.

Patterson's take: none of this is new. Presidents have used decoy planes and motorcades for years, precisely because it works. 

What worries him isn't the tactic, it's that someone on the inside leaked how it was done. 

Because so few people know the full playbook that a leak like this basically has to come from someone deep in the process.
