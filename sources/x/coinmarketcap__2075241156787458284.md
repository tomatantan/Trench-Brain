💡 CoinMarketCap | Crypto 101: Market Cap vs Liquidity 💡

A big market cap does not mean you can sell easily. 
Most beginners learn this the hard way.

Market cap is the size. Liquidity is whether the market can absorb your sell:

🔹 Market cap = price x circulating supply
🔹 Liquidity = how much you can sell before the price moves
🔹 A huge cap with shallow liquidity is a trap

So size tells you how big a coin is. 
Liquidity tells you if you can leave!
