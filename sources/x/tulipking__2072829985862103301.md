I’ve come around with a new take on Ansem’s coin: it might work. He’s essentially decided to CTO the token and run it like Zora and Friend Tech always wished their creator coins did.

I think the unique advantage here is that the token exists outside of the constraints of any protocol. It’s a basic pump coin where 100% of creator rewards go to Ansem & he holds 58% of the supply to do whatever he wants with. He’s not tied to any arbitrary bonding curves, vesting schedules or gated experiences within some B-Tier crypto app.

He can airdrop to people who create whatever he defines as value, leverage his network to get it integrated into other protocols (including stuff he owns like bullpen), and use his massive audience & media properties to funnel attention to it.

More importantly, the coin can actually push attention to himself. If working for Threadguy has taught me anything, attention has value. Ansem doesn’t have to monetize the coin if he can monetize the attention and reach it creates for him. Ask yourself what the value of an additional 100k followers or a doubling of engagement rate does for him? The answer is a lot.

If Ansem can avoid getting greedy (which is not easy when you’re sitting on that much money), he’s in a position where he never really has to sell to make money from this endeavor. Therefore he can avoid a lot of reputational damage that typically comes with CTO’ing or launching coins.

The aggregate benefit to the Solana ecosystem is obvious. I was fading the effect at first, but shoutout Adam tech, the rally in onchain activity seems pretty real. The trenches are reviving and with that comes renewed activity and interest in Solana.

Take a look at the coins Ansem is now shilling: Meteora, Backpack, Nest, … A mostly respectable list of tokens with real revenues downstream of Tokenized Stocks (a durable business). I imagine money that flows into those tokens from his own isn’t really money lost assuming he has reasonable exposure to them as well.

Memes are an emergent crypto phenomenon but as far as I can tell nobody who’s as much of a proficient operator AND as well intentioned as Ansem is has really been willing to run this playbook given the obvious reputational damage. I originally believed this had to end in a -EV way but I’m revising my perspective that this might actually end +EV.

I personally am not buying the coin but I have picked up some exposure to Solana and other ecosystem projects I believe have decent long term, let’s see where this goes.
