🇺🇸🇮🇷 Trump says Iran's Pickaxe Mountain is next on his target list

Pickaxe is an underground tunnel complex just south of Natanz, declared to the UN back in 2020 as a future site to build centrifuges, the machines that spin nuclear fuel.

Trump: “We'll probably give Pickaxe a shot relatively soon."

Tehran hit back today, a senior security source telling CNN that Iran would answer with a "devastating response" if he follows through.

Here's the catch. The Institute for Science and International Security, the weapons watchdog tracking the site, says it's still under construction, holds no enriched uranium, and isn't operational, which is likely why nobody has bothered striking it yet.

Trump's threatening to bomb an unfinished tunnel that isn't even doing anything yet.
