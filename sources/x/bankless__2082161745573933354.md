Did you know @AskVenice is building out their own compute and data infrastructure?

A question from the $VVV QnA: How will @venice owning their own compute and data center infrastructure impact $VVV burn?

@JonShapeShift: "One of the places we're deploying capital is you know building out data centers and owning our own GPUs as much as we can."

"This allows us to do a number of things, both from the privacy side of things, but also trying to get our inference margins down. So our expectation is that over time this will help us drive better margins in terms of the cost it actually costs Venice in order to produce inference for users. And the whole idea is if we can get those margins down, then we can use the difference in the margin to help supply more towards buying and burning of VVV."

"Increase the margins, get better margins, and be able to route more and more of that to buy and burn VVV."
