Johann Kerbrat reframes the "Ethereum doesn't charge enough rent" debate as a short-term way of looking at it:

"We wanted to focus on what we're good at, UX and financial products, not rebuilding Ethereum's security and decentralization." 

"That takes a long time, proof of work to proof of stake, multiple foundations. We just benefit from it, plus EVM liquidity."

"On the rent, I don't know if it's fair or unfair. That's just how Ethereum was set by default."

"But we only launched three weeks ago. If we bring tens of millions of customers and real utility, not just volatility, that's a long-term source of revenue for both projects." 

"That's how a side project becomes a major one."
