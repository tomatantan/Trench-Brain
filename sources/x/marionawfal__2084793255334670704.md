🇺🇸 Sec. Bessent addressed the massive U.S. debt and deficits.

He said the deficit as a share of the economy was extremely high in 2024, the worst outside of wars or recessions. They brought it down in 2025 and were on track to keep cutting it.

Military spending went up because of current conflicts and years of neglect. Set that aside, and the rest of the deficit is basically flat or only slightly higher this year.

He isn’t too worried. A big reason is the full expensing rules that let companies write off factories and equipment right away. That hits tax revenue now but builds real productive capacity, creates jobs, and should bring in more tax money down the road.

He called it a win-win that creates actual national wealth instead of just more government spending.
