The Umbra team walked through the entire onboarding flow:

Create an account, deposit funds...the standard process

The differentiator is the shielding: once you shield your tokens or stocks, they become completely private. You can still swap, transfer, and pay with them, just invisibly to the outside world. Web app users can even skip account creation entirely and connect straight through Phantom.

The payroll use case makes the stakes concrete. Without shielding, on-chain salaries are fully public, so colleagues can see exactly what each other earns, and treasury addresses expose every strategic move a company makes. Umbra Payroll fixes that specific pain point, hiding salary data completely, which is exactly why teams paying out onchain have gravitated toward it.
