🇺🇸 57 decomposing bodies were found inside a Chicago funeral home.

Authorities recovered the remains Thursday at South Chicago Chapel on the Southeast Side.

The bodies were stored in the basement, closets, and an attached garage, unrefrigerated, with rodents and maggots present.

The funeral director’s license was immediately suspended.

Neighbors said they had been smelling something like a dead animal for about two months.

The same operators had previous problems with another facility that was shut down last year.

What should have been a place of care turned into a storage site for the dead.

The investigation is ongoing.
