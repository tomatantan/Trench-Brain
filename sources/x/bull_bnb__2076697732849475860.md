Happy 9th anniversary Binance

I signed up on Binance in September 2017 after moving from Bittrex and Cryptopia.

I still remember my first trades and  $BNB was one of them.

Back then, BNB wasn't the giant it is today. We held it to pay trading fees. It was simply part of being on Binance.

Then came the brutal bear market.

2018 wiped out almost everything I believed in.

2019 introduced perpetuals.

Then the COVID crash hit in 2020, and like many others, I got absolutely wrecked. Thats when I sent that SS to scooter.

Ironically, the only meaningful bag I had left was BNB.

That conviction stayed with me.

In August 2020, I created the Bull_BNB account because BNB was the only token left in my portfolio.

From there I watched BNB Chain explode with Burger, CAKE, SafeMoon, DeFi, memes, NFTs, and every cycle that followed. I traded LUNC, PEPE, and countless other narratives. Some years were incredible, some were brutal, but BNB was the one constant.

Along the way, I was lucky enough to meet @cz_binance in Paris during Binance's 5th Anniversary celebrations, and later that year I met both CZ and @heyibinance again in Dubai when Binance lit up the Burj Khalifa. She was with then ceo of trust wallet.

I've made money.
I've lost money.
I've been humbled more times than I can count.

But after all these years, one thing never changed

Binance always felt like home. BNB always felt like home.

Here's to nine years of Binance.

And hopefully, many more.
