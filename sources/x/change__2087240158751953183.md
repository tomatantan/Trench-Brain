Giovanni Galante lost his wife, Grace, on 9/11. She was 29 years old and worked on the 105th floor of the North Tower.

Today, exactly one month before the 25th anniversary of the attacks, Giovanni is renewing his call for Mayor Zohran Mamdani not to attend the upcoming 9/11 commemoration ceremony. His petition calling for Mamdani's exclusion has gathered more than 80,000 signatures.

In a new Fox News op-ed, Giovanni explains why he believes Mamdani's attendance would distract from the families gathering to remember those they lost, and raises concerns about the mayor's public positions and associations. "Twenty-five years. One morning. Let us have it," he writes.

Asked about the petition late last month, Mamdani said he still plans to attend, saying he would "proudly honor the families, the survivors, the first responders forever impacted by that horrific terror attack."
