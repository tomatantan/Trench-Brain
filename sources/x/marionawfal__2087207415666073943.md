🇩🇪 🇺🇦 🇱🇹 A secret antenna found near Leipzig/Halle Airport was used to guide an explosive drone toward Ukrainian cargo planes.

German investigators say the antenna boosted control signals during the suspected sabotage attempt on August 4.

The drone had been specially modified with an extra battery, camera, 5G router and dual SIM cards.

It flew dark, no navigation lights, and was steered over the cellular network, raising the possibility it was operated from another country entirely.

DNA recovered from both the drone and 600 grams of unexploded Semtex has pointed investigators toward Lithuania.

Only a defective detonator stopped the device from exploding.

Leipzig/Halle Airport is a major European cargo hub and serves as a key logistics and maintenance base for Ukrainian Antonov cargo aircraft, including those supporting NATO-related transport operations (SALIS program).

Ukrainian An-124 freighters regularly operate from there, sometimes carrying military-related cargo.

The incident on the night of 4–5 August 2026 forced a temporary full closure of the airport, diverting multiple flights.

A second unidentified object was involved in a mid-air collision with a cargo aircraft that had to go around due to the security alert.
