🇩🇰 Denmark drafted its women and marched a princess into the barracks because its entire professional army is just 9,000 SOLDIERS

On August 3, roughly 1,600 recruits reported to 14 bases for eleven months of service, nearly triple the old four month term. 

Women are conscripted on equal terms for the first time, about 20 percent of this intake, with a lottery filling whatever volunteers leave short.

Among them is 19 year old Princess Isabella.

Now hold that against the size of the force. 

Denmark fields around 9,000 professional personnel. 

The plan pushes annual conscripts to 7,500 by 2033, which means teenagers on eleven month tours will nearly equal the standing army.

That is the actual state of European defense. 

Decades of shrinking left armies too small to hold anything, and the quickest fix available is calling up eighteen year olds.

Later this month, Danish conscripts deploy to Greenland for the first time.
