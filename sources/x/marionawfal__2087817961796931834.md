🇺🇸🇺🇦 A U.S. armored brigade got wiped out by Ukrainian drone operators in a NATO training exercise, and it wasn't even close.

During the drill in Germany, called Combined Resolve, Ukrainian drone units easily spotted and picked apart U.S. troops and armored vehicles rotated in from Fort Hood, Texas.

The heavy vehicles kicked up dust that made them easy targets for Ukrainian recon drones, which were followed by explosive-dropping drones and FPV drones that smash straight into the target. 

The Ukrainians were racking up "kills" so fast that units had to be respawned just to keep the exercise running.

This is years after the U.S. pioneered long-range drone warfare and just spent billions on new drone and counterdrone tech.

Ukraine has had nearly 4.5 years of fighting Russia to master this. 

The U.S. is still catching up, and the training ground is showing it.
