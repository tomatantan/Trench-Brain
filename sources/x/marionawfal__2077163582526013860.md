🇺🇸🇮🇷 Asked directly if he still plans to seize Kharg Island, Trump:

"I can't say that to you because if I did, it would be foolish."

The non-answer is the strategy. 

Kharg handles roughly 90% of Iran's oil exports, so leaving the threat alive and undated keeps Tehran guessing while Trump decides whether the island is worth taking. 

It's the same madman ambiguity that ran the whole war, "I may do it, I may not," applied to the crown jewel of Iran's economy.

What he's really protecting is the leverage of not knowing.

The moment he confirms or rules out taking Kharg, Iran can plan around it.
