🇵🇰🇸🇦🇹🇷🇮🇷 Pakistan just called Iran to explain exactly what it signed up against.

Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar walked Iranian counterpart Abbas Araghchi through the "key aspects" of the defense pact signed in Mecca on Friday. Araghchi thanked him for the heads up. Tehran's said nothing else.

The deal is the big one: attack one of the three, you've attacked all of them. NATO-style, and all three are U.S. allies.

So why the courtesy call? Because the pact is aimed straight at Iran. Tehran's spent the war hammering Saudi targets, energy sites, civilian infrastructure, U.S. interests. 

The message is simple: try that now and you're fighting Pakistan and Turkey too.

Iran's not sweating it. One lawmaker waved it off as a "paper agreement" that won't keep the Saudis safe.

Whether it's paper or not, the map around Iran got a lot tighter.
