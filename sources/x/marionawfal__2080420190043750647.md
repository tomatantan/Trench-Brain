🇸🇦 Saudi Aramco is rerouting its oil to dodge the Houthis, offering cargoes from Egypt's Mediterranean coast instead of sailing past Yemen...

-Aramco is putting extra crude cargoes on offer at Sidi Kerir, its terminal on Egypt's Mediterranean shore, so buyers never have to pass Bab el-Mandeb at the Red Sea's southern mouth

-The trigger was the blockade's first enforcement: a drone and missile strike on two Saudi tankers, the Encela and Laylia

-The stakes are enormous for Riyadh, since its Red Sea port of Yanbu now handles roughly 75% of the kingdom's exports and exists specifically as the escape hatch from Hormuz

-Hormuz itself is running at a trickle, with daily transits in the single digits against a prewar 125 to 140

The theory stopped being a theory the moment Aramco started moving barrels around it. 

Saudi Arabia built Yanbu as the answer to a closed strait, and now it's paying to route around the route it built.
