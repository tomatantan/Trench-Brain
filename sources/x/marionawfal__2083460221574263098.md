🇻🇪🇺🇸 Six months after Maduro's removal, U.S-backed talks on new elections in Venezuela are set to begin, and both camps are already showing signs of buyer's remorse.

The opposition's biggest names, María Corina Machado and Edmundo González, are staying out of the negotiations, while hardline Chavistas are burning U.S flags and denouncing the process before it's even begun.

Washington insists it's only "facilitating," but let's not pretend anyone believes the U.S flew in to be a neutral referee. The question is whether it has enough leverage to force compromises that years of failed negotiations never could.

Venezuela has seen this movie before: endless dialogue, dramatic handshakes, lofty promises... and somehow the people waiting for democracy are always told to check back next season.

This time, the scoreboard is simple: political prisoners released, an independent election authority, and an actual vote where the loser leaves.
