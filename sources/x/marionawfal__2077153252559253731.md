🇪🇸 Unai Simón just broke a record that survived since 1990.

Six clean sheets across his World Cup career, one more than the previous mark shared by Walter Zenga and Iker Casillas at five apiece. After shutting out France in the semifinal, that tally is already up to seven.

Spain's defense has been the real story of this tournament. Six clean sheets in seven games. The only team that got past this back line all summer was Belgium, who snuck out a 2-1 win in the quarterfinal.

Here's the full run: 0-0 vs Cape Verde, 4-0 over Saudi Arabia, a 1-0 win at Uruguay, 3-0 over Austria, 1-0 over Portugal, a 2-1 win over Belgium, and now 2-0 over France.

One goal conceded across the entire tournament. That's the number carrying Spain into Sunday's final in New Jersey, chasing their second star.
