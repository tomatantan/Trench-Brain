🇺🇸 California Governor Gavin Newsom dropped the diaper contract Friday night.

That's the classic move. Reporters have clocked off, nobody's watching, and the story dies over the weekend.

The $6.2 million deal went to Baby2Baby, a nonprofit whose CEO sits on the board of a charity started by Newsom's wife.

State records say it was non-competitively bid, so nobody else got to make an offer or undercut the price. It's already been extended by another $12.5 million.

Where the cash goes: $3.89 million on diapers, $750,000 shipping, $610,000 warehousing, $917,000 on 12 employees.

Republicans are asking why the state needs a middleman at all when California or the hospitals could just buy the diapers themselves.

Watchdogs want a full audit of how Baby2Baby got picked.
