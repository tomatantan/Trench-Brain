🇮🇷 Iran's bombed nuclear sites are still frozen

Months on from the war, the big three still aren't breathing.

Natanz sits dark. The power's still out, the air systems are still wrecked, and nobody can even get down into the underground halls where the real work happened.

Fordow's the same story underground. The tunnel mouths are still packed with dirt, and Iran quietly turned the approach roads into a slow winding crawl so nothing can drive straight in.

Esfahan, where they kept the enriched uranium, is buttoned up too, tunnels blocked, no fresh movement.

Then there's Pickaxe Mountain, the one nobody bombed. Trucks keep rolling up to the western tunnels, and the digging hasn't stopped.

Everything they hit is dead. The one thing they missed is very much alive.
