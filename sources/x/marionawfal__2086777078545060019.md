🇬🇧🇨🇳 British Royal Marines and special forces-linked drones were secretly transmitting data back to Beijing...

A £12 million fleet of K3 Scout surveillance drones used by the Special Boat Service and Royal Marines were found sending regular “heartbeat” signals to an IP address inside China.

The Chinese-made camera systems kept transmitting even when the drones were powered down.

Some of the aircraft had been used in planning for a UK freedom-of-navigation mission in the Strait of Hormuz and had operated near senior special-forces meetings.

The discovery lands while the Labour government is trying to improve relations with Beijing and has approved a major Chinese embassy expansion.

Opposition parties are now demanding a TOTAL audit of Chinese components across the military supply chain.
