X is nuking its creator monetization program and starting over

Starting September 8, the old system is being replaced by the Original Content Rewards Program, and every existing creator has to reapply from scratch.

The new message from X is basically: stop farming engagement and start making something yourself.

Creators will need 500,000 verified-user impressions over 90 days to qualify, while only impressions from Premium subscribers will actually generate payouts. Get hit with a Community Note? That post earns nothing.

And the classic "LIKE, REPOST, BOOKMARK AND COMMENT 👇" routine could now get creators booted from monetization entirely.

Reuploaded content won't qualify even if you credit the original creator, while memes and commentary will have to add "meaningful value" to count as original.

In the announcement, X said: 

"Creating original content takes time, effort, expertise, and creativity.

The Original Content Rewards Program is designed to reward creators who bring those things to X.

We want to recognize creators who break news, share expertise, tell stories, create entertainment, and contribute meaningful perspectives to the conversation.

Our goal is simple: reward creators who bring something new to X."
