President Trump announces that American citizen and former U.S. Marine Robert Gilman is finally coming home after more than four years of imprisonment in Russia, where he was detained in 2022 under the Biden administration. 

"After my discussions with President Vladimir Putin, Russia has agreed to release him, very much on a Humanitarian Basis. I just spoke to him, and he had one request — A GREAT cheeseburger when he lands. I will take care of that!" - President Donald J. Trump
