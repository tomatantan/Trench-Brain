🇺🇸 The S&P 500 is no longer a diversified index. Just 10 mega-caps, powered by the Magnificent Seven, now control a record 43% of its total value.

The top 10 heavyweights have held a market share at or above 40% for 12 straight months.

And their dominance has grown at a staggering pace. Over the past decade, the collective weight of these 10 giants has more than doubled.

At the same time, smaller companies are being squeezed out. The bottom 250 firms in the index have seen their total share cut in half, dropping to a mere 7%.

This creates an unprecedented financial gap: the 10 largest U.S. corporations now carry 6x the weight of the bottom 250 combined.

Buying the index today is largely a highly concentrated bet on a handful of tech monopolies.
