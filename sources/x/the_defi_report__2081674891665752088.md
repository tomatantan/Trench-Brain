If you still picture @ethena as a bet on funding rates, you're looking at an outdated version of the protocol.

Today, Ethena looks more like a "diversified onchain asset manager or credit fund" than a "synthetic onchain dollar" paying yield from one primary source.

In January 2025, ~92% of protocol revenue came from funding rates and basis trades. That figure is now roughly 1%. Yield now comes mostly from DeFi lending, liquid stablecoins, RWAs, and institutional credit.

The upside is a more diversified revenue base. The cost is a broader set of risks.

Looking at today's Ethena through last cycle's lens is an easy way to miss what's changed.
