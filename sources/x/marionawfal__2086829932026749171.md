🇮🇱🇱🇧 Israeli drone drops munitions on the Ali al-Taher area of southern Lebanon.

Ali al-Taher is strategic high ground in southern Lebanon’s Nabatieh district, north of the Litani River. 

It sits roughly between Kfar Tebnit and Nabatieh al-Fawqa and rises to about 600 meters.

It overlooks the city of Nabatieh and key approach routes, giving whoever controls it a key advantage in southeastern Lebanon.
