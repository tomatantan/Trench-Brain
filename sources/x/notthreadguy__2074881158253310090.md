been thinking a lot about this idea of a "solved game" and how a game becomes unsolved 

The obvious answer is introducing A new variable without a known pricing model. an unknown. in 2019 sneakers were fully solved. Every drop was a re release of something that already existed. the price target for the fifth yeezy was all but guaranteed. The same bots worked. everyone knew exactly how much to spend on their setup and could calculate almost exact returns. 

This destroyed the reselling game for obvious reasons (plus other problems like extreme over supply, fakes, cultural evolutions etc)

Then Nike announced the Travis Scott 1's (at the height of his fame) via raffle. Mania. nike Snkrs and the raffle system was unproven. Nobody knew how to get supply. Travis scotts resell motion was completely untested outside of crazy merch sales. Nobody knew how to price the shoe. Or if they could even get a pair. 

Most got none and they were worth WAY more than the predictions. 

It led to one of the most profitable sneaker drops ever and a mini resurgence in the market. All the ogs came back for one more ride. Sneakers were hot again. 

it's an interesting heuristic. A single unknown can make a known extremely fucking unknown again 

1) in the case of ansem it was someone with that much motion, reputation and influence backing a coin of his name, that hasn't really been done before (this time around) in a market of the same 100 people shilling the same coins. huge unknown. How high can it go? who knows. 

Fomo leads to more fomo leads to more ansem motion and all the sudden there's a massive wealth effect from one coin. 

Nobody else could have done it. And nobody expected ansem to try. that's why it's worked so well. 

2) in the case of hood/vlad it's the most popular retail exchange in the world launching a chain and a founder that has not yet fucked up the memecoin thing but instead vocalized support in day 1 (what if he doesn't pull a base) and oh my god what if they get all their users onchain. A lot of dreaming. But people need to be able to dream again. Dreaming drives fomo, which drives more dreaming.

The added friction of the chain being unusable while everyone wants to buy definitely helped. 

Does this "save memecoins" and bring back 2024? probably not. who cares. That's not the point. 

It's more important to identify good spots rather than predict the future. And we were just given two incredible spots
