Society has it completely backwards.

Getting rich has nothing to do with pure intelligence. 

And it certainly has nothing to do with learned intelligence (uni, school).

Especially in the AI-era where knowledge has been commoditised.

Truthfully, getting rich is just about taking action. 

The willingness to accept failure and how quickly you can dust yourself off and go again and again and again.

My first 3 business ideas failed, my 4th clicked. 

And that was probably "lucky".

You might have 11 tries, and only your 12th clicks.

But if you don't shoot you can't score - plain and simple.
