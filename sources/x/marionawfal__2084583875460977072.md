🇮🇶🇮🇷 Iraq’s Oil Ministry says its crude is still moving through the Strait of Hormuz, but the responsibility ends at the port.

Officials confirmed tankers loaded with Iraqi oil continue to transit the waterway.

Under the Free on Board sales system, once the vessel leaves the loading terminal the buyer takes full control of the voyage, including any transit permits.

The ministry does not own the tankers and bears no responsibility for their routes after departure.

It only monitors the shipments through global tracking systems until the cargo reaches its destination refineries.

Exports have shown steady month-to-month improvement following the earlier disruption caused by the Hormuz crisis.
