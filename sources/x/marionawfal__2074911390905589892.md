🇺🇸 America's nuclear modernization problem in one contract: $49.5M to rebuild 1980s electronics inside a cruise missile because the replacement isn’t ready yet.

The AGM‑86B has been flying since 1982. Its LRSO successor is still years away.

So for now Boeing has to redesign circuit boards with modern parts that mimic obsolete hardware, while the B‑52s carrying these missiles get new Rolls‑Royce engines, AESA radars, and glass cockpits just to stay useful through 2050.

Bottom line: the nuclear triad depends on a weapon older than most of the people who maintain it. That's why can’t let the old system fail before the new one exists.
