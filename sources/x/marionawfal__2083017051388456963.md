🇾🇪🇸🇦 Forget Hormuz for a second... Bab el-Mandeb might be the real choke point.

Saudi’s east-to-west pipeline has been the quiet safety valve keeping global oil markets from completely melting down while the Gulf stays messy.

That pipeline dumps crude onto the Red Sea side, right where the Houthis can reach it.

They don’t need to destroy every Saudi oil facility.

They just need to hit the pipeline or the terminals hard enough and often enough to take that backup route offline.

Suddenly the world’s remaining workaround for Hormuz trouble disappears.

Iran doesn’t even have to do the shooting itself.

The Houthis already sit in the perfect spot, and every successful strike raises the cost of moving energy through one of the planet’s most important shipping corridors.

Markets panic over the Strait of Hormuz.

They should be watching the southern entrance to the Red Sea just as closely.
