🇮🇶 Iraq just gave gun owners until December to register or become criminals.

The Interior Ministry says it has logged about 6 million weapons to get everything under state control. 

They have already seized 143,000 unregistered firearms and shut down 71 fake PMF offices, which are the umbrella of mostly Shiite armed factions.

Registration ends December 31, 2026. Keep an unregistered gun past that date and you are officially outside the law.

Loose weapons have messed up Iraqi security for years, but a database is not going to scare a militia. 

Real disarmament means picking a fight Baghdad is not sure it can win.
