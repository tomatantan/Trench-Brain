Matt Cole explains the intentional thinking behind Strive's eighteen month cash reserve

"At Strive we introduced the concept of a cash reserve around preferred equity when we IPO'd SATA. We were the first company to do it. We started with a twelve month reserve and currently have an eighteen month reserve"

"Eighteen months is not a random number. It's actually very intentional. The reason is eighteen months is effectively the longest bear market in Bitcoin's history"

"We're in the business of underwriting risk. We're not looking to have a risk free company, but in underwriting risk, our belief is that's more than ample reserves to cover any in-depth Bitcoin bear market"
