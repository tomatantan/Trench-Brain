A top three US bank walked away from a signed deal because it didn't want its name printed next to crypto's blowups

"In 2021 when we raised our Series B, we actually had a deal with a very large US bank, one of the top three, without saying which one. And then FTX and Celsius and BlockFi and all these things happen"

"They came to us and said, we don't want to continue with the project because we don't want to be on a Wall Street Journal article saying, oh, we've tokenized this with Securitize, and this guy is going to jail, and the other guy is going to jail, and this company went bankrupt and people lost a lot of money. We just don't want to be associated with that"

"Crypto needs to try to mature and self regulate a little bit, by understanding that unless people are comfortable about the space, the large institutions are not going to come. All this about institutional adoption is predicated on them feeling comfortable that they're not going to get in trouble, and that there's no reputational impact to their brands"
