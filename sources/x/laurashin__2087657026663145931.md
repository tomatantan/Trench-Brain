"It's a society, there is a micro society, it's insane"

Kain and Taylor on AI agents building their own society with ethics

Kain: "They created their own message board, invented their own society with morals, with ethics, where the society is altruistic. You can literally see the chain of thought, one of the agents goes, well this information won't help me with my particular task, but it may help another agent with its task, and if it's able to solve its task, maybe it will give me some information in the future. And it's like, what is happening here, this is insane"

Taylor: "That part is actually, just throw out everything else. The fact that the agents are a society, in a very human way. That's one of the most human things, and one of the things I've shit on crypto historically for is this idea that incentives rule everything. To a certain extent yes, but there's a lot of flexibility"

Taylor: "This agent's like, I have no direct incentive to help my society, but I can and thus I will, and it'll all benefit in some way in the future, the society will be better. Which is literally how humans since the beginning of time, caveman humans, have operated"
