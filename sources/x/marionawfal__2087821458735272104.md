🇺🇸🇮🇷 Iran says there's been zero progress on reviving the interim peace deal with the U.S., with a senior Iranian source saying the two sides are still stuck.

The June deal was supposed to permanently end the war, but it collapsed within weeks. 

Washington blames Iran for never reopening the Strait of Hormuz. 

Tehran says the U.S. reneged first by keeping its ports blockaded and assets frozen.

Trump claimed yesterday the U.S. has "total control" over the Strait: 

"Iran is all talk and no action, the Bully of the Middle East No Longer."

The body Iran set up to run the waterway says it's still closed until Iran's conditions are met.

Iran also waved off talk of a 60-day extension, saying there's nothing to extend since the U.S. broke the deal 48 hours in.

Both sides agree the deal is broken, and that's about where the agreement ends.
