🇸🇾🇮🇶 Nechirvan Barzani, President of the Kurdistan Region, is currently in Damascus, where he was welcomed by Syrian President Ahmed al-Sharaa.

It's the first official visit by the Kurdistan Region leadership to Syria since the fall of Assad's regime.

The most critical aspect of this visit is advancing the implementation of the January 29 integration agreement between the new Syrian administration and the Kurdish-led Syrian Democratic Forces (SDF).

Barzani acts as a mediator here, ensuring the integration of SDF fighters into the national military while still maintaining local Kurdish autonomy.
