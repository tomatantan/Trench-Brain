🇮🇷🇺🇸 Iran says it wrecked 11 U.S. jets and choppers on the ground

This comes straight from Tasnim, Iran's own state media, so take it with a big grain of salt.

An IRGC spokesman claims that over the 15-day fight from July 8th to the 21st, Iran destroyed 11 American jets and helicopters parked at bases around the region.

The list they're claiming got hit:

- 17 drones, 8 brand new
- an F-15 in its hangar
- a P-8 surveillance plane
- a C-17 transport
- 8 refueling planes

The catch: Iran's made similar claims throughout this war and CENTCOM keeps shooting them down, so take the number as a talking point until it's verified elsewhere.
