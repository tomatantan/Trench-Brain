🇺🇸 West Virginia lawmaker accused of using Roblox and Snapchat to groom student

David Elliott Pritt has been arrested on federal charges alleging he used Roblox and Snapchat to pursue a sexual relationship with a 15-year-old student.

According to court documents, investigators say the alleged contact began while Pritt was the student’s teacher and later moved from gaming platforms to social media before leading to alleged sexual encounters at school.

Pritt has been removed from his roles in the state House, and lawmakers are reviewing possible further action.

He has not been convicted, and the allegations will be decided through the legal process.
