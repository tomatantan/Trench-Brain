🇲🇦🇪🇸 Most of the tens of thousands who rushed into Ceuta this week are already heading back to Morocco.

Returnees told Reuters they were drawn in by viral videos promising easy access to Europe, only to find shuttered shops, no food, sky-high prices, and no way to reach the mainland.

One man from Fez said he survived 3 days on water alone.

Others described the enclave as “like a prison.”

Hunger, exhaustion and local hostility broke the momentum fast.

Of the 50–60 thousand who crossed, the vast majority have left within 72 hours.

The "dream" seemed to collapse the moment it met reality on the ground.
