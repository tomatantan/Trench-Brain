For @PaulFrambot, variable-rate lending fails the basic test of a true financial primitive:

“If you have variable rate, what does it vary on?”

“The answer is that it’s arbitrary. You rely on something to say what is the rate, which makes it not a primitive.”

“A primitive does not rely on anything for its existence.”

“Fixed rate, fixed term is the right primitive for financing. It is the true and the only true primitive.”
