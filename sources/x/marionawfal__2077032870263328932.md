🇺🇦 Ukraine’s PM Yulia Svyrydenko has resigned as part of a major government reshuffle ordered by Zelensky.

Parliament approved her exit on Tuesday. Zelensky has not named a replacement yet, saying Kyiv is shifting its political strategy amid new challenges.

Svyrydenko, appointed just a year ago, was seen as effective in managing U.S. relations and helped secure a minerals deal with Washington. She is now being offered a new role focused on a key partner.

The frontrunner to replace her is Sergiy Koretsky, CEO of state energy giant Naftogaz. Energy security is critical as Russia intensifies strikes on Ukraine’s power grid.

Zelensky is tightening control and realigning his team for the next phase of the war.
