"PE's juice is flip it. Berkshire's juice is insurance flow. And for Orange Juice, our juice is that we think Bitcoin is a little bit of rocket fuel to push you over that edge"

Lyn Alden, Co-founder of Orange Juice, on why every permanent capital vehicle needs an edge, and what theirs is

"You back up and say why aren't there just more permanent capital vehicles out there? There are a handful, but why aren't there a ton? The general answer is because you need some sort of edge. If you're buying small cashflow businesses at low multiples, you generally need an edge that would push your returns higher. For private equity, their answer is flip it. We don't want to hold these longer term, we want to buy a company, gut it, lever it, find ways to juice up the multiple and get out, and if you do that over and over again, that's their juice"

"If you're Berkshire Hathaway, buying companies and holding them long term, their main juice is that they have the best source of leverage in the world, which is their insurance flow. Berkshire is one of the biggest insurance companies in the world, and insurance flow is an incredibly low cost, incredibly stable source of leverage. They also have some of the lowest borrowing rates in the world, so they're able to go buy these businesses on insurance flow and some of the lowest bond yields in the world, and that's their juice"

"For Orange Juice, name implied, our general view is that our edge, our X factor, our juice, is what pushes you over that edge"
