🇸🇾🇺🇸🇷🇺 Syria has told the United States that it's ready to sharply cut its purchases of Russian oil.

The pledge emerged during talks aimed at removing the last major U.S. terrorism-related sanctions still hanging over Damascus.

American officials asked for the reduction; Syrian counterparts agreed it would help speed the process.

Russia currently supplies the bulk of Syria’s crude after other options dried up.

Finding replacements quickly will not be easy and could risk fuel shortages or higher costs.

Syrian officials say they want to diversify, but insist they need the sanctions lifted first to open new supply channels.

The move would also chip away at one of the remaining economic levers Moscow still holds in Syria.
