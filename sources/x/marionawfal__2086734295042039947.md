🇺🇸 Turns out the secret to happier airline passengers is putting Starlink on the plane.

United and Virgin Atlantic went all in on the satellite network, and the customer numbers are night and day. 

United says Wi-Fi satisfaction on Starlink jets is more than double the rest of its fleet.

Virgin's even sharper. Connectivity satisfaction jumped more than 40 points since launch, and 75% of flyers now hop online on Starlink A350s, versus just 10% on everything else.

The reason it feels so good is the low orbit. 

You get 20 to 40 milliseconds of lag instead of the 2,000 you'd suffer on the old satellites parked way up high, so people stream Netflix and work gate to gate like they're at a desk.
