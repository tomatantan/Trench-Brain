Crypto can thrive while token prices fall. We’re still so early that we judge the technology by the price of the token. Nonsensical. No one decides whether to use AWS or Claude based on where Amazon or Anthropic trade.

Price follows utility, sure. But a service can be genuinely valuable and still not be a good business. Crypto is delivering real consumer preference, and that may simply mean the tokens aren’t worth what people think. It doesn’t mean crypto is dead or useless. The opposite. As structured, these networks are closer to public goods, with the value flowing up to the apps and service providers sitting on top. No surprise the most profitable businesses are the exchanges and stablecoin issuers, not the networks themselves.

Utility? Yes, a lot of it. Especially for bringing financial services to people outside the US.

Stablecoins work. Prediction markets surface useful information. Perpetuals work.

Even if tokens fall another 50-90%, crypto still wins, because it has delivered innovations that are now woven into the fabric of finance. That’s not a bear cope. It’s just a reality. Most of these tokens were overvalued to begin with - especially given the lack of net new users we’ve been able to acquire and retain. That doesn’t mean they provide no value. The market just ran far ahead of fundamentals, and that gap always closes eventually. It takes time to convert new users but the regulatory framework now is wide open to do so. The most bullish people on crypto are not the crypto natives (if any are left) but enterprises like Visa, Stripe, Blackrock and so many others.
