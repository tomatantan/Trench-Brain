🇮🇷 The U.S. and Israel hit Iran with more than 13,000 strikes and the damage was real. 

CENTCOM's chief testified that more than 85% of Iran's ballistic missile, drone, and naval industrial base was hit. 

Netanyahu bragged about wiping out 70% of Iran's steel production. 

Trump did a victory lap over sinking the navy, with 155-plus vessels reported damaged or destroyed.

Then there's what survived. 

The IRGC's underground missile networks, some carved deep under granite mountains, mostly came through intact. 

Nearly 90% of those underground sites are reportedly back to full or partial activity, including almost every missile site near the Strait of Hormuz.

Drone production jumped tenfold since the fighting, and one-way attack drones are now cheaper to churn out than the missiles trying to shoot them down.

Iran says it downed more than 200 hostile drones during the war, an MQ-9 Reaper, and claims partial or full hits on an F-35, an F-15E, and an A-10. 

Its fast boats and projectiles are still disrupting Hormuz at will.

So when Israel's defense minister says targets are picked and jets are waiting on the order, Tehran's answer is a leadership that says it's more battle-ready than before, sitting on a missile arsenal that took a beating and kept working.
