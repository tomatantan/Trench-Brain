🚨🇺🇸 🇮🇷 Trump has ORDERED a fresh attack on Iran, and U.S. officials say it could start this weekend.

-The president ordered an attack aimed at forcing Tehran to surrender, expected to last a few days and possibly beginning within hours

-His prediction at the televised Camp David cabinet meeting: "We will be hitting them very hard. And you know at some point, they're going to say, 'We just can't take it anymore,'" adding he believes the regime will eventually "peter out"

-The mood shift is total from earlier this week, when he described "very friendly negotiations" as the reason for pausing, before Iran's surprise attack on Jordan and Wednesday's retaliation

-Behind closed doors he has called the Iranians "crazy" and told confidants the interim deal was meaningless and he always knew it would fail, saying Friday that "all they do is make me angry"

-Iranian diplomats are just as worn out, telling officials the trust is gone and that every American threat makes diplomacy harder, while the IRGC vowed Thursday to renew its attacks

-The bigger two-week campaign remains an option, though his own advisers keep flagging the munitions shortage as a risk

Nothing about this is new except the exhaustion. The same threats, the same "they'll break eventually," the same days-long campaign that was going to force a surrender the last five times.

What's actually changed is the arithmetic.

Six months in, Iran has absorbed everything and never surrendered once, and the people telling Trump he's running out of interceptors are the same people who have to plan whatever comes after the weekend.
