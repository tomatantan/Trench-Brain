🇮🇷 Iran turned a funeral into coded messaging

Every foreign delegation in Tehran was walked past the coffins to a short Quran recitation, and each verse was chosen to say something.

🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia
Caught the sharpest one of the day, and the verse did the talking. The line ran, "...two armies that met in battle: one fighting in the cause of Allah and the other denying Him," a nod to the Battle of Badr fought on what is now Saudi soil, where a small believing force crushed a far bigger disbelieving one.

🇹🇷 Turkey
Drew a verse ranking those who go and fight above those who sit it out, a nudge to a partner Tehran wants leaning in, not watching.

🇱🇧 Lebanon's government
Got a passage scolding those who won't sacrifice when the call finally comes.

🇱🇧 Hezbollah
A few feet away, got the mirror image, a verse telling them not to weaken or grieve because they end up on top.

🇵🇸 Hamas
Read the lines about men who kept their covenant with God, some already martyred, some still waiting their turn.

🇾🇪 The Houthis
Praised as believers who fought and never buckled, a pat on the back for staying in the fight.

🇶🇦 Qatar
Still shuttling between Tehran and Washington, got the softest text of the day, forgiveness and divine favor, a thank-you dressed up as scripture.

Calculated down to the last verse.
