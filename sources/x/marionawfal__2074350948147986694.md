🇱🇧 Lebanon has no functioning government and no working electricity, yet everyone demands it disarm the one force that works.

Beirut-based writer Sharmine Narwani, of The Cradle, dismantles the "state within a state" argument with the bluntest possible test: look at what the Lebanese state actually delivers.

The country has run without a functioning government for years.

The lights have never reliably worked either way.

And the only thing standing between Beirut and invasion is an armed group the state has no way to replace:

"Does the country need government? No. But does it need resistance? Yes."

The demand to disarm Hezbollah, she argues, collapses on that reality.

Until someone actually lets the Lebanese army buy real weapons, the math stays exactly where it is.
