🇺🇸 The CIA apparently wasn't buying the threat to Trump as much as Israel was.

Israel reportedly warned the U.S. that Iran might try to kill Trump as he left Turkey, but the CIA looked at the intel and had “low confidence” in it.

The Secret Service wasn't taking any chances though. Trump quietly switched planes in Ankara, got driven across the airport in a catering truck, then flew to Britain on a military jet with F-16s alongside.

But this part is pretty interesting. Some U.S. intelligence officials think Israel may have shared the warning partly to influence Trump's thinking on Iran and Turkey, not just to keep him safe.

Still, nobody wanted to be the guy who ignored the warning and got it wrong.
