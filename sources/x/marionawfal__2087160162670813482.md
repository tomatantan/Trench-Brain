🚨🇺🇸🇮🇷 BREAKING: U.S. forces fired on a Panama-flagged ship that attempted to run the American blockade of Iran's ports early this morning...

Maritime security firm Vanguard reported that a Panama-flagged containership, Vela Nova, was hit by a missile while transiting westbound through the Gulf of Oman, 71 nautical miles from the coast of Pakistan.

A U.S. military helicopter reportedly fired at the ship's rudder, after the crew ignored repeated warnings.

No reports of casualties, but the ship appeared to be trying to transfer its crew to another civilian vessel following the attack.
