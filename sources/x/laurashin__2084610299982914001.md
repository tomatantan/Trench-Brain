Securitize CEO Carlos Domingo explains why they tokenized $SECZ on both Solana and Avalanche

"First, we wanted to be in more than one chain to prove that you don't have to be in a single chain. We can track the cap table and the ownership of the securities across multiple chains simultaneously. We also wanted to pick two different ecosystems. Avalanche is an EVM chain very focused on RWA, and Solana is more focused on the trading side of things"

"The shares on Solana are the ones currently trading. If you go and buy the SECZ shares onchain, you do it through the Solana implementation. We're doing it together with Jump Crypto, our market maker, who has a prop AMM technology they've been using for digital assets that we adapted together for tokenized equities"

"We also tokenized some of the restricted shares. We offered all the shareholders the option to have their shares onchain, which aren't tradable yet, with a six month lockup period. We'll then enable trading them on Avalanche as well down the road"
