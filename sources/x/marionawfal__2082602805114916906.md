🚨🇺🇸 🇮🇷 A new analysis puts hard numbers on America's interceptor crisis: roughly 827 Patriots and 278 THAADs left, down from 2,330 and 452.

-Per the Center for Strategic and International Studies, Patriot inventories have fallen under 1,000 and THAAD sits around 250-278, after months of defending bases across the Middle East

-The air defense campaign has worked, with high though not perfect interception rates, but the price was burning through more than HALF the Patriot stockpile

-The warning attached: diminished stocks may force the U.S. and its partners to take MORE RISKS with interceptions, because there are no good alternatives to Patriot and THAAD against ballistic missiles, and Navy ships are generally too far away

-The bigger shadow: the expended munitions create a near-to-medium-term risk for U.S. readiness against CHINA in the Pacific, with rebuilding expected to take YEARS despite expanded production

-The administration's budget shows the urgency: $95 billion for munitions in FY2027 plus $21 billion in the war supplemental

These are the numbers behind everything that just happened: the pause Caine argued for, the massive attack Trump shelved, and the choice he now faces after Iran's two-night salvo.

Every ballistic missile Tehran fires forces million-dollar interceptors out of a magazine that took decades to fill and will take years to refill. 

Iran can see the same math CSIS published. 

That may be exactly WHY the missiles keep coming.
