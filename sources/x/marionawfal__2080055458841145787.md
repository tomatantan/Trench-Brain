🚨🇮🇷 🇺🇸 A missile struck an electrical power station near Iran's Bushehr Nuclear Power Plant

-Bushehr Province's governor says a missile hit an electrical power station in the south of the country, near the Bushehr Nuclear Power Plant

-The distinction is critical: the strike hit a power station NEAR the plant, not the reactor itself, with no radiological claim from Iranian authorities

-Bushehr hosts Iran's only operational nuclear power plant, the Russian-built reactor that has been the subject of strike rumors all week

-The city's IRGC bases, drone sites, and coastal infrastructure have been hit repeatedly, with the U.S. previously confirming those as the targets there

An electrical substation feeding the Bushehr complex is a legitimate infrastructure target that also happens to sit beside the one facility whose direct strike would change the war's entire character.

Iran's own governor framing it as a power station rather than the reactor is notable restraint from a state that amplifies every civilian-harm image.
