🇮🇹 Naples got a hard jolt from its restless supervolcano neighbor

A 4.7 quake hit the Phlegraean Fields west of the city this evening.

Shallow, sharp, and strong enough to send rockfalls crashing down, knock out power, and rattle buildings in Pozzuoli and beyond.

People poured into the streets, and some homes took damage.

The ground in this volcanic caldera has been rising for years, and tonight it reminded everyone it’s still very much alive.

Not the Big One, but nobody’s treating it lightly.
