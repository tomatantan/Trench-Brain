George Zeng and Gordon Liao discuss how to actually read agentic commerce:

 George: "All quantitative metrics we can track are largely backwards-looking. The most interesting data is qualitative and forward-looking, how much of our own day-to-day commercial transactions are handled by agents right now? Very little." 

"The moment we see agentic transactions solving real, important problems for us, that's the leading signal. The volume numbers show up after."

 Gordon: "Absolutely agree. Think of the last time you bought something because you took an AI suggestion."

"As for numbers, roughly 70% of transactions are already mediated by smart contracts, and I watch the count of transactions under a dollar as a signal for how small-value payments add up."
