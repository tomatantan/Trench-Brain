🇺🇸 The U.S. housing market is expensive, and it’s shutting people out.

Home affordability has reportedly fallen to its worst level in history, with high prices, punishing mortgage rates, insurance costs, and property taxes crushing buyers from every direction.

For millions of Americans, working hard and saving responsibly still isn’t enough. 

The down payment keeps growing, the monthly payment keeps climbing, and the dream of owning a home keeps moving further away.

Meanwhile, longtime owners are reluctant to sell and give up their lower mortgage rates, keeping inventory tight and prices stubbornly high.

This is bigger than a bad year for real estate.

An entire generation risks being locked out of homeownership… and the wealth, stability, and security that traditionally came with it.
