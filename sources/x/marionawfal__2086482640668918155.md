🇵🇸 Hamas says it remains fully committed to the 15-point plan for the next stage of the Gaza ceasefire.

The group stated it is prepared to carry out its side of the deal in a responsible manner.

Immediate priorities include a total halt to fighting, full Israeli withdrawal, reopening of crossings, delivery of aid and shelter materials, and the start of reconstruction so the National Committee can begin its work.

Hamas also urged mediators and guarantors to enforce the agreement and stop any breaches that could derail it.
