🇮🇷 Explosions reported in Bandar Abbas and Qeshm Island, southern Iran.

Residents heard several blasts from the east of Bandar Abbas and the Qeshm maritime area.

Bandar Abbas is Iran’s largest and most critical southern port on the Strait of Hormuz, handling the majority of its oil exports, imports, and naval operations.

Qeshm Island, nearby, hosts military bases, missile sites, and key infrastructure guarding the entrance to the Gulf.
