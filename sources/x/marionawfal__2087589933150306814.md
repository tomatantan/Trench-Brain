🇸🇦🇾🇪 Saudi’s entire fleet of VLCCs are ditching the Red Sea and sailing THOUSANDS of extra miles around the Cape of Good Hope to stay clear of the Houthis.

Almost every Saudi tanker still in the area has switched off its AIS trackers and is running completely silent. 

At the same time, more than a dozen empty ones are steaming toward the Sea of Oman and UAE ports, lining up for their next move.

This reroute isn’t cheap... 

The detour adds 25-30 days to each trip, burns way more fuel, jacks up insurance, and is further tightening the global tanker market. 

Freight rates have roughly DOUBLED on some routes, forcing Asian refiners to absorb steep logistical premiums ($5 or more extra per barrel).
