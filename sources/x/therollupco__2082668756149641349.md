Sam Kazemian (@samkazemian), founder and CEO of @FraxFinance, walks through the direct-with-Visa card thesis behind the novel Frax card launching in Q4.

"There are 200 cards because there are a lot of ways to issue them. But we're getting a direct BIN relationship with Visa, not just an API-endpoint partner."

"We're getting a direct BIN relationship with Visa." 

"That unlocks full 100% interchange pass-through and novel hybrid credit-and-crypto design that other cards can't ship."

"Other BIN sponsors won't issue credit against crypto architecture, they're fine with debit against safe stablecoins like USDC or frxUSD." 

"We had to go direct, take responsibility. Couldn't build what we're building otherwise."
