🇺🇸🇮🇷 Months of fighting with Iran have reportedly drained U.S. stockpiles of Patriot and THAAD interceptors, along with long-range precision weapons, to the point where officials no longer believe America could comfortably sustain a much larger war.

A U.S. official said depleted missile inventories and battle damage are now becoming a real constraint on military planning.

At the same time, Washington is rushing more F-16s, F-35s and aerial refueling tankers into the region while quietly pursuing diplomacy, a sign that the Pentagon wants to avoid opening another major front before its arsenals recover.

This is exactly the kind of problem military planners have warned about for years: modern wars burn through precision weapons far faster than defense industries can replace them.
