🇬🇷🇪🇺 The entire EU wants to punish Russia and one Greek billionaire won't let them.

Greece is single-handedly freezing the new sanctions package, demanding a carve-out from the 2027 Russian LNG ban so its tankers can keep shipping Moscow's gas straight to China.

Why is Athens burning political capital over this? Follow the ships.

It all traces back to Dynagas, owned by billionaire George Prokopiou, whose fleet of Arctic icebreakers is locked into Russia's Yamal gas project. The day that ban hits, those specialized ships turn into floating scrap metal.

So Athens is quietly going to war for one man's fleet, and the rest of the bloc is fuming. One EU diplomat summed up the mood in a single word: "shameless."

And here's the part that stings the most. While Brussels sits stuck in this fight, the deadlock has also stalled the Russian oil price cap review, handing Putin the exact windfall these sanctions were built to choke off.
