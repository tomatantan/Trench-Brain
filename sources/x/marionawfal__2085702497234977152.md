🇺🇸 A New Mexico court just ordered Meta to pay $567 MILLION towards a teen mental health fund, after ruling Facebook and Instagram are harmful to young people.

The judge found Meta’s platforms SIGNIFICANTLY contributed to the state’s youth mental health crisis. 

On top of an earlier $375 million jury verdict, the total now approaches $942 million.

Meta must also implement stronger protections for the next 5 years, including usage limits, tighter controls on adult contact with minors, private-by-default accounts for under-18s, and safeguards for AI chatbots.

Meta says it will appeal the decision...
