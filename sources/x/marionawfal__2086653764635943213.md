🇺🇸🇺🇦 The companies that make the Patriot might be the ones blocking it from Ukraine.

The Atlantic got a Republican official on record, and the story he tells is pretty damning. The ones holding Ukraine back from making its own Patriots aren't the Pentagon or the White House. It's Raytheon and Lockheed, the companies that build them.

Their public excuse will be the usual: IP theft, protecting the tech. But the actual fear, he says, is that Ukraine builds a better Patriot for a fraction of the price and shows everyone just how overpriced the American version really is.

And that's the nightmare for them. Ukraine's been doing exactly this with drones for two years now. Cheap, fast, deadly.

Worth noting it's one anonymous source, so it's a claim, not proof.

The people who make the shield are the ones hiding it from Ukraine. Let that sink in.
