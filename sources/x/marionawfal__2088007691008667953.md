🇺🇸 Bill Gates’ daughter is accused of taking credit for sales she didn’t generate.

Phoebe Gates’ AI shopping startup Phia makes money through affiliate commissions. If it sends someone to a store and they buy something, Phia gets a cut.

But its browser extension allegedly dropped tracking cookies even when shoppers hadn’t clicked an affiliate link, potentially letting Phia collect commissions for sales it didn’t drive.

Internal Slack messages and source code reportedly suggest the founders knew about the feature. Phia disputes parts of the allegations and has not been charged with a crime.

Phia’s average daily revenue reportedly dropped from around $80,000 to as low as $10,000 after the disputed features were turned off. Phia says it also disabled most of its monetization.
