🇺🇸 Why did a Florida city just name its airport after the incumbent president?

Most leaders get buildings named after them only after they’re gone, but State Rep. Meg Weinberger decided not to wait. 

She sponsored the bill renaming Palm Beach International Airport to “President Donald J. Trump International Airport.” 

Her reasoning: Trump is the first U.S. president to call Florida home. Palm Beach isn’t just a campaign stop; it’s where he built his life, family, and businesses. 

Critics chided the move as a "cash grab," but the legislation prevents that: Trump Org waived all trademark rights. Zero royalties. Zero dollars to the family. Any merchandise money stays with the county.

You can disagree with Trump’s policies, but his impact is real, and this deal protects taxpayers while giving the airport a globally recognized identity.

It's a bit unusual, but of all the things to be mad at Trump about right now, this is pretty far down the list.
