"The credit rating agencies hold the value of Bitcoin at zero, which makes no sense. That's why the preferreds have a junk rating"

Parker White on one of the reason Strategy sold Bitcoin and the holy grail they're chasing

"When S&P, Moody's, and the others evaluate the creditworthiness of the company, they hold the value of the Bitcoin at zero. That's why the preferreds have a B minus rating, because it's only the software business being used to service the liabilities in their eyes"

"The point Strategy has been trying to make to the rating agencies is, look, we can use this Bitcoin, it's as good as cash, we can use it to fund the dividends. Now they just proved that. They can sell some Bitcoin to fund at least a month's worth of dividends and the quarters for the other preferreds. Bitcoin is an actual asset, not just this accounting thing sitting on the balance sheet that's never gonna be touched"

"The holy grail is if Strategy can get their rating into that investment grade category because Bitcoin is an actual asset they could use. That opens up a whole bunch of additional fund mandates that are required to only invest in investment grade securities"
