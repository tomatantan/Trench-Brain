Another day another DeFi hack on a margin system.

Here's the simple solution to minimize the damage of hacks by 10x.

All margin systems are constructed the same way. 

- You deposit your funds into the margin system. 
- You no longer have custody of the assets.
- The margin system does. 
- To get your assets out, you have to withdraw from the margin system. 

Now, use that to your advantage.

Put all custody of funds into a smart contract omnibus. That program moves funds as instructed by another arbitrary program (or key) of your choosing. This program is the isolated kernel of custody risk. 

Make the contract formally verified and immutable (it's a simple program to write so immutability is definitely the way to go).

Impose a 1 day timelock on all withdrawals flowing through that smart contract.

Allow the smart contract to halt withdrawals with a security council. (Optional: allow the program to cancel withdrawals though this will be contentious; hence it being optional.)

- Main issue with this is that it doesn't work for native BTC, but the solution to that is to use cbBTC (or an alternative of your choice).

If the exchange gets hacked, then just pause withdrawals and give yourself time to react. All classes of hacks would be covered here to minimize damage. Oracle attacks. Wallet compromise. Margin system manipulation. Etc.
