During a recent ER visit for her son, a hospital employee approached petition starter Anna Harris with an iPad requesting payment while her son's imaging was still underway. Federal law bars hospitals from delaying emergency treatment over payment questions, but guidance on requests made once treatment has started is less clear.

Now, Harris, a registered nurse with a master's degree in healthcare administration, is asking CMS Administrator Dr. Oz to close that gap. Her petition calls for an explicit ban on mid-treatment payment prompts, proactive hospital audits, and real EMTALA penalties for violations.
