The best way to learn how to trade memecoins is to go out there and fail, unironically 

It’s a skill you develop over time. The more time you spend in the trenches the more you’ll learn what narratives work and which don’t. You’ll develop a feeling on where to buy or sell and how much.

For me and many others, the progress was and still is, exponential. For years my portfolio ranged around 5k until something suddenly clicks. A random multi 5 fig win outta nowhere. I still remember the first time lol, was euphoric asf. 

Next, you’ll become numb to the numbers. The multi 5 fig win turned into a high 4 one. It hurt a lot. And it happened multiple times again after that one. Rountrip after rountrip. Numb and number. 

Number means the emotional side gets killed. Less emotional = more objective descision making = more wins. 

The exponential improvement in trading itself beside the psychological aspect runs parallel to that.

Don’t expect results overnight, stay consistent with a hunger for improvement and you’ll eventually win exponentially. I’m 100% sure of that.
