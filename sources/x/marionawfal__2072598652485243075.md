🇺🇸 OpenAI reportedly floated handing the U.S. government a 5% stake in itself.

Sam Altman's $852 billion company has apparently been in early talks with the Trump administration, pitching public ownership as the way to share AI's upside with everyone. 

The idea would have other American AI labs cough up a similar slice.

All of this lands as Washington sours on AI, with both OpenAI and Anthropic reportedly having had model releases held up by government scrutiny and some Republicans itching for tighter rules. 

Handing the government equity buys goodwill. 

Trump publicly went after Intel's chief, then got behind the company the moment Washington took a 10% stake.

A tech giant offering the government a cut of itself to smooth over the politics. Doesn’t really sound like a free market, huh?
