Robbie explains why “buying the top” depends on your time horizon.

“So you just buy tops all day? No, that’s not the point.”

“I don’t use TA to time my entries.”

“These things are overvalued or undervalued relative to one another.”

“Time horizon is the other factor here.”

“To you, I might have bought a top when I bought Zcash last week.”

“But if I expect Zcash to eat more market share from Bitcoin and I have a flexible time horizon, I’m just gonna buy it, stay patient, and wait for it to play out.”

“That’s about as deep as I get into technical analysis these days.”
