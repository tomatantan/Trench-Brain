🚨🇫🇷  A man armed with three knives attacked police on a busy street in central Toulouse

-2 municipal police officers were wounded with a bladed weapon late Monday afternoon on rue d'Austerlitz in the Wilson district, in the heart of Toulouse

-Mayor Jean Luc Moudenc confirmed the attack, writing that the officers were assaulted while on the ground keeping the city safe, and adding that remarks of a terrorist nature were also reported

-The suspect allegedly threw himself at their patrol vehicle while gesturing wildly, and struck as one of the officers rolled down the passenger window, according to the city's deputy for security

-He was carrying THREE knives and was arrested at the scene after a struggle officers described as extremely difficult, then taken to the hospital

-The city's security deputy said the officers came within a hair of the worst, and reports circulating online claim one was nearly slashed across the throat

-Reports also claim the attacker shouted "Allahu Akbar"

-The Toulouse prosecutor's office has taken charge of the case
