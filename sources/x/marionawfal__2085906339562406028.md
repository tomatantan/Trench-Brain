🇺🇸 A Rhode Island startup is putting autonomous boats in the hands of the U.S. military with the goal of saving lives on the front lines.

Havoc AI’s crewless Rampage vessels, run by software and controlled from a tablet, have already been used by the Army in the Pacific for target hunting and ship protection, plus logistics runs at major exercises.

One operator can task a whole group while the boats figure out the details themselves, keeping sailors and soldiers out of some of the dull, dirty and dangerous work.

Cheap, expendable and easy to run.

Exactly the kind of systems the Pentagon wants more of.
