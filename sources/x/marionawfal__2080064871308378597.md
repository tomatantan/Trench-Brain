🚨🇺🇸 🇮🇷 U.S. intelligence is investigating whether RUSSIA helped Iran target CIA facilities in the Gulf

-Four people familiar tell Reuters that analysts are probing whether Moscow provided targeting information or advanced drone tech behind Iranian strikes on CIA sites, citing the attacks' precision and Russia's broader support for Iran

=At least two CIA facilities were struck in March: the station inside the U.S. embassy in Riyadh and a site in eastern Iraq, with sources saying additional sites were hit, "more than one and fewer than a dozen"

-One Western intelligence memo concluded Russia has likely played a role in targeting regional CIA facilities

-The Riyadh attack reportedly used two Russian-enhanced Shahed drones: one blew a hole in the embassy's exterior, the second flew through and detonated, with analysts pointing to the Kometa-M satellite navigation system Russia supplied to sharpen and harden Iran's drones against jamming

-The concern intensified after the Jordan barracks strike that killed the soldiers, raising the same question about Russian targeting help

The CIA-station strikes turn a regional war into a great-power probe.

Hitting facilities whose locations are among the most closely guarded secrets in U.S. intelligence isn't something Iran's arsenal does by luck, and the short list of states that could supply that targeting begins and ends with Moscow.

It's the same suspicion the WSJ raised about the maneuvering missiles at Jordan: Iran fights better than its own capabilities explain, and the tech gap keeps pointing north.
