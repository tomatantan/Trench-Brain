🇺🇸 Judge blocks Philadelphia from banning masks for federal agents

A federal judge has blocked Philadelphia from enforcing a new law that bans federal agents from wearing masks.

The law was also set to require agents to show visible ID, use marked vehicles, and face penalties if they didn't comply.

The Trump administration says cities can't tell federal agents how to do their jobs and that revealing agents' identities could put them at risk.

For now, the masks stay on.
