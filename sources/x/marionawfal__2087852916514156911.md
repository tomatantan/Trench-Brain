🇫🇷 France’s Interior Minister has ordered heightened security for the Christian feast of the Assumption on August 15.

Laurent Nuñez instructed prefects and security officials to maintain “extreme vigilance” around churches and religious gatherings, citing the persistent high terrorist threat and ongoing international tensions.

He noted the clear rise in anti-Christian incidents: 843 recorded in 2025 (up 9% from the previous year) and another 458 in the first half of 2026 alone.

Historically, Europe served as the cradle of Christian civilization, deeply shaping the continent's laws, art, architecture, and social structures for over 1,700 years.

Nowadays, Christians need special protection in their own land, on their own holy days...
