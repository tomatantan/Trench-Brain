Ok, in all seriousness.

If you want to tweet better, become more successful, become unstoppable in life:

Be an original.

You grew up hearing "if you have nothing nice to say, say nothing."

Instead, I offer "if you have nothing original to say, say nothing."

Never be expected, or generic. Start with no, be generally disagreeable. Be the strawman. Find the extreme ends of your opinions and express those views (watch how quickly this drives decisions).

Conformity & agreeableness are strategies that most people use to seek comfort because it feels good to get along. But first principles & original thinking will lead you to the promised land.

Be the loudest person in a room, or the quietest. The smartest, or the most stupid (that's me). Reject the middle or you will always be mid.

Sharing is not caring. Literally, and I mean this seriously, you should live the opposite of every maxim & common rule. Those are creative deathtraps for your mind that appear to be of the natural world order, when in fact they were created by people no more capable than you and are entirely made up.

Lastly, do not use AI to write. AI is incredible at thinking, but it does not produce outlier content (yet). It's designed to do the average, and very few people have the taste to get the best out of it.
