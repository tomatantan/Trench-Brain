.@nibblefun found a clever way to give dead tokens a second life...

Here's the mechanism explained:

- Dead liquidity enters different cohorts

When a cohort's timer ends, Nibble uses DVRF (a verifiable random function) to select an active token, then merges the dead liquidity into it.

There's careful engineering underneath that: a cap on how much liquidity can merge into any single active token, because dumping too much in at once could spook existing holders into selling. 

Once the merge happens, holders of that dead token receive a pro-rata share of the now-active token they got matched with.
