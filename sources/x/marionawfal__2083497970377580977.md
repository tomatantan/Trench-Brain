🇪🇸🇪🇺 Spanish PM Pedro Sánchez wants Europe to solve... Pedro Sánchez's problem

After Spain was rocked by the mass illegal migrant influx into Ceuta, he has asked for an emergency meeting with the European Commission over growing calls for Spain to face Schengen restrictions.

The backlash intensified after Italy reinstated border controls on travelers from Spain, arguing it could no longer ignore the security implications.

Instead of asking how Spain ended up in this mess, Sánchez seems more interested in asking why the rest of Europe isn't pretending everything is fine.

Flood your borders, lose your neighbors' trust, then call an emergency meeting because your neighbors noticed. That's one way to redefine crisis management.
