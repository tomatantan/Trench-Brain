"Privy made DeFi a lot better, how did it do it? Made it more centralized"

Kain on the trade off that made onboarding into DeFi so much easier

"Privy made DeFi a lot better. How did it do it? Made it more centralized, not in the custody model, but in the model of there's a giant database of all of your information, and you can spin up these wallets easily against an email or Google login or whatever, which was very nice"

"That was arguably part of why Friend Tech and some of these weird little Ponzis were able to get so much traction at the time"

"Because it was super easy to spin up a wallet, throw some ETH in there, and start doing the game"
