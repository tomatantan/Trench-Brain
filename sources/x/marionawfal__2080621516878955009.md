🇺🇸 The Trump administration has imposed new 10–12.5% tariffs on goods from 60 trade partners, citing failures to stop forced labor in supply chains.

The duties cover nearly all U.S. imports and replace the temporary global tariff that expired today.

The White House frames it as both a human rights measure and a way to keep a tariff floor after the Supreme Court struck down earlier reciprocal duties.

Trading partners from the EU to China and Australia strongly dispute the justification, calling the move unjustified. Many note the rates still respect existing trade deal ceilings, limiting immediate damage.

A second investigation into “excess capacity” is expected to bring more tariffs soon.
