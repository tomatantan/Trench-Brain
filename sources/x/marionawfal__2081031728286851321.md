🇮🇷🇺🇸 Iran isn't able to win this war, but it can try to make the war too expensive to keep fighting 

Gas at $4, dead U.S. troops, a strait nobody can fully reopen. That's a strategy.

A deal Tehran can sell at home. A price Washington decides is too high. Or an escalation that makes both irrelevant.
