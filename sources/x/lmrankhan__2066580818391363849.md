To be a successful hip3 deployer, you first need distribution.

The challenge is that you can’t get distribution by launching another copy pasta trading app. There’s no real differentiation, and no compelling reason for users to choose your product over the next 10 competitors.

The only path is to build something different. 

Once you have user distribution, you can leverage that audience and eventually become a hip3 or even hip4 deployer. But until then, the focus should be to build a product that gets people to come back. 

Tradexyz earned a lot of its distribution because they were early and pioneered many of the products that exist on Hyperliquid. It makes sense that they captured share of users 

Unfort there is no shortcut. The hard part is to build something different, win users, and earn distribution
