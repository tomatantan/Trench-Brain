One of the trickiest problems with building on Uniswap V4 hooks: screeners can't see your liquidity. 

@Furyoku0x1 explained how @SazareProtocol solved it
 
The issue: when liquidity is held inside the hook, indexers like DEXScreener only read the pool, so they show a fraction of what's actually there. You can't fix that for an existing token, going to indexers and asking them to change their infrastructure for one small protocol isn't happening.

So he rebuilt the factory around it. New launches on Sazare show their liquidity correctly on bots and screeners from day one (except exactly at the floor), and all liquidity is permanently locked in a contract that automatically manages the concentrated positions.
