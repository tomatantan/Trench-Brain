🇺🇸 The cyclospora outbreak tied to recalled iceberg lettuce just jumped to 15 states, with Arkansas, Iowa, Missouri, Nebraska, New Hampshire and North Carolina added this week.

6,358 illnesses in this outbreak alone. Two dead in Michigan. 

Nationally, over 10,000 lab-confirmed cases and 517 hospitalizations, with another 12,000+ cases awaiting confirmation.

Taylor Farms recalled all iceberg lettuce sourced from central Mexico.

Taco Bell dropped the supplier entirely.

The FDA expects the numbers to climb. It takes up to six weeks to link a sick person to the outbreak.

Go check your fridge.
