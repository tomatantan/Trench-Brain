🇦🇷 Jorge Messi, father and lifelong agent of Lionel Messi, has died at 68.

He passed away yesterday at a hospital in Rosario, Argentina, after a long illness. His old club Newell's Old Boys confirmed it, calling him the man who, alongside his wife Celia, built the career of the greatest player of all time.

Jorge was the one who took a 13-year-old Leo to Barcelona for the trial that led to the famous napkin contract, back when Argentine clubs wouldn't pay for his growth treatment.

That was the emotion behind the tears. Messi broke down after scoring against Algeria at the World Cup in June and said it had nothing to do with football. His father's health was the reason.

He leaves behind his wife Celia and four children: Lionel, Rodrigo, Matías and María Sol.
