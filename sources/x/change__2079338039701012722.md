"Haunting Adeline," a bestselling dark romance novel that follows a woman being stalked by a dangerous, mysterious man, is officially being adapted into a movie, author H.D. Carlton confirmed this month. The book carries extensive reader warnings for graphic depictions of sexual assault and stalking.

Now, the news has divided readers, some excited to see the story on screen, others calling for the project to be scrapped. More than 7,300 people have signed a petition that argues that the book "takes the things women are taught to fear most — a stranger in their home, being drugged, being restrained, being unable to say no — and frames them as desirable."
