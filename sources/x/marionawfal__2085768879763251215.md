🇸🇦🇵🇰🇹🇷 Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, and Turkey just signed the Makkah Joint Defense Agreement in Mecca 

MBS, Erdogan, and Shehbaz Sharif, together. Any armed attack on one signatory is treated as an attack on all.

This builds on the Pakistan-Saudi mutual defense pact from last September, itself a reaction to Israel's strike on Hamas negotiators in Doha.

Each country brings something different to the table. Pakistan brings a nuclear arsenal and one of the world's largest militaries. Turkey brings NATO membership and a fast-growing drone and defense industry. Saudi Arabia brings oil money and regional weight.

This lands of course mid-war between the U.S., Israel, and Iran, with Gulf states already nervous about depleted American interceptor stockpiles.

Turkish officials say it's defensive, not aimed at anyone specific, and open to other countries joining. Israel hasn't commented.
