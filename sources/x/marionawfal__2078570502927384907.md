🇧🇪🇵🇸🇮🇱 Belgium has greenlit a ban on goods from occupied Palestinian territories, one of the firmest European responses yet to Israel’s actions in Gaza and repeated ceasefire breaches.

The move was finalized in the government’s last session before summer recess, following last year’s agreement over the rising civilian toll.

Gaza authorities say Israeli violations since the October 2025 ceasefire have killed more than 1,100 Palestinians and wounded over 3,600, on top of the war’s massive destruction.

The EU struggles with full unanimity on bloc-wide sanctions against Israel, so individual countries are increasingly taking their own steps.
