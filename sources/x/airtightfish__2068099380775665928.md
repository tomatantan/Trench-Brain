Saylor selling means BTC would go up?

Seen a lot of commentary around the STRC depeg and its potential impact on BTC. The uncertainty and speculation around this are likely a massive overhang on price.

What's interesting is no one has current visibility on holdings as they're only released periodically.

Saylor could have already sold a significant amount of BTC; the market just won't know until it's announced.
Say he did sell a large amount, the removal of that overhang would likely be good for BTC.

There's a lot of nuance to this. This is not something that's easy to price, and there's a lot of dissent on what will happen and what it means. (Seems like the perfect topic for conditional markets to not only provide clarity but offer people exposure)

A note here: the market definition is tricky and significant.  Like if he only sold a bit / some tells a very different story than 1B+. Also does he even have to repeg it? Are there other actions he could take?
