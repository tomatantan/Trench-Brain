"Our view at Veda is that DeFi protocols and markets like Aave and Morpho are not securities"

Veda CEO Sun Raghupathi on which onchain vaults actually implicate securities laws

"You have some vaults that don't take exposure to securities. Our view at VEDA is that DeFi protocols and markets like Aave and Morpho are not securities, that's a view we hold pretty strongly. So vaults that do onchain lending, there are questions, but this is a product that's not actually taking exposure to underlying securities"

"On the other hand, as more securities move onchain, we're increasingly going to see vaults that take direct exposure to securities"

"And those clearly do implicate securities laws in various ways"
