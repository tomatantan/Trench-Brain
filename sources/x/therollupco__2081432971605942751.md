Ryan Rasmussen on how institutions actually allocate to crypto:

"Institutional investors are long-term and systematic. When they make a crypto allocation, they're betting on crypto as a theme."

"They don't have time for the nuance of FDV versus circulating supply and unlocks. It's just not something they think about."

"Their logic is simple: am I hearing every asset gets tokenized? Is Bloomberg quoting things happening on Hyperliquid? Then I should have some exposure in case it's true, and size it so it doesn't hurt if it's not."

"For most, crypto is 2 to 5% of the portfolio, split across many assets. They just want broad exposure to the mega-trends."
