🇮🇷 Five countries have faced maximum pressure and not one of them surrendered over money

Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria and Venezuela all got the treatment in Trump's first term. 

Venezuela only broke when American special forces captured Maduro in January, meaning the economics never closed a single case on their own.

Iran has spent longer than any of them learning the workarounds. Its exports rebounded after 2021 and by 2024 exceeded what Tehran moved under the nuclear deal, running near 1.3 MILLION barrels a day with roughly 90% going to China and almost a third of sales handled through barter that sanctions struggle to reach.

Saddam absorbed six weeks of bombing, a ground invasion and an uprising across 15 of Iraq's 18 provinces, then called survival a dignified victory while Bush lost an election to a recession at home.
