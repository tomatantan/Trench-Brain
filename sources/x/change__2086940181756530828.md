A petition calling on the Edinburgh Festival Fringe to cancel Amanda Knox’s show “Cartwheel” has surpassed 40,000 signatures. Started by Meredith Kercher family friend Sally Alington, the petition argues that staging the one-woman comedy show is disrespectful to Kercher’s memory and family.

Kercher’s sister, Stephanie, told the BBC that she believes the show “normalizes and trivializes violence against women.”

Knox has responded to Alington's petition, saying it mischaracterizes the show and that “Cartwheel” actually “honors Meredith Kercher’s memory and calls out the violence done to her and to me.” The show, she says, is "about motherhood, raising a daughter in a world that is unfair and dangerous for women, and what it costs to be the kind of mother who tells her the truth about that."
