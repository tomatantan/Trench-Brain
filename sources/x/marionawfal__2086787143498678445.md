🇨🇳🇮🇳 China is SLAMMING India over a border dispute... 

Beijing just blasted New Delhi for officially naming 27 sites in the disputed Arunachal Pradesh region, which China calls “Zangnan” and claims as its own.

India’s move (passes, a lake, a war memorial) is “illegal and invalid,” per China’s Foreign Ministry. “It cannot change the fact that Zangnan BELONGS to China.”

The spat comes as both sides attempt to stabilize ties after years of border tensions. India has long rejected China’s own renaming campaigns as “mischievous.” 

The map wars in the Himalayas continue.
