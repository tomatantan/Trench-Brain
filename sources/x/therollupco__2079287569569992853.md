Jacobc.eth explains how you let an AI agent trade for you without praying it doesn't drain your wallet:

"Most agents have their own wallet. You send your funds there and blindly give control, and pray it doesn't do something stupid with your money. That's not sustainable."

"Our model is different. For basic transactions you connect your existing wallet, Ledger through MetaMask, and still approve each one, like a dApp."

"For automation, you grant narrow onchain permissions, these tokens, up to this amount, for this period. Even if the agent goes completely rogue, it can't act outside the guardrails you set."
