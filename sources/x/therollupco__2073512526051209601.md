Joseph Chalom on the step function ETH thesis from an institutional perspective:

"The Ethereum Foundation is reducing gas fees and doing 3x throughput increases, mainnet being built for the volume coming from tokenization, stablecoins, and agentic micropayments."

"Incremental growth won't trigger the burn. But step function increases, 10x, 20x, 50x+ transactions, will create the demand that triggers it."
"When that happens, you need ETH as the trust layer to secure those transactions. That is the thesis."
