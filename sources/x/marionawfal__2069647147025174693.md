A viral story is sparking debate around delivery app security in apartment buildings

An influencer claims a friend in Paris was attacked after ordering Uber Eats, raising concerns about how easily entry codes and building access details can be shared through delivery instructions.

Reports suggest the attackers were minors allegedly recruited via messaging apps, where access info like codes and entry details were being circulated.

Security experts say the real issue may be that apps treat these instructions as harmless notes not sensitive access data.

Now there are growing calls for safer systems like temporary codes or auto-expiring access… before “leave at door” becomes something more serious.
