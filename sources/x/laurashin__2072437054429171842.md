"Governance gets dominated by people who have reasons to want to allocate the treasury the way they want. As time goes on, the votes concentrate in the hands of the people who can make money off owning them"

Nick Johnson on the fundamental problem with DAOs and why ENS is facing it

"Delegation rates have only been going downwards. Despite me having fewer tokens than when ENS launched, it's still a proportionally larger impact on voting. That's symptomatic of the problems we're having with the ENS DAO and really everyone's having with DAOs in general. Participation gets harder"

"At the same time, we have this massive honeypot of a treasury calling out for people to find ways to allocate it and reasons to justify it. Increasingly, governance gets dominated by people who have reasons to want to allocate the treasury the way they want it"

"For the most part these people aren't malicious. They have pet projects they think will be useful, valuable, or fun to build, and they want to see them funded. It's endemic to any money buys influence voting. As time goes on, the votes concentrate in the hands of the people who can make money off owning them"
