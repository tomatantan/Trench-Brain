NYT's satellite assessment found a significant number of Black Hawks damaged at the same base. WSJ counted fighters, transports and helicopters.

Washington put $143 million into expanding it starting in 2019, and by this year it was holding F-15s, F-22s, F-35s and Reapers. 

The densest concentration of advanced U.S. airpower in the Levant.
