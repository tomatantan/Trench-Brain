🇨🇳 China Claims Right to Target People OVERSEAS Under New "Ethnic Unity" Law 

Beijing just doubled down: A senior official says the new law lets them go after anyone abroad who "undermines ethnic unity" or incites separatism. That includes in Taiwan, among Uyghurs, Tibetans & diaspora critics. 

It becomes effective July 1. 

Vice Justice Minister Hu Weilie called it "legal, necessary & consistent with international practice" while slamming Western media for "distorting" it. 

Rights groups warn it's another tool for transnational repression (think Interpol red notices). Taiwan is on high alert. China says it won't touch "normal exchanges." But many aren't buying it. 

Looks like China is taking a move right out of Israel’s playbook.
