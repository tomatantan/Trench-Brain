🇾🇪🇸🇦 The Houthis want everyone to know the Red Sea is still open... just not for Saudi Arabia.

Chief negotiator Mohammed Abdul Salam said reports of Bab al-Mandeb being closed are false, and that the blockade only targets Saudi shipping, in response to Riyadh's siege on Yemen.

Bab al-Mandeb is one of the tightest chokepoints on earth, and a serious chunk of global oil and container traffic squeezes through it every day.

Even the rumour of a closure lifts insurance costs and pushes carriers onto the long route around Africa, adding weeks and millions.

The group has been hitting ships there since late 2023 over Gaza. Now the stated target list is down to one country.
