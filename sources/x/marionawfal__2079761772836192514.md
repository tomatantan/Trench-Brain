🚨🇺🇸 BREAKING: The House passed a bill funding the government until AFTER the midterms, as the Pentagon runs urgently low on cash from the Iran war.

-The stopgap passed 220-205, funding the government through December 4 and pushing any shutdown fight past the November 3 elections

-House Democratic leaders opposed it, saying it hands the Trump administration "even more power over the federal budget" and funds the "mass deportation machine"

-The bill is a nonstarter in the Senate, where seven Democrats are needed and appropriators Murray and Collins are drafting their own version, with Thune promising a bipartisan floor deal before August

-The next move is the real war bill: a $95 billion reconciliation framework with $73 BILLION for a Pentagon the Post describes as "urgently low on cash largely due to the Iran war," plus $10 billion for election measures and $12 billion for farmers

-Trump originally demanded $350 billion for defense plus the SAVE America Act, and fiscal hawks are balking at zero offsets against the $39 trillion debt

Look at what this bill actually admits. 

The Pentagon, eleven nights into bombing Iran, is running out of money, and the plan to fix it can't survive a normal vote, so it's riding reconciliation where Democrats can't touch it. 

Meanwhile the party writing the checks can't even agree with itself: Burchett says leadership hasn't made a compelling pitch, Senate Republicans doubt the thing passes at all, and the debt clock reads $39 trillion while nobody offsets a dime. 

And the one point of total Republican unity? 

Making sure the government doesn't shut down until the votes are counted. 

That tells you everything about which war they're actually worried about losing.
