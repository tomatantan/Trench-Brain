Read the recent Delphi report on token markets and agree with the broader theme. We’re already seeing two dominant forms of tokenization emerge (a) tokens that represent equity ownership and (b) tokens that represent direct value accrual through buybacks.

Everything in between will eventually be pulled toward one extreme or the other. Don’t expect your token to reach fair value until revenue and buybacks are brought onchain in a programmatic way.

On the other side as more companies IPO, tokenization creates a path to bring a global pool of buyers onchain and expand access to ownership far beyond trad capital markets.
