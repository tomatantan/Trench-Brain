🚨🇸🇦 BREAKING: Sirens are sounding in Saudi Arabia, confirmed at a U.S. air base and reportedly at the kingdom's biggest oil pipeline terminal.

-Sirens are confirmed at Prince Sultan Air Base in Al-Kharj, home to American forces and Patriot batteries

-Unconfirmed reports place sirens at Yanbu on the Red Sea coast, terminus of Saudi Arabia's largest oil pipeline

-Saudi Arabia has largely stayed off Iran's siren map all week, while Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, and Jordan absorbed the barrages

-Iran warned it could target "a network of key ports" in Gulf states

Yanbu is the name that raises the stakes. 

The Red Sea terminal is the heart of the East-West pipeline, the exact bypass route the Gulf is counting on to escape Hormuz dependence, and Iran threatening it would mean going after the workaround, not just the strait. 

The base alert is real.

The pipeline alert, if confirmed, is the escalation.
