I believe the phase we’re entering will benefit onchain users more than any phase of crypto before it.

Ethereum created the first major onchain era with ICOs/defi. Solana pushed apps, memes and trading forward, basically productizing onchain trading and turning it into a real consumer behavior.

Base brought Coinbase distribution onchain, along with the defi mullet via Morpho and the AI x crypto meta via Venice.

And now Robinhood is entering onchain via tokenizing stocks. We are entering the most competitive onchain market ever. 

Every one of these ecosystems understands that liquidity is everything. Meme, defi, RWAs, AI, payments and trading all matter. And they are going to compete aggressively for capital, users, developers, apps and distribution.

I expect every player to pull out all the stops to win. And when platforms compete this aggressively, the end user usually benefits the most.

This may be the best onchain has ever been. Let the onchain wars begin.
