Offchain CEO Steven Goldfeder on how they stemmed the bleeding on bridge risk across Arbitrum

"The first thing we did was harden the native bridge and invest a lot there, but also educate folks on how to use it correctly. This is probably counterintuitive, but initially many of the large exchanges were doing immediate sequencer-only confirmed withdrawals, and we did a campaign that you should wait until it lands, gets L1 finality, or just have some tolerance, like okay I'll keep a million dollars at bay, but if a really large withdrawal comes in, past whatever your threshold is, have that explicit. So both hardening and education"

"There was definitely a lot of consolidation around a few of the larger bridges. They're all over the place, but it's the same underlying tech being deployed. We take less of an active role in actually auditing the third party bridges on the platform"

"When teams say hey, can we get your endorsement, we don't audit their code, but we try to check, are these legitimate people, do our due diligence to make sure we're not promoting scams. It's not foolproof or 100%, but we do second level diligence, do these people look like they're taking the right steps. It's an open platform, we don't control that, but we can try to influence things using our influence and association"
