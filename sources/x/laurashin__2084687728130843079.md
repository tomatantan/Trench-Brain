"Self custody doesn't completely eliminate trust, you're trusting the manufacturer of the device, the engineers, the auditors"

Tuongvy Le on why self custody doesn't remove trust, it just moves it

"For me it was a reminder that while self custody is still incredibly important and a right that should be protected, it doesn't completely eliminate trust, which is sort of how I always viewed it"

"Sure, you're no longer trusting a centralized custodian, but you're trusting the manufacturer of the device. You're trusting the engineers who designed the hardware and the firmware that generated your keys. You're trusting the people who reviewed the code, the auditors"

"So I think it does raise some hard questions about whether self custody is for everyone, or even whether cold wallet makers have to go through some sort of certification process. I don't know"
