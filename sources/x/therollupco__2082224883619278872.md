Salman Banaei on the structural conflict in crypto exchanges that TradFi outlawed decades ago, and that CLARITY would force apart:

"A lot of crypto exchanges are vertically integrated. The brokerage function is integrated with the exchange function, which is integrated with the custody and clearinghouse function."

"Traditionally in US markets, we've managed those conflicts of interest by making it hard for one layer of the supply chain to be vertically integrated with another."

"Some Democrats are pushing for stronger conflicts language. That's a policy gap I think is narrowing, and should narrow."
