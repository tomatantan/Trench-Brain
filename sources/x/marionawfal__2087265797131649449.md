Chris Martenson says that the global oil market has entered a “truly bizarre” phase where historical patterns have been completely broken

That's creating a massive disconnect between official trading and economic reality.

He says that while a combination of geopolitical shocks in the Red Sea, Libya, and Russia should have driven crude oil to $120 a barrel by now, the futures market remains suppressed even as retail prices for finished products tell a different story.

“I'm pretty sure the futures price is a fake price. The real price is what you have to pay to get it.”
