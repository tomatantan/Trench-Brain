After her father was severely injured in an ATV crash and died months later, a Kentucky woman is calling for stronger helmet laws for ATV and four-wheeler riders.

Experienced ATV rider William Adkins wasn't wearing a helmet when his four-wheeler's tie rod snapped on Christmas Eve. His daughter Brooklynn is now calling on Kentucky lawmakers to require helmets for all riders, regardless of age or whether they're on public or private property. Current state law requires helmets for riders under 16 and for riders over 16 who are on public property.

"A simple piece of safety gear could have rewritten the ending, preventing severe trauma and saving his life," her petition reads.
