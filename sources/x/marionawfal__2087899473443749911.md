🇮🇷 Could Iran start legally charging ships to use the Strait of Hormuz?

After the MoU, Iran made clear it wants to introduce maritime fees for ships once the 60-day window closes. 

Oil tankers and cargo ships would have to pay up.

International law bans straightforward tolls through territorial waters, but payments for “navigational safety services” sit in more of a gray area. 

Denmark already charges oil and gas tankers for pilotage through its straits. 

Turkey bills certain tankers around $130,000 for a round trip through the Turkish straits, framed as maintenance of lighthouses and buoys.

Iran and Oman could point to those exact precedents and call them "service fees" rather than tolls. 

Washington has said repeatedly that it will not allow it.
