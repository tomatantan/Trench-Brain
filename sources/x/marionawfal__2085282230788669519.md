🇮🇷🇴🇲🇺🇸 Iran's Ministry of Foreign Affairs just announced the details of an agreement made with Oman on the management of the Strait of Hormuz. 

The plan reportedly includes the following arrangements: 

1.⁠ ⁠Iran will close the Northern and Southern sea lanes. 

2.⁠ ⁠A temporary shipping lane will be established in the area for 2–4 Months, and most maritime traffic will pass through Iranian waters

3.⁠ ⁠During this period, negotiations must take place on Phase 2 of the Hormuz Agreement, which includes discussions on maritime Fees

4.⁠ ⁠The full reopening of the Strait of Hormuz is contingent on the U.S lifting the naval blockade and energy sanctions and unfreezing Iran’s frozen assets. 

TLDR: expect the deadlock between Iran and the U.S. to go through another cycle of delays and failed agreements like it's June again.
