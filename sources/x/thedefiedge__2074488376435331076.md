Robinhood's endgame is moving millions of users onchain without them ever knowing it.

I dug through what's actually live on Robinhood Chain, and everything on it points the same direction:

Stablecoins and tokenized stocks are dominating

Stablecoins are still crypto's clearest PMF. Around $302B+ in supply and doing trillions in monthly volume.

Tokenized stocks are the next obvious one because people already understand them. Stocks, ETFs, treasuries, yield, collateral. You don't need to explain why those exist.

So @RobinhoodCrypto is starting exactly where normal people already are.

Their Stock Tokens give eligible non-US users exposure to assets like AAPL, NVDA, and GOOG through ERC-20 tokens.

One detail worth knowing is these are tokenized debt securities that track the underlying stock, so you're not getting actual shares or voting rights.

The ecosystem already has 130+ apps and tools across trading, lending, wallets, bridges, analytics, ramps, and a bunch of other infra.

Still early, but their strategy play is pretty clear.

Robinhood has millions of tradfi users, and the chain is just the rail to move them onchain.

The real test is whether they can do it without those users ever feeling like they're "using crypto."

If they pull it off, most of them will never know or care what an L2 is.
