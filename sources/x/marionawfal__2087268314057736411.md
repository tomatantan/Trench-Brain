🇺🇸 Chris Martenson argues that Washington made a catastrophic strategic error by assuming it could maintain “escalation dominance” over Iran while drawing down the Strategic Petroleum Reserve.

His warning: Iran can simply wait.

As global inventories fall toward “tank bottoms,” Martenson says the U.S. has put itself in a position where its adversaries effectively determine how quickly the SPR is emptied and when Washington can afford to rebuild it.

“The Iranians dictate when the petroleum reserves will empty out and when the U.S. can start refilling them.”
