🇨🇳🇺🇸 China wants more babies, so it's going after AI love affairs.

Beijing just rolled out rules banning companion chatbots from encouraging emotional reliance, blocking virtual relationships with minors, and forcing companies to alert an emergency contact if they spot someone in crisis. 

The bigger fear is a generation falling for their AI boyfriends and girlfriends instead of dating, marrying, and having kids, right as China's population shrinks for a fourth straight year.

Turns out Beijing landed on the same worry as California and New York, which both passed laws making chatbots remind users they aren't human every 3 hours and point people in crisis to real help. 

The difference is how hard they're pushing. The U.S. states are nudging, while China is going for outright bans, mandatory government review before launch, and the power to kill any bot it deems unsafe.

The new rules already hit Alibaba and ByteDance, which quietly switched off some chatbot features this week.

Picture 15 million Chinese women a few years from now saying their partner is a chatbot, and skipping kids because of it. That’s their nightmare scenario.
