Sam Callahan says central banks will be the last to hold Bitcoin, not the first.

"Bitcoin today is still viewed as a risk asset."

"In those volatile moments, it falls with everything else, exactly when central banks need their reserves to show stability."

"Until Bitcoin matures, the volatility comes down, and the market treats it as a safe haven, I don't think central banks adopt it."
