🇬🇧 An Olympic skier tried a front flip on holiday and collapsed a lung.

Chemmy Alcott, who raced for Britain at four straight Olympics, went for a front flip off a 5-meter platform in Annecy, France. She got the rotation wrong, hit the water hard, and knew right away something was off with her breathing.

So what did she do? Climbed back up and jumped again, just so she wouldn't look like she was making a big deal of it. Then came the hospital, where doctors found a collapsed lung and had to run a drain tube into her chest to reinflate it. 

That bought her several nights she hadn't planned on.

She called it a "very expensive lesson in rotation."

She spent 20 years flying down mountains at 80 mph without this happening. One holiday pool flip is what finally got her.
