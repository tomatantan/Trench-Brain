Trading terminals are great. Copy trading is a bit in a gray area.

In a market where nothing is held for more than 10 minutes, everyone is playing hot potato fr.

Buy the top? Skill issue.

Sell on your followers? Just taking profits.

Farm your own copy traders? Somehow that's acceptable too.

The problem isn't the technology. The problem is the lack of conviction.

Bring back belief. Bring back longer term thinking. Bring back the idea that not every trade is a race to dump on the next guy.

Then copy trading becomes powerful.

Until then it often feels like
you want my money, I want yours, and we're all pretending it's a strategy.

As the saying goes, when everyone's trying to get rich off each other, nobody's building anything worth believing in.
