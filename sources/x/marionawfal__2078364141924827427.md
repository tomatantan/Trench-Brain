🇺🇸🇸🇴 More Somali fraud? 

The top fugitive in the huge Feeding Our Future fraud case finally faced a judge today.

Abdirahman "Idlay," seen as the main partner to founder Aimee Bock, showed up in St. Paul court after more than 4 years hiding in Somalia.

He was extradited to Minnesota this week.

He’s facing 31 federal charges for allegedly helping steal millions of dollars meant for feeding kids during the pandemic.

The judge called him a major flight risk, so he’s staying behind bars until his next hearing later this month.
