Ethereum researchers proposed cutting validator rewards to zero if staking hits 50%.

Rob explains why he's against it, right now:

"If you think ETH is money and want the protocol as simple and value-accretive as possible, you'd argue the network's just as secure with fewer validators." 

"Lower emissions to validators, lower the protocol's security 'expense,' burn more, and accrue more value to token holders."

Rob's actual view: "The heart and soul of Ethereum is its DeFi ecosystem, and it's just now hitting its stride with tokenized equities." 

"Cutting validator rewards lowers the onchain risk-free rate, and you don't want to handicap DeFi right as it's heating up, Tom Lee cooking, DeFi cooking, the market waking up."

"I get wanting ETH more deflationary. But issuance is kind of fine right now. Maybe do this later, as ETH scales."
