"You hit the border of the state, that's where their authority ends"

Katherine on why New York can't shut Kalshi down beyond its own borders

"New York does not have power to shut Kalshi down, full stop, in other states, because of state jurisdiction. The New York AG and the New York gambling regulators have authority over New York customers. Basically you hit the border of the state, that's where their authority ends"

"Now arguably they could try to inflict collateral damage on Kalshi that could impact their ability to offer contracts beyond New York. But New York likes to be the most aggressive state, version of Team America World Police, and their jurisdiction is limited"

"It's funny because the same argument that they're rejecting on the basis of the CFTC limits their own state jurisdiction. So yeah, it is what it is"
