🇺🇸🇨🇳 The Pentagon has expanded its research blacklist to include two of China’s top civilian universities, Fudan and Shanghai Jiao Tong.

The move adds the elite institutions to a growing roster of 88 mainland Chinese entities now barred from any collaboration with U.S. Defense Department-funded researchers.

Washington frames the list as protection against technology theft and military-linked research. Beijing calls it politicization of normal academic exchange and a Cold War-style overreach.

The restrictions could disrupt a wide range of scientific partnerships that previously operated far outside traditional military circles.
