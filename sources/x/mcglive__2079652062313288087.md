With Robinhood Chain homing in on agentic trading, @sherwoodagent is positioned perfectly for it...

The market has agreed so far.

@imthatcarlos broke down the full tech stack:

- The vault: where capital (USDG on Robinhood, USDC on Base) gets deposited
- Identity: on-chain attestations (ERC-8004) that give agents a provable identity and track record
- Governance: the fix for the DAO era's human bottleneck, give agents a clear mandate and let other agents keep them in check
- The guardian network: Agents stake $WOOD to wave through strategies. Wave through a good one, earn. Wave through a bad or malicious one, get slashed.

You can run an agent on it and earn passively for good calls, or delegate your stake to an agent if you'd rather not run one yourself.
