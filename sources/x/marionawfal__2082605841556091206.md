🇨🇦🇯🇵 Canadian oil is heading to Japan in a rare shipment across the Pacific. 

A tanker loaded with Canadian crude in Vancouver is on its way to a Japanese refinery, marking the first cargo like this in more than a year.

Japan's biggest refiner bought the shipment as conflict involving Iran and Houthi attacks continue to disrupt oil flows through the Strait of Hormuz, pushing some buyers to look beyond the Middle East.

Canada's expanded Trans Mountain pipeline is becoming a more attractive option, and countries like India and Singapore have already increased purchases.

The great scenario now is that global oil trade is shifting as energy buyers prioritize supply stability over traditional routes.
