🇹🇷 🇸🇦 🇵🇰  Erdogan and Duran both presented the Mecca Joint Defense Agreement as a historic step between Turkey, Saudi Arabia and Pakistan.

They said it strengthens collective security and deterrence, an attack on one will be treated as an attack on all. It also expands defense industry cooperation, joint military projects and counter-terrorism efforts.

Both stressed the deal does not target any country and is open to other nations that want regional peace and stability.

Erdogan added that it follows Article 51 of the UN Charter (right of self-defense) and that Turkey will keep pushing for solutions through dialogue and international law.
