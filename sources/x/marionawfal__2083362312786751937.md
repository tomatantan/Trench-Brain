🚨🇪🇸 The crisis has jumped to Melilla, with crowds scaling the fence tonight and some breaking through.

-Large groups are climbing the border fence at Melilla, Spain's other North African enclave, trying to force their way into Spanish territory

-Spanish security forces fired tear gas to push them back, and some people made it across

-It follows the surge at Ceuta, where roughly 50,000 to 60,000 crossed by land and sea into a city of just 83,000, with 57 dead and around 48,000 already returned to Morocco
