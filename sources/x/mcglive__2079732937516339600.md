Why does @ripcarsio think the Hot Wheels market is about to grow? 

@allocateur pointed to the eBay data on

They track eBay sales daily: over 4,000 collector-grade Hot Wheels sell every day, around $170,000 in volume, and it keeps growing. Collectors he talks to say their collections have roughly 5x'd over the last five years.

His thesis: the secondary market is still only ~$100M, so even collectors who think it's "overhyped" may not see how much room is left, especially against Pokemon's much larger market.

The demand is measurable but the secondary infrastructure is what's missing.
