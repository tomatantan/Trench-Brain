Jeff Yan framed Hyperliquid as the AWS of finance. Rob and Andy on why that thesis is real:

"Consumer front-ends plug into Hyperliquid's 24/7 permissionless liquidity layer. MetaMask, Phantom, Valor already. Interactive Brokers, TradingView, Revolut potentially next. That's the AWS-of-finance thesis."

"US regulatory headwind is the structural cap, KYC-regulated platforms can't route through the liquidity layer. Kalshi couldn't plug in. What good is elegant global liquidity if US users can't reach it?"

"The moat has started to deteriorate. Lighter × Robinhood signaled that platforms won't hand pricing power over to a single incumbent. Liquidity begets liquidity, but the compounding effect is decelerating."
