🇮🇷🇺🇸 IRGC Spokesperson Brig. Gen. Hossein Mohebbi laid out Iran’s biggest claimed scorecard yet against U.S. forces.

Speaking to Tasnim, he said that over a 15-day stretch Iranian strikes destroyed or damaged:

- Dozens of radars and air-defence systems (including multiple Patriots)
- Hangars, fuel tanks, command centres and logistics sites
- 11 fighter jets and helicopters on the ground
- 17 drones
- An F-15 inside a shelter, a P-8, a C-17, and eight refuelling aircraft

It’s the most detailed list Tehran has put out so far, but also unconfirmed by U.S.
