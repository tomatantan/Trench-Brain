"The number one impediment to Ethereum winning is inertia at the largest institutions. It's not Solana, it's not Canton"

Joseph Chalom on the biggest thing standing between Ethereum and institutional adoption

"I'm gonna give you a controversial answer, but one I genuinely believe. The number one impediment to Ethereum winning is inertia at the largest institutions and the fear of changing their rails. It's not Solana, it's not Canton"

"Institutions have been running on rails that are predictable. They know how they perform, they know it takes T plus 1 in the US to settle a trade, T plus 2 and 3 in Asia. It's the predictability and the millions of proprietary databases and vendor lock-in that are the number one impediment to the progress we know is coming on stablecoins, tokenization, and DeFi"

"My mental model is stablecoins are the money layer, the first proof point of tokenization. Tokenization of real world assets is the asset and exposure layer. DeFi and other rails will be the transaction layer of the future. The biggest impediment isn't a particular L1 or L2, it's fundamentally people getting ready for change"
