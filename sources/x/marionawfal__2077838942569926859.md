🇺🇸🇮🇷 Trump's former Defense Secretary Mark Esper says airstrikes alone won't win the Iran war

The bombing campaign won't change Tehran's strategic objectives. Washington needs to strangle Iran economically instead.

"One option is full military onslaught. The other is you strangle them economically."

His 2 benchmarks for success: fully open Strait of Hormuz, and a nuclear deal stronger than Obama's.

His bigger concern: "What are we doing to our readiness and stockpiles? Because my big concern globally is China."

U.S. crude inventories are at their lowest since 1984. Brent is at $85. Analysts say $100+ if this drags on.

Is the Iran strategy costing America its ability to deal with the next crisis?
