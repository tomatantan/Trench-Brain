🇺🇸🇮🇱 Iran reportedly dominated the Netanyahu-Trump meeting, with Trump weighing three paths: a nuclear deal, a tougher economic blockade, or renewed military strikes.

A senior Israeli diplomatic source says Israel did not explicitly push Trump toward a strike and is focused on the outcome, with the final decision left to him. 

The source claims Iran is already under severe pressure, citing fuel and diesel shortages, roughly 90% inflation, and the beginning of public protests.

They also discussed increasing pressure on Tehran through both economic measures and military action.

The source says Israel has serious doubts that a nuclear deal can be reached. It currently does not believe uranium enrichment is happening at Pickaxe Mountain and claims to have solid intelligence on Iran’s nuclear material.

Israel also estimates Iran has only 1,500 to 1,600 missiles left after much of its production capability was destroyed. The source says eliminating 29 nuclear scientists was a deliberate Israeli priority because it removed a critical concentration of expertise from the program.

If Iran rebuilds its nuclear program, Israel says it will strike the “metastases” too.

One final mystery remains around Mojtaba Khamenei: Israel says it knows for certain he is alive, but no one has seen him since Operation Rising Lion.
