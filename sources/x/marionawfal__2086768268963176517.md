🇱🇧🇮🇱 Lebanon is threatening to WALK AWAY from its direct talks with Israel, unless certain demands are met.

The two sides have been holding U.S.-mediated talks aimed at implementing a framework agreement that was reached back in June.

Lebanon said NO new round will take place without Israeli acceptance of Beirut’s conditions, including a complete ceasefire and an IMMEDIATE halt to Israeli demolition in southern Lebanon.

The agreement, put simply, was supposed to trade a phased Israeli withdrawal in exchange for the disarming of Hezbollah (historically, no easy task).

But things may be stalling before they can get off the ground, with Israeli elections looming and fears of a renewed civil war in Lebanon if all this moves forward.

We'll just have to see what happens...
