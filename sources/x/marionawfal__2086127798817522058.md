🇺🇸🇷🇺 The Senate just pushed through a big new round of sanctions on Russia.

It's called the "Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026," and it now heads to the House for a vote in the coming weeks.

The bill targets Russian officials, and it hands Trump the power to slap tariffs of up to 100% on any country in the top five buyers of Russian energy, or anyone helping Moscow dodge sanctions.

That aims straight at China and India, the two biggest customers keeping Russian oil money flowing.

Still just the Senate for now. The House gets the final say.
