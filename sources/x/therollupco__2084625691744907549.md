Read it twice, this is @SECGov commissioner @HesterPeirce on the principles regulators can't cross:

Core Principle 1 — Self-custody:

→ Ability of people to self-custody is very important
→ Should not be compromised
→ Foundational blockchain-native property

Core Principle 2 — Financial privacy:

→ Ability of people to protect their privacy in financial transactions is very important
→ Should be protected
→ Historically applied to cash, progressively eroded elsewhere

Core Principle 3 — Permissionless code:

→ Ability of developers to develop code and put it out there
→ Without having to get permission
→ Counter to traditional financial-services regulatory model

The cypherpunk manifesto, entered into the record by a commissioner.
