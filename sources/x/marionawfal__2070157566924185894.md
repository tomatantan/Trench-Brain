🇺🇸 A Florida cardiologist is charged in an $89 million scheme that ran unnecessary heart screenings on college athletes, then signed off on the results without reading them.

Prosecutors say Jason Finkelstein preyed on athletes' fears of sudden cardiac death, billing insurers for tests they didn't need using diagnoses they didn't have.

In one 2024 case, the indictment says he certified 63 of a patient's test images as normal just 11 seconds after opening them. 

The scan actually showed a significantly enlarged heart, and the teenage patient later collapsed and died on a basketball court.

It's the marquee case in a DOJ sweep that charged 455 people over $6.5 billion in alleged healthcare fraud.

Dr. Oz, head of CMS, called it "a predatory scheme dressed up in medical clothing."
