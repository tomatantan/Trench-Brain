K9 Chilli, a 4-year-old bomb detection dog with the Pitt County Sheriff's Office in North Carolina, died June 11 after being left inside a patrol vehicle on a day that reached 99 degrees. The department's internal investigation ruled it an accident, but the sheriff's office didn't disclose the death publicly until six weeks later and has yet to share how long Chilli was inside the vehicle.

A petition is calling on Pitt County officials to open a criminal investigation and release the full record of what happened.
