The recording of my talk at the Bankless summit is out!

I talked about the phase change Ethereum and crypto broadly has undergone over the past ~2 years, from pre-adoption ("decade of exploration") to Real World Ethereum.

I really think this is an even more fundamental shift than most people acknowledge, and changes how we should operate on all levels, including on the L1 and in the application space.

Credit to @dcposch and @daimopay for having the foresight to clearly see this shift as it started to happen, and coining the Real World Ethereum term, which I think perfectly expresses the nature of this new era of Ethereum.

If you have the time, do listen to the talk, I am quite happy with how it turned out - feedback very welcome! 👇
