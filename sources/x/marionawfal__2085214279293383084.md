🇳🇴 🇺🇸 The U.S. has approved a $270 million sale of 155mm artillery shells to Norway.

-The package covers M795 high-explosive projectiles plus technical assistance, documentation, engineering, logistics and program support

-The M795 is the standard high-explosive round for American Army and Marine howitzers, built for more range and more lethality than the shell it replaced

-Norway shares a roughly 120-mile Arctic border with Russia and has been steadily buying artillery ammunition for its K9 howitzers

-State Department approval is not a completed deal, it clears the sale to move forward after Congress is notified

For some reason, America is selling artillery shells to allies while its own shelves are close to bare, with commanders calling stockpiles dangerously low and long-range missiles described as virtually gone.

Every 155mm round has been the most fought-over munition on earth since Ukraine, and now the same production lines that cannot refill the U.S. arsenal are taking orders from NATO.
