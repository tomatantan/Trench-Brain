🇺🇸 Families say the crew of a top U.S. warship is falling apart.

The USS Abraham Lincoln is now in its ninth month at sea backing U.S. operations against Iran. 

It went 208 straight days without a real port stop before a quick pull-in at Oman, a modern record for a Navy carrier.

Relatives describe endless shifts, moldy showers, broken facilities, and sailors who can't even get basics like soap and toothpaste.

Here's the scary part. Families say they fear this much exhaustion on a carrier packed with jets and thousands of people is how disasters happen.

The sting: families say many of them voted for Trump because he promised no new wars. Now their kids are stuck in one.

The Navy insists the Lincoln is fully capable and adequately supplied.

This is the cost of a war nobody wants to talk about.
