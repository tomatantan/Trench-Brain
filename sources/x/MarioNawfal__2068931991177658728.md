🇺🇸 Red Flag - Alaska 22 Explained:

A major multinational air combat training exercise led by Pacific Air Forces (PACAF), designed to simulate real-world air combat at scale.

Pilots and crews are split into:
🔵 “Blue” forces: defensive coalition teams working together to protect airspace
🔴 “Red” forces: aggressor units that mimic advanced enemy tactics and near-peer capabilities

The idea is to make training as realistic as possible, with complex scenarios that mirror modern high-threat aerial warfare.

Essentially: it’s one of the closest things to real combat training pilots can get.
