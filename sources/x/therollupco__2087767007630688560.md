Banks have been circling stablecoins for years but Visa stopped circling.

Edward Woodford of @zerohashx on what changed:

"This is not a proof of concept for Visa. This is a true GA application to all eligible customers."

"Over 12 billion endpoints, over 190 countries. Stablecoins for Visa are a way to move value instantly, real time, global, in a very cost efficient way."

"Conventionally you'd hold money at a partner like Visa Direct. Stablecoins allow just in time funding, which frees up capital for partners."
