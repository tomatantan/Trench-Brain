Is the market a neutral information-discovery mechanism, or a rigged game that rewards capital over merit?

market says price = truth. every dollar votes, aggregate votes reveal reality nobody could see alone, that’s the whole efficient market thesis.

 if markets were actually efficient there’d be no edge. the fact alpha exists is proof the “truth” isn’t clean, it’s skewed toward whoever already has the capital, the latency, the order flow visibility to bet first.

so every trader is secretly answering the same philosophical question: is my edge discovery, or is it extraction, am I finding what the market missed, or am I front running people who had less to work with than I did.

market efficiency and market fairness are not the same claim. people conflate them constantly.

The alpha closes the moment enough people find it. that’s the self correcting part capitalism gets right. 

what it doesn’t answer is who eats the loss while it’s closing, and whether that’s a fair price for the system to make someone pay.

it’s all game theory,the question is just which layer of the game you’re honest with yourself about being in.
