🚨🇺🇸 ICE has suspended traffic stops nationwide after officers killed two men in roughly a week, per the WSJ.

-ICE leadership instructed officers not to stop moving vehicles to arrest or question occupants, per current and former officials, eliminating one of the agency's most common arrest tools

-The trigger: a 26-year-old man shot dead by an immigration officer in Biddeford, Maine, Monday, a week after a man was killed during an operation in Houston

-Lawmakers in both states say NEITHER man was the intended target of the operations

-The WSJ identified 28 shootings by federal immigration agents since Trump took office, 10 involving American citizens, with more than half beginning as traffic stops

-New rules also require a body camera on every arrest team, though ICE only has enough for about half its officers, and fleeing immigrants are now to be re-arrested later rather than pursued

-Sen. Collins personally pressed DHS Secretary Mullin three times after the Maine shooting, and he agreed policy had to change

Enforcement matters and the border chaos of the last administration was real. 

But when 28 shootings happen under one agency and body cams are still optional for half the officers, this became recklessness.

Both sides let this get out of hand, the activists who turned every operation into a riot and the agency that let quotas outrun caution.

Pausing and fixing the process is the right call.
