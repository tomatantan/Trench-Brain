🇺🇸 The richest 0.00001% of Americans now hold wealth equal to 12% of U.S. national income, the widest gap on record.

That's a tiny sliver of people, a few dozen households, sitting on a share that tops even the Robber Baron peak of the early 1900s.

The line barely moved for most of the 20th century, then took off in the 2000s as stocks, real estate and private companies pulled far ahead of wages. 

The tech boom and the AI surge stretched it further, with only a brief dip during COVID.
