🇷🇺🇺🇦 Russia has been blowing up the gas stations along a key Ukrainian highway.

The strikes hit the Kharkiv-Poltava stretch of the E40, one of the two big roads moving supplies from Kyiv to the eastern front. 

All part of a campaign that's wrecked more than 200 stations throughout the country.

One Kharkiv official says every station on that stretch is gone now. But the region's military chief says that's "not true" and that there's still plenty of fuel around.

Ukraine says the whole point is to make life miserable in places Russia can't take on the ground, and to hit back for all the Ukrainian strikes on Russian refineries.

People are back to keeping fuel in cans in their trunks, same as they did in early 2022 when the pumps ran dry.
