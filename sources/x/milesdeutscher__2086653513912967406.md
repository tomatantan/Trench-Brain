This is the most important AI automation article I've ever written, and it blew up.

To summarise how to automate your ENTIRE life with Claude (5 steps):

1. Audit your workflows

Let Claude interview you about every recurring task in your life or business, one question at a time. It scores each on repeatability and how much human judgment it actually needs, so you know exactly what's worth automating first.

2. Turn workflows into skills

Either talk Claude through the process out loud (Whisper Flow makes this faster than typing), or use "Record a Skill" and just do the task while narrating it. Claude turns the demonstration into a reusable skill.

3. Build a folder system

A "business brain" folder for context (meetings, finances, whatever your agents need to know), and a "specs" folder holding every skill you've built.

4. Mapping workflows

Turn each spec into an interactive HTML map, purple nodes for AI-executed steps, yellow for manual input, green for external tools. Forces you to actually see your automations instead of reading markdown files.

5. Deploy

Claude agents, Hermes, or /loop inside Claude Code. Mix all three depending on the task.

Start with a few workflows. Don't try to automate everything at once.
