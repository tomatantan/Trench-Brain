"Last year's concerns centered primarily on disturbance to adult eagles. This year's concern is far more serious."

Big Bear Lake's bald eagles Jackie and Shadow have been watched by millions through Friends of Big Bear Valley's live nest cam. Last summer, supporters launched a petition to protect them from the city's July 4th fireworks show. Now Jackie and Shadow are parents — and their eaglets, Sandy and Luna, are still in the nest with the holiday 10 days out.

A petition is urging the City of Big Bear Lake to relocate its annual fireworks show while Sandy and Luna remain in a critical stage of development.
