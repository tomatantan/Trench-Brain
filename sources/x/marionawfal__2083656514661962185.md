🇺🇸🇲🇦 The U.S. Embassy in Morocco posted Trump’s Throne Day message to King Mohammed VI.

It reaffirms, in clear terms, that the U.S. recognizes Moroccan sovereignty over Western Sahara and sees Morocco’s autonomy plan as the only realistic path forward.

Anything else, the message says, simply prolongs an unacceptable status quo.

The timing is hard to ignore.

The massive migrant surge into Spain’s Ceuta enclave was days ago, another piece of territory Morocco has long claimed.

Washington is doubling down on its alignment with Rabat while Spain is still dealing with the fallout on the ground.
