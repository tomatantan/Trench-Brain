🇺🇸🇮🇷 Everyone is watching the wrong number. It isn't how many ships the Navy can escort, it's how many crews will board and how many insurers will underwrite

This is the mechanism that makes Iran's weak hand strong. 

Tehran cannot blockade Hormuz, it has no navy for that. 

But it doesn't need one. 

It only needs to make the passage frightening enough that crews refuse to sail and underwriters refuse to cover the hull. 

American escort capacity is irrelevant if the ships won't move regardless. 

Fear closes a strait that firepower can't.

The data already shows it working.

Transit fell from roughly 25 percent of prewar levels last week to 5 to 10 percent in a single 12-hour window, and the blockade will drive it lower. 

The tanker isn't afraid of the odds. 

It just needs one cruise missile in the neighborhood to make the whole voyage uninsurable.

That is the asymmetry the entire war keeps circling. 

America needs the strait to function to prove the guarantee holds. 

Iran only needs it to feel dangerous.

Denial is cheaper than protection, always, and Iran has spent 138 days proving it can afford its side of that math longer than Washington can afford its own.
