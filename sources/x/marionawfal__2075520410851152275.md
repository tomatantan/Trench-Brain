🇺🇸 American support for Israelis has collapsed since the Gaza war began.

Just 52% of Americans now hold a favorable view of the Israeli people, down from 67% in 2022. Views of Palestinians barely moved, holding around 50%.

The real divide is generational. Among Americans under 30, Palestinians are viewed far more favorably than Israelis, 58% to 32%.

Among young Democrats, the gap is even wider: 72% view Palestinians favorably, just 26% say the same of Israelis.

A generation is watching a genocide unfold in real time, and drawing its own conclusions.
