🇺🇸🇮🇱 New York's mayor is asking his lawyers if he can arrest Netanyahu on U.S. soil.

Zohran Mamdani says it's an active conversation with the city's legal department ahead of Netanyahu's expected UN General Assembly visit in September.

He stopped short of repeating his campaign promise to order the arrest, saying instead: 

"Whatever the law allows me to do in New York City, that's what we will do."

The ICC issued a warrant for Netanyahu in November 2024 over alleged war crimes in Gaza. Israel rejects the charges and the court's authority entirely.

The problem is the U.S. never signed on to the ICC, and Governor Hochul has already said a mayor has no power to make this arrest.

Netanyahu is coming to New York in September either way.
