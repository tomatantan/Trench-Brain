The DOJ just moved to drop all charges against the alleged mastermind of a $722 MILLION crypto Ponzi.

His name is Matthew Goettsche, founder of BitClub Network.

Prosecutors say he raised $722 MILLION selling shares in Bitcoin mining pools with faked earnings numbers.

In private messages, he allegedly called his own investors "idiots" and "sheep."

The case was heading to trial in October. Now it's being dropped entirely, and permanently.

The part drawing scrutiny is that he hired a legal team with ties to Trump to lobby the DOJ.

That team included a former contestant from The Apprentice and a lawyer who's secured Trump pardons.

Three of his co defendants already pleaded guilty. The alleged ringleader is the one walking free.

The DOJ says the case was seven years old and it's still recovering money for investors, and that the lobbying had nothing to do with it.

It follows a wider pullback on crypto enforcement, as both Trump and his acting attorney general hold crypto investments of their own.

The people who ran the scheme are going to prison. The man accused of building it is not.
