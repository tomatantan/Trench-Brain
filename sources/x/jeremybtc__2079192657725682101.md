The Houthis just banned all Saudi shipping through Bab al-Mandeb, one of the most important oil routes on earth.

The Houthis are the Iran aligned group that controls most of Yemen, and they sit directly on the strait connecting the Red Sea to Asia.

This matters because of where Saudi oil has been going lately.

When Iran shut the Strait of Hormuz, the Saudis started pumping crude across the country by pipeline to Yanbu on the Red Sea instead.

Shipments out of that port jumped from under 1 MILLION barrels a day to 4.7 MILLION in a matter of weeks.

Every one of those cargoes heading to Asia has to sail through Bab al-Mandeb.

The ban traces back to a Saudi airstrike on Sanaa airport last week, which the Houthis called a breach of a four year truce.

Hormuz is still shut. Now the alternative is under threat too.

Saudi Arabia spent years at war with the Houthis. Now most of its oil has to sail right past them.
