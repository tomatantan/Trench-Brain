🇸🇴🇪🇹 The Somali National Army’s 60th Division is now overseeing the Buurhakaba forward operating base in Somalia’s Bay region, after Ethiopian troops formally handed it over. 

This ends about 12 years of continuous Ethiopian presence at the base, dating back to 2014. 

Buurhakaba sits on a key logistics corridor and has been important in operations against Al-Shabaab. 

Local reports warn the area is SURROUNDED by the Islamist terror group, with limited Somali forces on the ground.

More Ethiopian withdrawals from nearby bases are expected soon.
