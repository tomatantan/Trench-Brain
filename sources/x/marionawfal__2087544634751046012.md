🇺🇸 The Trump Admin is set to spend at least $900 million on White House construction projects...

New records show the total for the planned overhaul, centered on a lavish East Wing ballroom, has climbed higher than earlier estimates and will be funded mostly by taxpayers.

Instead of seeking a direct appropriation from Congress, the White House funneled money from other agencies and private donors into a small maintenance account that normally holds just a few million dollars.

A federal appeals court ruled last week that the ballroom itself still needs congressional approval. 

Trump has vowed to appeal, setting up a larger fight over how far a president can go in reshaping the White House without lawmakers’ consent.
