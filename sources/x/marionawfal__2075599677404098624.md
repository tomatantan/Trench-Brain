🇺🇸Circle jumps 15% after landing a bank charter

Circle, ticker CRCL, popped more than 15% after U.S. regulators cleared it to run as a bank.

The nod came from the OCC, the federal watchdog that licenses national banks, so a crypto firm just got waved in to play by the same rules as the big lenders.

And that's a real win for the company behind USDC, the digital dollar built to sit steady at $1. A bank charter hands it the kind of legitimacy that pulls in the big institutions who'd been keeping their distance.
