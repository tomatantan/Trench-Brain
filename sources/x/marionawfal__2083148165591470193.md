🇺🇸🇮🇱🇮🇷 The U.S. and Israel are reportedly discussing a full LAND BLOCKADE of Iran...

After months of strikes failed to put the regime on its knees, Trump and Netanyahu talked this week about cutting off everything that moves in or out... by land.

That would mean pressuring neighbors like Iraq, Pakistan, Turkey, and others to close key border crossings and choke Iran’s trade off completely.

A senior Israeli official put it simply: what if Iran can’t bring anything in and can’t take anything out?

Iran shares borders with 7 countries, several of which are not exactly close to Washington, so it would be difficult to enforce effectively.

But the goal is clear: isolate the economy, block shipments, and pile more pressure on a country already experiencing economic woes.

If the naval blockade and Hormuz strategy have reached their limit, a land blockade COULD be the next step in the maximum pressure campaign.

Or it could just be a distraction while something bigger is planned...
