The Stripe comparison sounds ambitious until you understand what @crediblefin is actually building

@NiceGuyShri says they're not competing with Stripe as an acquirer. They're the orchestrator layer that sits on top.

Local acquirers handle central bank approvals and local rails. Credible partners with them, then underwrites risk per transaction using their own AI (built originally for trade finance). If the risk score is above 9.5, Credible fronts liquidity from their DeFi pool and instant-settles the merchant. 85% of transactions instant-settle while 15% wait for the acquirer.
