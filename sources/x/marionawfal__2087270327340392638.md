🇺🇸 A plate of raw oysters left a Marine vet in a coma and changed his family forever.

Joe Belgio, 44, a Marine vet and retired firefighter, had just welcomed a baby girl when he ordered raw oysters at a family dinner near Chicago last June. Within hours he was in cardiac arrest.

His old fire department brought him back, then he slipped into a coma for months. Doctors found two nasty bacteria, EPEC and Plesiomonas shigelloides, in his system.

A year on, he's still in a hospital bed, severely brain damaged and likely bedridden for life. Gibsons denies any link and says nobody else got sick. It's headed to court.
