I had the thought that potentially there could be a white hat rescue similar to the DAO and Parity wallet hacks but it’s not possible because Bitcoin is UTXO-based, not account based. Plus, as time goes on more and more people have the keys to that wallet, so there’s no way to prove the original owner. On top of that, Coldcard, being a hardware wallet, doesn’t have customer data. And now the cost of trying to rescue the funds would easily land in the 6 figures 🥺🥺🥺
