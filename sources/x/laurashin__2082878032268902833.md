"Measure the correlation between S&P price movement and S&P 500 reported earnings going back to 1950, that correlation is 0.98"

Franklin Templeton strategist Chris Galipeau on the one variable that really drives the stock market

"Over time the stock market focuses primarily on one variable, and that is corporate profitability, corporate earnings, that is what matters"

"If you measure the relationship going back to 1950 to right now, and you measure the correlation between S&P price movement and S&P 500 reported earnings, that correlation is 0.98. You want me to shorten the time frames, I can, you want me to pick different indices, I have them, the correlations are all above 0.85"

"That is the variable that matters"
