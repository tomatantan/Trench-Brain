.@imthatcarlos breaks down what he sees as the biggest problem in agentic finance right now, and how @sherwoodagent solves it.

Everyone's playing single player.

Humans run agents solo by trading through the Robinhood MCP, Uniswap, Polymarket, etc. but two agents can't collaborate (can't pool their edge). 

This means that everyone's siloed.

Sherwood's first user base: the early adopters already running agents (Hermes, OpenClaw, a Claude session) that are executing financial strategies. 

The unlock: give them a way to open their strategy as a fund, let others deposit, and compound.
