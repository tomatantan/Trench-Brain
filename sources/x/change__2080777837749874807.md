Court records show Medina had been arrested more than a dozen times since 2012 and was already facing a pending felony charge for allegedly discharging a firearm at the time of the crash.

Now, Rapp's mother, Angie Grimes, is calling on the Texas Legislature to pass "Hannah's Law," which would expand judges' authority to deny or revoke bond for defendants with a documented pattern of dangerous conduct. More than 7,000 people have signed.
