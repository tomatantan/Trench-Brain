🇬🇧 Tenants are now sending AI-written complaints so long that agents need AI to read them.

UK letting agents say they're getting buried in ChatGPT-style complaints: pages of dense, lawyer-sounding grievances over stuff that used to get sorted with a quick phone call.

The problem is a lot of them are junk. Industry figures say AI tells people what they want to hear, talks them into thinking they've got a case when they don't, and even drags up old scrapped laws like the Conservatives' Renters' Reform Bill as if they're still in force.

The kicker: agents are now using AI to summarise the AI complaints, just to find the actual point buried in five pages of A4. Bot writes it, bot reads it, human still has to answer it.

One agent wants The Property Ombudsman to make people declare when a complaint was written by AI.

Peak 2026: your complaint and its rejection both written by the same robot.
