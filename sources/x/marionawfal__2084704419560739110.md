🇮🇷 Trita Parsi believes tolling the Strait isn't the core issue. It's the control it represents.

He says the real question is whether any Strait of Hormuz agreement guarantees the free flow of commerce while being jointly managed by Iran and Oman. 

In his view, that's very different from a system where Iran could selectively restrict shipping.

"I think that's why it's important now to start distinguishing what actually is the core interest. Is it control or is it free flow of the commerce?"
