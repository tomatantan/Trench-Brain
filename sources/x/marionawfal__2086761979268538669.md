🇦🇺 2 packed jets came way too close to disaster on the ground at Sydney Airport.

A Jetstar A320 taxiing for the Gold Coast had to slam on the brakes after a Qatar Airways 777 being towed across the tarmac moved straight into its path.

Both aircraft stopped just short of each other.

A Jetstar crew member doing final checks was thrown to the floor and injured during the sudden stop.

The Qatar 777 also reportedly suffered damage to its nose wheel.

Early reports suggest the tug crew towing the Qatar jet failed to follow ATC instructions.
