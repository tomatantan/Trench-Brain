The Hagleys offered to adopt him and two no-kill rescues also offered to take him in for behavioral rehabilitation. The shelter refused all three, citing liability.

Over 1,000 people have signed a petition demanding the county require shelters to exhaust every rescue and adoption option before ordering euthanasia.
