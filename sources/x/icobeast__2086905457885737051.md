Feel like everyone was screeching for the last year that distribution is the only thing that matters in the age of AI...

I'm not convinced. I think product plays a lot larger role than people realize.

My case study is The Base App in late 2024-2025. 

It undoubtedly had the largest distribution mechanism of any crypto app we've seen in quite some time, and *could* have been what fomo is (they literally spent over a year trying to push social <> trading). 

But it didn't work, and nobody uses it to trade socially.

Think that's because the product was totally wrong. 

Nuanced....they were directionally correct on product vision - "social trading" - and had every distribution advantage imaginable, but completely wrong on the actual product design/execution.

These sort of odd/unexpected outcomes make the industry feel 'wide open' for winners to win. That alone makes the intersection of crypto <> markets still very very very exciting to work in.
