🇷🇺🇺🇦 Russia's latest missile and drone strikes on Kyiv may reflect a much broader shift in strategy.

Alex Christoforou of The Duran argues these coordinated waves were unlikely to have been assembled overnight, suggesting they formed part of a longer-planned campaign aimed at degrading Ukraine's logistics, fuel infrastructure, and military support network. 

In his view, the objective is not a single decisive strike but a sustained war of attrition that gradually weakens Ukraine's ability to defend the Donbas front.

Alex believes this slow, methodical pressure is intended to set the conditions for Russia's next major phase of operations rather than deliver an immediate breakthrough.
