Mike Dudas says founders are paralyzed on how regulated to go, because going compliant early has gotten firms punished before.

"In past cycles, firms got penalized for taking the regulated approach too early while competitors just YOLO'd."

"Look at tokenized private stock, tokenized Anthropic shares. Some firms have robust structures with protections. Others are just YOLOing. And the YOLO ones are growing faster right now. I don't believe that's sustainable."

"The 'alien' token model breaks down for traditional assets, equities, structured private credit, where you want big trusted names. That's why you haven't seen serious capital move into RWA issuance yet."
