🇱🇰 Sri Lanka just handed two of its own security chiefs the death penalty.

A court sentenced ex-police chief Pujith Jayasundara and ex-defence secretary Hemasiri Fernando to death over the 2019 Easter Sunday bombings that killed 279 people at three churches and three hotels.

Both were convicted of negligence for getting a warning the night before that suicide bombers were about to hit, and doing nothing with it.

It's likely symbolic since Sri Lanka hasn't executed anyone since 1976.

A darker theory still hangs over the case. A former intelligence chief was arrested this year on conspiracy charges, and a whistleblower says the bombings were allowed to happen for political gain. 

Two days after the attacks, a candidate launched his campaign on national security and rode it to the presidency.

Something tells me this goes far above two officials.
