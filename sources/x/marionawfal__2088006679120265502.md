🇺🇸 Matt Bracken says the number of ships the U.S. has isn't the real constraint, but how quickly they can burn through their missile inventories.

A Burke-class destroyer can carry roughly 128 vertical launch cells. But once its defensive missiles are depleted, Bracken says the ship has to leave the fight for days to reload, potentially making the long trip to Diego Garcia.

“As these ships deplete their missiles... they have to go out of the line.”

In a war dominated by missiles and drones, that creates a major logistical bottleneck.
