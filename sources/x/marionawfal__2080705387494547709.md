🇧🇭🇰🇼🇮🇷 Bahrain and Kuwait quietly flew strikes into Iran this month.

The targets were drone and missile depots, and it was their first direct retaliation of the war. The UAE fed them targeting intelligence and flew air cover.

Both countries host American bases, and both have been absorbing Iranian fire for weeks. 

Kuwait says a power plant and desalination facility were hit on Sunday.

Five months in, the Gulf is out of patience. Hormuz is choked, the Houthis are hitting Red Sea shipping, tourism is gone and investment is under pressure.

Saudi Arabia struck Iran earlier in the war and is reassessing. Oman and Qatar are still trying to stay out of it.

Analysts say Gulf capitals are starting to entertain the idea of regime change in Tehran, on the logic that sharing a neighborhood with this Iran means living under it.
