"The argument to increase issuance is in my mind actually stronger than the argument to decrease it"

EtherFi CEO Mike Silagadze reacts to the EIP-8361 proposal to reduce ETH staking rewards

"There are two sides of this. There's an argument, and I wouldn't make this argument, but there's an argument that issuance should be higher, and you can say a number of things that actually improve DeFi, it'll make ETH staking more attractive, which is helpful to ETH price because ETH stakers don't sell ETH"

"That's the particularly brain damaged aspect of this proposal, that somehow reducing the issuance, suddenly your yield goes down to like 20 basis points, which is probably where it'll end up. So now you're going to have $10 billion of ETH unstaked out there in the market. If you're worried about ETH price, this is actually going to be much worse"

"But the strongest thing is just don't fuck with it, this is just not the most important thing by a long shot"
