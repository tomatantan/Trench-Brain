🇷🇺🇮🇳 Ukraine has hit so many Russian refineries that Moscow is now, for the first time ever, importing gasoline from India.

The first shipment landed last week, moved through a chain of Russian-linked tankers and a ship-to-ship transfer off Egypt to dodge sanctions.

Russia's crude processing ran about a third below normal in July, and Kyiv has only stepped up the strikes since.

When Russia has to import gasoline from an oil buyer 4,000 miles out, things are bad.
