People massively underestimate how powerful compounding is in this market

Everyone is obsessed with finding the next 100x

You don’t always need to

A 2x here
A 3x there
Another 2x after that

If you can consistently find good opportunities and protect your capital when you’re wrong, those gains start stacking on top of each other

Some of the biggest portfolios aren’t always built from one lucky trade, they’re often built from years of making good decisions

Don’t overlook a trade just because it “only” has 2-3x potential
