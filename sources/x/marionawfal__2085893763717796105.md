🇸🇪🇺🇦🇷🇺 Sweden is handing a seized Russian shadow fleet ship over to Ukraine after the Supreme Court shut down the owners’ final appeal.

The cargo vessel Caffa was boarded in March for sailing under a false flag and safety violations.

Ukraine says it was used to move grain stolen from occupied territory, and Swedish courts agreed the evidence supports transferring it for a war-crimes probe.

Officials in Kyiv are calling the decision a landmark precedent.

Ageing, poorly insured ships with murky ownership have been Russia’s main tool for dodging sanctions.

This one is now heading to Ukraine instead.
