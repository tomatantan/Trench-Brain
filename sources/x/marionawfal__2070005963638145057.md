Tucker Carlson had an interesting take on power

He basically argues that once people’s basic needs are met, the drive for “more” often turns into a desire for control over other people not just money anymore.

His point is that everyone has that impulse to some degree, but the difference is whether people keep it in check or let it take over.

He also suggests that a lot of people drawn to power positions may not be the most well-balanced psychologically but the ones most driven by that internal need to fill a void.

A pretty blunt way of looking at how power and personality might overlap.
