Gold and silver investors may be focusing on the wrong signal.

Peter Schiff argues markets are placing too much weight on the Federal Reserve's hawkish rhetoric while overlooking where monetary policy is ultimately headed. He believes the Fed is maintaining the appearance of independence and restraint today to preserve room for future rate cuts as economic pressures build.

In Schiff's view, the recent pullback in precious metals has created an attractive buying opportunity. 

He argues that once inflation reasserts itself and the Fed pivots toward easier policy, both gold and silver could benefit while investors who focused only on short-term Fed messaging risk being caught off guard.
