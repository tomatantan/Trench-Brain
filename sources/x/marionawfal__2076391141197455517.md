🇺🇸🇺🇦 Incredibly, perhaps the last photograph taken of Lindsey Graham was this one: holding a drone.

It was during a tour of the production facility of SkyFall, a leading Ukrainian defense-tech firm.

Here, he saw advanced unmanned systems, such as “Vampire” drones, “Shrike” FPVs, and “Shahed” interceptors. 

Graham praised Ukraine’s innovation in drone warfare, saying it would be a “huge mistake” for the U.S. not to forge a robust partnership with the Ukrainians.

About 24 hours later, he was dead.
