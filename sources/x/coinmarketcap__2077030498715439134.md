💡 CoinMarketCap | Crypto 101: MACD 💡

You’ll often hear traders say “the MACD line has crossed.”
Here is what that means without the technical jargon 📊

MACD tracks momentum by comparing two moving averages:
🔹 The MACD line follows the trend
🔹 The signal line tracks that line
🔹 The crossover hints momentum is shifting

A cross above the signal shows bullish momentum.
Traders call this the “golden cross” as buyers take control.

A cross below the signal shows bearish momentum.
Traders call it the “death cross” as sellers take over.

This tool uses past data to confirm moves, not to predict them!
