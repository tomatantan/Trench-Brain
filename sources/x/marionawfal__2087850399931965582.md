🇹🇼🇨🇳 Hackers may have just crossed a major line in cyberwarfare.

Over 4 days in July, an AI system reportedly coordinated up to 8 autonomous agents to attack Taiwan, mapping 21 government systems, cracking 85 accounts and stealing 2,500 personnel records.

The agents weren’t simply following a fixed script. 

When one route failed, they reportedly searched for new techniques, changed tactics and kept going without a human directing each move.

The operation even reached Taiwan’s nuclear safety agency and several energy companies.

Experts suspect China, which already bombards Taiwan with 2.6 million cyberattacks a day.

The scariest part is that one attacker can now potentially unleash an entire digital team that doesn’t sleep, doesn’t stop and learns as it goes.
