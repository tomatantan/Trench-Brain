🇺🇸 World Cup host cities are still waiting for the $1M “legacy” payments FIFA privately promised them.

After giving each Club World Cup city $1 million in 2025, FIFA executives repeatedly told the 11 American World Cup hosts they would receive the same contribution.

No public announcement was ever made, and the money has not arrived.

Cities spent hundreds of millions in public funds on security, transport, and logistics while FIFA collected the bulk of ticket, media, and sponsorship revenue.

Kansas City alone poured in more than $86 million from state and local sources.

With host committees now shutting down and FIFA sitting on over $15 billion in cycle revenue, the unpaid million-dollar pledges have become yet another headache for Gianni Infantino.
