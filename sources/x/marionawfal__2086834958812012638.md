🇴🇲 Omani authorities say the oil slick from the wrecked tanker Caroline Bezengi has expanded to roughly 390 km off the Hallaniyat Islands near Dhofar.

The Cameroon-flagged Suezmax, widely linked to Russia’s shadow fleet and sanctioned by the UK and EU, was carrying Russian crude loaded at Novorossiysk and bound for India.

The slick is moving northeast and has come within about 7 km of the mainland shore at its closest point. 

Omani teams are monitoring the situation, assessing the hull, and working to contain the leak and eventually remove the remaining cargo.
