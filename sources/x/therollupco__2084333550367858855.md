Binance US CEO Steve Gregory's playbook runs opposite to the industry: win retail first, and the institutions follow.

"Volume dominates market share, but I want a much healthier retail-institutional balance."

"Look at Coinbase, all the volume's institutional, all the revenue's in retail. And that split has gotten more and more skewed against retail over the past few years."

"So I want to do a lot of retail products early to bring users on. Once the market chooses that product, the institutions follow. They're much more fickle, but it's very clear what they'll want."

"Binance US was a backup exchange during our regulatory hibernation. I want to make it people's primary exchange."
