VanEck's NODE ETF has beaten Bitcoin by nearly 100 percentage points in 15 months. 

Matthew Sigel on the one insight that did it.

"That came from identifying early that Bitcoin miners were valued at a very low multiple of the megawatts of power they control, relative to the few data-center REITs that existed."

"The old miner model was a melting ice cube: dilute shareholders to buy ASICs, chase hashrate, watch revenue halve every four years." 

"Now, funding AI data-center leases with debt instead of equity, their cost of capital has collapsed. Each lease is at better economics, and that's created a lot of value."
