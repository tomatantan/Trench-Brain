🇷🇺🇬🇧 A Russian warship opened fire 46 miles off the British coast.  

The same day Andy Burnham was sworn in as UK Prime Minister.  

The frigate Neustrashimy, which has been escorting Russia's "shadow fleet" oil tankers through the English Channel, ran a 30-minute live-fire drill south of Plymouth on Monday, shadowed the whole time by the Royal Navy's HMS Tyne.  

A defense source called it "highly unusual" this close to the coast and said the timing on Burnham's first day may not have been an accident.  

The Kremlin appears to be introducing itself to the new man at Number 10.
