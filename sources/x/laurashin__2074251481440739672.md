"Part of the problem is we've called so many damn things tokens. A token is not a new form of capital instrument. It's just an approximation of other forms"

Austin on why the word token has become almost meaningless

"It's hard to have a formalistic discussion about this because when I say token, I'm talking about everything from Bitcoin to stablecoins, to literal tokenized equities, to protocol tokens, to project tokens, to meme coins, to NFTs, to tokenized ownership of real objects"

"I think Ram is correct that there's not gonna be anything functionally new within a capital stack. A token is not a new form of capital instrument"

"It's just an approximation of other forms, sometimes a very well done one, sometimes a very poorly done one"
