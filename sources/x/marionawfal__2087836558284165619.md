🇩🇪 65 years ago today, East German soldiers and police started building the Berlin Wall, sealing the border overnight to stop the flood of people fleeing to the West.

It stood for 28 years before falling on November 9, 1989.

German Chancellor Merz:

"65 years ago, the GDR leadership had the Wall built. 

Today, our country has been united for longer than it was divided, and we live in freedom and peace. 

The lesson of the Wall remains: Freedom and democracy are not a given. We must always fight for them."

Back then it was a wall to keep people in. Today plenty are building walls to keep people out, and it remains to be seen if that works any better.
