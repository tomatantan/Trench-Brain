🇸🇾 Bashar al-Assad sentenced to death in absentia

A Syrian court convicted the ousted leader of killings, torture, arbitrary arrests and crimes against humanity during the nearly 14-year civil war. 

It’s the first time a Syrian court has convicted him.

His brother Maher got the same sentence, along with a former security official who was actually in court. 

Assad has been living in Russia since rebels forced him out in late 2024.

Crowds celebrated in Damascus and other cities.
