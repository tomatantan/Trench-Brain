The merit of the wealth tax is that it’s destroying our future prosperity.  Literally destroying the means of production.  

You are taxing capital not wealth. A farm that is worth a billion dollars doesn’t have a billion dollars in cash in the bank. It’s not hoarding a billion dollars worth of grain.  The valuation is the best estimate total extra food the farm grows in the future.  

If you tax the farmer 5% on the value of the farm, the farmer has to sell 5% of the ownership of the farm.  The shares that represent the farm.  

Now the farm can’t raise as much money to expand, to buy tractors, to grow more food.  The valuation of the farm drops.  Which literally means there less food grown in the future.
