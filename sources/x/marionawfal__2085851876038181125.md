🚨🇺🇸 The S&P 500 has hit its highest close EVER, soaring 3.6% higher this week.

Soft July jobs data (payrolls -23k, big downward revisions) convinced markets the Fed is less likely to hike in September.

Under new Chair Kevin Warsh, who’s deliberately giving less forward guidance than Powell ever did, traders are reacting straight to the numbers instead of waiting for Fed hand-holding.

Powell used to get hammered from both sides for over-communicating and for resisting political pressure.

Warsh is trying the opposite playbook. So far the market likes the simpler version.
