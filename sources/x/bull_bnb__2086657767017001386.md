I think I finally understand where the game is going.

Its not about making money anymore, its about proving it.

It’s not just better UI UX.

It’s UI UX and public flex.

Hyperliquid figured it out first.

Trading existed forever. But now you could take a massive position publicly, sit on a leaderboard and basically say my size is bigger than yours.

Then prediction markets came.

Same psychology. Different casino.

Then PumpFun, Fomo and all these apps.

Connect your X account and suddenly even if you’re a nobody, one good trade can make you somebody.

You hit 50x.
You trend.
You climb the leaderboard.
People follow you.
People copy you.

Money became content. PnL became social status.

Bertrand Russell said

Beggars do not envy millionaires, though of course they will envy other beggars who are more successful.

That’s literally the game.

People don’t need to see some billionaire winning.

They need to see someone who looks exactly like them winning slightly bigger than them.

That creates envy.
Envy creates competition.
Competition creates activity.
Activity creates volume.

And this is why I think people misunderstand Fomo.

To me it’s basically a dumbed down Phantom with a leaderboard and social flex attached to it.

And dumbed down is not an insult.

That’s the genius.

No bridging.
No gas bullshit.
No terminals.
No 17 f,in buttons.

Tap Buy  Win and Flex.

Now imagine someone makes it even dumber, faster and cheaper while making the public flex even stronger.

There’s your Fomo 2.0.

Crypto spent years building better financial infrastructure.

Maybe the next winners simply build better stages for human ego.
