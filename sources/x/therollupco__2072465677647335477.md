Henry McPhie explains how GLDY generates yield from gold leasing.

“The yield itself is generated through something called gold leasing.”

“This is a market that’s been around for a very long time, but traditionally has only really been available for large institutions.”

“We rent that gold to manufacturers, companies, and large institutions using it as working capital.”

“A good example would be a jeweler.”

“They pay us to be able to use the gold.”

“We’re able to pass that yield onto our holders while keeping title of the gold the whole time.”
