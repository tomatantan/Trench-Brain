🇺🇸🇮🇷 Rep. Massie laid out Iran’s waiting game in 4 cards

He says Tehran is watching 3 things: whether the U.S. burns through its strategic oil reserves, whether the region runs out of missile interceptors, and whether Republicans lose their majority.

His advice is simple. Leave the table before any of those get played.

Some analysts go further and argue the deeper U.S. interest is not just keeping Hormuz open, but preventing any long term shift where Iran (and by extension others) can set terms for oil transit or settle energy trades outside the dollar system.

Iran has spent years trying to price and settle oil in other currencies.

A prolonged conflict that forces the issue back into dollar channels would protect a key pillar of American financial power.

Massie’s warning is about attrition and politics.

The competing view is that the fight is also about who controls the rules of energy trade.

Both can be true at the same time. The longer it runs, the more those pressures, military, economic, and monetary, stack up.
