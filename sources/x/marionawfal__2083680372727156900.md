🇺🇸🇰🇵 DOJ just subpoenaed a NYT freelancer over that SEAL Team 6 North Korea story, that revealed a botched 2019 Navy SEAL Team 6 mission in North Korea.  

According to the reporting, the SEALs were trying to plant a listening device near the coast when the operation went sideways. 

They ended up killing several unarmed North Korean civilians. 

The article relied on roughly 2 dozen anonymous sources. They want years of his notes and every source.  

If reporters know that exposing sensitive government actions can lead to subpoenas, home visits by the FBI, and jail time for protecting sources, many will simply stop pursuing those stories.

Sources dry up. 

The public hears less about what its own military and intelligence agencies are doing.  

That's the heart of the First Amendment: the press must be free to gather and publish information the public has a right to know.
