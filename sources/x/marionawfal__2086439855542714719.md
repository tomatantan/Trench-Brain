Ever wondered why TSA makes you put your laptop in a separate bin?

It's not because the laptop itself needs special treatment.

Large electronics are dense enough to obscure other objects in an X-ray image, so removing them gives security officers a much clearer view of whatever was hiding underneath.

Turns out the laptop isn't necessarily what they're looking at, it's what they couldn't see because of it.
