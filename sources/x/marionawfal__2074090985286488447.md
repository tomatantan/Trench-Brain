🇺🇸🇮🇷 The southern route through the Strait of Hormuz is open again for all traffic.

A new Joint Maritime Information Center advisory says the corridor's been expanded, and ships can move through day or night as long as they keep their AIS on, radars going, and lights on.

The threat level is still substantial though, with a mine danger area to watch for and naval forces hailing ships over VHF.

Checking in with NCAGS is encouraged but not required. Ships can make the run on their own.
