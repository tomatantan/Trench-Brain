Se Yong Park says AI will make financial social networks about risk, not content.

“Once AI is getting so good, that’s probably replacing manual content entirely.”

“It doesn’t really take much to be the best thread guy, the best article guy, or the best short-form content guy.”

“These things are gonna commoditize.”

“What can’t be commoditized by AI is dollars. It’s risk.”

“A social network is gonna be grounded on who is right most of the time.”

“It’s gonna be how much did you risk, how much were you right, and how often are you able to articulate these views.”
