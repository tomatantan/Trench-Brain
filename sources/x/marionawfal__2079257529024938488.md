🇬🇧 Rachel Reeves just stepped down as Chancellor right after Andy Burnham took over as Prime Minister.

She used to compare him to Liz Truss, the PM who only lasted 49 days back in 2022 after unfunded tax cuts wrecked the pound and spiked borrowing costs.

Reeves bowed out claiming the economy is now "stronger, fairer and more resilient," while wishing Burnham's new team good luck.

A few months back, she was warning that Burnham wanting to ditch the bond markets would end in total disaster.

The people lending Britain money only stuck with Reeves for one reason: she promised to keep borrowing on a tight leash.

That promise just walked out the door with her.
