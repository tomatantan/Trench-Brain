Argentina's peso just fell to its WEAKEST level ever against the dollar.

This goes back decades. Argentina has been hit by repeated strikes of extreme inflation, where prices exploded and savings were wiped out almost overnight.

People lived through it enough times to stop trusting the peso entirely.

So Argentines protect themselves by holding dollars instead. 

The moment they're paid, many convert straight to dollars, because a dollar today will still be worth something next year and a peso likely won't.

That constant rush out of pesos is what keeps pushing the currency down and it feeds itself. The weaker the peso gets, the faster people dump it, which makes it weaker still.

For anyone earning in pesos, it means the money in their pocket loses value every single month.

Fixing this is exactly what Milei was elected to do but a currency at record lows shows just how much trust there is still left to rebuild.
