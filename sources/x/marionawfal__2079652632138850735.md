🇺🇸 Governor Mikie Sherrill announced that a system error, "a glitch," improperly registered 6,600 noncitizens to vote between 2023 and 2024, leading to roughly 400 ballots being cast.

State officials insist the applicants explicitly stated they were not citizens on keypads, but the software enrolled them anyway. All 6,600 are now being scrubbed from the voter rolls.

Even with Dems leadership calling the glitch appalling and firing the vendor, the fallout is huge.

That only fuels the argument that current motor-voter systems are leaky and don't prevent these things from happening.
