Actually, that is literally, exactly what it does, but better.

1. Like Bitcoin, total supply on $CRV is capped.
2. The 15% annual reduction in inflation on $CRV amounts to a 50% reduction in emissions every 4 years, exactly like Bitcoin's halving.
3. Even Bitcoin had high inflation in it's early days. Bitcoin at Year 6 (2015) had 9-10% annual inflation. CRV at year 6 (now) has ~4%. 
4. While the inflation rate of CRV over time mimics that of Bitcoin, unlike Bitcoin, $CRV has a built-in supply sink in that 35% of all existing $CRV has been voluntarily locked for at least 4 years (much of it forever).
