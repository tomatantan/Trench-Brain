The Venice team has been teasing 'Minds' lately, so @TrustlessState asks "What are Minds?" 

@JonShapeShift: "Minds are custom AI experiences" 

"We have a subset of really strong power users that understand these models really well. They're really good at chaining these things together in such a way, using system prompts in certain ways, to create a toolset that lets them get a much better output from the same prompt, versus just giving it to a kind of naked model without any of these things. 

And so we're creating tools for users to create these experiences and then potentially even to monetize them.  And we think in many ways this is going to become one of the more popular ways for people to use AI and that there will likely only be a small subset of these kind of power users who kind of become creators, although we're trying to make the tools easy enough for them to create whatever they can and they want. 

But that a ton of consumers will be very interested in this as kind of our thesis, that they will want to use these custom experiences because they will get a substantially better output."
