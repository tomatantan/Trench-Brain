🇹🇼🇨🇳 Taiwan just rehearsed the day China invades

Over 2 days this week, more than 370 officials in Nantou, the island's only landlocked county, ran a single nightmare on a loop.

A Chinese blockade, an earthquake Beijing exploits to sow chaos, hijacked TV channels pumping propaganda, sabotaged infrastructure, a bank run, then a full-scale invasion.

They moved hospitals underground, brought in real hackers to hit government networks, drilled food rationing, and scrambled to a backup command center after a simulated drone strike wiped out the first.

Chi Lien-cheng, the minister running it: "Our adversary is right on our doorstep, just across the Taiwan Strait. That is very close."

Every scenario was lifted straight from Ukraine and the Middle East, real wars, real lessons, no theater this time.

Preparation is the strategy. An island this ready is one Beijing has to think a lot harder about invading.
