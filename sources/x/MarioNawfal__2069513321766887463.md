🇺🇸🇮🇷 Iran's squad gets in 2 days before kickoff

Weeks after the two countries were trading fire, the Trump administration is letting Iran's national football team into the U.S. for their final World Cup group match against Egypt. That window is 1 day longer than they were first given, and not a minute past it.

Team Melli plays their third match in Seattle on June 26, then they are out the door the moment it ends Friday.

DHS confirmed it to Al Jazeera without spelling out why, though it lines up neatly with the ceasefire and the diplomatic talks now underway.

A DHS spokesperson kept it cold:

"The Iran team will still be required to leave the day the match ends. The overall security measures and protocol are the same"

One extra day. That's as warm as it gets.
