"We have several levers we could pull if we wanted to reduce the interest rate. But we're focused more on buying Bitcoin right now"

Matt Cole on the levers Strive could pull but is choosing not to

"If we wanted to reduce the interest rate, we could do it by having a lower amplification ratio, by having more cash on our balance sheet, or as the credit improves in a Bitcoin bull market. It just depends on if the company actually wanted to"

"We don't have any plans to do this, but let's say we wanted to. We could sell Bitcoin, buy back SATA, push the price up to reduce the interest rate. Anybody that sells could get effectively par for it because we would be the buyer of this security"

"We don't want to do that because we want a higher amplification ratio. We're very constructive on what Bitcoin's gonna do over the next several years"
