🇪🇺🇹🇷🇷🇺 Europe wants a receipt proving its gas isn't Russian and Turkey says good luck with that

EU buyers want Turkey to certify the gas in its pipelines isn't Russian. Energy Minister Alparslan Bayraktar's answer: he can't hand them a paper that proves that.

Different sources get blended in Turkey's pipes before the gas reaches Europe, so its origin is impossible to trace. And 40% of Turkey's own supply is still Russian, with no plans to drop it.

So the certificate is theater. Turkey mixes the gas, stamps the papers, and Europe pretends it's clean. TurkStream, a Russian pipeline, is how Hungary and Slovakia still get theirs anyway.

Meanwhile Brussels swears it'll ban all Russian energy, even as EU storage sits at record lows and operators warn the tanks won't be full for winter.

The gas still flows. All Europe managed to ban was the word "Russian" on the invoice.
