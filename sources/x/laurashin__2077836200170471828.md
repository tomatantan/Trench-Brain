"We don't know when the Fed solstice is going to be, that moment at which we collectively feel comfortable that we know when the hikes are going to end"

Andy Baehr, Managing Director of Asset Management at GSR, on what has to happen before a rally becomes sustainable

"Zooming out, the best thing that feels comfortable to say is that we're in an environment where we don't know where peak hawkishness is in terms of market sentiment. It hasn't been really since like 2022 pre-FTX, when the Fed hiked very aggressively to burn off the fiscal stimulus after COVID. Crypto assets were very challenged, as were equities in that environment, because we didn't know what peak hawkishness was going to be"

"It doesn't help of course that we have a new Fed chair, one who we're not that familiar with yet, but certainly one who doesn't seem very comfortable or very eager to soothe us"

"Until you really collectively, globally reach this idea of the Fed solstice of peak hawkishness, and you can see over the crest of the hill to what might unfold beyond that, it's very difficult to feel like any kind of rally is going to be reliably sustainable"
