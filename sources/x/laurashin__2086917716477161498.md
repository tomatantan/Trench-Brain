Spark CEO Sam Macpherson on how the sub-DAO model fixes what broke with flat DAO governance

"We went through this DAO issue back in 2021, 2022. Maker was one of the first DAOs operating a large scale protocol, and really tried to do the DAO thing for real, the Maker token voting, with teams operating within it. But we quickly realized this flat structure just doesn't work"

"It's not much different than any governance with humans, you get political factions forming and informal alliances and structures. So you really need a more natural place for that to sit, and this is why the sub-DAO model was conceived"

"The core DAO no longer has to make decisions about what to do. It just sets the rules and the risk policy the sub-DAOs have to follow, and then the sub-DAOs can splinter off into political factions even, that then compete in the free market to deliver the most value to the core DAO"
