Robbie asked Founder of @panoptic_xyz what happens to LPs after the Uniswap fee switch.

Guillaume says "If everyone removes liquidity there's a bit more for those sticking behind. But at some point you hit a wall where there's not enough liquidity to back trading."

"There's inertia. You're providing liquidity to a dozen pools, you shift back and forth. Some people have a contract, a revenue source outside the razor thin margin. They don't mind staying in the V3 pool."

"The worst case is the withdrawal is progressive. Apathy forming over time. I didn't make money over the past three months, let's close shop. That's when you lose the original of the ecosystem."
