Coinbase joined Robinhood this week in giving agents access to their platforms, turning buried research and data into usable investing flows for users who rarely have time to read full reports.

Investing is a full time job.

But now, instead of users manually stitching together trading, research, etc. these agents handle the entire investing loop (from research to monitoring), letting users take full advantage of all a platform has to offer.

For the platforms, this drives more volume and more people wanting to access the premium data reserved for paying subscribers which could inform their agents.
