🇺🇸 Treasury Secretary Bessent says the weak jobs report is hiding a stronger economy underneath

His case, tied to a fresh Treasury graphic: look past the headline number and the fundamentals are solid.

The three signals:

105K goods-producing jobs so far this year, the best 7-month start since 2023 and a 5th straight month of growth
Productivity up 2.2% in Q2, more than double what was expected
Atlanta Fed's GDPNow projecting 5.8% Q3 growth

His argument is that this is durable supply-side strength, factories producing and workers getting more productive, rather than a "temporary sugar high." Real numbers behind a real bet that the economy is set to accelerate.
