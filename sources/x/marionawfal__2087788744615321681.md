🇺🇸 3 people vanished into the Michigan woods after a simple river float ended with shredded rafts and no way out.

Their inflatables got ripped open by submerged logs on the Muskegon River, cell phones died, and for three full days they wandered barefoot through thick brush, surviving on tree bark and wildflowers.

Rescuers finally locked onto a faint yell and found them scratched to hell, one of the women with legs so cut up it looked painful just to walk.

The little boy got carried out on a searcher’s back.

It’s the kind of story that reminds you how fast the outdoors can flip from fun to survival mode, and how lucky they were that someone kept looking.
