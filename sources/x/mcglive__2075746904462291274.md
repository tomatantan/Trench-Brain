We asked @RickCrosschain & @0xDaes how @ggdotxyz plans to compete with other terminals and trading applications.

They believe it boils down to who gets paid (rewarded).

The model: a 1% trading fee, split 50/50 with creators. Post a thesis, drive volume through it, and half the fees come back to you. Referrals earn another 10-15%.

They are framing themselves as a social trading network built around attribution, getting incentives to the right people instead of letting creators generate volume for free.

No token yet but one is on the way.
