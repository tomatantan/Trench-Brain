"We're a structured finance company putting on a carry trade. Carry trades are not risk free, and they're not cost free. We're underwriting a multi decade investment opportunity around Bitcoin"

Matt Cole on why Strategy raising cash in a bear market actually makes sense

"Some of the critiques we hear are focused on, is a single transaction accretive? Is it dilutive? Is the strategy broken? But when you think about any long term carry trade, there's costs and there's risk to these things"

"When I see things happening like Strategy raising cash, to me it makes sense. In the depths of a bear market, a common equity designed to trade and behave like amplified Bitcoin exposure is not magic. It's just structured finance, and that amplification amplifies returns to the downside and the upside"

"We're underwriting a long term bull thesis around Bitcoin. The company is taking actions to support its long term health, whether it's Strategy or Strive. Right now you're seeing both fund operations primarily through issuance of common equity. That's a good thing"
