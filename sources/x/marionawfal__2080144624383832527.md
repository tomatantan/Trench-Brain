🇦🇷 🇺🇸 Argentina's football body is fighting a story that won't die: did the FBI really detain its president at JFK?

Argentine outlets reported that FBI agents held AFA president Claudio "Chiqui" Tapia and other officials at New York's JFK airport on Monday, seized phones and computers, and delayed the World Cup delegation's charter flight home by roughly two hours, with Tapia summoned to a Florida federal court on July 30.

The AFA calls that version false.

In a statement, it said it is "completely untrue" that Tapia or treasurer Pablo Toviggino were summoned to testify or had any devices seized, insisting no personal legal measure was imposed on either man.

What both sides agree on is narrower and still serious. 

A U.S. grand jury has summoned a third party who may give evidence about AFA figures, part of a probe into more than $300 million in association-linked commercial contracts.

The dispute now is over who exactly got caught in it.
