🇮🇪🇦🇪🇺🇸 Kinahan cartel boss is back in Ireland to face trial.

Daniel Kinahan, long believed to run one of Europe’s most powerful crime networks, has been handed over from Dubai after a multi-year international chase by Irish, European and U.S. forces.

His group is blamed for flooding the continent with cocaine, partnering with Colombian and Mexican cartels, the Italian Mafia, and even alleged links to Hezbollah financing plus shielding an Iran-tied assassin.

The family fortune is pegged at over €1 billion by Irish police.
