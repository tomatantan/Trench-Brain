🚨🇾🇪  🇸🇦The Houthis promise "escalation with escalation."

-The Houthi foreign ministry says the Saudi airstrikes on Hodeidah mark the start of a new phase in which "escalation with escalation" will prevail

-Its framing of the strikes: "Instead of complying with the legitimate and just demand to lift the blockade on Yemen, the Saudi regime committed a major folly that will cost it dearly"

-The ministry says a separate Saudi attack hit Kamaran Island, injuring a woman, while Houthi-affiliated Al Masirah claims air defenses "forced several Saudi combat formations to leave the airspace"

-The targets in Hodeidah included Telecommunications Corporation facilities, per the Houthi statement, while the Saudi-led coalition insists it hit only sites "linked to threats against commercial shipping" and left the port itself untouched

-It comes as the Houthi naval blockade of Saudi Arabia enters its enforcement phase, with two tankers already struck and Brent having crossed $100
