Trade relative to your own port size

A whale throwing 5 SOL into a low cap to front run a narrative might only be risking 1% of their port

A smaller trader sees that and thinks they need to be doing the same

But for them, 5 SOL might be 20-40% of everything they have

It is a completely different game

The whale can be wrong 10 times and still be fine

The smaller trader gets a few wrong and they’re basically starting again

But the biggest difference is not even the amount

It’s how the trade feels

When the money matters more to you, you don’t think the same

You hesitate on good exits
You panic on normal pullbacks
You hold losers because you need them to come back
You cut winners because you’re scared of losing what you finally made

The person with the bigger port can treat it like a calculated bet

The person with the smaller port starts treating it like life and death

You stop trading the narrative and start trading your emotions

That is where everything goes wrong
