🇺🇦🇷🇺 Ukraine has knocked out 42% of Russia's refining capacity, and nobody else this year comes close

A map of every major refinery struck worldwide since January shows how lopsided this is. 

Ukraine has hit 16 Russian refineries, several of them repeatedly, with Syzran alone struck five separate times. 

The US and Israel have hit two in Iran, Iran has hit six across Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Kuwait and Israel, and the Houthis keep returning to Jazan.

Russia is rationing fuel now and importing refined products while discounting its own crude, with the Moscow refinery possibly offline until 2027.

The reason Kyiv can keep swinging this freely is that all six of its own major refineries were destroyed before the year even started, leaving Moscow everything to lose in an exchange where Ukraine has nothing.
