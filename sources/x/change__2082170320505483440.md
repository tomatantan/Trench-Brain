Neha Patadia's husband died in 2025 at age 55. After his death, she received a $255 lump-sum payment from Social Security, the same amount the benefit has been capped at since 1954. "Coping with this loss was harrowing; however, an added blow was the meager $255 lump-sum death benefit offered by Social Security," she writes.

Patadia has launched a petition calling on Congress to raise the death benefit to $2,900. A Senate bill reintroduced in December 2025 would do that, but it is still awaiting action in committee.
