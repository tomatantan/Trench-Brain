🇺🇸🇵🇸 Trump's Board of Peace was created in January to rebuild Gaza. Its first actual construction contract, 7 months later: a 150-person military outpost. 

Not for civilians. For Moroccan troops.

The base is tents, cots, chemical toilets, handwashing stations; that's the board's own language for "habitable." 100 by 120 meters, a mile from the Israeli border, with a quick extraction route built in case it comes under attack.

Kushner's original pitch at the first board meeting was a waterfront "New Gaza" with gleaming towers. This is what got built first.

80% of Gaza's buildings were damaged or destroyed. Ten months after the ceasefire, most of the 2 million people there are still in unsanitary makeshift camps.

This base was supposed to be phase one of a 5,000-person international force. Nobody's confirmed when the rest actually deploys, or which countries beyond Morocco are even committing troops.

The board says more contracts are "in the final stages." None of them are for the people still living in tents.

When the first thing built in Gaza is housing for the peacekeepers instead of the people who need peace, what does that say about the order of priorities?
