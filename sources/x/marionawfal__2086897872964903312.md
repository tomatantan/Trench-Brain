🇺🇸🇬🇧 The U.S. embassy in London is dropping grants up to half a million bucks, and Brits are calling it meddling.

The money goes to "public education" projects about free speech, small government, and rule of law, timed to next year's 70th anniversary of the Magna Carta memorial.

Critics think Washington's buying influence. One MP called it "Trump's Maga money meddling in our democracy." Some U.S. officials aren't comfortable with it either.

The embassy says relax, it's just about U.S.-UK ties, and nobody whined when Biden's grants paid for climate and LGBTQ stuff.

Then there's a second grant that straight up asks UK groups to drum up "support for Israel's right to defend itself." Even a former official said that one's weird for a foreign country.

So either it's cash for free speech or cash to nudge British opinion. Take your pick.
