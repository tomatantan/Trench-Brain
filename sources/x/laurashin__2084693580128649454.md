Tuongvy Le on the legal gray area around hardware wallet exploits

"I find this incident really fascinating because I'm sure the three of us have been thinking about things like, what duty does a hardware wallet manufacturer owe to its users? If there's a defect in the firmware that makes users less secure than they were promised, what legal framework even applies here? Are we talking about negligence, product liability, consumer protection laws, breach of warranty, or something else? These naturally came to mind for me as a lawyer"

"The other issue is disclosure. There are conflicting reports coming out, but if Coldcard had become aware of this flaw, does it have a duty to tell users? And how do you communicate that risk?"

"Once the flaw exists, it might not be enough to just update the software. You might need to actually recall the devices and require users to migrate their assets to entirely new wallets"
