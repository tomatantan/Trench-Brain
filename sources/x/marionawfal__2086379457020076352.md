🇮🇪🇬🇧 The New IRA has apparently found a new enemy: migrants

Intelligence officials reportedly fear they're preparing a campaign of violent intimidation aimed at driving migrants out of Ireland, exploiting growing anger over migration and crime on both sides of the border.

The group still wants a united Ireland, naturally. But apparently overthrowing British rule wasn't enough of a workload, so "all migrants out" has been added to the revolutionary to-do list.

Security sources say factions within dissident republicanism may even be competing over who can take the harder line, raising fears that anti-migrant sentiment could become fuel for renewed political violence.

Ireland spent decades trying to bury paramilitary politics.

It would be quite the historical achievement to dig it back up over immigration.
