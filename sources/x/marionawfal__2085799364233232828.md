🇺🇦🇺🇸 Ukraine keeps asking Elon to turn Starlink into a weapon, and he keeps refusing to escalate the war

Kyiv wants Elon to expand Starlink's military role and help target Russian missile launchers. His answer: no, because it risks blowing the war wide open, and "it's time to make a deal."

Ukraine can fight without him, but not the way it wants to here. And on this, Elon's answer is no.
