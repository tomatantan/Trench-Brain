i like y22 a lot and have a ton of respect for him, but i think this is totally backwards.

you buy a token because you believe the product will win. you don’t decide which product you want to win because it happens to have a token you can buy.

a liquid proxy isn’t free upside. you buy it at a market price, take the downside risk, and it still isn’t the same as equity.

as a trader, you can already capture fomo’s value through the edge its social graph gives for example. not every product needs to tokenize its value for users to benefit from it.

and the irony is that once you buy $PUMP, you become one of the financially interested parties described in the last paragraph.

the product should determine the investment, not the investment determine which product you root for.
