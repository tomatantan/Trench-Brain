🇾🇪🇸🇦 The Houthis just set a new red line inside Yemen: any Saudi troop movement will be treated as a target.

Military presence or transfers of Saudi-linked forces, in either direction, can now expect direct attacks.

The aim is to cut off those units from one another and prevent them from shifting between provinces.

Yesterday, Houthis launched coordinated missile and drone strikes in Yemen and southern Saudi Arabia. 

The attacks killed dozens of Saudi-backed Yemeni government troops in Marib and Hadramou, although the Houthis claimed to have killed many more.
