🇪🇸 Spain just learned that its border isn't really its own to control.

Sixty thousand people crossed into a city of eighty three thousand in a single day. 

Fifty seven died in the water and at the fence. 

Ceuta's hospitals, shelters and police were overwhelmed within hours, and by Friday most of the arrivals had turned around and walked back, having discovered that nothing waited for them on the other side.

Madrid is taking the blame, and some of it belongs there. 

But a fence has two sides, and Morocco has spent years easing its patrols whenever it wants something from Spain, most memorably in 2021 when ten thousand crossed weeks after Madrid hospitalized a Western Sahara leader Rabat despises. 

Sánchez visited Algiers days before this surge.

Which leaves Spain with the uncomfortable truth every European capital should be studying: a border you cannot police alone is a lever in someone else's hand.
