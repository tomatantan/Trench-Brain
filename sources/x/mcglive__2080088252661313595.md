How does @longdotxyz work, and where is the launchpad headed?

The user flow: it's a familiar launcher experience, but instead of pairing to SOL or USDC, you pick a stock from the list, and your token pairs to it. LONG curates a list of the most liquid, battle-tested stock assets that can handle high volume. From there, communities build their own story and lore around the pairing.

The bigger vision he floated while pitching to vc's: why couldn't a solo AI dev building on Nvidia or Anthropic's infrastructure also build on their liquidity layer, pairing their project to that stock? As he put it, these assets are becoming as big as fiat currencies.

For now, the focus is on pushing stock-pairing infrastructure to the limit and building the community.
