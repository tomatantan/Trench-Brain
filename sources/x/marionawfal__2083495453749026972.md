🇾🇪🇸🇦 The Houthis don't have to sink Saudi's ships, they just have to scare them away

At least six Saudi-owned oil tankers have reportedly ditched the Red Sea and started the much longer voyage around Africa after Houthi threats against vessels linked to Saudi ports.

The detour adds roughly two weeks to every trip, driving up costs and tying up valuable tanker capacity. That's the uncomfortable reality of asymmetric warfare: you don't have to close a shipping lane if you can make insurers, operators, and captains decide it's not worth the gamble.
