🇺🇸 PJM, the largest U.S. power grid serving 67M people across the Mid-Atlantic, South, and D.C., just hit emergency mode during this heat wave.

They're activating load reductions on big customers (including some data centers with backup generators) while generator outages and overloaded lines add pressure.

Demand nearly broke all-time records this week, pushing prices over $2,000/MWh in northern Virginia.

A Hot Weather Alert runs through at least July 4.

Stay cool and maybe ease up on the AC if you can.
