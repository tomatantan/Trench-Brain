Advertisement Cover image via depositphotos.com Institutional Zcash miner Fortitude is significantly expanding its mining operations after signing a definitive agreement with Bitmain to purchase 9,000 Antminer Z15 Pro ASIC miners, one of the industry's leading machines for the Equihash algorithm. Advertisement According to the company, the new hardware is expected to add roughly 7.56 GSol/s of Equihash hashrate once fully deployed, marking one of the largest publicly disclosed investments in Zcash mining infrastructure this year. The miners will be shipped in two batches, with 3,000 units scheduled for delivery in October and the remaining 6,000 in November. Fortitude said it committed approximately $31.5 million to the purchase, paying $3,499 per unit.
