Buyers are climbing, the crowd is leaving. A rising price while holder counts fall usually means stronger hands are absorbing the supply that weaker ones are dumping, and retail hasn&#x27;t yet chased the move. The setup cuts both ways. Fewer holders soaking up a price move is a sign of conviction from whoever is buying, but it also means the rally is thin. If retail returns and holder counts turn back up, it confirms the move. If the price keeps climbing and wallets keep falling, it is a smaller group holding up the tape. There&#x27;s actual ecosystem work behind it , not just a bounce.
