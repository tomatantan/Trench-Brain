It buys digital assets from, or sells them to, third-party liquidity providers before transferring the assets to or from member accounts. SoFi books money from member buys and sells in its gross crypto revenue line, along with transaction fees after rewards. Most of that money flows straight back out to cover the assets SoFi buys for members and the payments tied to member sales.
