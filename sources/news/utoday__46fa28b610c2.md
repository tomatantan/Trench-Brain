According to ESMA's interim register, out of approximately 23 authorized issuers of electronic money tokens (EMTs), only nine companies have obtained full authorization covering both EMT issuance and CASP services. The remaining 14 players have found themselves caught in a regulatory trap. "Most stablecoin issuers in the EU cannot provide custody of their own tokens on behalf of clients.
