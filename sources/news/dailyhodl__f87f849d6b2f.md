This Private Information included names and Social Security numbers. The Defendant denies all of the Plaintiffs’ claims and maintains that it did not do anything wrong.&#8221; Claims must be filed by October 28, 2026, with opt-outs due by September 28, 2026. The final approval hearing is set for November 16, 2026. The company denies wrongdoing but settled to avoid further litigation costs.
