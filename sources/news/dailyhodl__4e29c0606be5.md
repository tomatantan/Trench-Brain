Galaxy Research says Bitcoin now trades at $78,114, already over 32% above the June 30th low, while the 50-week moving average (MA) sits at $82,470, about 6.4% overhead. The firm tested every completed Bitcoin bear market since 2011 using a strict mechanical rule of at least 50% drawdown lasting 90 days or more. &#8220;11 of 13 times BTCUSD reclaimed the 50w MA during completed bear markets, the bear-market low was in. It currently sits around $82k. If BTCUSD reclaims it on weekly close, history suggests the bear market would likely be over.&#8221; Only two of those 13 reclaims failed, both during the 2021-22 period before Bitcoin dropped to $15,758. In contrast, the faster 50-day moving average proved much noisier, with 43 of 106 reclaims failing and leading to new lows. &#8220;A weekly reclaim of 50w MA could be strong confirmation that the current cycle low is in. Until then, the daily reclaim is encouraging but not conclusive.&#8221; The analysis adds that even successful 50-week signals can experience whipsaws, yet Bitcoin never printed a new bear-market low after those events. &#8216;The May 2019 reclaim gave way to crossings around the COVID crash, but BTC never made a new 2017–18 bear-market low. The May 2020 reclaim then held for 427 days.&#8221; Reclaiming the 50-week average on a weekly close could provide a strong historical confirmation that the bear market low is in.
