That distinction matters because the minutes cover a group wider than the formal vote. All Reserve Bank presidents participate in FOMC discussions, while only 12 committee members vote. The document can therefore show broader hawkish sentiment without establishing that additional voters were ready to raise rates. For Bitcoin, the stronger signal from the Bitcoin Fed minutes would be whether Treasury yields rise as traders price a higher-for-longer path. The official Aug. 18 curve put the two-year yield at 4.19% and the 10-year at 4.71% , while Bitcoin traded near $64,400 on Coinbase at 9:51 p.m. ET Tuesday. Limited positioning readings did not establish a marketwide cushion. At 9:52 p.m. ET Tuesday, an OKX snapshot showed positive BTC perpetual funding and roughly 32,900 BTC of open interest, both single-venue measures.
