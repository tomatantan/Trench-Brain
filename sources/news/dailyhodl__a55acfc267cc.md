Company officials quickly altered passwords, blocked remote connections and engaged cybersecurity specialists to investigate and secure the environment. Operations returned to normal shortly afterward, though certain data files could have been accessed by the unauthorized party. The breach was reported to Massachusetts officials on August 7th. A law firm is examining the incident for potential legal claims on behalf of impacted individuals seeking recovery for related costs and losses.
