He has stated that "total safety" does not actually exist.&nbsp;&nbsp; Advertisement Gauthier claims that hardware wallet makers must design systems that remain safe even when people make mistakes. Recently, the hardware wallet ecosystem was hit hard by the Coldcard debacle. On July 30, Coinkite warned that Bitcoin wallets generated by affected Coldcard devices could be vulnerable because of an entropy problem in the process of seed generation. Losses had already exceeded 1,000 BTC by July 31, and he warned that affected seeds should be treated as compromised.
