That concentration turns the current range into a break-even zone where a large group of holders keeps changing its mind about selling. Long-term holder supply fell by about 210,000 BTC from its July 29 peak, the first weekly decline of 2026 and the steepest two-week drop since December 2024. Bitfinex's long-term holder profitability measure printed 0.86, 0.90 and 0.86 across Aug. 9 through 11, implying coins moving from that cohort were sold at a 10% to 14% loss. The selling looks concentrated among the youngest long-term holders, whose average acquisition cost sits closer to $71,000 to $76,000.
