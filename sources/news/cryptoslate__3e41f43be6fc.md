Greg Abbott ordered the Public Utility Commission and ERCOT to audit every data center project seeking a grid connection before regulators approve any new projects. The order puts AI infrastructure tied to Bitcoin miners under scrutiny, while the audit verifies each project's power sourcing, water use, cooling plans, and ownership. The order covers roughly 474 GW of pending connection requests, and Abbott said about 90% of that demand comes from data centers. That figure runs more than five times the grid's record peak load. ERCOT paused its Batch Zero transmission-planning study once the order landed, and a separate PUCT survey shows that only 28 of 377 companies responded when asked about their data center plans. Public Bitcoin miners have spent the past year pitching investors on multi-gigawatt AI infrastructure pipelines . Those numbers can bundle very different assets under one label , such as raw land near a substation, a queue position still waiting on interconnection studies, and a financed campus already under construction. Texas just forced a test of that distinction, and FERC is building a matching one at the national level. River Bend sits in Louisiana, outside the Texas order entirely. Beacon Point sits in Texas, fully commercialized across roughly 1 GW, and is financed with $4.25 billion in secured notes. It also comes with signed tenants, dedicated financing and construction already underway, a level of de-risking most queue-stage developers cannot match. Alongside Hut 8's financed sites, the company reports a roughly 8.66 GW pipeline that still includes capacity under diligence and exclusivity — the less mature rungs of the same ladder. IREN energized its 1.4 GW Sweetwater 1 site in May, and its existing Childress capacity already backs signed contracts with Microsoft and Nvidia . The company's filings still warn that ERCOT's batch review could affect the timing of Sweetwater's next phase . About 700 MW of HPC capacity is under Cipher Digital &#8216;s contract, while roughly 2 GW of its Texas pipeline, including the McLennan, Colchis and Mikeska sites, remains subject to that batch process . MARA's operating fleet and behind-the-meter power strategy give it a base outside any single approval path. Its proposed 2 GW Matagorda project relies on a different structure , with milestone payments tied to regulatory approval , power authorization and a signed tenant lease. CleanSpark has a contracted project in Georgia entirely outside ERCOT's reach. Its Texas sites, 285 MW near Houston and up to 600 MW near Brazoria County, still depend on approvals and power availability that have not yet been secured. Riot Platforms holds a 700 MW interconnection at Rockdale and an AMD lease that starts at 25 MW, with room to expand toward 200 MW. Its larger Corsicana plan, roughly 600 MW, is still at an earlier stage . Core Scientific operates about 300 MW of mining capacity at its Pecos campus today, while its plan to expand that site to 1.5 GW rests on power and leases that don't yet exist .
