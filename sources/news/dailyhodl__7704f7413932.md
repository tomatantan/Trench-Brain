Instead, the exposure stemmed from Claude&#8217;s conversation-sharing feature, which lets users generate public links to individual chats. Many users were unaware that those publicly shared conversations could be indexed by search engines, making them discoverable through targeted online searches. Discovery occurred when people applied a site-specific search operator to claude.ai/share directories, revealing hundreds of conversations across multiple pages. Some indexed threads contained sensitive details such as personal health information, company documents and cryptocurrency passwords that users had not meant for broad exposure. Source: AOTF Users on Reddit also said some of the publicly accessible conversations included adult material in addition to discussions about cybersecurity. Several commenters noted that while the pages disappeared from Google search results after the issue was addressed, similar searches briefly continued to surface the conversations on other search engines, including Bing and Brave. The indexed Claude chat pages have since been removed from Google, and the search queries that previously exposed them no longer return those conversations.
