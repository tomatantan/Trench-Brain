Shelia Williams, a 70-year-old resident of Golden, Mississippi, faces multiple forgery and theft charges, as well as a charge of financial exploitation of an elderly person, following an investigation by the Franklin County Sheriff&#8217;s Office in Alabama, reports the NBC and ABC affiliate WTVA. The case began in late June of this year after the elderly victim discovered that approximately $18,206 had disappeared from a bank account and reported the suspected theft to authorities. According to investigators, Williams took five checks from the victim&#8217;s vehicle without permission and forged the victim&#8217;s signature on them. Authorities say Williams admitted to the scheme during an interview with investigators and told them she spent the stolen money on personal expenses. The victim&#8217;s bank later reimbursed the full amount that had been taken, according to the sheriff&#8217;s office. Authorities have set a $20,000 bond for Williams. Following the arrest, the Franklin County Sheriff&#8217;s Office urged the public to regularly monitor their bank accounts, keep checkbooks secure and report suspicious financial activity to their bank or local law enforcement.
