Autheo confirmed that THEO, the native utility token powering its Internet Operating System, is now live on Hydrex, the Base-native Omni-Liquidity MetaDEX. THEO entered the public market at a reference price of $0.05 against USDC, with Enflux serving as official market maker. Unlike many blockchain projects that enter the public markets before meaningful adoption, THEO launches into an operational ecosystem — powering Autheo’s Internet Operating System, with Mainnet secured by 125 validators. TOKENOMICS THEO has a maximum supply of 7.0 billion tokens, with approximately 5.33% designated as initial circulating supply at TGE — for an initial market capitalization of approximately $18.66 million at the $0.05 reference price. The substantial majority of this allocation is designated for liquidity, with most of that liquidity locked or otherwise unavailable for trading. The fully diluted valuation of $350 million represents the theoretical value if all tokens were circulating, but emissions are intentionally distributed over a 10-year period, introducing supply very gradually. The model was developed over several years, with contributions from two Ph.D.-level tokenomics researchers, to support sustainable network economics and align long-term incentives. THEO UTILITY THEO powers the economics of the Autheo Internet Operating System — the native utility and coordination asset connecting users, developers, infrastructure providers, and validators. It pays for compute, storage, networking, messaging, AI services, containers, and other decentralized infrastructure, while rewarding the providers that contribute resources to the mesh network. THEO also supports Validator and Infrastructure Node participation, staking, network security, governance, transaction fees, and provider incentives. As applications and enterprises consume resources, THEO provides the economic mechanism through which demand is measured, paid for, and distributed to participants providing and securing the infrastructure.
