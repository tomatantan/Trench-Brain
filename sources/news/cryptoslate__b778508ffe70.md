Make CryptoSlate preferred on Share Share The Cardano Foundation announced a partnership with Reef Data eG on July 28, backed by Hamburger Volksbank, ARIC, VALIO, and Disruptive Elements. Reef plans to pool members' personal data, license it to businesses, and pay royalties back to contributors. Cardano's Reeve system sits at the accounting layer, recording payment flows and royalty distributions, and the underlying data stays elsewhere. The European Commission Joint Research Centre identifies six models of data intermediary : personal-information management systems, data cooperatives, data trusts, data unions, data marketplaces and data-sharing pools. The EU Data Governance Act gives that market a regulatory backbone , requiring recognized providers to register with national authorities and appear in a public list. That list was updated on July 28 and names 36 providers across 10 countries, with Germany's only entry being Law & Innovation Technology GmbH, trading as Consenter. Whether Reef intends to seek a listing of its own is an open question the cooperative has yet to answer publicly. It operates as a nonprofit and centers participation in research over payment. Swash rewards users for permitted browsing activity , paying out in cash, gift cards or crypto whenever a business buys the resulting data, insights or advertising. DataVaults, an EU-funded project spanning 17 partners in nine countries , built personal data hubs years before this week's Cardano announcement. The hubs let contributors control sharing and receive compensation directly. Spain's SalusCoop asks citizens to conditionally donate health data for research , prioritizing collective and public benefit over individual royalties. Each project encodes a different theory of what a data company owes its contributors: research access, consumer reward, infrastructure experiment, civic donation. Reef pushes further into commercial territory, selling datasets to buyers and distributing royalties under published, auditable rules. Reef's published workflow says members request their own information from companies under the EU Data Act. The Data Act, applicable since September 2025 , centers on data generated by connected products such as cars, smart devices and industrial machinery. GDPR Article 15 gives people a right to access data companies hold on them, and Article 20 covers portability in a machine-readable format. The distinction decides whether Reef is executing a right the Data Act just created or repackaging a right GDPR already gave consumers years earlier. Keeping personal data off the chain Reef's app keeps personal information off Cardano at the source, reviewing and anonymizing each member's data on their phone, and only the member's own choice sends anything into the cooperative's pool. From there, Reeve logs the sale, timestamps the royalty payment, and anchors the record cryptographically, so members and auditors can verify the distribution followed the published rules. The workflow shows personal data remaining off-chain while Cardano records dataset sales, timestamps, royalty calculations and verification. That architecture answers a question the European Data Protection Board raised just weeks earlier, when it adopted final guidance on blockchain technologies on July 7. The guidance warns that immutable ledgers sit awkwardly against GDPR rights such as erasure and correction, and it recommends that companies keep personal data off-chain wherever possible. Reeve's own privacy policy describes it as a financial-data integrity and reporting tool for transaction records, a scope Reef's design already respects. Whether by foresight or convenience, Reef built its architecture inside the boundary regulators just drew. One caveat complicates the on-device anonymization claim, as EU data-protection authorities separate true anonymization, which removes data from GDPR's scope entirely, from pseudonymization.
