The company's quarterly filing shows that the new money sits inside $750 million of fully drawn facilities entered into on Aug. 4. Coinbase provided a $450 million facility, including $300 million of new borrowing and the refinancing of an existing $150 million loan. Two Prime provided a separate $300 million loan. MARA said the proceeds can fund general corporate purposes, including part of the cash consideration for Long Ridge.
