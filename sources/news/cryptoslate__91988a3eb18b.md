SGP-0003 ties burns to resource use SGP-0003 rewires the fee split. Today, each signature carries a 5,000-lamport base fee, half burned and half paid to the block leader. The proposal would replace that with a 2,500-lamport inclusion fee paid entirely to the leader and a separate, usage-based resource fee burned in full. Priority fees would keep flowing to the leader. The resource charge would step through 0.1, 0.25 and 0.5 lamport per requested cost unit. Using May 2026 network data and the stated throughput, the authors estimate daily burns of 1,500 to 1,800 SOL at the first rate, then 7,500 to 9,000 SOL at the terminal rate.
