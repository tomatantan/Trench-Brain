An investigation by the City of Jacksonville Office of Inspector General uncovered the scheme after complaints from residents who had handed over the payments specifically for rent. Officials traced at least $10,625 in suspected to have been misdirected from the residents to Grant&#8217;s accounts. &#8220;Further analysis by the Jacksonville Sheriff’s Office (JSO) revealed that between May 5, 2021, and April 8, 2022, GRANT stole over $10,000 by receiving residents’ rent money orders, depositing them into her financial account, and transferring funds into her PayPal account. The investigation traced and verified at least $10,625.50 in stolen money orders deposited into Grant’s accounts.&#8221; The State Attorney’s Office of the Fourth Judicial Circuit filed charges of exploitation of an aged adult in the $10,000 to $50,000 range. Jacksonville Sheriff’s Office deputies made the arrest in June, and the housing authority later fired the employee for theft while pursuing full repayment and a prison sentence. Residents confirmed to investigators that the payments were intended solely for their housing obligations.
