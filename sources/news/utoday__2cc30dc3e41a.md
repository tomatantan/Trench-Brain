According to him, Ledger&rsquo;s implementation enables &ldquo;clear signing&rdquo; for every spending path, allowing users to verify exactly what they are approving on their hardware wallet. For developers, Miniscript makes it easier to build custom wallet software, Guillemet said. Advertisement He claimed he recently created a functional Bitcoin wallet supporting advanced spending policies in just a few hours with the help of AI assistant Claude. He had added that Ledger&rsquo;s clear-signing approach made him comfortable testing the wallet on Bitcoin mainnet. He also pointed to MuSig2, a cryptographic alternative to traditional script-based multisig.&nbsp; Guillemet said Ledger hardware wallets are, to his knowledge, the only devices currently supporting MuSig2.
