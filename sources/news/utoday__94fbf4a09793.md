That put the BIP-110 branch 48 blocks behind the standard chain. Advertisement Bitcoin's normal block-production target is approximately one block every 10 minutes. BIP-110 is meant to restrict the amount and types of arbitrary non-financial data that can be embedded in Bitcoin transactions. The proposal was authored under the pseudonym Dathon Ohm, with Luke Dashjr, a longtime Bitcoin developer and maintainer associated with Bitcoin Knots, being its main cheerleader.&nbsp; Its supporters argue that Bitcoin was designed as a peer-to-peer monetary system, so it does not have to function as a general-purpose data-storage system.&nbsp; However, Bitcoin Core developers argued that restrictive policies were ineffective because data could find alternative routes into blocks.
