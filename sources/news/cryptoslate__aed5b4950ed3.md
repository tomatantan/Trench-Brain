Make CryptoSlate preferred on Share Share Solstice announced a tranched product built on Strategy's Bitcoin-related STRC preferred stock, and said its senior tranche would not begin absorbing losses until STRC trades below $47.66. STRC currently trades near $95.315, putting that threshold roughly half of today's market price and about 52% below the security's $100 par value. Solstice COO David Plisek described the $47.66 level as a modeled senior-impairment threshold under the protocol's current coverage structure and assumptions, both of which the team can revise. How Bitcoin holds the line Strategy has spent the past six weeks actively managing STRC's price. The company announced a Digital Credit Capital Framework on June 29 , combining a dollar reserve policy, a revised dividend structure, and repurchase authorizations, with an explicit goal of keeping STRC trading between $99 and $100. Between July 20 and July 26, Strategy repurchased 288,930 STRC shares for about $25 million at an average price near $86.52. In early August, the company sold $108.6 million worth of Bitcoin and used the proceeds to repurchase 1,152,020 STRC shares , on top of a $4.65 billion dollar-denominated reserve reported as of Aug. 9. Solstice built its risk model on that policy, and its documentation lists any move away from it among the named risks. That 50/50 split produces the 200% senior coverage ratio. Junior absorbs realized losses first, in exchange for a yield target well above the senior side's roughly 7% APY. STRC's stated 12% annual dividend implies close to a 12.59% Bitcoin -fueled yield on its current price before Solstice's tranching and fees split that spread between the two sides. Plisek framed the tranching as a way to give investors a NAV-based return on STRC, separate from exposure to the security's price swings. The product provides structured exposure to STRC's economics, a claim on the security's performance that stops short of share ownership. STRC bottomed near $73.62 during that stretch, still about 35% above the current $47.66 threshold. In that retrospective model, senior stayed unimpaired regardless of what junior holders did. Junior's outcome depended entirely on senior behavior. Plisek said junior would have remained unimpaired as well if senior holders had stayed invested through the drawdown. If every senior holder had redeemed during that window, junior would have taken a roughly 50% drawdown. A price drawdown becomes a realized loss only when redemptions force the structure to sell the underlying position. Senior holders heading for the exit at the same time as a decline trigger junior losses. What happens if selling Bitcoin is not enough and STRC keeps falling Solstice has built-in stages before a senior ever takes a loss. Once STRC trades below a specified threshold, the protocol enters a restricted mode that halts junior redemptions and new senior minting simultaneously.
