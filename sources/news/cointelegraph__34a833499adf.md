Sam Blackshear, the co-founder and chief technology officer of Mysten Labs, has announced he will be stepping down to join Anthropic to work on defensive security research. Blackshear, along with four former Meta executives, founded Mysten Labs — the creator of the Sui blockchain — in September 2021. Blackshear said Mysten Labs co-founder and CEO Evan Cheng will step in to set the “technical vision” for Mysten going forward. “When I reflect on my career, I have been happiest doing hands-on technical work while exploring new problem domains,” said Blackshear. “This is an opportunity to do both, and I’m motivated to work on security at a time when the balance of power between attackers and defenders is shifting.” His departure comes as AI is increasingly being used to identify software vulnerabilities, automate phishing campaigns and accelerate attacks on blockchain infrastructure . AI arms race is “getting scary:” Blackshear In April, Blackshear hosted a panel with security experts about how AI coding agents are reshaping smart contract security. He said for him, a turning point that led him to realize that something had fundamentally changed was when he ported a static analysis tool he built at Facebook written in OCaml-Java over to Move, the programming language used to build smart contracts on Sui. Sam said he used Claude to make the move and flag any potential vulnerabilities, and “it just did it,” he said. “And then when I saw the results, I was like, ‘whoa,’ this is a new world that we’re in terms of like the speed and the capabilities.” “The triage results was completely manual before, and the coding would have taken a long, long time,” he added.
