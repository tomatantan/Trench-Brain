Officials say the woman withheld necessary physical care from the individuals between August and December 2025 and submitted false claims that resulted in theft of more than $10,000 from the Medicaid program on at least two occasions, reports Statesman Journal. The woman was charged with multiple counts of criminal mistreatment in the first degree, making a false claim for health care payment and aggravated theft in the first degree, says Oregon Attorney General Dan Rayfield. She was arrested July 29th by Salem Police detectives and remains held in the Marion County Jail. Court records indicate she is prohibited from contacting the two individuals involved in the case. The woman is scheduled for arraignment on August 12th. An adult protective services worker at NorthWest Senior and Disability Services first reported the allegations to investigators. This case forms part of a greater statewide effort in which Oregon authorities have now charged six defendants across four similar investigations involving patient neglect and false billing to Medicaid. Authorities said the investigations originated from referrals made by county officials, adult protective services workers and organizations serving communities throughout Oregon.
