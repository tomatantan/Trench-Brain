According to polling conducted by Democratic research firm Normington Petts and obtained by Semafor, 84% of Democratic primary voters hold an unfavorable view of candidates backed by the cryptocurrency industry. The survey also found that crypto is viewed more negatively than oil companies, Wall Street banks and data centers by Democratic voters, highlighting the political headwinds facing lawmakers who support the industry. The poll comes at a particularly sensitive moment for the crypto sector, which has made passage of the Clarity Act its top legislative priority.
