Covered activity includes a single occurrence of exchanging, transferring or storing a digital asset as part of a business or on behalf of a customer who agreed to receive those services. The rule does not make every direct self-custody transfer a broker-collected event; the taxable transaction requires activity supplied by a digital asset broker for valuable consideration. For a remote broker headquartered outside Illinois, the collection nexus applies when gross receipts from covered digital asset business activity sales to Illinois customers reach at least $100,000 over the preceding 12 months. Illinois requires the test quarterly. Once the threshold is met, the broker is treated as maintaining a place of business in the state and must collect, remit and file returns for one year. Registration requirements are addressed separately.
