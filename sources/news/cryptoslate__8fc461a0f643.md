AI absorbs most of the investment budget SpaceX directed $15.828 billion into AI infrastructure during the second quarter, more than 21 times the $749 million spent a year earlier and about double the amount deployed in the first quarter. The AI outlay accounted for 86% of SpaceX’s $18.369 billion in total quarterly capital expenditure. It also exceeded Wall Street’s $13.09 billion estimate for AI spending , even as overall capex came in slightly below the $18.58 billion expected. Across the first half, AI capital expenditure climbed to $23.551 billion from $3.316 billion a year earlier. That represented nearly 83% of SpaceX’s total $28.476 billion investment during the period. The spending supports SpaceX’s effort to build large-scale data centers , lease high-demand computing capacity to major technology companies and develop its own AI models and coding products. That investment has already begun generating commercial returns. AI revenue reached $2.561 billion during the quarter, driven by computing agreements with customers including Google and Anthropic , alongside revenue from Grok and X subscriptions.
