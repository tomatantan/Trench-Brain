Shiba Inu's acceleration is yet to complete According to the latest on-chain metrics, the Exchange Outflow (MA7) indicator climbed to approximately 630.8 billion SHIB, representing a 5.9% daily increase and roughly a 62% acceleration compared to the levels seen just hours earlier. Rising outflows are generally interpreted as a reduction in immediately available selling supply, since tokens transferred to private wallets are less likely to be sold in the near term.
