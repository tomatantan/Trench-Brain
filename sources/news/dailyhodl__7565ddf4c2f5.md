Maryjean “Mary” Victoria Small, 26, worked through Concierge Care as an independent contractor for the victim in St. Johns County from early 2025 until mid-2026, reports News4JAX. She now faces seven felony charges that include theft of $50,000 or more from a person 65 or older, defrauding a person 65 or older of $50,000 or more, illegal use of credit cards, exploitation of an elderly person exceeding $50,000, use of a two-way communication device to facilitate a felony, uttering a false check or bank instrument, and fraudulent use of identification without consent with a victim 60 or older. Authorities accuse Small of depositing 63 unauthorized checks worth more than $82,000 into her own account and moving another $70,000 from the victim&#8217;s savings. Small allegedly prepared checks for signature while claiming they covered bills and kept others from questioning the finances. She also allegedly used the client&#8217;s debit and credit cards for personal expenses such as children&#8217;s clothing and food deliveries. The victim authorized only grocery purchases and told investigators she never used a computer due to her vision impairment. Records indicate the total unauthorized transfers and purchases reached approximately $180,321. Small was taken into custody on August 17th and booked into the St. Johns County jail under a $340,000 bond with orders barring contact with the victim.
