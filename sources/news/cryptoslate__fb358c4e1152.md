The picture changed on July 8. Cantor Equity Partners I (CEPO), a publicly traded SPAC, and BSTR scrapped the deal’s original terms and began discussing a new structure . The private placements fell away, CEPO postponed its shareholder meeting indefinitely, and investors got their redemption shares back. BSTR and CEPO are still talking, though the financing behind the 30,021-BTC figure has unraveled.
