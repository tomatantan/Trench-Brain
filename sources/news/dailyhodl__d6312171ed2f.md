According to the report, the firm&#8217;s Q2 revenue climbed 92% to $7.8 billion, yet the company recorded a $541 million net loss alongside $18.4 billion in capital outlays. Of that spending, $15.8 billion targeted artificial-intelligence infrastructure, which weighed on investor sentiment toward near-term profitability. Market observers note that elevated investment demands in growth areas continue to pressure valuations even as top-line expansion remains robust.
