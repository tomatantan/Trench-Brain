The near-match puts the latest wallet activity at the center of a question about what remains of Trump Media’s quarter-end Bitcoin treasury once note collateral is separated out. Trump Media reported 9,542.16 BTC as of March 31, including 4,260.73 BTC serving as note collateral. Subtracting those figures produces 5,281.43 BTC, though the company did not label that difference liquid or unencumbered.
