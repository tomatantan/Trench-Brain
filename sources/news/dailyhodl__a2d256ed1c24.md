Trump Media and Technology Group (TMTG), Crypto.com and the special purpose acquisition company Yorkville Acquisition Corp have all agreed to abandon plans for a CRO treasury venture announced in Q2 2025, reports Axios . The companies note that &#8220;prevailing market conditions and shifting business and stakeholder priorities&#8221; ultimately led to the decision. TMTG interim CEO Kevin McGurn says the firm &#8220;wanted to get focused&#8221; amid the increasingly crowded digital asset treasury market. The companies also dropped plans to integrate prediction markets directly into Truth Social, but noted that Crypto.com&#8217;s prediction-market products will be marketed to the social media platform&#8217;s users. McGurn says the company now looks to efficiently monetize Truth Social&#8217;s audience and data while finalizing an all-stock merger with TAE Technologies valued at more than $6 billion. The December 2025 agreement with TAE aims to create one of the first publicly traded fusion companies and advance plans for a 50-megawatt utility-scale fusion power plant in 2026, subject to regulatory approvals. TAE Technologies, founded in 1998, develops hydrogen-boron fusion technology for abundant clean energy and has raised over $1 billion in funding from investors including Google and Chevron. McGurn is optimistic that the deal will close before 2026 expires.
